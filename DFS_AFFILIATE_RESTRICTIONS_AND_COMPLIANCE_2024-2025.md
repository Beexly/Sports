# DFS Sportsbook Affiliate Programs: Geographic Availability & Regulatory Restrictions (2024-2025)

**Document Date**: June 2026  
**Research Scope**: DraftKings, FanDuel, and major DFS sportsbook affiliate program availability, licensing requirements, state restrictions, international limitations, and compliance checkpoints.

---

## Executive Summary

- **Geographic Reach**: Affiliate programs for DraftKings and FanDuel are restricted to **38+ US states** + **Ontario, Canada**. No operations in EU/UK/Asia/Australia.
- **Licensing Reality**: Licensing is **mandatory in some states** (Arizona, Colorado, Nevada, New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania) and **optional/absent in others** (Iowa). Fees range from **free (NJ) to $2,500+ (PA, Michigan)**.
- **DFS vs. Sportsbook Affiliate Distinction**: DFS affiliates face minimal/no licensing in most states; sportsbook affiliates face state-by-state licensing requirements.
- **Compliance Floor**: AGA Responsible Marketing Code (March 2023 update) applies to all affiliates; no "risk-free" claims, 21+ targeting only, responsible gaming messaging mandatory.
- **Offshore Ban**: FanDuel and DraftKings explicitly ban affiliates from co-promoting offshore sportsbooks—violation triggers program termination and blocks future regulatory licensing.
- **Ontario Discontinuation**: FanDuel and DraftKings have **discontinued DFS in Ontario** citing regulatory rules limiting contests to in-province participants only.

## Part 3: Compliance & Regulatory Checkpoints

### 3.1 AGA Responsible Marketing Code (Applies to All Affiliates)

**Effective Date**: March 2023 (most recent update)  
**Scope**: Binds all affiliates and agents of AGA member operators.

**Key Prohibitions:**
- ❌ "Risk-free" bet language (banned March 2023)
- ❌ "Guaranteed wins" or similar certainty claims
- ❌ Targeting college-aged populations; no partnerships promoting wagering to colleges
- ❌ Targeting audiences under 21 in advertising
- ❌ False or misleading win-rate/performance claims without historical data

**Mandatory Requirements:**
- ✅ Responsible gaming helpline link (NCPG 1-800-GAMBLER) in all promotional materials
- ✅ Clear age verification (21+) in all affiliate materials
- ✅ Disclosure of terms and conditions in advertising
- ✅ Internal review process for affiliate marketing
- ✅ Training provision to affiliates on Code compliance

**Affiliate-Specific Obligations (AGA Code):**
1. Operators must distribute the Code to all third-party partners (affiliates, agencies, media buyers)
2. Operators must implement contractual compliance requirements in affiliate agreements
3. Affiliates must warrant compliance with AGA Code in their agreements
4. Monitoring and audit rights are contractually enforceable

**Enforcement:**
- Code Compliance Review Board (CCRB): 2 independent co-chairs + 5 AGA member reps (3-year terms)
- Self-regulatory (industry), not government-enforced, but violations can trigger regulatory penalties

**Reference**: [AGA Responsible Marketing Code Updates](https://www.americangaming.org/new-updates-to-aga-responsible-marketing-code-for-sports-wagering-prohibit-risk-free-enhance-college-aged-protections/)

---

### 3.2 State-Specific Advertising Restrictions

**States with Explicit Advertising Regulations:**
- **Illinois, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina**: Prohibit advertising in media outlets or channels where >X% of audience is reasonably expected to be under 18/21
- All states: Prohibit false/misleading claims and require responsible gaming messaging

**FTC Compliance:**
- No "risk-free" language (FTC enforcement aligns with AGA)
- No endorsement-based claims without substantiation
- Affiliate disclosure required when compensation flows

---

### 3.3 Affiliate Agreement Compliance Checkpoints

**Mandatory Affiliate Agreement Clauses** (per regulatory guidance):

1. **Permitted Markets**: Explicit list of states where affiliate can promote; geo-restriction enforcement
2. **Prohibited Conduct**:
   - No offshore sportsbook co-promotion
   - No false claims, no "guaranteed" outcomes
   - No targeting of minors
   - Compliance with AGA Code and state advertising law
3. **Responsible Gaming Messaging**: Affiliate must include RG helpline in all ads; "past performance ≠ future results"
4. **Monitoring & Audit Rights**: Operator reserves right to audit affiliate's website, ads, email lists, social media
5. **Termination for Breach**: Violation of geo-restrictions, false claims, or offshore promotion = immediate termination
6. **Data Protection & Privacy**: GDPR (if EU affiliate), CCPA (if CA resident), other state privacy laws
7. **Indemnification**: Affiliate warrants no illegal activity and indemnifies operator for third-party claims

**FanDuel Specific Requirement** (from Affiliate Terms):
> "By participating in the FanDuel Affiliate Program, you represent and warrant that your site does not currently promote any illegal activities, including offshore gambling."
> Violation triggers immediate program termination and regulatory licensing impact (suitable licensing assessment).

**DraftKings Specific Requirement**:
- Cannot use outside promotional codes not approved by DraftKings
- Traffic must originate from legal jurisdictions only
- No co-promotion of competing sportsbooks (implicit in affiliate exclusivity)

### 4.2 Ontario-Specific Restrictions (Important Change)

**DFS Discontinuation (2024-2025):**
- FanDuel and DraftKings have discontinued daily fantasy sports operations in Ontario
- Regulatory requirement: DFS contests must include only Ontario residents; cross-border liquidity pooling not permitted
- Result: Neither operator finds Ontario-only DFS economically viable

**Sportsbook Status**: Sportsbook operations continue in Ontario (regulated under iGaming Ontario, AGCO)

**Implication for GSE**: If GSE ever targets Ontario, affiliate promotion of DFS is not viable; sportsbooks only.

## Appendix C: "Risk-Free" Ban Examples (Prohibited Under AGA Code)

**PROHIBITED Phrases (as of March 2023):**
- "Risk-free bet"
- "Money-back guarantee"
- "Guaranteed win" / "Guaranteed profit"
- "Lock" (colloquial for sure bet)
- "No-risk" promotion
- Any language implying certainty of outcome

**ALLOWED Alternatives:**
- "Enhanced odds" / "Boosted odds"
- "Deposit bonus" / "Sign-up bonus"
- "First bet insurance" (describes actual mechanism, not guarantee)
- "Cashback offer"
- "Incentive promotion"
