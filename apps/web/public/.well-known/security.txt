# Guidelines
#
# - Report vulnerabilities privately via the Contact address above.
# - Do not exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to
#   demonstrate impact.
# - Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data belonging to other
#   accounts.
# - Do not run automated scanners against production at a volume that
#   degrades service.
#
# We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 business days and to provide a
# status update within 30 days. Coordinated disclosure timelines are
# negotiated case by case.
