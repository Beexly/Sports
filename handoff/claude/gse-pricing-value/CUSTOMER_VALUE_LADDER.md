### Free unlocks (trust preview — never the paid product)
Methodology preview · glossary/education · responsible-gaming framing · a limited
sample signal · partial board with paid rows gated · No-Bet examples · public
proof snippets · feature explainers.
**Free gates:** today's full board · full signal reasoning · confidence detail ·
line-movement history · props depth · alerts.

### Pro adds
Full daily board · core signal detail + factor trail · confidence reasoning ·
board filters · recent edge history · full No-Bet reasoning · proof ledger ·
Parlay MRI · basic alerts (where available).

### Elite adds
Galaxy Twin / Edge Map · Market Gravity + deeper movement · props depth (where
supported) · advanced filters + watchlists · calibration reports · CLV (where
available) · premium Academy + briefings · richer alerts.

### Operator adds (waitlist — launches when infra is real)
Exports · scenario analysis · board history · custom alerts · automation ·
exposure/portfolio tracking · priority support.

### Why each step up exists (no plan confusion)
- Free → Pro: the preview becomes the **daily product** (full board + reasoning).
- Pro → Elite: from reading the board to reading the **market behind it**.
- Elite → Operator: from understanding to **operating** (workflow control).

The final feel:
Free "I can see how they think." · Pro "I can use this daily." · Elite "I
understand the market better than alone." · Operator "I run my whole workflow."
