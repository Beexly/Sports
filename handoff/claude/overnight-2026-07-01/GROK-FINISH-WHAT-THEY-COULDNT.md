# Grok — Finish What They Couldn't (The Half-Finished Monuments) — 2026-07-02

The most ambitious prompt of the series, and the most rigorous — because raw
ambition without the contract produces hallucinated numbers (proven this
session). This channels the ambition through the rigor. Copy-paste below.

Thesis: the sports-prediction industry is a GRAVEYARD OF HALF-FINISHED
MONUMENTS — things everyone knows matter, that the incumbents (ESPN, Action
Network, the books, the touts, the analytics industry, academia) STARTED and
abandoned, botched, or are structurally forbidden from completing. GSE's play is
to FINISH them to a definitive standard that forces the whole industry to cite
us. Not features. Category ownership.

You are GSE's R&D division, and your mandate is category domination. Forget
incremental edges. Your job: find the HALF-FINISHED MONUMENTS of the sports-
prediction and sports-analytics world — the things everyone knows are important,
that the incumbents started and never finished, botched, or CANNOT finish — and
design the definitive completion that makes GSE the canonical reference the
entire industry is forced to cite. The bar is not "a good feature." The bar is
"the new standard; the thing a journalist, a quant, and a competitor all have to
acknowledge exists and is ours."

GSE (context): a live, solo-founder, trust-first sports-prediction platform whose
moat is VERIFIABLE HONESTY (SHA-256 pick receipts, public /verify, sealed-vault
backtests, honest calibration, a public loss ledger with reproducible BCa
confidence bands, a reconstruction engine that manufactures proprietary features
from cleared public data). We have NO conflict of interest — we don't take a
book's affiliate money, we don't hide losses, we don't sell certainty. That is a
WEAPON: we can finish exactly what the conflicted incumbents are forbidden from.

═══════════ THE THREE VEINS (mine all three) ═══════════
VEIN 1 — THE GRAVEYARD OF ABANDONED WORK. Academic papers with promising sports-
forecasting results that were never productionized; open-source projects that
died half-built; datasets built once and abandoned; methods proven in ONE sport
and never ported to the others; "everyone cites this 2011 paper but nobody
shipped it." Find the best corpses and design their resurrection to production.

VEIN 2 — STANDARDS NEVER TRANSPLANTED. Other adversarial-trust fields SOLVED
problems sports-prediction still fakes: finance (audited track records, GIPS
performance standards, tamper-evident reporting), medicine (pre-registration,
CONSORT, adverse-event disclosure), election integrity (risk-limiting audits,
public verifiability), open science (reproducibility, data provenance),
provably-fair gaming (cryptographic commitment). For each: the mature standard,
and the definitive transplant that makes GSE the first sports-prediction entity
to meet a REAL assurance standard — a thing regulators and press recognize.

VEIN 3 — THE CONFLICT-OF-INTEREST GAP (the deepest vein). The most powerful
"half-assed" category is what the incumbents CANNOT finish because completing it
would cannibalize them: a book cannot publish an honest map of its own
mispricing; an affiliate media site cannot rank books by sharpness because the
books pay it; a tout cannot show a losing month; a network selling picks cannot
run a public calibration curve that would expose it. GSE has none of these
conflicts. Enumerate the things the incumbents are STRUCTURALLY FORBIDDEN from
finishing, and design GSE's completion of each — the "we did the thing you're not
allowed to do" monuments. These are the ones that make people notice.

═══════════ THE COMPLETION DOSSIER (required for every idea) ═══════════
1. THE HALF-BUILT THING: what exists, done badly or abandoned — CITED (paper,
   repo, product, standard, with a link/quote). No "I think someone..." — find it.
2. THE DIAGNOSIS: exactly why it was never finished — laziness, funding,
   conflict-of-interest, regulatory chill, technical ceiling, or data gap. This
   diagnosis IS the moat; an idea with no good "why unfinished" is already done.
3. THE DEFINITIVE COMPLETION: the design that finishes it to a canonical
   standard — works across sports/markets, not a toy. The mechanism, in detail.
4. PROOF OF COMPLETION: the verifiable test that shows it is actually done and
   correct (data, metric, the confound controlled) — un-fudgeable.
5. THE SPECTACLE: the public, receipted, attention-DEMANDING form — the "we
   finished what you couldn't" artifact that forces notice. Libel-proof if it
   names a competitor (literal data only, never an adjective).
6. THE POST-COMPLETION MOAT: once GSE ships it, why can't a competitor just
   copy? (First-mover proof-history, conflict-of-interest they still have,
   standard-ownership, manufactured-data lead.)

═══════════ THE CONTRACT (a fabrication voids the round — this is how ambition stays real) ═══════════
- Primary source per factual claim, quoted/linked, or [SPECULATION]. Reality-
  Ladder tag everything. NUMBERS LAW: cited numbers with units, never invented.
- Legal conclusions about GSE conduct = [PROPOSED, counsel-required]; only a
  cited case/statute holding is [VERIFIED]; separate CFAA from contract/ToS;
  quote vendor/platform terms or mark [UNVERIFIED]. Verify LABELS, not just
  substance (you have mislabeled real sources before).
- MANUFACTURE-THE-DATA: an empty record is a monument to BUILD; give the inputs
  + method. CONFOUND CONTROL on every proof. No fake code / file trees / "shipped."

═══════════ FORCING FUNCTIONS (break past the obvious) ═══════════
- CANONICAL BAR: for each, state who would be FORCED to cite it (which
  journalist, which quant community, which regulator) and why they couldn't
  ignore it. If nobody is forced to notice, it is not a monument — cut it.
- CROSS-DOMAIN QUOTA: at least SIX distinct non-sports fields represented across
  your dossiers, with the exact method/standard transplanted.
- PRIOR-ART BURDEN: prove it is unfinished by SEARCHING for the finished version
  first; show the trail; if it exists, say who has it and how yours is
  definitively better, or cut it.
- SELF-ADVERSARIAL LOOP (show your rounds): generate >=25 candidate monuments;
  red-team each with its strongest kill-reason; keep only survivors; sharpen the
  survivors (dossier + prior-art trail + spectacle); repeat generate->kill->
  sharpen until a full round yields nothing new that survives. Report the rounds.

═══════════ DELIVER ═══════════
- The ranked COMPLETION DOSSIERS (most-noticed-and-defensible first), each full.
- THE CATEGORY WE OWN: if GSE finishes the top monuments, what CATEGORY does it
  become the canonical reference for — the "Baseball-Reference / Statcast /
  audited-fund of trust-first prediction" — and the 3 monuments that anchor it.
- THE FIRST MONUMENT: the single one to build first — highest notice x
  defensibility x buildability — defended against its strongest objection.
- BLIND SPOT: the monument WE (GSE) are structurally unable to see we should build.

Depth over breadth. A finished dossier with a cited half-built origin, a
prior-art trail, a proof, and a spectacle beats fifty slogans. The winners feel
audacious but are provably legal, complete something the incumbents literally
cannot, and carry an artifact that makes ignoring GSE impossible. Run the loop
until it is dry. Do not stop early. Go build the reference standard.
