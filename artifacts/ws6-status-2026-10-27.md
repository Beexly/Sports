## 2. Canonical 4-step wiring sequence

Full specification: `artifacts/rag-telemetry-wiring-2026-10-27.md` §2.

1. **Ingest** — validate schema, scrub PII/secrets, attach the runtime-resolved sealed hash.
2. **Append** — `append_delta_record(...)`, enforce `NO_OVERWRITE_VIOLATION` on `record.id`, flush
   with `os.fsync()`.
3. **Retrieve** — `retrieve_prior_claims(...)` by `sealed_hash` + subject, chronologically.
4. **Residual / overlay** — Path 4A only (Class A eligible, serializer parity green, official status
   change), then `applyInjuryOverlay()` and `assertTauSealed`.
