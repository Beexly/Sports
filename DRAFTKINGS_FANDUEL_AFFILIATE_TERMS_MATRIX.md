| **Geo-Blocking** | Traffic must originate from legal states only; no VPN/proxy bypass attempts | Real-time geo-blocking; traffic audits |
| **Responsible Gaming Messaging** | All promotions must include NCPG helpline link (1-800-GAMBLER) and "past performance" disclaimer | Content audits; affiliate website monitoring |
| **Approved Promo Codes Only** | Cannot use outside promotional codes; must request approval for custom codes | Operator review before launch |
| **No Offshore Co-Promotion** | Cannot promote DraftKings alongside offshore sportsbooks (Bovada, BetOnline, etc.) | Operator monitoring; breach = termination |
| **Disclosure of Terms** | Affiliate must clearly state terms, conditions, and wagering requirements in promotional materials | Marketing material submission required |
| **No False Claims** | No fabricated win rates, guaranteed profits, or beating the book claims | Content audit; immediate removal order |
| **State Licensing** | Affiliate responsible for obtaining required state affiliate licenses (varies by state) | Affiliate self-attestation; audit spot-checks |
| **Affiliate Agreement** | Mandatory signature of affiliate agreement; breach triggers termination | Legal enforcement; breach = account suspension |

## Part 6: Offshore Sportsbook Ban — Detailed Comparison

### DraftKings Position

**Policy**: Cannot co-promote DraftKings alongside unlicensed/offshore sportsbooks.

**Mechanism**: Operator monitoring of affiliate websites and traffic sources.

**Enforcement**: Account suspension (with potential cure period) → termination if not resolved.

**Regulatory Impact**: Termination may flag affiliate as unsuitable for future state gaming licenses (state commissions consult operator compliance records).

**Example**: Affiliate runs website promoting both "DraftKings + Bovada." DraftKings will require affiliate to choose: promote DraftKings only, or promote Bovada (offshore) only. If affiliate maintains both, account is suspended and terminated.

---

### FanDuel Position

**Policy (Explicit & Strict)**: "FanDuel no longer wishes to associate itself with websites that promote offshore gambling sites."

**Mechanism**: 
1. FanDuel discovers affiliate co-promoting offshore book (e.g., Bovada, BetOnline)
2. FanDuel sends 150-day notice: "You have 150 days to cease promoting [offshore book] OR cease promoting FanDuel"
3. Affiliate must choose one or the other
4. If affiliate fails to comply within 150 days, FanDuel terminates participation

**Enforcement**: Immediate account termination; commissions withheld; affiliate flagged as unsuitable for future gaming licenses.

**Regulatory Impact**: **Most Severe** — FanDuel coordinates with state gaming commissions; affiliate flagged in licensing databases; future affiliate applications in any state may be denied.

**Example Compliance Language (from FanDuel T&Cs)**:
> "By participating in the FanDuel Affiliate Program, you represent and warrant that your site does not currently promote any illegal activities, including offshore gambling. FanDuel will monitor your site for compliance. If FanDuel determines you are promoting offshore sportsbooks, FanDuel may require you to cease such promotion within 150 days, terminate your participation in the FanDuel Affiliate Program, and report your non-compliance to state gaming commissions, which may impact your suitability for future licensing."

## Part 10: GSE's Obligations as Affiliate Marketplace

### When GSE Promotes DraftKings/FanDuel Offers on Behalf of Affiliates

**GSE's Compliance Responsibility**:
1. **Screening**: Only allow affiliates into GSE marketplace who have signed their own affiliate agreement with DraftKings/FanDuel
2. **No Direct T&C Assumption**: GSE does not assume operator obligations; GSE is a curator/platform only
3. **Due Diligence**: GSE must verify affiliate identity, state licensing status (if applicable), and attestation of compliance
4. **Monitoring**: GSE must spot-check affiliate promotional content on GSE platform for:
   - Banned phrases (AGA Code violations)
   - Offshore co-promotion
   - False claims
5. **Escalation**: If violation detected, GSE must:
   - Notify affiliate (48 hours)
   - Notify DraftKings/FanDuel (if required by GSE's operator agreement)
   - Suspend promotion until cured
6. **Termination**: If affiliate refuses to cure, GSE must terminate affiliate relationship

**GSE's Agreement Language (Recommended)**:
> "GSE acts as an affiliate marketplace curator and does not substitute for individual affiliates' obligations to DraftKings, FanDuel, or other operators. Each affiliate is responsible for their own affiliate agreement compliance, state licensing, and marketing standards. GSE will monitor for gross violations (banned phrases, offshore co-promotion, false claims) and reserve the right to suspend or terminate any affiliate for non-compliance. GSE's monitoring is not a substitute for each operator's monitoring; operators retain independent audit and termination rights."
