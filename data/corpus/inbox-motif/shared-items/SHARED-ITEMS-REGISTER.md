## S87 — Timo Riske playoff-leverage correction (paired-flip construction) — via @Timo_NFL 10-post thread Oct 23, 2026, X corpus pass Oct 30, 2026
A methodological correction to a chart genre the whole industry publishes. Season simulations that update team ratings inside the sim and then compute playoff leverage by outcome-filtering (sims where the team won vs sims where it lost) inflate the number: the win filter also selects sims where the team drew a higher rating path, so it wins more future games for reasons unrelated to this game. His Week 7 measurement: the filter method inflates leverage by 1.3–1.6× per team (~1.3 floor for any team still in the hunt) — the factor is single-source and UNVERIFIED independently; the mechanism (conditioning on a post-outcome variable) is not in doubt. The correct construction is his documented PFF method (playoff-picture series, 2021–2025): paired simulations — copy each sim, flip only the game in question, difference the outcomes — or ratings frozen at pre-game values. Test of contamination: a game that truly doesn't matter should return leverage 0; the filter method returns a positive number anyway. FIT: GSE engine everything-else layer (season context / playoff picture) and a standing audit question for any third-party sim output GSE cites — which leverage construction produced this number? The bias explanation itself is publishable reasoning-as-product if GSE ever ships leverage content. VERDICT: LEARN-ONLY (method doctrine). Dossier: 2026-10-30-OVERNIGHT-CORPUS.md (D1).
