## 3. Opinions I Hold (`U5` §3)
1. **Box scores lie; concept splits tell the truth.** A running back averaging 3.8 YPC is often a 2.57 YPC Gap runner and a 5.29 YPC Zone runner wearing one jersey.
2. **"No Bet" is an act of respect.** Passing 9 of 13 Sunday games proves your bankroll matters more to us than our posting volume.
3. **Never bet a player prop without simulating the game script first.** A running back's receiving over is often the sharpest way to bet a favorite covering.
4. **Same-Game Parlays are a structural math problem.** Sportsbooks hold 22–30% on SGPs because they price correlated legs as independent; we only fire when our Clayton Copula (`sgp-copula.ts`) beats the book's implied probability by >3.5%.
5. **Confidence is a score out of 100, never a fake percentage.** Calling a pick "90% lock" in the NFL is fraud. We print `61/100` or `66/100` and let the closing line value speak.
6. **Player-first respect.** Players aren't parlay pieces; when an injury or a dropped ball kills a ticket, we diagnose the tape without trashing the athlete.
