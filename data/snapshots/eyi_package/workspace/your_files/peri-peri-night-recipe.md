# Homemade Peri Peri Night — serves 6, budget version
Target: ~$10–13 total if you have spices + oil on hand.

## CHEAP SWAPS (whole sheet in a nutshell)
- Leg quarters, family pack (~8 lbs at $0.60–0.90/lb). Cheapest cut, and dark meat is what peri joints serve.
- Long-grain white rice, not basmati.
- Green bell pepper, not red (half the price). Dried bird's eye chilies if fresh Thai chilies are pricey; cayenne powder in a pinch.
- Bouillon cubes/powder for the rice stock. Skip butter, all oil.
- Bagged lemons are cheapest per unit. Garlic heads, not pre-peeled.
- Regular paprika works; smoked is the signature flavor but not worth a $5 jar if you don't have it.

## SAUCES — all 3 from one base batch

**Base (makes ~4 cups; covers marinade + basting + serving)**
2 onions, 16 garlic cloves, 2 green bell peppers, 2/3 cup white vinegar, 1/2 cup oil, 2 tbsp paprika, 4 tsp oregano, 4 tsp salt, 2 tsp sugar, 3 bay leaves.
Blend smooth, simmer 15–20 min with bay, remove bay, re-blend, stir in zest + juice of 4 lemons (off heat — lemon in early = bitter).

**Split:**
- Mild: 4–6 bird's eyes (or 2 jalapeños), simmer 5 min, blend. Pull 2 cups for marinade BEFORE it touches raw chicken.
- Hot: 10–14 bird's eyes (+2 habaneros if the table wants fire), simmer 10 min, blend.
Salt/lemon to taste. Fridge 2–3 weeks; better the next day.

**Garlic sauce (toum)**
18 garlic cloves + 1/3 cup lemon juice + 1.5 tsp salt → blend to paste. With blender running, drizzle in 3 cups cheap veg oil in a slow stream until thick and white. Still runny = oil went too fast. Breaks? Start with 2 tbsp ice water in clean blender, blend the broken sauce back in. Fridge 2 weeks; 10 min at room temp before serving.

## CHICKEN
6–8 leg quarters (or 12–15 thighs). Score, rub with 2 cups mild sauce + juice of 2 lemons + 1/4 cup oil. Marinate 2 hrs minimum — overnight next time is the restaurant match (24-hr marinate + open-flame char is their real edge).
Grill/pan medium-high: skin down 6–8 min for char, flip, to 165°F. Baste with CLEAN hot sauce at the end (reserve 3/4 cup before marinating). Batch it; hold done chicken in a low oven. Rest 5 min.

## RICE
3 tbsp oil → soften 1 big diced onion + 4 garlic + 1 green bell pepper (5 min). Bloom: 1 tbsp turmeric, 1.5 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, pinch cayenne + 3 tbsp mild sauce (1 min). Stir in 3 cups rinsed long-grain, add 6 cups bouillon-stock, boil, lid, low 12–15 min. 1.5 cups frozen peas in the last 5 (optional). Rest 5, fluff.

## TIMELINE
6:40 — sauces (base, split, garlic)
7:10 — chicken marinating, rice on
7:30 — grill in batches
~7:55 — eat. Plate rice → chicken → all 3 sauces in bowls so everyone sets their own heat.
