## Disability provisions this application captures
1. **No gross-income test.** Households with a disabled member skip SNAP's gross-income limit entirely — only *net* income (after deductions) must be under the limit. This is the single most important box: Section K, Q1 = Yes.
2. **Medical expense deduction (Section Q).** Out-of-pocket medical costs over $35/mo are deducted from countable income. List everything.
3. **No cap on the shelter deduction.** For most households the excess-shelter deduction is capped; for elderly/disabled households it is **uncapped** — high rent + utilities help more.
4. **No ABAWD work requirement.** The 80-hours/month work rule for adults 18–54 without dependents does not apply to people with disabilities.
5. **Phone interview.** Section E lets you claim the disability reason and do the required interview by phone — no office trip.
6. **Vehicle exclusion.** If your vehicle is used for disability-related transportation, check the box in Section M.
