## Challenge pass caught a scaling defect in v1 — killed, fixed, proven
v1 used integer wavenumbers as the derivative operator, which silently places the flow on a
2π box and makes the effective Reynolds number ≈ 628, not the specified 100. Its symptom was
suspicious: 45 of 46 draws "rose" (R ∈ [1.05, 1.28]). The v1 run was **killed at 46 draws and
preserved as a labeled rejected artifact** (`ns100_draws_v1_2pi_convention_REJECTED.jsonl`),
not deleted and not shipped as results.

v2 uses the unit box (physical wavenumber = 2π × integer mode), so ν=0.01, U≈1, L=1 gives
Re=100 exactly. Before any production draw, v2 passed a 4-test validation battery with known
answers (**4/4**):
- **Diffusion exactness:** shear-mode amplitude ratio 0.673825 vs analytic e^(−ν(2π)²T) = 0.673825 — agreement to 5.4×10⁻¹³.
- **Divergence preservation:** |k·û|/|û| ≈ 9×10⁻¹⁶ before and after stepping.
- **Hou-Li filter:** ρ(0)=1 exactly; ρ(single-axis Nyquist) = e⁻³⁶ = 2.3195×10⁻¹⁶ exactly.
- **Taylor-Green:** E₀ = 0.125 (the theoretical 1/8), strictly monotone decay over T=0.5.

## What this is not
A diagnostic continuation harness at Re=100, T=1.0 — not evidence about finite-time blowup
in either direction. The BKM integral ∫‖ω‖∞dτ stays O(15) here, orders below any blowup
signature; the honest reading is that smooth random draws at these parameters amplify
vorticity transiently and then dissipate, exactly as classical theory expects.
