The thesis

Most picks sites compete on volume of bold claims. We compete on the math you can audit.

Every pick we publish ships with the model version, the confidence range, the risk profile, the matchup context, the factor breakdown, and the freshness timestamp on the data we used. The public Performance page exposes the same record the model calibrates on — gated until enough canonical outcomes have settled to make a record meaningful.

The pipeline

Four phases. Every one visible.

No black box, no proprietary hand-waving. Each phase declares its inputs, its outputs, and the gate that controls whether it runs at all.

  1. Phase 01 Ingest

    Pull live markets from multiple sportsbooks.

    The pipeline runs on a 30-minute cadence (more often pre-kickoff). Every odds row is timestamped with bookmaker coverage so the pick card can show you how thin or deep the market actually is.

    Inputs
    Live odds across 7 sports
    Outputs
    Normalized markets, timestamped per fetch
    Gated by
    no gate — runs always
  2. Phase 02 Score

    Project the edge with explicit uncertainty.

    The engine computes implied probabilities, weighs sharp line movement, evaluates head-to-head context, and looks at venue form. Each contributor is exposed in the factor breakdown.

    Inputs
    Normalized markets + canonical team-game logs
    Outputs
    Confidence range, edge, risk profile
    Gated by
    DERIVED_MODEL_HISTORY_ENABLED
  3. Phase 03 Publish

    Ship the pick — with its reasoning attached.

    A pick is only published when the public-picks gate is open. Until calibration completes, confidence is shown as a label (Lean / Strong / Top Pick), never a number, and the page surfaces a Collecting note.

    Inputs
    Engine output + readiness gates
    Outputs
    The card you see — pick, line, risk, freshness
    Gated by
    PUBLIC_PICKS_ENABLED
  4. Phase 04 Calibrate

    Learn from outcomes, not from prior reasoning.

    The model only learns from real settlement results paired with the signal state at prediction time. When a proposed weight change improves out-of-sample calibration, it lands in a versioned model bump — reviewed by a human, surfaced on every pick card.

    Inputs
    Settled outcomes + PickSignalSnapshot
    Outputs
    A calibration proposal, never a silent weight change
    Gated by
    OUTCOME_LEARNING_ENABLED

How we evaluate picks

No social proof. No fabricated testimonials.

Just a plain description of what the engine does, and what we choose to show you.

Live odds ingestion

We ingest live odds from multiple sportsbooks on a regular schedule and score every available matchup.

Bookmaker coverage as a transparency signal

Every pick exposes how many sportsbooks cover the market, so you can see how thin or deep the consensus is.

Data freshness on every pick

Every pick carries a timestamp showing when the odds it scored against were last pulled.

Calibrated confidence presentation

Until we have enough settled outcomes to calibrate against, confidence is shown as a label, not a number.

Risk level on every pick

Spread of confidence and market volatility surface as an explicit risk profile per card.

Public performance is gated, not advertised
