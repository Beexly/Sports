# Domain Glossary - Galaxy Sports Edge (GSE) & Galaxy Science Institute (GSI)

Canonical ubiquitous language shared across GSE and GSI. Definitions stay strictly pure-domain (devoid of implementation paths, database table names, or transient weekly record counts).

## Sports & Market Domain (`GSE`)

**Market Foundation Layer**:
The consensus opening or live de-vigged market line treated as the baseline prior before situational reasoning is applied.
_Avoid_: Uncalibrated raw model baseline, Layer 0 guesses.

**Situational Overlay**:
Structured contextual adjustment derived from verified coaching tendencies, trench pressure disruption, active injury availability, weather physics, and rest/travel differentials prior to simulation.
_Avoid_: Post-hoc fudge factor, manual override.

**De-Vigged Fair Probability**:
The bookmaker-margin-removed implied probability of a two-sided market outcome computed via Shin closed-form or multiplicative power de-vigging.
_Avoid_: Raw implied odds, single-sided synthetic price.

**Conjunction Gate**:
The deterministic final decision boundary requiring calibrated probability, positive expected value against the two-sided de-vigged market, independent estimator agreement, anti-cannibalization clearance, and conformal bound clearance before publication.
_Avoid_: Heuristic filter, confidence threshold alone.

**Pass Discipline**:
The mandatory invariant that any side, total, player prop, or same-game parlay failing the Conjunction Gate receives status `PASS` with publication blocked rather than being forced onto the board.
_Avoid_: Lean-only filler, forced card fill.

**Call-Sheet Ticket**:
A publication-cleared wager or player prop backed by full-game snap-by-snap simulation, source-grounded context, and signed conformal bounds.
_Avoid_: Unverified lean, unpriced play.

**360-Degree Full-Board Review**:
Comprehensive evaluation of every market suite on a slate: quarters, halves, full-game sides/totals, quarterback and skill props, kicking markets, individual defensive player (`IDP`) sacks/tackles, and touchdown scorer markets.
_Avoid_: Mainlines-only review.

**Sealed Slate Packet**:
An immutable, policy-cleared weekly window snapshot holding verified plays, 360-degree watchlist triggers, honest passes, plain-English matchup breakdowns, and DFS pool projections.
_Avoid_: Stale static JSON card, week-hardcoded fixture.

**One-Time Slate Pack**:
A single-payment commercial offering granting time-bounded entitlement to a specific NFL slate window and its unlocked Call-Sheet Tickets.
_Avoid_: Unscoped global unlock, lifetime subscription.

**Entitlement**:
The resolved access tier and active pack capabilities granted to a buyer from either a recurring Subscription or an unexpired verified One-Time Slate Pack purchase.
_Avoid_: Subscription-only check, client-side trust flag.

**Three-Noon Master Lock**:
The canonical weekly content-ready lock schedule locking Thursday Night Football by Wednesday `12:00 PM CT`, all Sunday games by Friday `12:00 PM CT`, and Monday Night Football by Sunday `12:00 PM CT`.
_Avoid_: Late Sunday morning bulk runs, ad-hoc midnight locks.

**T-90m Pre-Kickoff Adjustment**:
A differential-only re-evaluation triggered ninety minutes before each kickoff window strictly against official NFL inactive announcements, late weather shifts, and market steam.
_Avoid_: Full-slate full rebuild at T-90m.

**Governed Receipt**:
A deterministic SHA-256 and Ed25519 cryptographically signed policy attestation recording why a candidate slate packet was admitted or refused before publication.
_Avoid_: Best-effort log line, shadow-only audit.

**Vacated-Volume Conservation**:
Exact redistribution of injured or inactive starters' historical target and carry shares across healthy active depth-chart teammates with bounded residual attribution and zero phantom bucket sinks.
_Avoid_: Phantom team sink, flat scaling across injured backups.

**CollegeGuard**:
The domain isolation boundary separating college football variance and home-field parameters from NFL probability calibration heads.
_Avoid_: Mixed-league pooling.

## Dynamical Systems & Biophysical Domain (`GSI`)

**Phase-Transition Criticality**:
Measurable pre-collapse regime of a coupled dynamical system characterized by rising susceptibility, spectral radius approaching unity, and slowing recovery rates.

**Spin-Glass Susceptibility**:
Frustration metric quantifying how close an interacting network is to structural breakdown under external stress.

**Ollivier-Ricci Curvature**:
Wasserstein-1 transport curvature across graph edges quantifying whether pathways are bottlenecked or redundantly supported.

**Sulcal Waveguide Focusing**:
Geometric concentration of shear wave energy along curved stadium or anatomical boundaries.

**Kelvin Circulation Anomaly**:
Departure from ideal circulation conservation across a fluid or hemodynamic loop.

**Comparator Challenge**:
Formal specification verification test detecting whether a candidate mathematical policy is genuinely faithful or a trivial tautology.
