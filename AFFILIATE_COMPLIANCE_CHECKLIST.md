# GSE Affiliate Program: Compliance Checklist & Quick Reference

**Last Updated**: June 22, 2026  
**Purpose**: Quick operational checklist for GSE affiliate onboarding, monitoring, and policy enforcement.

---

## Pre-Launch Checklist (Legal & Operational Setup)

### Legal & Contract
- [ ] Affiliate Agreement drafted with qualified sports/gaming counsel
- [ ] Agreement includes:
  - [ ] AGA Responsible Marketing Code compliance clause
  - [ ] Geo-restriction language (state-by-state allow-list)
  - [ ] Offshore sportsbook co-promotion ban (explicit, with 150-day termination clause)
  - [ ] Indemnification clause (affiliate warrants no illegal activity)
  - [ ] Monitoring & audit rights reserved
  - [ ] Termination triggers documented (false claims, geo-violation, offshore co-promotion)
- [ ] Terms reviewed by attorney; ready for signature
- [ ] Affiliate program Terms of Service published on GSE site

### State Licensing & Regulatory
- [ ] State licensing guide published (which states require affiliate licensing, fees, application steps)
- [ ] Affiliate onboarding form includes affidavit: "I have reviewed state licensing requirements for my jurisdiction(s)"
- [ ] Affiliate dashboard flags licensing requirements for their target states
- [ ] Disclaimer added: "GSE does not provide legal advice; affiliates responsible for state licensing compliance"

### Brand & Marketing Standards
- [ ] Approved promotional language examples published (showing AGA-compliant phrasing)
- [ ] Banned phrase list published (no "risk-free," "guaranteed," etc.)
- [ ] Responsible gaming messaging template provided (NCPG link, "past performance" disclaimer)
- [ ] Logo/brand guidelines confirm 21+ targeting only
- [ ] Social media content guidelines include geo-targeting restrictions

### Technical Setup
- [ ] Affiliate referral links include geo-blocking (no traffic from prohibited states)
- [ ] Geo-blocking tested on at least 5 prohibited states (Arizona IP, California IP, etc.)
- [ ] Affiliate dashboard displays state restrictions for each partner (DraftKings, FanDuel)
- [ ] Monitoring system configured: daily scan of affiliate websites/social for banned phrases
- [ ] Termination workflow documented (legal notice, 10-day cure period, then account suspension)

### Operator Coordination
- [ ] DraftKings Affiliate Program Terms reviewed; compliance mapped
- [ ] FanDuel Affiliate Program Terms reviewed; compliance mapped
- [ ] Written communication sent to both: GSE is affiliate promotional hub; confirm compliance expectations
- [ ] Quarterly compliance reporting plan established

## Affiliate Onboarding Checklist

### For Every New Affiliate:
1. **Before Approval**
   - [ ] Affiliate completes onboarding form (affidavit on state licensing, no offshore co-promotion)
   - [ ] Affiliate confirms they are 21+ and legal US resident (or Ontario resident)
   - [ ] Target states provided; GSE maps to allowed states for DraftKings, FanDuel
   - [ ] Affiliate T&C signature collected
   - [ ] Affiliate completes AGA Code training (or attestation they've read it)

2. **Approval**
   - [ ] Affiliate account activated
   - [ ] Personalized state restriction list emailed (e.g., "Your traffic must come from: AZ, CO, CT, IL, IN...")
   - [ ] Approved promotional materials (templates, sample posts) provided
   - [ ] Affiliate dashboard walkthrough (geo-restrictions, RG messaging, banned phrases)
   - [ ] Support contact info provided (compliance@gse.com)

3. **First 30 Days (Monitoring)**
   - [ ] Affiliate's website/social media audited manually (banned phrases scan)
   - [ ] Referral traffic spot-checked (geo-blocking verification; are clicks coming only from legal states?)
   - [ ] Performance metrics reviewed (unusual patterns flagged)
   - [ ] Affiliate sent reminder: compliance expectations, reporting mechanism for questions

## Ongoing Monitoring Checklist (Monthly/Quarterly)

### Monthly (Automated + Spot-Check)
- [ ] **Traffic Audit**: Sample 50 affiliates; verify referral traffic originates only from allowed states
  - Tool: IP geolocation on referral source check
  - Violation: Refer to legal for potential termination
- [ ] **Phrase Scan**: Automated scan of affiliate promotional content for banned phrases
  - Monitor: Affiliate websites, social posts (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram)
  - Flag: Any "risk-free," "guarantee," "lock," "sure bet" language
  - Action: Email affiliate within 48 hours; require correction within 5 business days
- [ ] **RG Messaging Check**: Spot-check 25 affiliates; confirm NCPG link and "past performance" disclaimer present
  - Violation: Request correction; document in audit log
- [ ] **Offshore Co-Promotion Scan**: Search affiliate domain + "offshore" or "Bovada" or known offshore books
  - Violation: Immediate escalation; legal review for termination

### Quarterly (Strategic Review)
- [ ] State licensing requirement changes? (Subscribe to state gaming commission alerts)
  - Update affiliate guide; notify all affiliates in affected states
- [ ] AGA Code updates? (Subscribe to AGA website)
  - Review changes; update affiliate training materials
- [ ] Operator (DraftKings/FanDuel) terms changes?
  - Map changes to GSE compliance; communicate to affiliates
- [ ] Affiliate complaints/escalations review
  - Are there patterns? (e.g., repeated geo-blocking failures in one state = investigation)
- [ ] Compliance metrics report
  - Total active affiliates / affiliates terminated for cause / violations flagged
  - Share with DraftKings, FanDuel, GSE leadership

### Annually
- [ ] Legal review of affiliate agreement (are we capturing new regulations?)
- [ ] Affiliate education webinar (AGA Code, state updates, case studies)
- [ ] Affiliate survey (what are their compliance pain points? Gaps in guidance?)

## Affiliate Violation Response Flowchart

```
VIOLATION DETECTED (geo-blocking, banned phrase, offshore co-promotion, etc.)

    ↓
    
1. SEVERITY ASSESS
   ├─ 🔴 CRITICAL (offshore co-promo, offshore traffic, targeting minors, repeated geo-violations)
   │  └─ → IMMEDIATE: Legal review for termination
   │     → Send 7-day cure notice (optional; operator discretion)
   │     → If not cured, terminate account + flag for operator notification
   │
   └─ 🟠 MODERATE (single geo-violation, banned phrase, missing RG link, single-incident pattern)
      └─ → 48-hour notice to affiliate
         → 5-business-day cure period
         → Escalate if not cured

    ↓

2. NOTICE SENT
   ├─ Document violation (screenshot, URL, timestamp)
   ├─ Email affiliate with:
   │  ├─ Violation description + evidence
   │  ├─ Requirement & policy reference (AGA Code, GSE T&Cs, state law)
   │  ├─ Cure deadline & remediation steps
   │  ├─ Escalation warning (repeated violations = termination)
   │
   └─ Log in Compliance Audit Trail

    ↓

3. CURE PERIOD
   ├─ Affiliate has 5 business days (or 7 days for critical) to respond/cure
   ├─ GSE follows up mid-period (friendly check-in)
   ├─ If affiliate responds with questions, support@compliance provides guidance
   └─ If no response by deadline → escalate to legal

    ↓

4. OUTCOME
   ├─ ✅ CURED: Affiliate corrects issue; account remains active; incident logged
   ├─ ⚠️ PARTIAL CURE: Issue partially addressed; re-audit required; warnings escalated
   └─ ❌ NOT CURED: Escalate to legal; prepare termination notice (10-day final notice recommended)
      └─ If terminated: operator notified; account suspended; compliance report updated

    ↓

5. TERMINATION (If Necessary)
   ├─ Final 10-day notice sent (email + certified mail)
   ├─ Affiliate given last chance to respond
   ├─ If no cure, account suspended + affiliate removed from dashboard
   ├─ Operator notification sent (DraftKings, FanDuel)
   ├─ Incident documented in audit trail
   └─ Compliance report updated

```

## Common Compliance Violations & Remediation

| Violation | Severity | Cure | Policy Ref |
|-----------|----------|------|-----------|
| **Geo-violation**: Traffic from prohibited state (UT, HI, WA, etc.) | 🔴 CRITICAL | Geo-blocking fix + revert traffic 30 days; audit affiliate's ad settings | State law; GSE T&Cs |
| **Banned phrase**: "Risk-free bet," "guaranteed," "lock" | 🟠 MODERATE | Remove all instances within 5 business days; attestation that copy updated | AGA Code (March 2023) |
| **Missing RG messaging**: NCPG link not visible in ads/site | 🟠 MODERATE | Add NCPG link + "past performance disclaimer" within 5 days | AGA Code; GSE Affiliate T&Cs |
| **Offshore co-promotion**: Website promotes Bovada, BetOnline, etc. alongside DraftKings/FanDuel | 🔴 CRITICAL | Immediate removal; written attestation that offshore book removed; 150-day window to terminate one or the other | FanDuel T&Cs; GSE Affiliate Clause |
| **False performance claims**: "90% win rate," "beat the book guaranteed" | 🔴 CRITICAL | Immediate removal; written attestation; legal review for termination | AGA Code; FTC Act; State UDAPs |
| **Targeting minors**: Content on college campuses, TikTok ads without 21+ gate, etc. | 🔴 CRITICAL | Immediate review & removal; escalate to legal | AGA Code; state ad law |
| **Promoting to prohibited state**: Running ads in California (where sportsbook illegal) | 🔴 CRITICAL | Cease ad campaigns; geo-restrict ads to legal states; audit account for other violations | State law; operator T&Cs |
| **Missing state licensing disclaimer**: Affiliate in state requiring licensing but no mention | 🟠 MODERATE | Add disclaimer: "Affiliate has obtained required state license(s) as of [date]" | State law; GSE guidance |

## Affiliate Termination Letter Template

```
Subject: NOTICE OF TERMINATION — [Affiliate Name] GSE Affiliate Program

Dear [Affiliate Name],

Effective [date + 10 business days], your participation in the GSE Affiliate Program is TERMINATED due to material breach of the Affiliate Agreement dated [date].

REASON FOR TERMINATION:
[Specific violation, with dates/evidence]

EXAMPLES:
- Promotion of offshore gambling sites (Bovada, BetOnline) alongside DraftKings/FanDuel (prohibited under Affiliate Agreement Section 4.2)
- Use of banned marketing language ("risk-free bet") on [website URL] dated [date] (prohibited under AGA Responsible Marketing Code)
- Geo-violation: Promotional traffic sourced from Utah (prohibited state) on [date] (prohibited under Affiliate Agreement Section 3.1)

REQUIRED ACTIONS (by [date]):
1. Cease all promotion of GSE, DraftKings, and FanDuel within 48 hours
2. Remove all GSE/DraftKings/FanDuel promotional content from your website and social media within 5 business days
3. Return all promotional materials and logos
4. Provide written attestation that all prohibited activity has ceased (offshore co-promotion, banned language, etc.)

ACCOUNT STATUS:
- Your affiliate dashboard is suspended as of [date]
- All pending commissions through [date] will be paid per original schedule (if applicable)
- Future commission payments are withheld pending completion of required actions

APPEALS:
If you believe this termination is in error, you may submit a written appeal to compliance@gse.com within 10 business days, including:
- Explanation of cure/corrective action taken
- Evidence that violation has been resolved
- Request for account reinstatement review

Your appeal will be reviewed by GSE legal within 10 business days.

REGULATORY NOTIFICATION:
GSE is required to notify DraftKings Affiliate Program and FanDuel Affiliate Program of this termination per our Operating Agreements. This may impact your suitability for future affiliate participation with these operators.

If you have questions, contact compliance@gse.com.

Regards,
[GSE Compliance Team]

cc: Legal; Operators (DraftKings, FanDuel)
```

## State Licensing Quick Reference (Affiliate Onboarding)

When affiliates ask, "Do I need a license?", use this matrix:

| State | Sportsbook Legal? | Affiliate License Required? | If CPA | If RevShare | Estimated Cost | Affiliate Action |
|-------|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| AZ | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | $1,500 initial + $500 renewal | File with AZ Dept Gaming; 6-8 weeks |
| CO | Yes | Yes (CPA) / Yes (RevShare) | $350 | $500+ | $350–$2,000 | File with CO Limited Gaming Control Comm |
| IN | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | ~$500 | File with IN Gaming Commission |
| IA | Yes | No | Free | Free | Free | No action required |
| LA | Yes | Varies | Varies | Varies | Varies | Check with state; 3/50 parishes restrict |
| MI | Yes | Yes | $200 | $2,500+ $5,000 annual | $200–$7,500+ | File with MI Gaming Control Bd |
| NJ | Yes | Yes (Free reg.) | Free | $2,000 | Free–$2,000 | Operator files on your behalf (free); RevShare requires separate Ancillary license |
| NV | Yes | Unclear (historically $3,000) | Unclear | Unclear | $3,000 (historical) | Check with Nevada Gaming Control Bd |
| NY | Yes | Yes | Varies | Varies | Varies | Local gaming advertising license required in some cases; check with state |
| PA | Yes | Yes (likely RevShare) | Unclear | $2,500 | $2,500+ | File with PA Gaming Control Bd |

**Affiliate Guidance**:
> "GSE does not provide legal or tax advice. Before promoting in any state, you are responsible for:
> 1. Verifying whether your state requires affiliate licensing
> 2. Obtaining necessary licenses or permits
> 3. Complying with state advertising laws and responsible gaming requirements
> 
> If in doubt, contact your state's gaming commission directly. Links to state regulators: [links]"
