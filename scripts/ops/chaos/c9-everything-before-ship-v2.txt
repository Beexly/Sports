For every factual claim you make, you must be able to say what you would OBSERVE that distinguishes it from its opposite. If you cannot, mark the claim INFERRED, not verified.

    FOUR of five leaves lose to the global mean. The weighted leaf model (0.2440) beats it only because the 10+ leaf carries the other four.
    The 6.5-9.5 leaf also drifts hard: train base 65.86%, test actual 57.12%, n=576, z=-4.42, p=9.7e-06 (Bonferroni 4.9e-05).
    The UNIFORM COMMON-DRIFT MODEL is formally rejected, not hand-waved: Cochran's Q = 19.57 on 4 df, p = 6.07e-04, I-squared = 79.6%; and 6.5-9.5 (-8.74pp) against the other four pooled (+0.59pp) gives z = -4.17, p = 3.03e-05. Scope it precisely: Q tests only whether the five leaf drifts share ONE common value, so rejecting it does NOT rule out a league-wide effect — a league-wide component can coexist with leaf-specific ones and Q cannot separate them.
