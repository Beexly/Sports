PRECISION NOTE ON THAT NUMBER, because it is easy to get wrong: the AUC 0.4965 came from

today show a visitor what the replay found.

11 MARKET SLICES TESTED (favourite/underdog, four spread bands, over/under, four total

CLV IS UNMEASURED, NOT DISPROVEN. Our entry line IS the closing line by construction, so

ONE OPEN ANOMALY, now characterised: the calibration leaf covering spreads 6.5-9.5 drifts

concentrated in that one band and the mechanism is unexplained. The leaf model still beats
the global mean overall (0.2440 vs 0.2478), but that margin is carried entirely by the 10+
leaf (0.1979); it survives the other four rather than being helped by them.
