## Statement (CRC-S, finite abelian case)

Let G be a finite connected graph, A a compact abelian structure group
(here A = U(1) or SO(2)), and {θ_e} edge angles defining a connection.

Define cycle residual on an oriented cycle basis {γ_i}:

r_i = (Σ_{e∈γ_i} ε_e θ_e) mod 2π.

**Definition.** The connection is **CRC-flat** if r_i = 0 for all i.

**Proposition 1 (Holonomy ↔ residuals).**  
For abelian A, Hol_γ_i = I iff r_i = 0.
