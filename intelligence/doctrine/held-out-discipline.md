# Held-out discipline

A fit that includes the rows it is scored on is not a validation. Report it as contaminated and discard the number. Do not average it with the held-out number.

Reference, measured 2026-10-02: TauFitter fit on 2022-2026 and scored on 2024-2025 opponent-half fourth downs produced +13.47 percentage points. The same scorer, trained only on 2022-2023, produced +6.7703 on n=3,988. The 13.47 figure was not recorded as the gate. The served tau_hat.csv is a separate point fit and is not that gate.

Do not refit the served table to make the held-out number appear on it.
