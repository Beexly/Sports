=== THE VAULT LESSONS (documented for teaching) ===

LESSON 1 — THE ENGINE IS ONE EQUATION.
DK's entire prop market = Φ((L−μ)/σ) per player-stat, hold applied per rung.
262/266 players reconstructed at r² 0.96-0.99. Once you fit (μ,σ) from the ladder,
you can compute DK's fair price at ANY rung — including rungs they haven't posted.

LESSON 2 — THE POSTED LINE IS NOT THE MEDIAN, BUT IT'S CLOSE (base rate vs edge set).
Across 262 fits: median (dk_stat − μ_fit) = −0.4, 49% above/below split.
BUT 183/262 sit >0.15σ off — the shade DISPERSION is the signal. Jack Foley +2.71σ,
Dylan Raiola −2.41σ. Those outliers = DK's own model disagreeing with itself across rungs,
or stale lines — either way they're the live-edge candidate list (dk_shade_outliers.json).

LESSON 3 — DK PRICES VARIANCE TIGHT (σ_ratio median 0.95, empirically 0.91-0.95).
The book's σ is ~5-9% BELOW realized player σ. Underpricing variance = systematically
cheap OUTCOME TAILS. Over-priced mains, under-priced alts 3+ rungs out. This compounds
with Lesson 4.

LESSON 4 — SEPARATE BASE RATE FROM EDGE SET.
The "+34 shade" number came from a 7-player file SELECTED for big gaps. The 262-fit base
rate says shade ≈ 0. Teaching students to spot selection bias in their own artifacts is
worth more than any single equation.

LESSON 5 — THE WAF IS A PROTOCOL CHECK, NOT A WALL.
DK: h1→403, h2→200, same bytes. Akamai fingerprints the TLS/ALPN layer FIRST; everything
else (cookies, JS) is downstream. Test protocol layer before touching auth.

LESSON 6 — THE CLIENT SHIPS NO ENGINE.
3.75MB app.js + 22 chunks: UI, routing, state. Zero math. Every sportsbook works this way
(Kambi-family, nash BFF). The "secret equations" live server-side; the client leaks them
only through OUTPUTS (ladders, trueOdds fields, ownership aggregates). Harvest outputs,
fit the engine yourself — that's the whole game.

LESSON 7 — THE FIELD IS THE LAST PARAMETER.
μ, σ, hold — all book-side. The last unknown is ownership (what everyone else believes).
The census (tonight's mini-MAX pull) closes that loop. Edge = |your p − book's p| ×
|book's p − field's p| divergence, sized by posterior-Kelly.
