## Key Findings Summary

### Geographic Availability

**North America Only**:
- DraftKings and FanDuel affiliate programs operate in **38 US states + Ontario, Canada** (sportsbook only)
- **No international expansion**: UK, EU, Australia, Asia — all unavailable for affiliate programs
- **Ontario DFS Discontinued**: Both operators discontinued DFS in Ontario citing regulatory constraints (contests limited to in-province participants only)

**Prohibited States** (12):
Alaska, Alabama, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Mississippi, Nevada, New Mexico, South Dakota, Utah (constitutional ban), Washington

---

### State-by-State Affiliate Licensing

**Free Licensing**:
- New Jersey (vendor registration; operator must submit on affiliate's behalf)

**Low-Cost ($200–$500)**:
- Colorado ($350 for CPA basis)
- Indiana ($500)
- Michigan ($200 for CPA)
- Tennessee ($500, reported)
- Virginia ($500, reported)

**Medium-Cost ($1,000–$1,500)**:
- Arizona ($1,500 initial + $500 renewal)
- West Virginia ($1,000)

**High-Cost ($2,000+)**:
- New Jersey RevShare ($2,000)
- Michigan RevShare ($2,500–$5,000 + $5,000 annual)
- Pennsylvania ($2,500+)

**No State Licensing Required**:
- Iowa (no state requirement for marketing affiliates)

**States with Limited/Unclear Guidance**:
Most states do not publish detailed affiliate licensing requirements; affiliates must contact state gaming commissions directly.

---

### AGA Responsible Marketing Code Compliance (March 2023 Update)

**Mandatory Requirements** (all affiliates):
- ❌ No "risk-free," "guarantee," "lock," or similar certainty claims
- ✅ 21+ targeting only; no college partnerships or minor targeting
- ✅ NCPG helpline link (1-800-GAMBLER) in all promotional materials
- ✅ Disclosure of terms and wagering requirements
- ✅ Responsible gaming messaging ("past performance ≠ future results")
- ✅ Internal compliance review process (operator responsibility)
- ✅ Training and distribution of Code to affiliates and agents

**Enforcement**:
- Self-regulated by AGA Code Compliance Review Board (2 independent co-chairs + 5 AGA member reps)
- Violations can trigger state regulatory penalties; operators hold affiliates accountable via contract

---

### Offshore Sportsbook Ban (Critical Finding)

**FanDuel Position** (Strictest):
- Explicit 150-day notice to cease co-promoting offshore sportsbooks OR cease FanDuel affiliation
- Failure to comply triggers:
  - Immediate termination
  - Regulatory coordination with state gaming commissions
  - **Affiliate flagged as unsuitable for future state gaming licenses**
- Language in T&Cs: "By participating in FanDuel Affiliate Program, you warrant your site does not promote illegal activities including offshore gambling"

**DraftKings Position** (Strict):
- Cannot co-promote DraftKings alongside unlicensed/offshore sportsbooks
- Termination + regulatory impact if breach detected
- Less explicit than FanDuel but same outcome

**Implication for GSE Affiliates**:
- GSE must explicitly prohibit offshore co-promotion in affiliate agreement
- Breach must result in immediate termination + operator notification
- Affiliate suitability for future gaming licenses at stake

---

### Commission Structures

**DraftKings**:
- RevShare: 25%–40% of net gaming revenue (higher rates first 30 days)
- CPA: $100–$600 per acquisition (state/vertical-dependent)
- Attribution window: 30 days
- Min payout threshold: $50

**FanDuel**:
- CPA: $25–$35+ (tiered by volume)
- RevShare: Flat 35% (no tiering)
- Attribution window: 30 days
- Min payout threshold: ~$50

### DFS vs. Sportsbook Affiliate Distinction

| Factor | DFS Affiliates | Sportsbook Affiliates |
|--------|---|---|
| **Federal Classification** | Skill-based (UIGEA exempt) | Gambling (state-regulated) |
| **Licensing Required** | Minimal/None in most states | Required in most regulated states |
| **Restricted States** | 6 (WA, ID, MT, NV, HI, OR) | 12 (+ CA, TX where DFS legal) |
| **Age Requirement** | 18+ (varies: some 19 or 21) | 21+ (standard) |

**Opportunity**: Affiliates can operate DFS in California, Texas, Florida (no sportsbook) where sportsbooks are prohibited. Geographic arbitrage exists.

## Conclusions

1. **Geographic Reality**: DraftKings and FanDuel affiliate programs are **North America only** (38 US states + Ontario). No international expansion as of 2026.

2. **Licensing Complexity**: Affiliate licensing varies dramatically by state ($0 NJ to $7,500+ MI RevShare). GSE must provide guidance and require affiliate attestation.

3. **Offshore Ban is Critical**: FanDuel's 150-day offshore co-promotion notice + regulatory coordination makes this the **highest compliance priority**. GSE must enforce explicitly in affiliate agreement.

4. **AGA Code Compliance is Non-Negotiable**: Banned phrase list (no "risk-free," "guarantee," etc.) applies to all affiliates. GSE needs automated monitoring + manual audits.

5. **Operator Coordination**: DraftKings and FanDuel expect operator-grade affiliate compliance. GSE should establish quarterly compliance reporting to build trust and reduce audit risk.

6. **Legal Review Essential**: All affiliate agreements, terms, and monitoring procedures must be reviewed by qualified sports/gaming counsel before launch.
