## What the result teaches (decision rules)
- 0 paid but 100+ emails → interest exists; the paid offer is unclear.
- 0 paid and <25 emails → positioning, traffic, or trust is weak.
- Many game submissions, no paid → Ask Galaxy is the wedge; make it the product.
- Good engagement, no conversion → the CTA / landing path is broken.
- Confusion on "what do I get?" → simplify the offer.
- Fear around legality/trust → strengthen Trust Room + non-sportsbook positioning.
