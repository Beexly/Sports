# Objection Ledger — Galaxy Founding Desk

> Every real objection, confusion, or hesitation captured from outreach, Ask Galaxy submissions,
> feedback forms, and conversations. This is product fuel, not a complaint log. One row per
> distinct objection. **Real entries only — never fabricate.** Empty is honest until people respond.

## How to use
- Log the objection in the customer's own words where possible.
- Tag the source (DM, Ask Galaxy, feedback form, call, comment).
- Map it to the surface/copy it implicates and the change it suggests.
- When a change ships, link the commit and mark resolved.
