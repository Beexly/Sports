## Headline findings

1. **ESPN BET no longer exists as an operator.** ESPN and Penn terminated the
   sportsbook agreement effective 2025-12-01; DraftKings is now ESPN's
   exclusive Official Sportsbook and Odds Provider; Penn's book is rebranding
   toScore Bet (US affiliate program UNVERIFIED). Remove ESPN BET from every
   plan; its traffic significance transfers to DraftKings.
2. **Best first applications: Caesars + FanDuel (BetMGM third).** All three
   have open application intake. DraftKings is the hardest gate for a new
   small site (weeks-to-months manual review; likely harder post-ESPN deal).
   bet365 US is only in ~9 states — low priority.
3. **CPA-only is unambiguously the first deal shape.** Every state that
   distinguishes comp models makes CPA the cheap tier: NJ free vendor
   registration vs ~$2,000 ancillary license; MI $200 vs ~$10,000 yr-1;
   CO $350 minor vs ~$11,200 major; PA vendor tier vs $2,500-5,000 certified.
   NC BANS rev-share for affiliates outright (CPA is the only legal model
   there). Defer rev-share until PROVEN-tier volume justifies the fixed costs.
4. **Zero-license states (affiliate marketing only):** NY (operator reports
   affiliate list; conspicuous compensation disclosure required), OH (explicit
   exemption), IL (no marketing-only tier), NC (no registration; rev-share
   banned), IN (deregulated 2025-07-01), LA (below $500k/yr from LA operators).
5. **Registration states (CPA tier):** NJ free (operator files VRF; affiliate
   supplemental within 30 days), MI $200, CO $350, PA vendor (operator
   initiates), VA $500/3yr, MD $100, AZ $1,500 ancillary supplier (flat,
   regardless of comp model).
6. **UNRESOLVED — get written confirmation before accepting commissions from:
   KY (KHRC), TN (SWC.Licensing@tn.gov), MA (mgcvendorlicensing@massgaming.gov).**
7. **FTC 2023 endorsement-guide bar:** disclosure must be unavoidable, adjacent
   to the link, plain-language ("Paid partner link" style — the bare words
   "affiliate link" are called out as inadequate). Publisher AND operator share
   liability; operators impose 21+/responsible-gambling/state-eligibility and
   prohibited-claims rules (no guarantees, no "risk-free") contractually even
   in zero-license states.
