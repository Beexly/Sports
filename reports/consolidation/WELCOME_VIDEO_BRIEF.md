# Welcome Video — Creative Brief + Script (Phase 1)

**Purpose:** the branded onramp a first-time visitor meets on `/`. It answers, warmly and
fast, *what GSE is / who it's for / where to go* — then routes by intent. It is the human
face of the 10-second test.

**Producer:** the local session (Higgsfield + the established in-house reporter / soul-id
asset). **Founder Action:** generate freely; **publishing the final cut is gated to the
owner.** Do not auto-publish.

**Hard rules:** founder voice (operator, not announcer; never sounds like AI; forbidden
line "we're not AI"). No fabricated stats, records, or win rates. Not a sportsbook — we
accept no wagers. One quiet responsible-play beat. Captions required (a11y).

---

## Creative direction

- **Tone:** warm, sharp, a little bubbly — a smart friend who respects you, not a hype man.
  Confidence is *quiet and earned*, never loud.
- **Look:** matches the site — obsidian black, orbital-cyan accent, the nebula/aurora
  atmosphere, restraint over glassmorphism. NOT the generic "AI explainer" look: no
  purple-gradient template, no stock "data" b-roll, no floating-screenshot hero.
- **Length:** 45–60s (a 20–25s cut-down for social/loops).
- **Host:** the in-house reporter (use the existing soul-id/character asset — do not invent
  a new likeness). On-camera to open and close; VO over UI motion in the middle.

---

## Script (VO + on-screen + shot direction)

**[0–6s] Cold open — the problem, named.**
- HOST (on camera, direct): "The sports-pick world runs on a quiet trick: show the wins,
  hide the losses, sell you certainty."
- ON-SCREEN: *The market is full of noise.*
- SHOT: host mid-frame, obsidian set, faint aurora behind; cut on "certainty."

**[6–16s] What GSE is — the turn.**
- HOST (VO over the board UI animating in): "Galaxy Sports Edge does the opposite. We turn
  live market data into calibrated signals — and we show our work."
- ON-SCREEN: *Sports intelligence — not a sportsbook.*
- SHOT: the board telemetry counting up; a pick card revealing its factor trail.

**[16–30s] The three reasons to trust it.**
- HOST (VO, three quick beats, each with a UI moment):
  1. "Every pick is graded against the number the market closes at." → *Closing-line value*
  2. "A confidence score only counts if it means what it says — measured in public." → *Calibrated confidence*
  3. "And when the math doesn't support a bet, we say so. No-bet is a real call." → *The No-Bet gate*
- SHOT: CLV chart, a reliability curve, the No-Bet badge — real UI, honest empty states ok.

**[30–44s] Who it's for + where to go.**
- HOST (back on camera, lighter): "If you make sports decisions and you're done being sold
  certainty — this is for you. Three ways in:"
- ON-SCREEN (three chips, routed by intent):
  - *Today's board* — what's worth a look right now.
  - *A sample read* — see exactly how we think before you commit.
  - *The Founding Desk* — the daily brief, for members getting in early.

**[44–58s] Close — the line.**
- HOST (on camera, calm): "We detect. You decide. That's the whole deal."
- ON-SCREEN: *Galaxy Sports Edge* + small print: *Not a sportsbook. Bet responsibly — only
  what you can afford to lose.*
- SHOT: host holds, faint smile; logo resolves; cut.

**[social cut-down 0–22s]:** beats 1 (problem) → 2 (the turn + not-a-sportsbook) → the three
chips → "We detect. You decide." Drop the trust-triple detail.

- Zero fabricated numbers on screen; not-a-sportsbook + responsible-play beat present.
- Reads hand-made and human — no AI-explainer template tells.
- Owner reviews and approves the final cut before it goes live (Founder Action).
