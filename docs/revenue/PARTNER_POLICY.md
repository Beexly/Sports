# Partner Policy

Updated: 2026-07-04

Partner approval and offer approval are separate.

An approved partner does not make an offer approved. An approved offer does not matter if the partner is expired, rejected, suspended, or not approved.

- sportsbook
- dfs
- fantasy_tool

- cloud_tool
- local_sponsor
- general_sponsor

## Sponsor Control Boundary

Sponsors cannot control picks, model outputs, no-bet decisions, loss autopsies, calibration claims, or editorial conclusions.
