# Responsible Gaming Partner Policy

Updated: 2026-07-04

Sportsbook, DFS, wagering, contest, prize, deposit, or similar high-risk offers fail closed unless the offer includes:

- disclosure text
- terms URL
- responsible-gaming text
- minimum age policy
- eligible states
- restricted states where applicable
- approved surfaces
- separate partner and offer approval

Unknown user state fails closed for high-risk offers.

## Code Surface

- `apps/web/lib/revenue/responsible-gaming-policy.ts`
- `apps/web/lib/revenue/offer-eligibility.ts`
