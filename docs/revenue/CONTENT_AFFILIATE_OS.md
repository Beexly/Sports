# Content Affiliate OS

Updated: 2026-07-04

Status: draft-only operating system. No affiliate tracking or live links are added.

## Allowed

- workflow tool reviews
- creator stack notes
- sports data/API workflow commentary
- fantasy/tool education
- local sponsor mentions

## Not Allowed Without Review

- sportsbook/DFS offer placement
- deposit, contest, or prize language
- state-specific regulated offer claims
- sponsor copy without disclosure
- performance claims without evidence

## Required Flow

1. Score partner fit.
2. Review partner risk.
3. Approve partner.
4. Review offer separately.
5. Check disclosure.
6. Check responsible-gaming policy when high-risk.
7. Approve surface.
8. Publish manually only.
