markets (our own MONEYLINE data proves it). Edge is **calibrated divergence
from price** — fire on e = p − q with a selective lower-confidence-bound gate,
never on raw confidence κ (binding invariant #2). Hunt where q is soft:

  p-model for exactly this (leak-safe NGS aggregates via covariate-bus,

  every fire against the close, unfakeably.
- **NOT the mainstream spread/total** — our own 990-pick sample just showed
