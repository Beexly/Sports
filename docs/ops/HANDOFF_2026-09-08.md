## 1. The launch recommendation, and why

**Postpone.** Not on polish. On the premise.

Three findings, each independently sufficient, and they compound:

1. **The board does not rank on edge.** The product's own strategy doctrine
   (`_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md` in the intel repo) names one rule above all others: fire and
   rank on `e = p - q`, never on confidence, because confidence-gating is "scores24's fatal
   error." Measured: the live plan sets `selectiveRecommended.edge = null`, so public selection
   is `|p - 0.5| >= 0.12`; and 387 of 428 published moneyline picks carry no market probability
   at all, so `rawEdge` on those rows is literally `trueProb - 0.5`. Full working:
   `docs/ops/EDGE_DOCTRINE_AUDIT_2026-09-08.md`.
2. **The settled record is contradicted by the feed it came from.** C-247: 25/169 MLB, 14/48 MLS,
   2/37 NCAAF stored FINAL scores disagree with ESPN's own API; 68 settled published picks sit on
   them; 8 moneyline results are the opposite of what happened. Every calibration number in the
   repo is a measurement over those inputs. `docs/ops/SCORE_INTEGRITY_2026-09-08.md`.
3. **The DFS optimizer has no simulation engine**, no lineup cap, no tier gate, and runs
   client-side on a fictional sample slate. The category leaders (SaberSim, Stokastic) are
   simulation engines first and optimizers second.

Any one is survivable. Together they mean the two headline claims — "math you can read" and a
world-class optimizer — are not yet true.

### "Did you align our rankings / GSE score to those findings?"

**No, and the misalignment is now documented rather than assumed.** The PropFinder teardown's
central finding is that PF Rating is "a scorecard, not a model": a hand-weighted arithmetic sum
where 30 of the NFL formula's 100 points are binary yes/no threshold tests that discard
magnitude. The teardown then names our seam: "a calibrated `edge = p - q` against a de-vigged
line is a categorically different object."

We are not currently shipping that different object. On 89 per cent of the published moneyline
board there is no `q` at all. Wave 2's `_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md` is the gap analysis; the fix needs a
real second book (WP-27 / C-104) and a MODEL_VERSION decision, both founder-gated.

**One item indicts us directly.** The teardown's "WHAT WE SHOULD NOT COPY" list opens with
**client-side scoring** — PF Rating computes in the browser, so its weights are public and third
parties farm it. Our DFS optimizer computes lineups **client-side** too
(`components/fantasy/dfs-optimizer.tsx` calls `generateLineups` in a React component), with no
server route and no entitlement check. We named the mistake and shipped the same shape.

### "Are we running hundreds of simulated tests for the optimizer?"

**No. Zero.** `grep -rl "monteCarlo\|MonteCarlo\|Monte Carlo\|nSims\|numSims\|simulateSlate\|runSimulation"`
over `apps`, `packages`, `workers` (excluding tests) returns three files, none in the DFS path.
`apps/web/lib/fantasy/dfs-optimizer.ts` is an exact deterministic salary-cap DP over one point
projection per player: no per-player distribution, no correlation matrix, no contest-field
simulation, no ownership model, no payout curve.

### "Do customers get a capped number of lineups, with anything above gated to a higher tier?"

**No cap, no gate.** The count is a client slider,
`<input type="range" min={1} max={20}>` (`components/fantasy/dfs-optimizer.tsx:95`).
`generateLineups` runs in the browser. `app/fantasy/dfs/page.tsx` performs no entitlement check
and there is no server route in the optimizer path that could perform one. A frontend-only limit
is not a paywall (CLAUDE.md rule 3). Wave 2's `_TIER-DESIGN.md` proposes the concrete gate.

### "Do all the systems funnel into each other? Are the calibrations, APIs, workflows, data in and out accurate?"

Commissioned as a ten-dimension audit this session; reports land in
`docs/ops/audit-2026-09-08/`. What was verified directly, by hand, before that:

- **Calibration sample construction is CORRECT.** `proven-path-rows.ts` excludes rows with no
  market probability (`no_market_probability`), excludes three-way moneylines structurally, and
  never scores `confidence/100` against the floors. The market anchoring is real. The numbers are
  still measurements over the C-247-contaminated settled record.
- **A real inconsistency between the tuner and the runtime.** The threshold sweep resolves
  `marketP` as `independentEdge.marketFairProb ?? factorBreakdown.marketFairProb ?? receipt.marketFairProb`.
  The runtime (`/api/picks/route.ts:186`) reads only the top-level `factorBreakdown.marketFairProb`,
  present on 48 of 435 moneyline picks. A threshold tuned on one population would be enforced on
  another. Left alone deliberately: changing what publishes is a founder decision, and it is a
  no-op while `edge` is null.

## 4. Founder-owned, untouched, blocking

1. **C-247** — re-settle the affected picks, root-cause the writers (more than one mechanism:
   the MLB consecutive-day series-propagation pattern does not explain MLS), and schedule
   `npm run ops:verify-scores`. Needs database writes.
2. **PROVEN eligibility structural conflict** — settlement grace is 6h
   (`settlement-health.ts:58`), `HEALTHY` requires `overduePending === 0` exactly (line 86), and
   the zero-sit lane deliberately waits 24h past kickoff (`zero-sit-lane.ts:104`). One ungradeable
   pick therefore forces eligibility RED for up to 18 continuous hours by design. No agent may
   touch any of those three constants: each would turn the light green without making anything
   more true.
3. **The edge threshold** — needs a real second book on the moneyline path (WP-27 / C-104) before
   a threshold tuned on rows carrying `q` means anything.
4. **v5.2.7 stratum caveat** — the pooled ECE sits below every model version it is built from;
   the deployed version measures roughly twice the floor on its own rows.
