## Estimand, likelihood, priors and algorithm

**Observation:** a fixed population of N competitors, with `w_ij` counting observed wins by i over j. Conditional on the observed comparison schedule, matches are assumed independent. Match ties are explicitly excluded (II.1, retained lines 54–74). The likelihood, up to data-only counting constants, is

`L(pi; W) = product_ij [pi_i / (pi_i + pi_j)] ^ w_ij`, with `pi_i = exp(s_i) > 0`.

**Estimand:** ordered equal-strength groups `r`, their number `R`, and shared strengths `sigma_r`, such that `pi_i = sigma_(r_i)`. This is an ordered partition/weak ranking, not a general incomparability graph. Same group means exactly equal fitted latent strengths, hence `P(i beats j | fitted model) = 1/2` in the basic neutral binary BT model—not an observed draw and not equal probabilities for all future betting contracts involving those teams.

**Prior/objective (II.3, Eqs. 14–23):** uniform `R` in 1…N; uniform positive ordered size composition `n_1+...+n_R=N`; uniform assignment consistent with those sizes. Thus

`P(r) = 1 / [N * choose(N-1,R-1) * multinomial(N;n_1,...,n_R)]`.

The published score factor is `sigma/(1+sigma)^2`, inherited from a logistic prior on log strength. Eq. 22 minimizes

`log N + log choose(N-1,R-1) + log multinomial(N;n)`
`+ sum_r log[(1+sigma_r)^2/sigma_r]`
`+ sum_rr' omega_rr' log[(sigma_r+sigma_r')/sigma_r]`.

There is an explicit reference-measure caveat below. Lack of a tuning parameter is not absence of a prior or structural bias.

**Algorithm (II.4):** initialize separate nodes with BT estimates, evaluate adjacent-strength group merges, choose the best merge, re-estimate strengths, continue to one group and retain the best visited objective. This searches a greedy path; it is not exhaustive partition optimization or posterior sampling. The text explicitly limits its optimization guarantee (line 398). Complexity is density-dependent, with cubic worst case and approximately quadratic empirical scaling—not universally quadratic. Ordinary BT's unregularized MLE requires a strongly connected win graph; the reference-player regularizer prevents divergence but cannot manufacture cross-component evidence.

**Uncertainty:** Appendix F explicitly distinguishes PR's MAP output from RC-BTL's posterior (lines 1027, 1114, 1124). A merge trace is not posterior samples or a confidence interval. The LPOR field is a difference of fitted objectives, not a future-event probability, and should not be relabeled a marginal Bayes factor without integrating parameters and settling normalization conventions.

## Source-level mathematical caveats

1. **Density coordinates:** II.1 Eq. 7 correctly derives a logistic density in `s=log pi`. Eqs. 8/19 then label the same factor as a strength prior. Against `d pi`, the proper transformed density is `1/(1+pi)^2`, not `pi/(1+pi)^2`. The published objective is interpretable as a log-strength-coordinate MAP; it must not be transplanted verbatim as a proper strength-coordinate sampling density. The latter integral diverges. The ordered-strength Heaviside factor in Eq. 18 also needs a declared normalization/reference measure when comparing different R or designing MCMC. Preserve the implemented optimization objective; do not silently “fix” it and claim a reproduction.
2. **Normalization wording:** mean log strength zero implies geometric mean strength one, not arithmetic mean strength one as the paragraph after Eq. 6 suggests. A synthetic pair `s=(-1,1)` has arithmetic mean strength `1.5430806348152437`.
3. **Appendix F comparison formulas:** the HTML shows Eq. 59 as a ratio of log posteriors despite calling it log odds; Eq. 60's first denominator is a product rather than the BT sum; Eq. 62 has an extra inverse strength inconsistent with rearranging Eq. 61. Treat these as formula/transcription issues requiring TeX/author confirmation before implementing Appendix F. The central Eq. 22 and inspected Python objective agree on the BT sum.
4. **RC-BTL prior description:** Appendix F's “draw R from Poisson” is not literally the per-partition prior of 2406.19563 Eq. 9; see the companion dossier for the combinatorial factor and author-generator mismatch.

## Synthetic falsifier and next gates

Executed `sources/rankreview-counterexamples.py`; all assertions passed, output retained in the adjacent JSON.
