## Correct core method

The observations are **logits of de-vigged moneyline win probabilities**, NOT point spreads, totals-implied margins, observed score margins, or normal probabilities on the raw probability scale.

1. Convert American prices to boundary probabilities: `r(l)=100/(100+l)` for `l>=100`, and `r(l)=|l|/(100+|l|)` for `l<=-100`.
2. Normalize the two sides: `p_ij=r(l_i)/(r(l_i)+r(l_j))` (Eq. 1).
3. Separately for league `q`, game `i` home versus `j` away in season `s`, week `k`:

```text
z_qskij = logit(p_qskij)
z_qskij ~ Normal(theta_qski - theta_qskj + alpha_q0 + alpha_q,i*, sigma_q,game^2)
```

IHA has a league-wide home term plus a **city/franchise-specific, time-constant home effect**; the city index distinguishes relocations. CHA removes the individual term. Strengths are centered to sum to zero at each league-season-week; individual home effects are also described as centered in the paper. In column-vector notation the vector likelihood is `z ~ N(X theta + alpha0*1 + Z alpha, sigma_game^2 I)` (the article prints the matrix products in a different orientation).

Dynamics (§3):

```text
theta_(q,s+1,1) | theta_(q,s,Kq) ~ N(gamma_q,season * theta_(q,s,Kq), sigma_q,season^2 I)
theta_(q,s,k+1) | theta_(q,s,k)  ~ N(gamma_q,week * theta_(q,s,k), sigma_q,week^2 I)
theta_(q,1,1),i ~ N(0, sigma_q,season^2)
alpha_q,i* ~ N(0, sigma_q,alpha^2)
```

Priors **as printed in v3**: all four precisions `1/sigma^2` (game, season, week, individual HA) are Uniform(0,1000); `alpha_q0 ~ N(0,10000)`; `gamma_season ~ U(0,1)`; `gamma_week ~ U(0,1.5)`. Thus within-season AR need not be stationary: it can exceed one. No cross-sport hierarchical pooling is performed; §3 explicitly says the leagues were fit separately.

The normal game variance measures **unexplained movement in market log odds conditional on strength and HA**, not score-margin noise and not a directly estimated irreducible score MAE floor. Outcomes are used to validate forecasts, not as the state-space likelihood observations.

### Exact paper sampler settings

§3: **three rjags Gibbs chains; 40,000 iterations after 4,000 burn-in draws; thinning 5**. The paper says this yields “8,000 posterior samples in each q.” Literally 40,000/5 is **8,000 retained per chain**, hence 24,000 across three chains; retain the paper's wording but do not silently represent 8,000 as the combined chain count. These calculations were checked in Python.

Footnote 3: **sequential fits use 2,000 iterations and burn-in 1,000**. The footnote does not separately restate thinning/chains; do not invent a separate sequential retention count. Convergence assessment: visual trace plots/parallel chains, not a reported modern R-hat/ESS threshold. Posterior predictive checks use **20 simulated datasets per model**. Parity simulations use **1,000 matchups per league** and **1,000 postseason tournaments per setup** (§5.5).

Public `R/jagsFunction.R2.R` independently corroborates the main invocation: `num_adapt=2000`, `num_update=4000`, `num_draws=40000`, `n.chains=3`, `thin=5`. **Adaptation 2,000 is code evidence, not a setting stated in the paper.** Function defaults differ from the actual call; never mistake defaults for experiment configuration.

Cumulative fits smooth with future information; predictive checks instead use sequential fits and previous-week posterior means. Repeating MCMC each week is the implemented procedure; sequential Monte Carlo is suggested as a more efficient extension in §6.2, not claimed as the original sampler.

### Parity (§5.5)

Median probability the better team wins on neutral ground: **NBA 67%, NFL 64%, NHL 57%, MLB 56%**. “Better” is defined by the higher a-priori game win probability, with the better team's home assignment in home scenarios. Approximately 27% of NBA and 20% of NFL neutral matchups exceed 75%; almost no MLB/NHL matchups do.

`RegParity_q = 2 integral_[.5,1] P(p_tilde_q <= x) dx`, 1=coin-flip parity, 0=predetermined. Neutral/home-better-team: **MLB .87/.79; NHL .84/.73; NFL .70/.55; NBA .66/.47**. Under common 16-team best-of-seven tournaments, PostParity: **NBA .43, NFL .51, MLB .88, NHL .85**. PostParity is one minus a normalized squared deviation of expected finish by seed from the seed-independent expected finish, not an outcome accuracy metric. MLB would need approximately **75-game series in a 16-team tournament** to match NBA postseason lack of parity; NBA would need an eight-team single-game tournament to match the other leagues. These are simulated design comparisons, not real future forecasts.

## What GSE already implements

- `packages/prediction-engine/src/team-strength-filter.ts:1–23,111–145,305+`: a **shadow bootstrap particle filter** with latent AR(1) strengths, additive interventions, home advantage, and **Bernoulli outcome** likelihood with `P(home win)=sigmoid((w'(s_home-s_away)+HA)/temperature)`. Seeded dynamics, log weights, ESS resampling, posterior summaries/snapshots already exist. Defaults include 1,000 particles, `a=.98`, process SD .02, temperature 1, HA .2. This is **related, not the paper's Gaussian market-logit model**; it has a single fixed reversion/noise setting, no estimated separate season/week hyperparameters, and caps `a` at 1.
- `packages/prediction-engine/src/elo-from-results.ts:1–52`: chronological outcome-only Elo. It is a legitimate independent-results baseline, unlike a model fitted to market quotes.
- `packages/prediction-engine/src/edge-lab/devig.ts:34–69`: proportional normalization already implements the de-vig operation (decimal-odds interface). No need to rebuild generic de-vigging.
- Searches in package/web TypeScript found no exact IHA/CHA rjags implementation, RegParity/PostParity functions, or separate weekly/seasonal market-observation model. Static presence does not establish deployment.

## Genuinely new build delta

1. **Offline market-derived benchmark adapter**, clearly separated from independent fair values: ingest timestamped two-sided moneylines, freeze as-of training windows, use existing de-vig, transform to logits, preserve team/city/season/week identities.
2. Implement/reproduce the **Gaussian market-logit observation channel**, distinct season/week transitions, centered city HA hierarchy and fitted hyperparameter posterior. Reuse particle-filter infrastructure only with explicit model changes; alternatively retain R/JAGS for reference and port only after parity tests. Audit public-paper prior discrepancies first.
3. Compare chronological forecasts against outcome-only Elo, existing particle filter and same-time/closing market probabilities using Brier/log loss/calibration; no post-lock training data. A market feature/label is not automatically leakage—**future** quotes and pretending a market-derived output is independent are the problems.
4. Optional regular/postseason parity simulations and posterior uncertainty diagnostics. These are descriptive product research, not an automatic bootstrap cap or a model-floor unlock. MLS needs a separate draw-capable specification, not copying binary NHL/MLB constants.
