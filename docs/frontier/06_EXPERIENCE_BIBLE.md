## The ten elements

1. **The enemy.** The confident tout who deletes his losses — the industry that sells a number and hides
   the conditions, the time, and the misses.
2. **The moral position.** *We will refuse before we will pretend.* Honesty is not a disclaimer; it is
   the product's spine. On a fixture, the instrument marks its own output INFO_ONLY — out loud.
3. **The iconic interaction — The Fork.** Change one condition; the consequence cascade and the
   authority ceiling move *together*. The user feels reality bend and feels the leash at the same time.
4. **The iconic visual — The Light-Cone.** A narrowing cone of the knowable; the binding authority layer
   glows; each observer a fixed hue, so disagreement is literally visible.
5. **The recurring motif — Convergence (parallax).** Two viewpoints resolve into one object; depth comes
   from the disagreement. On every fork, many moving parts *settle* into one calm number + one calm
   ceiling. Complexity resolves into clarity — the Mozart move.
6. **The category sentence.** *PARALLAX is a counterfactual sports-reality instrument — rewind what was
   knowable, fork one condition, and see exactly what the evidence permits.*
7. **The creed (≤6 words).** **See what was knowable. Fork it.**
8. **The launch film — "0.88."** One number on black; a drag; the board re-derives; PASS becomes
   WATCHLIST at the boundary; the authority ladder pins INFO_ONLY. No specs, no claims — the category in
   twenty seconds.
9. **The sound/motion language.** *Convergence* (a soft two-tone "set" when conservation holds);
   *The Hold* (motion stops, a low held tone, on a refusal — deliberate, not broken).
10. **The onboarding moment — Refuse first.** The first screen shows the instrument *declining* to
    overclaim on a fixture, then inviting the user to fork it. Trust by stolen-thunder.

## How form follows the law

The governing law (Conserved Authority under valid intervention) is *visible*, not decorative:
- The **meet** is the hero of the autopsy — the most-restrictive layer glows; the claim stops where it
  stops. Visual hierarchy = the lattice.
- The **light cone** is the time model made shape — nothing outside it can touch the answer.
- **Convergence** is conservation made motion — things rearrange but the total holds, and you *see* the
  Σ = 1.000 land.
- The **refusal Hold** is fail-closed made felt — absence of evidence stops the machine, calmly.

## The standards — met (acceptance)

| Visual form follows the governing law | The Light-Cone + the glowing binding layer |
| Recognizable without the logo | The light-cone/parallax form + the fixed observer hues |
| Launch idea dramatizes the enemy | "The Deleted Tweet" / "0.88" |
| Campaign line earned by behavior | Every line in `07` maps to a real interaction; accuracy lines deferred to post-PROVEN |

## What the experience must never do (guardrails as design)

- Never show a number stronger than the meet permits (enforced by `composeAuthority` + the claim bound).
- Never imply live advice on fixtures (watermark + INFO_ONLY everywhere).
- Never blend disagreeing observers into one "truth" (disagreement is surfaced).
- Never let the future touch the past (the light cone).
- Never celebrate an unearned win (the credit verdict separates EARNED from LUCKY; correct refusals win).

The brand is recognizable by a posture, not a logo: *the sports product that can't show you a pass it
didn't earn.*
