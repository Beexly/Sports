# 07 · Category & Campaign — the exploration

PROJECT PARALLAX · Pass 6. Divergent creative exploration, red-teamed, reduced to one integrated
system. The creative system is **earned by product behavior** — every line below maps to something the
instrument actually does (`09`). No imitation of any living designer's or brand's specific style.

> Discipline borrowed (not style): identify the enemy and change the category without reciting specs;
> a compact moral identity larger than the product; one motif transformed across contexts; advantage by
> constraint, not by one spectacular trick; change the instrument to change what can be observed.

## The enemy (named once, everywhere implied)

**The confident tout who deletes his losses.** The whole industry sells a number and hides the
conditions, the time, and the misses. PARALLAX's existence is an accusation: *if you can't rewind it,
fork it, and show what you're not allowed to claim, you're guessing with confidence.*

## 5 signature interactions

1. **The Fork** — drag one condition; the cascade and the authority ceiling move together. ★ (selected)
2. **The Rewind** — scrub the Time Lens; facts and observers appear in knowable order.
3. **The Autopsy** — tap a claim; the eight-layer ladder shows the binding layer.
4. **The Refusal** — fork too early; the instrument declines and shows the missing fact.
5. **The Replay** — re-open a sealed receipt; same inputs, same digest.

*The Fork is the one people describe to other people* — "you flip the WR1 toggle and the whole board
re-derives, but it still tells you it's only allowed to say so much." That sentence is the growth loop.

## 5 launch-film treatments (no living-style imitation)

1. **"0.88"** ★ — a single number on black. A hand drags a slider; the board re-derives; at 0.88 the
   word PASS becomes WATCHLIST. Cut to the authority ladder pinning INFO_ONLY. End: "See what was
   knowable. Fork it." *(selected — dramatizes the boundary + restraint in 20 seconds, no specs.)*
2. **"The Deleted Tweet"** — a tout's winning screenshot; rewind; reveal the loss he deleted; cut to a
   PARALLAX replay receipt that can't be deleted.
3. **"Two Eyes"** — the parallax motif: two viewpoints converge on one object; depth appears only from
   the disagreement.
4. **"The Refusal"** — every other app shows a confident pick; ours says "not enough evidence yet" — and
   that honesty is the hero beat.
5. **"Rewind the Week"** — Monday to kickoff in 10 seconds; the cone of the knowable widens; the answer
   forms only from what was inside it.

## 3 visual systems

1. **The Light-Cone** ★ — a narrowing cone of the knowable; the binding authority layer glows; observer
   hues are fixed (BOOK blue / FANTASY violet / CROWD grey / GSE cyan). Disagreement is literally
   visible as hue separation. *(selected — it is the system's governing law made into form.)*
2. **The Ladder** — vertical strength rungs; the claim stops where the meet stops.
3. **The Order Book** — borrowed market aesthetic; rejected as too close to existing tools (Unabated/
   Betfair) and not expressive of the counterfactual idea.

## 3 motion/sound motifs

1. **Convergence** ★ — on a fork, elements slide and *settle*; complexity resolves into one calm number
   plus one calm ceiling. A soft two-tone "set" when conservation holds. *(selected.)*
2. **The Hold** — when refused, motion *stops*; a held low tone. Refusal feels deliberate, not broken.
3. **The Rewind** — a reverse shimmer as the cone narrows.

## 3 onboarding rituals

1. **Refuse first** ★ — the very first screen shows the instrument *declining* to overclaim on a fixture,
   then inviting the user to fork it. Trust by stolen-thunder. *(selected.)*
2. **Fork-in-5** — drop the user straight onto the WR1 slider; value in five seconds.
3. **Your first boundary** — the user finds an x* themselves and is shown its replay receipt.
