## The winner: PARALLAX — *the instrument that shows you what was knowable, lets you fork it, and refuses to pretend*

### Why this and not the other two

The tournament produced three coherent systems. Two are good. One is a category.

**The Calibration Republic lost** because it is a *forecasting platform*, not an *instrument*. Its
power comes from settled history and a crowd — and GSE has **0 settled picks** and no crowd yet. It
asks the user to wait for proof we have not earned, and its novelty is the lowest of the three
(Metaculus, Polymarket `/accuracy`, and Good Judgment already own scored calibration). It is a
*destination on the One Ladder*, not the vehicle. We keep its discipline (alpha-spend, calibration);
we reject it as the headline.

**The Provenance Vault lost** because it is *descriptive, not counterfactual*. It proves the past
beautifully (hash chains, source X-ray) — and that is exactly W3C PROV + the aviation FDR, the *most*
prior-art-heavy territory we surveyed. It can show you what happened; it cannot let you **fork** it.
A vault that you can only read is a museum. We keep its receipts; we reject it as the headline.

**PARALLAX won** on the one test that matters — *would this change the category's mental model of what
sports intelligence is?* — because it is the only system whose central act is something **no surveyed
product (18 sports, 8 science areas) can do**: take one decision, *rewind it to what was knowable*,
*juxtapose every observer who believed something*, *change one condition and watch the consequences
propagate through a conserved market*, *recompute exactly what GSE is permitted to say*, **visibly
refuse to overclaim**, and *replay later whether the decision deserved its outcome*. Each verb is prior
art somewhere; the **welding is unoccupied** (`01`, `02`). And — decisively for a cash-constrained solo
launch — it is **buildable on fixtures at $0 today** and it **unifies everything GSE already built**
rather than adding a parallel artifact.

## The nine load-bearing commitments

1. **One governing law.** *Conserved Authority under valid intervention.* Expression ≤ the meet of all
   eight authority layers; a counterfactual is admissible only if it is a true `do(x)` intervention
   that conserves what must be conserved. The fork inherits the meet — it can never out-express the law.
2. **One central object.** The **Decision Object**: `{ state, lightCone(T), observers, authorityVector,
   permittedExpression, intervention?, boundary, autopsyHook, replayHash }`. Every surface reads it;
   every fork returns one.
3. **One signature interaction.** The **Reality Fork** — change one condition, watch consequences
   propagate across role/props/team-total/fantasy/DFS, and watch the **authority ceiling and
   decision-state move with it.**
4. **One memorable public artifact.** The **Boundary Card / Replay Receipt** — "the action flips when X
   crosses Y; here is the knowable-at-T proof," hash-stamped and replayable.
5. **One data flywheel.** **Counterfactual residual memory** — at settlement, compare the outcome
   against the forked branches; the residuals (which fork came true, which the ontology couldn't
   explain) are proprietary and compound. No competitor has our prediction-time + fork corpus.
6. **One proof mechanism.** The **Authority Autopsy** — the eight-layer composition with the binding
   layer lit, provable by the tensor theorem.
7. **One learning mechanism.** **Residual → DRAFT discovery**, gated by FDR + confirmation window +
   owner approval. The instrument can *propose* a new concept; it can never *implement* one alone.
8. **One commercial wedge.** Sell the **fork and the boundary**, not a pick. Pro unlocks the Time Lens,
   Observer Arena, and Reality Fork; the refusal and the public boundary card are the acquisition hook.
9. **One enemy, one creed.** Enemy: **the confident tout who deletes his losses.** Creed (≤6 words):
   **"See what was knowable. Fork it."**
