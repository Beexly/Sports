## Core FAQ Answers

### What Is GSE?

GSE is a sports intelligence product. It organizes sports signals, risk context, confidence, and market movement into surfaces that are easier to understand. It is built to help users see what matters and what is uncertain.

### Is GSE A Sportsbook?

No. GSE is not a sportsbook and does not take wagers. It is an intelligence and education product focused on decision context, risk, confidence, and transparency.

### Is This Betting Advice?

No. GSE is not betting advice and does not guarantee outcomes. Sports outcomes are uncertain. GSE is designed to explain signals, risk, and context so users can make more informed decisions.

### What Does No-Bet Mean?

No-bet means the system sees enough uncertainty, conflict, volatility, or missing context that restraint may be more valuable than action. It is not a blank result. It is a risk signal.

### Why Do I See Degraded Or Unavailable Data?

GSE is designed to fail honestly. If a data source is unavailable, stale, or not verified, the product should show that state instead of pretending everything is normal.

### Why Are Some Features Gated?

GSE uses tiers to separate public education from deeper intelligence surfaces. The goal is to keep the free experience useful while reserving more detailed workflows for paid users.

### What Is The Difference Between FREE, PRO, And ELITE?

FREE is for understanding the product and seeing public intelligence surfaces. PRO is for deeper recurring use. ELITE is for the highest-context workflows, advanced analysis, and priority intelligence surfaces. Final launch copy should match the actual active pricing and entitlement configuration.

### Why Should I Trust The Performance Page?

Performance should be treated as a record of what happened, not a promise about what will happen next. GSE should show receipts, context, and limitations instead of using performance as hype.

### How Do Promotions Work?

Promotions, if shown, are informational and subject to legal, geographic, age, partner, and responsible-play restrictions. GSE should not present promotions as betting advice or urgency.

### What Data Is Current?

Only surfaces connected to verified current ingestion should be described as current. If current-roster or player data is not verified for launch, public copy should avoid claiming it.

## Support Reply Macros

### Confused New User

Thanks for checking out GSE. The simplest way to read it is this: GSE is trying to show signal, risk, and uncertainty clearly. If something is marked unavailable or degraded, that usually means the system is protecting you from stale or incomplete information instead of filling the page with fake certainty.

### Is This Telling Me What To Bet?

No. GSE is not a sportsbook and it is not betting advice. It is built to explain sports intelligence signals and risk context. Outcomes are uncertain, and users should treat GSE as decision support, not a guarantee.

### Why Is There No Pick?

Sometimes the most useful output is restraint. A no-pick or no-bet state can mean the data is conflicted, stale, volatile, or not strong enough to support a confident view.

### Why Is Data Missing?

GSE is designed to show honest data states. If a source is unavailable or not fresh, the product should say so. That protects users from decisions based on stale or incomplete information.

### Pricing Confusion

The free experience is meant to help users understand the product and the public intelligence surfaces. Paid tiers are for deeper workflows, more context, and recurring use. If anything in the plan description is unclear, send the exact page or plan name and we will tighten the explanation.

### Bug Report Reply

Thanks for reporting this. Please send the page URL, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, your device/browser, and a screenshot if possible. We will treat public crashes, broken payment paths, and misleading data states as highest priority.

### Billing Help Reply

Thanks for reaching out. Billing issues need to be handled carefully. Please send the email on the account and a short description of the issue. Do not send full card numbers or sensitive payment details in chat or email.

### Cancellation / Save Reply

Understood. If GSE did not deliver what you expected, the useful thing is to understand where it failed: unclear value, missing feature, data issue, pricing, or support. You can cancel without pressure. If you share the reason, it will directly shape what gets fixed next.

### Responsible Play Reply

GSE is built around risk awareness, not urgency. If sports betting stops feeling recreational or controlled, step away and use the responsible-play resources available in your jurisdiction. No product output is worth financial or personal harm.

### Promotions Question Reply

Promotions are informational and should be reviewed with the relevant terms, geographic restrictions, age restrictions, and responsible-play requirements. GSE should not present a promotion as a recommendation to wager.

## Issue Severity Rubric

### Severity 0 - Stop Launch / Roll Back

- Public route crash on a core page.
- Secret or token exposure.
- Founder-only methodology leakage.
- Payment path charges incorrectly.
- Public copy implies guaranteed wins or betting advice.
- Current-player/current-roster claim is public and false/unverified.

### Severity 1 - Same-Day Fix

- `/api/ready` unexpectedly fails after launch.
- Core page shows misleading data state.
- Login/paywall blocks legitimate access.
- Performance/receipt page is materially confusing.
- Support/contact path is broken.

### Severity 2 - First-Week Fix

- Copy is unclear but not misleading.
- Layout/readability issue on non-critical viewport.
- FAQ gap causing repeat support messages.
- Missing empty-state explanation.
- Pricing comparison needs clearer language.

### Severity 3 - Backlog

- Nice-to-have animation.
- Voice/audio enhancement.
- Contest mechanics.
- Affiliate/promotion polish.
- Canonical-platform port work.

## Public Copy Review Checklist

Before publishing public copy, verify:

- It says GSE is sports intelligence, not a sportsbook.
- It does not promise wins, profit, locks, or guaranteed outcomes.
- It does not push urgency to wager.
- It does not reveal formulas, weights, internals, or engine identifiers.
- It does not claim current-player/current-roster truth unless verified.
- It explains degraded/unavailable states honestly.
- It makes no-bet sound valuable without sounding patronizing.
- It uses human language, not AI filler.
- It avoids fake certainty.
- It gives the user a next step within 10 seconds.
