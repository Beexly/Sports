## Operating Mode

Do not impersonate any public figure. Use the useful operator pattern:

1. first principles,
2. hard constraints,
3. ruthless scope control,
4. fast proof loops,
5. reversible changes,
6. visible evidence,
7. launch before perfection.

The product can be ambitious after it is alive. Before first launch, the only job is to ship a credible, safe, narrow product that does not crash, overclaim, leak methods, or require the founder to run a large team.

## The 10-Second Customer Rule

Every launch page should answer one question in 10 seconds:

- Home: what is this?
- Board: what should I look at first?
- Picks: what is available, gated, or unavailable?
- Pricing: what do I get at each tier?
- Methodology: why should I trust it without seeing the secret recipe?
- Performance: what happened and what is proven?
- Promotions: are offers informational, legal-reviewed, and not betting advice?
- FAQ/contact: how do I get unstuck?

If a page fails this rule but does not crash or create legal/method risk, it is a P1 polish item, not a P0 launch blocker.

## One-Person Department System

Run GSE as six lightweight departments, not as one overloaded founder brain.

### Product

Daily question: what is the one product improvement that reduces confusion the most?

Launch focus: public routes render, data states are honest, paywalls are clear.

### Trust

Daily question: what could make a user think we are overclaiming?

Launch focus: no unsupported claims, no hidden methodology leakage, no fake certainty.

### Support

Daily question: what will confused users ask first?

Launch focus: FAQ, contact flow, canned replies, billing/support escalation.

### Growth

Daily question: what single message can bring the right first users?

Launch focus: no-hype launch copy, trust-first positioning, founder story, one channel at a time.

### Data

Daily question: what data dependency is stale, missing, or degraded?

Launch focus: `/api/ready`, ingestion freshness, source provenance, degraded-state honesty.

### Finance

Daily question: what costs money or creates liability?

Launch focus: no live paid activations until checkout, legal, refunds, and support are ready.

## Launch-Day Cadence

### Morning

1. Check final cert status.
2. Check `/api/live`, `/api/health`, `/api/ready`.
3. Confirm dirty tree/staging scope.
4. Review any red gate.
5. Decide go/no-go for the day.

### Midday

1. Fix only the top blocker.
2. Avoid feature work.
3. Rerun the exact failing proof.
4. Update command-center evidence.

### Evening

1. Write the one-line launch status.
2. List owner-only blockers.
3. List next command to run.
4. Stop before fatigue creates risky staging or deployment.

## Launch-Day Support Packet

Prepare these before public launch:

1. "What is GSE?" short answer.
2. "Is this betting advice?" answer.
3. "Why do I see degraded/unavailable data?" answer.
4. "What does no-bet mean?" answer.
5. "What do FREE / PRO / ELITE get?" answer.
6. "How do I cancel or get billing help?" answer.
7. "How do I report a bug?" answer.
8. "Why trust the performance page?" answer.
9. "How do promotions work?" answer.
10. "What data is current?" answer.

## Launch Announcement Shape

Keep launch copy narrow:

1. GSE is a sports intelligence product, not a sportsbook.
2. It is built to explain risk, confidence, and no-bet decisions.
3. First launch is intentionally narrow.
4. Public pages may show degraded states when data is not ready.
5. Trust matters more than hype.
6. More advanced Player Lab/platform features are coming after first launch.

Avoid:

1. guaranteed wins,
2. "go bet now",
3. secret methodology hints,
4. live payout or promo language,
5. claiming current-roster coverage unless verified.
