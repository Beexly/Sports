# Post-Launch R&D Finish-Line Queue

Date: 2026-06-09

## Purpose

This file converts the broad GSE research universe into a sequenced post-launch execution queue.

It is intentionally excluded from the P0 staging manifest. It should not block first launch.

## Strategic Rule

Launch 1 proves the narrow product is stable and trustworthy.

Launch 2 turns the larger IP into a platform.

Launch 3 turns the platform into a growth engine.

## What This Queue Protects Against

- More research becoming launch blockage.
- Porting the canonical platform into a dirty deploy tree.
- Adding contests, affiliates, voice, or live payments before safety gates.
- Losing high-value ideas because Launch 1 must stay narrow.
- A one-person founder trying to run product, support, growth, data, legal, and ops from memory.

## Launch 2 Admission Criteria

Do not start Launch 2 until:

1. Launch 1 has deployed.
2. `/api/ready` remains healthy on production.
3. first 24-hour support issues are reviewed.
4. no Severity 0 or unresolved Severity 1 launch issue remains.
5. the P0 commit is clean and separated from research/docs.
6. owner confirms the next objective.

## Execution Tracks

### Track A - Product Clarity

Goal: make every surface understandable in 10 seconds.

First builds:

1. Route-by-route 10-second copy audit.
2. Dense table readability pass.
3. Empty/degraded state explanation system.
4. Help overlay shell.
5. "What am I looking at?" microcopy library.

Why first: clarity reduces support load and increases trust without needing new data sources.

### Track B - Trust And Method Boundary

Goal: make GSE transparent without leaking the protected method.

First builds:

1. Expanded founder phrase/methodology leakage scanner.
2. Public trust-claim registry review.
3. Performance receipt explainer.
4. Source freshness banner system.
5. No-bet education page or component.

Why first: trust is the moat, and leakage is one of the few real existential risks.

### Track C - Canonical Platform Port

Goal: deliberately port the larger canonical IP into the deploy clone after Launch 1.

First builds:

1. Canonical-vs-deploy diff inventory.
2. Feature ownership map.
3. Player Lab dependency map.
4. GSE Rating data contract comparison.
5. Safe migration branch plan.

Do not start with UI. Start with contracts, dependencies, and proof.

### Track D - Player Lab / Current Roster

Goal: ship Player Lab only when current-roster truth is verified.

First builds:

1. Current roster source policy.
2. Player identity/entity resolution contract.
3. Roster freshness check.
4. Player profile degraded-state design.
5. Player Lab public/private boundary review.

Launch condition: no public current-player claim without source freshness proof.

### Track E - Support And Customer Success

Goal: make the product feel guided even with a one-person team.

First builds:

1. Support reply macros in app/docs.
2. FAQ triage tags.
3. Contact form issue classification.
4. Customer confusion ledger.
5. Support-to-build-card workflow.

Why first: early users teach the product what must be clearer.

### Track F - Growth And Content

Goal: build trust-first acquisition without sounding like a tout.

First builds:

1. Launch announcement variants.
2. Weekly track-record recap.
3. No-bet education article.
4. Market movement explainer.
5. Founder build-in-public updates.

Rule: every growth artifact must respect responsible-play and no-guarantee language.

### Track G - Voice OS / Galaxy Studios

Goal: make GSE easier to understand through transcript-first audio and short help experiences.

First builds after P0:

1. Transcript-first audio card spec.
2. Voice script safety linter.
3. Daily Brief voice script template.
4. Audio disclosure component.
5. Method-leakage audio guard.

No ElevenLabs API calls, public audio, or keys until approval.

### Track H - Promotions / Affiliate

Goal: create revenue without damaging trust.

First builds:

1. Promotions compliance checklist.
2. Partner inventory with jurisdiction notes.
3. Affiliate disclosure component.
4. Outbound click event taxonomy.
5. Admin approval workflow.

No live links until legal/founder approval.

### Track I - EdgeBall / Contest OS

Goal: turn contest research into a safe, first-of-kind engagement product.

First builds:

1. EdgeBall Lab free sandbox rules.
2. No-live-prizes gate.
3. Responsible leaderboard guardrails.
4. Friend-history consent model.
5. Sponsor-funded model memo.

No paid entry, prize, or live contest without legal review.

### Track J - Internal Operating System

Goal: let one founder run like a small team.

First builds:

1. Founder cockpit one-page dashboard.
2. Weekly launch health digest.
3. Customer feedback radar.
4. Build queue groomer.
5. Decision log template.

Why: the founder needs operating leverage more than another idea list.

## What To Build First After Launch

If Launch 1 is stable, build in this order:

1. Route-by-route 10-second copy audit.
2. First-week support ledger.
3. Degraded-state explanation system.
4. Expanded method/secret phrase scanner.
5. Player Lab cut-or-verify memo.
6. Canonical-vs-deploy diff inventory.
7. Founder cockpit one-page dashboard.

This order maximizes trust, reduces support load, and prepares platform expansion without breaking the deploy clone.

## What To Delay

Delay until after the first stable week:

- canonical platform port implementation,
- live promotions,
- live Stripe changes,
- generated audio,
- contests,
- complex sales/CRM automation,
- advanced personalization,
- broad design overhaul.

## What To Kill Or Merge

Kill or merge anything that:

- duplicates an existing build card,
- cannot name a user-facing or founder-facing value,
- needs legal approval but lacks a compliance gate,
- depends on current-roster data before source truth is solved,
- increases support load before basic support flow exists,
- adds another queue without retiring an old one.

## One-Person Weekly Operating Rhythm

### Monday - Product And Data

- Review readiness.
- Review user confusion.
- Pick one product clarity fix.

### Tuesday - Trust And Copy

- Review claims.
- Review no-bet/risk language.
- Update FAQ/support copy.

### Wednesday - Growth

- Publish one trust-first update.
- Avoid hype.
- Track what questions come back.

### Thursday - Platform

- Work on one Launch 2 integration plan.
- No broad merge.
- Keep a decision log.

### Friday - Finance And Risk

- Review costs, legal gates, paid features, support burden.
- Decide what remains parked.

## Human Approval Gates

Founder approval:

- Launch 2 scope.
- Canonical platform port.
- Player Lab public scope.
- Pricing/plan changes.
- Public methodology language.

Legal/compliance approval:

- affiliate links,
- sportsbook/casino/DFS references,
- contests/prizes,
- promotions,
- responsible-play language,
- generated voice/audio in promotions.

Technical approval:

- production DB migration,
- new external service,
- auth/paywall changes,
- payment changes,
- ingestion source changes.

## R&D Parking Lot

High-upside ideas that should stay parked until Launch 1 is stable:

- EdgeBall free sandbox.
- Galaxy Studios help overlay.
- Personalized audio Daily Brief.
- Customer-facing research assistant.
- No-bet education mini-course.
- Public source transparency snippets.
- Enterprise demo portal.
- Sponsor-funded contest model.
- Affiliate partner cockpit.
- Founder-only Method Brain.

## Final Rule

The best R&D after Launch 1 is not the biggest idea.

It is the smallest next system that:

1. reduces confusion,
2. increases trust,
3. improves retention,
4. protects methodology,
5. creates leverage for a one-person founder.
