### Question 2: What problem does your business solve, and for whom?
**Submission Draft**:
> The 70+ million sports fans and bettors in North America face an ecosystem saturated with noise, predatory touts, and un-calibrated formulas that consistently lead to negative expected value (-EV). Casual fans and analytical operators alike lack honest, transparent tools that explain *why* games unfold the way they do from a scheme, physics, and situational standpoint.
> 
> GSE eliminates the tout culture by building an autonomous engine that prioritizes reasoning over blind predictions. We provide full factor transparency, honest calibration curves, and strict pass-discipline (declining to recommend wagers when no genuine structural edge exists), protecting our users' capital and providing deep analytical entertainment.
