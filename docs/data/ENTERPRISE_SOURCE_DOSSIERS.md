# Enterprise Source Dossiers

*Institutional sources. Tracked, not bought. An enterprise feed is acquired ONLY when its monthly
incremental product revenue + B2B value + proof/calibration lift + defensibility lift exceeds its
monthly vendor cost + integration cost + rights burden. Until then it lives here as a dossier.*

## Dossier format (per source)

provider · coverage · products · minimum viable package · estimated annual cost · redistribution
rights · display rights · derived-model rights · historical depth · latency SLA · sport priority ·
GSE modules unlocked · decision-leverage score · current recommendation.

## The enterprise set (all `ENTERPRISE_DOSSIER`)

### Sportradar
Enterprise breadth across major/global sports; official-data posture and a coverage matrix spanning
NFL/NBA/MLB/NHL/NCAA/WNBA/golf/NASCAR. **Unlocks:** breadth + low-latency + official posture for
Market Twin and B2B. **Buy when:** a revenue tier or licensed-data B2B deal requires official feeds.

### Stats Perform / Opta
Proprietary data + AI + tracking + editorial + betting layer; advanced metrics, predictions, Opta
Vision, Opta for Fantasy / Prediction Markets. **Unlocks:** advanced role/tracking metrics that
deepen the Role State Vector. **Buy when:** the projection edge needs tracking-grade inputs.

### Genius Sports
Official-data lane for sportsbooks/leagues (e.g. the NCAA partnership), with integrity constraints.
**Unlocks:** official, low-latency market data with a compliance posture. **Buy when:** building a
licensed B2B or regulated product.

### PFF (Pro Football Focus)
Football-specific player grading and charting (offensive line, coverage, pressure, route, receiver/
DB detail). **Custom commercial license only — never scrape.** **Unlocks:** charting-grade role
quality for the Role State Vector and Fantasy Twin. **Buy when:** the projection bake-off shows
charting features clear the strict flip criteria.

### Second Spectrum / tracking sources · LSports / STATSCORE
Tracking-layer and betting-trading data. Track as future dossiers; evaluate only against a specific
unlocked product.

## The discipline

Do not buy enterprise because it is impressive. The Acquisition Governor deliberately rates these
below cheap, high-coverage sources for any near-term experiment, because their cost dominates the
denominator. They graduate to a purchase only when a named revenue/B2B/defensibility outcome clears
the ROI bar above — and even then through a separate, owner-approved decision, never autonomously.
