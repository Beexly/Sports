FAQ

  • WHAT IS FTN DATA?

    FTN Data is a sports data company that specializes in delivering extraordinarily detailed and accurate football data with short turnaround times upon game completion. We use a robust charting and base collection system to paint a vibrant picture that quantifies exactly what happened on every NFL play. Our data offerings include both traditional statistics as well a multitude of advanced data points and analysis.

  • Where can I find the API documentation?

    Full documentation is available at charting.ftntools.com/api/docs.

  • How far back does your historical data go?

    Charting and all-22 participation data are available back to 2019. Expanded participation data is available back to 2021.

  • What is the turnaround time for charted data?

    Games are charted roughly 24 hours after completion, with overnight releases. For example, Sunday games are released Monday night into Tuesday morning.

  • How do you handle revisions and quality assurance?

    Endpoints are available that indicate when a game has been updated and when charting has been officially approved.

  • Can we display your data publicly?

    Yes. Partners may use and display any charting metrics we collect, along with any derivative metrics they create. Note: DVOA data is excluded from this permission.

  • Should we store the data ourselves?

    Yes, we encourage partners to store data on their end to avoid constant pulls from our API. We ask that partners follow best practices to avoid overloading our database.

  • What does the charting feed cost?

    Pricing starts at $5,000 annually for commercial use and $3,000 annually for private use, and varies depending on your specific use case.

  • Can we see a sample of the data before committing?

    Yes, data samples are available upon request.

  • Is attribution required?

    Attribution isn’t required unless otherwise specified in your agreement, but we always appreciate acknowledgement from our partners.
