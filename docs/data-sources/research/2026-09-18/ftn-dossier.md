## 3. DVOA — exclusive home, creator employed (CONFIRMED)

Official FTN DVOA page (https://ftnfantasy.com/learn-more-about-dvoa, © 2024):
- "DVOA, originally developed by Aaron Schatz under 'Football Outsiders,' has transitioned to FTN as its exclusive home (August of 2023)." — answers ownership: FTN is the exclusive home; legal ownership of the metric itself not further specified.
- Definition: "DVOA is a method of evaluating teams, units, or players. It takes every single play during the NFL season and compares each one to a league-average baseline based on the situation. DVOA measures not just yardage, but yardage towards a first down: Five yards on third-and-4 are worth more than five yards on third-and-12. Performance is also adjusted for the quality of the opponent."
- Consumer access: "With a subscription to FTN Fantasy, you can access historical DVOA, going back to 1979."
- FAQ: "FTN is the only place you'll find it." (https://ftnfantasy.com/faq)
- Note discrepancy: an FTN subscription explainer says "In 2022, FTN Fantasy added DVOA to the arsenal" (https://ftnfantasy.com/nfl/everything-to-know-about-an-ftn-fantasy-subscription), vs the DVOA page's "exclusive home (August of 2023)." Treat both as FTN's own claims; timeline of licensing vs exclusivity unclear.
- **Aaron Schatz** (DVOA/DYAR/Adjusted Line Yards creator, Football Outsiders founder 2003) is **FTN Fantasy's Chief Analytics Officer**; also ESPN+ and ESPN BET writer. Source: https://espnpressroom.com/us/bios/aaron-schatz/
- Schatz: "Now that I'm associated with FTN, I do [have route data]." — FTN's route charting enabled Route DVOA / Route DYAR (2024-08-14, https://www.acmepackingcompany.com/2024/8/14/24219745/packers-2024-projections-advanced-stats-aaron-schatz-ftn-interview-route-dvoa-dyar-jeff-hafley). Route DVOA "correlates better year over year" than reception/target-based denominators.
- FTN Football Almanac: published under FTN (listed in their tools nav).
- Football Outsiders lineage: founded 2003, sold to EdjSports 2018, collapsed July 2023, original staff left. Sources: https://defector.com/the-people-who-acquired-football-outsiders-are-screwing-the-people-who-built-it, https://defector.com/three-newsrooms-imploded-around-me-in-under-a-year

## 6. Charting operation (CONFIRMED)

- SDBJ: Kevin Adams on the team of charters breaking down "every aspect of every play, and every player in every play of every game," "using a data process developed over 18 years by Armchair Analysis that FTN bought and rolled into its own network."
- Action Network: CDO Frank Brank on paid human charters — "There are quite a few people — ex-coaches, ex-players — who do this or have done this in the past... It's a slog. A lot of them would do it for free, but we do pay." Contrasted with Sportradar's camera-based collection.
- NBA charting (19 categories): possession type, play type, shot type, defensive alignment, after-timeout and short-clock situations, ball-screen coverages, screen locations and participants, off-ball coverage, post defense, deflections, potential assists, shot contest type/hands/player, rebound locations, rebound contests.
- **nflverse integration (CONFIRMED)**: FTN charting feed is accessible via `nflverse::load_ftn_charting()` — season-long data under a CC-BY-SA license. (espn/nflverse GitHub wiki). Note: this is FTN *charting* data made available through nflverse's open-data pipeline, not DVOA ratings.
- Charter count, turnaround times, QA/double-charting, job listings, pay rates: **not yet found** (searched).
