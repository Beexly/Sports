Build, in `Sports` or under `agent-bus` if importing the engine would touch a frozen path, a pure module for the four missing touchdown checks. Inputs are counts you computed from play-by-play you actually loaded. Outputs are shares and a one-line verdict: real role, shared, or not the guy. No guarantee language. No pick. No wire into `scoring.ts`. No MODEL_VERSION bump. If the column is absent, the check returns null. Null is not a vote.

The four:

- **Script.** Given a spread or a win probability, say whether a lead, a trail, or a close game raises or cuts this player's scoring chances. A lead that turns the offense into a run game is not the same as a shootout.
- **Long score.** Split prior touchdowns into inside-the-5, inside-the-20, and beyond 20 yards. A player who only scores from distance is priced differently from a goal-line finisher.
- **Soft spot.** Opponent touchdowns allowed by position group, with the sample size beside the rate. Yards allowed are not the question.
- **Quarterback sneak.** Share of inside-the-5 carries that are the quarterback. That share is taken away from the back. The engine does not have this input today.

Goal line and red zone already exist as league rates. Do not rebuild them. Do not run an Instagram prompt against a chatbot and call it the engine.
