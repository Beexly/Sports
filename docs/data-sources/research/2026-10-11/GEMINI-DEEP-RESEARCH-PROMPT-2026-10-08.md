You are researching methods, not placing bets. Do not recommend a wager. Do not claim a model can approach 100% accuracy. Do not claim a method beats the closing line. Do not design an organization chart. Do not recommend a new agent framework. Do not recommend a paid fine-tune or a dedicated endpoint. Do not recommend lowering a sample floor.

Question: How should a sports prediction engine attach a weekly note to a structured projection, so the note can propose a change only when it is sourced, timed, and about a specific game or player?

Keep this frame.

The number is the stat or the close. Everyone can compute it. It does not know this week's game.
The situation is what changed for this game: inactive, pitch count, rest, travel, roof, wind, quarterback change, short week.
The context is why that change is allowed to move the number: who said it, when it was retrieved, and what kind of source it is.
The join is the number, the this-week fact, and the source tier, on one game or one player. If any piece is missing, abstain.

Source tiers, already written, not implemented. Do not invent a new ladder.

- Tier 1: official report or a person on site. Can be evidence.
- Tier 2: licensed data. Can be evidence, under the license.
- Tier 3: credentialed reporter. Evidence only with corroboration.
- Tier 4: a line move. Context. Not proof of sharp money.
- Tier 5: X, forums, community. Cannot be a pick reason by itself.
- Tier 6: a model paragraph, including your own answer. Never evidence.

The engine already writes signals. It does not yet have a consumer that applies an attached fact to a projection. Research the smallest safe consumer: a shadow adjustment the board can show as a footnote. The published number does not move. Eligibility stays locked until the deployed version has 100 of its own settled games, Brier at most 0.22, ECE at most 0.05, Murphy reliability at most 0.05, then 3 consecutive passing runs. Do not argue these floors.

Notes already exist and are not on the prediction path: SiriusXM NFL transcripts, YouTube film and injury clips, X posts, arXiv papers, Kaggle tables. The failure mode is pasting the article in as truth. Thursday 2026-10-08 context only, not a pick: Buccaneers at Cowboys, 8:15 PM ET. Do not produce a side.

Research these. Primary sources. Date every paper. If you cannot open the file, write unverified.

1. Point-in-time joins. How production sports models as-of join injuries, depth charts, inactives, weather, and travel so a feature cannot see the future. What breaks when the report is revised after lock.
2. Narrative to a structured claim. Methods that turn one transcript sentence into a quote, url, speaker, retrieved time, tier, and a game or player id, and that refuse when the name is ambiguous. Published error rates. Do not propose stuffing the transcript into the model as a coefficient.
3. Measured effects, separate from commentator claims. Injury, rest, travel, weather, and quarterback change on spread and total. Name the sample and the years.
4. Selective prediction and abstention on a small sample. Compare conformal selective risk control (SCoRE, arXiv:2603.24704) and structure-aware graph abstention (arXiv:2610.08322, 6 Oct 2026) with a simple knowability floor. Say which one still means something when the deployed version has 13 settled games and the floor is 100.
5. The smallest consumer. A sourced fact proposes a delta. The published number does not move until the lock is met. Find the closest published pattern.
6. Datasets that contain situational context, not just box scores. For each: name, url, years, whether 2026 weeks are in the files, license, and the field that would attach to a game id. A title is not proof.
7. What not to do. Scoring on the games used for tuning. Undated injury notes. Model summaries cited as facts. Training on unlabeled transcripts. Treating a line move as sharp confirmation. Lowering the floor to publish.

- A one-page method for the shadow consumer: inputs, output, and why it must not publish.

Stop when every finding has a url and a date. If you cannot verify it, write unverified. Do not invent a record.
