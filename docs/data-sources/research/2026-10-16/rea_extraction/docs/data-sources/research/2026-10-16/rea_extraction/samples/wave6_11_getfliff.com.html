What is Fliff?

Fliff is a social sportsbook that has pioneered play-for-fun sports gaming by reimagining sports predictions as a free-to-play social game. We integrate promotional plays and loyalty rewards to elevate the social experience and offer sports fans the opportunity to earn rewards and win prizes.

Is Fliff available in my state?

Eligibility to participate in Fliff's social gaming and promotional games varies by state and may vary month-to-month. For more information on eligibility and availability please see our Terms of Use and Sweepstakes Rules, or check our product availability map.

What’s the difference between Fliff Coins and Fliff Cash?

Fliff Coins are used to play for fun. They provide the opportunity to win more Fliff Coins, earn XP, and move up the leaderboards while earning achievement badges and building a status within the Fliff community. Fliff Coins are a freemium product that can be claimed every two hours, or purchased through our shop, but cannot be transferred outside the game or redeemed for prizes.

Fliff Cash is free to claim and serves as an entry into our promotional sweepstakes. Fliff Cash won from gameplay can be used in additional games or redeemed for prizes, provided the minimum redemption level is met and the account holder’s identity has been verified in accordance with our Terms of Use.

You can also obtain Fliff Cash for free as a bonus with the purchase of select Fliff Coin packs or via one of our alternative methods of entry. NO PURCHASE IS NECESSARY to obtain Fliff Cash; please see our Sweepstakes Rules for complete details.

What are Promotional Games?

Promotional games provide participants with an opportunity to win real prizes. They are used to promote the sale of a product (in our case, Fliff Coins) and have been used for decades across the United States and worldwide.

Is Fliff the only company that uses Promotional Games?

No. Many companies leverage promotional games and sweepstakes to promote their products or services at any given time. These promotions have existed for decades and are prevalent both in the United States and around the world. Notable companies, including American Airlines, Expedia, Kellogg’s, McDonald’s, Pepsi, and Publisher’s Clearing House, in addition to dedicated sweepstakes sites and aggregators, all have well-established and long-standing sweepstakes promotions.

Is Fliff Legal?

Yes. Fliff is a leading social sportsbook platform. Social sportsbook, like social casino, differs from gambling, as it is a freemium play-for-fun form of entertainment. Fliff also uses promotional games and loyalty rewards to allow our customers to acquire prizes and gift cards through gameplay with no purchase necessary ever being necessary. Fliff is partnered with trusted, industry-leading payments gateways to allow for the secure redemption of Fliff Cash. We are in full compliance with applicable laws and regulations in all jurisdictions where we operate.

Do I need to verify my identity to use Fliff?

Yes. Like other responsible gaming platforms, every customer on Fliff must verify their identity prior to making any purchase or redeeming a prize. Our identity verification process is automated and performed securely by our partners at Persona. As part of that process, at a minimum, each customer is required to validate their identity through submission of their social security number and address, and must submit a photo of their valid, government-issued ID with a live selfie photo that matches the submitted photo ID. Additional documentation may be required depending on the value of the prizes won.

How do I deposit to my Fliff wallet?

You cannot deposit funds into your Fliff wallet. We are not a real money gaming operator, which typically has cash deposits and withdrawals. We are a social sportsbook with a dual currency format – Fliff Coins to play for fun and Fliff Cash to participate in our promotional games. Both currencies can be claimed free of charge, but only Fliff Coins can be purchased. Fliff Cash can be obtained promotionally and with the purchase of select Fliff Coin packages, as well as via multiple other alternative methods, which are detailed in our Sweepstakes Rules.

Does Fliff have customer service?

Our customer support team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to answer your questions and concerns. Each time you submit a new inquiry, a ticket is generated and our responses will be in reply to the open ticket. Response times may vary depending on the total number of inquiries our support team is fielding, but we promise to reply promptly and as soon as feasibly possible. Opening multiple tickets for the same inquiry will not result in faster response times.
