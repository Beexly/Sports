BetQL is a sports-betting analytics platform designed to help bettors identify value, track trends, and make informed wagers across all major sports. By combining robust data modeling, real-time odds monitoring, expert insight, and quantitative trends, we aim to elevate both casual and serious bettors from guesswork to strategy.

Types of Betting Predictions

We offer a variety of betting predictions including moneyline, spread and over/under picks for all major sports. All BetQL computer model predictions created using a proprietary algorithm designed to identify trends and patterns that can help you make informed betting decisions. Each game or match is simulated 10,000 times based on all available data providing BetQL subscribers with a fact based prediction and clear probability projections.

Computer Analyzed Prediction Program

Every bet on BetQL receives a star rating based on historical data, matchup details, homefield advantage, weather, injuries, and narratives. Bets are rated on a scale of 1-5 stars indicating their advantage for the bettor. The higher the number of stars the bigger the edge. As external factors (e.g., a new injury or favorable weather shift) alter betting lines, BetQL updates our star ratings throughout the day to reflect real-time opportunities.
