If you flip one time and the lighter side lands up right you prove nothing. But over time, the more times you flip the heavier weighted side will land upright more often than not, just like +EV bets.       Appreciate the detailed feedback. Worth addressing the substance for anyone reading:On "picks go against recent stats and trends": that's the point, not a flaw. Not a black box if you read it.
