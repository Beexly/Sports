The Basics of Expected Value

Expected Value (EV) is a measure of the potential return on a bet over the long term. In the world of sports betting, there are two types of EV: positive and negative expected value. Positive EV occurs when your estimated probability of winning a bet is higher than the sportsbook's implied probability, while negative expected value indicates the opposite.

Understanding the difference between positive and negative EV is vital for any bettor looking to make consistent profits. Positive EV bets, with their higher probability of success, can lead to long-term gains. On the other hand, consistently placing negative EV bets can result in losses over time. Therefore, the key to becoming a successful sports bettor is to identify and exploit positive EV opportunities.

The EV Calculation Formula

In this section, we will provide a step-by-step guide on how to calculate EV in sports betting, including the formula and its application to real betting scenarios. The formula for calculating EV in betting is as follows: (Amount won per bet probability of winning) – (Amount lost per bet probability of losing). The components of this formula include the probability of an outcome occurring and the potential payoff for that outcome. Probability is a crucial factor in the EV calculation, as it determines the implied probability of winning a bet.

The expected value (EV) of the bet is calculated by multiplying the implied probability with the potential profit or loss. Comprehending the components of the EV calculation formula and their interaction is key to determining a bet's true value and making educated betting decisions.

Converting Odds to Implied Probabilities

Converting betting odds to implied probabilities is a vital skill for any bettor looking to make informed betting decisions and identify value bets. The formula for converting odds to implied probabilities varies depending on the type of odds being used. For decimal odds, the implied probability can be calculated using the formula: Implied Probability = 1 / Decimal odds.

For fractional odds, the formula is slightly different: Implied Probability = 1 / (Fractional Odds + 1). By understanding these formulas and how to apply them to different types of odds, you can evaluate the true value of a bet and make more informed decisions when placing wagers.
