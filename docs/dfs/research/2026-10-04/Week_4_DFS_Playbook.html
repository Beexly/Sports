Stack 4 · Pay-up ceiling
Patriots at BillsO/U 48.5 · BUF -7

Pair him with Dalton Kincaid 5,300 — 70% route rate, and the Patriots' "best vs TE" ranking is a schedule mirage (AJ Barner, Pat Freiermuth, Brenton Strange). James Cook 7,400 works as the correlated RB. Drake Maye is the other side of this game and he is a hard fade (see Fades).

Stack 5 · Sunday night (showdown)
Lions at PanthersO/U 50.5 · DET -3.5 · Rain

On the Sun–Mon slate this is the primetime piece. Rain (54%, wet field) but only 9 mph wind, and both banged-up defenses point to points: Carolina is likely down both starting corners and can't stop the run. Jahmyr Gibbs and Amon-Ra St. Brown are the top captain shelf, Sam LaPorta the intermediate target the weather favors, and Darren Waller is the leverage Panther — Detroit allows the most fantasy points to tight ends. Jameson Williams is the flagged disagreement (see Radio Consensus).

Stack 6 · The squeeze play
Dolphins at VikingsO/U 38.5 · MIN -10.5
