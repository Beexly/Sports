## Source yield

- **numberFire** — high yield. Week 1 2026 per-team fantasy projection tables (numberfire.com/external/widgets/teams/<slug>) gave numeric stat-line projections + FP for DET, PHI, BAL, IND, BUF, CIN, TB, MIA, NYJ. numberFire showed **no salaries and no ownership** in the free widget tables.
- **FantasyLabs** — high yield. "Week 1 NFL DFS RB Breakdown: Top Cash Game and Tournament Picks" (updated ~Sept 12, 2026) gave FD salaries, ownership (Gibbs ~40% FD; Love <5% FD; no other RB above 18–20%), and Plus/Minus model ranks. No raw fantasy-point projection numbers in the free article text.
- **RotoGrinders** — essentially no usable yield. Noto's "NFL DFS Top Picks & Plays: Noto's Key Personnel for Week 1" (2026) has the entire RB breakdown behind the **NFL Premium paywall**; only the intro strategy note and the QB tier were publicly visible. Per the paywall rules, no RB numbers were pulled from RG. Their free strategy note: 3 RBs with 20+ touch upside was the winning structure last season; Noto plans more RB-in-FLEX exposure to start 2026.
