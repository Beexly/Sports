## Critical slate-format finding

The sites covering this exact slate (RotoBaller, FantasyLabs, FantasyPros, Fox Sports) define the FanDuel "main slate" as **12 Sunday games** — they explicitly exclude the Sunday-night (Cowboys @ Giants) and Monday-night (Broncos @ Chiefs) games. RotoBaller (Sept 12, 2026): "Teams Playing Prime-Time Matchups (Not on Sunday Slate): … Dallas Cowboys at New York Giants (Sunday PM); Denver Broncos at Kansas City Chiefs (Monday)." FantasyPros cheat sheet (updated Sept 12, 2026, 8:30 PM ET): "the NFL DFS main slate features 12 games on Sunday afternoon."

Consequence: **Travis Kelce and Jake Ferguson are not on the FanDuel 12-game main slate** (they would appear only in Showdown slates, a separate format — no Showdown salaries verified). Also, the Thursday game (49ers vs Rams, already played) and the Wednesday opener (Seahawks vs Patriots, already played) are irrelevant. The 12 main-slate games are the ones listed in the TE table below, minus Cowboys@Giants and Broncos@Chiefs.
