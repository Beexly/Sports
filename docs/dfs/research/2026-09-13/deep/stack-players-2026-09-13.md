## STACK 1: Minnesota — GB @ MIN, Sun 3:25pm CT (dome). MIN -1.5, O/U 46.5.

- Matchup: GB without Micah Parsons (torn ACL, out ≥4; sack rate 7.3%→3.7%), new DC Jonathan Gannon, "suspect" corners (FantasyPros). Murray is 14-3 TD/INT in six career Week 1 starts.
- RISK (reporter-sourced, not vibes): ESPN's Seifert — won job "despite a rocky series of practices," merely "shown enough"; AP — didn't clearly outplay McCarthy in open practices; Purple Daily (Aug 17) — "clearly a work in progress" with live shotgun-snap issues with C Brandel that O'Connell called potentially costly. thehuddle tags him "!!" (under 2.5x value).
- Verdict: tournament weapon, not a cash core.

- Matchup: same Parsons-less GB secondary as above; Jefferson's last big 2025 game came vs GB.
- RISK: new-QB timing, though his route-running/contested-catch profile is less timing-dependent. thehuddle "!!" is the lone quantitative caution.
- Verdict: soft salary for the ceiling; best piece of the MIN stack.

### T.J. Hockenson, TE — $5,000 (single source: thehuddle; DK $3,300)

### Dallas Goedert, TE — $5,300 (single source: thehuddle; verify in lobby)
