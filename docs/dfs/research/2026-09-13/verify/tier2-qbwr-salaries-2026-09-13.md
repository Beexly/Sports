## 3. Slate-confusion hazards (do not treat as corrections)

1. **Ja'Marr Chase $8,900 (FD export + Fantasy Life, main slate) vs $9,200 (Fantasy Leagues Info WR page).** The FLI WR page mixes contests that include Thursday-game players, i.e. it is not a clean Sunday-main slate, and its $9,200 conflicts with FanDuel's own export. This demonstrates that slate identity matters; it does NOT establish that the submitted $8,900 is wrong on the Sun–Mon slate.
2. **Dak Prescott $8,300.** The only source found pricing Dak on FanDuel ($8.3k in its FD lineup) is thehuddle's "DFS Fantasy Domination Primetime Slate" piece — banned, and covering only the two-game Sunday-night-to-Monday-night slate, not the full Sun–Mon Classic. It is not evidence for the target slate.
3. **Do not infer Sun–Mon = main salaries.** The fact that all 12 Sunday players match the main-slate export exactly means the submitted list was very likely sourced from the Sunday main slate (or the Sun–Mon salaries genuinely coincide) — but coincidence is not verification. FanDuel can and does price players differently across overlapping slates (see the Chase $8,900/$9,200 discrepancy above).
4. **DraftKings ≠ FanDuel.** Several pages (Fantasy Life's "DraftKings NFL DFS Week 1 Pricing First Look", thehuddle's DK lineups, rotoballer stacks) quote DK salaries; none were counted toward FD verdicts.
