## Best plays / best values / leverage pivots

1. **Best plays:** Burrow ($8,200) is the slate's best spot (51.1 total, ~30% chalk for a reason) and Hurts ($8,300) is the best pt-per-dollar at QB vs a WAS defense that bled passing TDs last year.
2. **Best values:** Herbert ($7,600) is the consensus value king (28.5 implied total vs ARI's bottom-tier pass D); Mayfield ($7,500) is the bring-back pivot off Burrow chalk with real milestone incentives today.
3. **Leverage pivots:** Lamar (~5% owned, $8,400) is the tournament cheat code — elite ceiling nobody is playing; Allen ("barely owned" at $8,900) is the premium pivot if BUF/HOU shoots out.
4. **Fades:** Shough is an over-owned trap at $6,900; Mahomes is a universal expert fade (UDFA LT vs #1 sack D); Daniels' rushing floor doesn't survive an IR'd LT vs the Eagles' front — he's the slate's most polarizing QB.
5. **Logistics:** Confirm FD salaries for Dart, Nix, and Mahomes in the contest lobby (not published free); Tua and McCarthy are NOT in the player pool (out / not starting); keep an eye on CLE@JAX weather (storms, medium concern) before locking Lawrence/Watson pieces.
