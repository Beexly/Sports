Chiefs at Raiders O/U ~48 · KC -4.5

Stack 4 · Pay-up ceiling
Patriots at Bills O/U 48.5 · BUF -7
Josh Allen 7,700 is the best QB in fantasy right now (31.7 points per game) and New England is down CB Christian Gonzalez and DT Christian Barmore. Pair him with Dalton Kincaid 5,300 — 70% route rate, and the Patriots' "best vs TE" ranking is a schedule mirage (AJ Barner, Pat Freiermuth, Brenton Strange). James Cook 7,400 works as the correlated RB. Drake Maye is the other side of this game and he is a hard fade (see Fades).
Stack 5 · Sunday night (showdown)
Lions at Panthers O/U 50.5 · DET -3.5 · Rain
On the Sun–Mon slate this is the primetime piece. Rain (54%, wet field) but only 9 mph wind, and both banged-up defenses point to points: Carolina is likely down both starting corners and can't stop the run. Jahmyr Gibbs and Amon-Ra St. Brown are the top captain shelf, Sam LaPorta the intermediate target the weather favors, and Darren Waller is the leverage Panther — Detroit allows the most fantasy points to tight ends. Jameson Williams is the flagged disagreement (see Radio Consensus).
Stack 6 · The squeeze play
Dolphins at Vikings O/U 38.5 · MIN -10.5
The lowest total on the slate, and that's the point: Minnesota's defense is the play ( 4,000 , #1 below) and the Vikings' passing pieces are cheap because Jefferson is out. Kyler Murray 5,300 + Jordan Addison 5,300 + T.J. Hockenson 3,300 against a Miami defense allowing an 80.3% completion rate, a 118.9 passer rating, and zero sacks.
