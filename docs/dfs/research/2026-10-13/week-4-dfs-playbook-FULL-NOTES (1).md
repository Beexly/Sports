## Methodology & honesty labels

- **One "mention"** = one feed document recording an outlet/analyst as bullish, bearish, or neutral on a player/play. A single outlet bullish in two different articles still counts once per feed document processed (duplicates inside one feed are not double-counted).
- **Agreement %** = bullish mentions ÷ (bullish + bearish mentions). Neutrals noted separately.
- **Timestamps** are when the take was published (article/podcast date), not when we read it. "No evidence of change" is stated where the feeds don't support a mind-change claim.
- **Nothing is invented.** No fabricated quotes, numbers, or mind-changes. Data-integrity conflicts between feeds are flagged, not resolved by guessing.
- Feeds marked **[PENDING]** had not landed as of v2 and are excluded from counts.
