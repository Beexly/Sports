- Under 250 words per email.

## Email 2 - Day 1

Subject: Why Vault exists.

Preheader: A short note on what we won't do.

```text
[First name],

Quick note while it's fresh.

Vault exists because the public site can't carry the whole conversation. The site shows you what the model produced. Vault shows you why.

That distinction matters because it's also what Vault won't be:

- Not extra picks. The public slate is the slate.
- Not private information. There's no information layer above the published rationale.
- Not a Discord trading room. We don't tail picks. We don't run pools.

What Vault is: the room where the published work gets reasoned about, openly, before and after the fact.

I'll send the first digest Wednesday. If you have a question between now and then, the Discord is the right place. That's where the conversation lives.

G
```

## Email 3 - Day 3

Subject: What's in the digest.

Preheader: A short walkthrough so the first Wednesday isn't a surprise.

```text
[First name],

The first weekly digest lands Wednesday. So you know what to expect:

Structure. Each digest covers one publication: the call from the prior week that I think was the most consequential to understand. Could be a high-confidence call we got right. Could be a high-confidence call we got wrong. Could be a pass, a game we considered and didn't publish.

Five sections, every time:

1. The publication, plainly stated.
2. The factor that drove the call.
3. The assumption the factor relies on.
4. What we were watching that could flip the call.
5. What we'd do differently if we ran the publication again, knowing what we know now.

That's the contract. If a digest deviates from that structure, it's because the publication required it, but it'll always be flagged in the opening line.

Length. 500-900 words. Reads in 4-6 minutes.

Tone. Same as the Loss Room. Honest about what we don't know. Specific about what we do.

Wednesday.

G
```
