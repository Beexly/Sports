## Section 2 — The five things you get (the offer, plainly stated)

Section heading (left-aligned, medium weight):
> What's inside.

Five items, each a small block. No bullet icons. Just clean rows with a numeral.

### 01 — Weekly internal-rationale digest

Wednesday email. The thinking behind the week's most consequential publication or pass — written by Garrett. The version we would have sent ourselves before the model existed.

500–900 words. Includes the factor that swung the call, the assumption it relies on, and what we're watching that could flip it.

### 02 — Monthly group office hours

One hour, second Tuesday of the month, live on Discord. We talk about whatever you want to talk about. The model, the losses, the pass list, the changelog, the sport you wish we covered.

Recorded. Replays available in the Vault Discord, members-only.

### 03 — Quarterly private data review

Internal performance details that don't appear in the public Almanac. Calibration drift, factor decay, the version-over-version comparisons that are honest enough we keep them off the open site.

PDF and a 30-minute recorded walkthrough. Once per quarter.

### 04 — Early access to the Model Journal draft

Saturday's draft, before Sunday's publication. You see what the model said about the week before the public does, and you see what got edited out.

### 05 — Vault-only Discord channel

One channel, members and Garrett. Slow. No spam. No tout-trading. The conversation we wish existed elsewhere on the internet about how this actually works.

## Section 8 — Four quiet questions (FAQ as restraint)

Section heading:
> Things people have asked.

Standard FAQ accordion or four small blocks. Plain language only.

**Q. Will Vault give me information I can use to bet?**
A. Maybe. Probably not in the way you'd expect. Vault tells you how the publication came together. Whether that helps you bet better is up to your own discipline, not to us. Galaxy doesn't promise edges. We promise transparency about how we computed them.

**Q. Do I get more picks than Elite gets?**
A. No. The published slate is the published slate. Vault doesn't unlock additional games — that's a violation of the brand we built on.

**Q. Can I get the digest without the office hours?**
A. No, and the reason matters. The office hours are where Garrett answers what was edited out of the digest. They're the conversation the digest can't capture. Without them, the digest is half a product.

**Q. What happens if Vault doesn't fit?**
A. 30-day refund window, no questions. Past that, you can cancel anytime and your access continues to the end of the prepaid year. We won't make leaving harder than joining.
