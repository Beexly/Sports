# Vault Customer Development — Outreach Templates

Six templates total: three primary (one per source pool), three follow-ups, plus a thank-you and a no-show reschedule.

**Voice rules:**
- Galaxy brand voice (research-shop, not tout-shop). No exclamation marks. No emojis except in template 3 where a single 🪐 is allowed.
- Personal, not corporate. Send from Garrett's account.
- Always end with a clear ask (book a 30-min call) and a Calendly link.
- No pitching Vault in outreach — that's for the interview itself. Outreach is "I want to ask you questions," not "I want to sell you something."

---

## Template 1: Galaxy Elite subscriber (email)

**Subject line variants (A/B test if you want; pick one for batch 1):**
- A: "30 minutes to help shape what's next for Galaxy?"
- B: "A research call before I build something new"
- C: "Quick favor — would you tell me what you actually want?"

(Recommended: B. It signals respect for their time and clarifies the ask.)

**Body:**

> Hey [first name],
>
> Garrett here. You've been on Galaxy [tier] for [X months] and I wanted to ask you directly — would you spend 30 minutes with me on a research call?
>
> I'm validating a premium tier above Elite before I build it. Before I write a single line of code, I want to talk to the people most likely to use it. You're one of those people.
>
> What's in it for you: 30-day extension on your current subscription whether or not you'd be interested in the new tier. And first claim on the founding-member spots if you do want in.
>
> What I'd ask of you: just an honest reaction to what I'm thinking. No prep needed. No selling on my end — research only.
>
> Calendly link: [URL]
>
> If those times don't work, just hit reply with what does.
>
> Thanks,
> Garrett
> galaxysportsedge.com

**Send time:** Tuesday or Wednesday morning, 9:00 AM Eastern. Best open rates for B2C research outreach.

## Template 2: Twitter follower / unsubscribed (DM)

DMs are tighter than emails. Keep under 600 characters in the first send.

> Hey [first name or handle],
>
> Garrett from Galaxy Sports Edge — you follow us on Twitter. I'm building a premium tier and want to validate it with 30 actual bettors before I build anything.
>
> Would you give me 30 min on a call this week? No pitch — research only. As a thank-you, I'll extend or grant a Galaxy subscription on me.
>
> If you're in: [Calendly URL]
>
> If you're not — totally good, no hard feelings.
>
> — Garrett

**Send time:** Monday or Tuesday afternoon. People are more receptive to DMs mid-day than morning.

**Variant for high-engagement followers** (those who've liked/replied to Galaxy tweets):

> Hey [name] — I've noticed you've engaged with a few Galaxy tweets, which is why I'm reaching out personally.
>
> I'm building a premium tier above what's currently on the site, and before I build it, I want to talk to ~30 actual bettors about what they'd want from it. 30 minutes, research only, your honest reactions.
>
> [Calendly URL]
>
> If yes: pick any slot. If no: zero pressure, appreciate you reading this.
>
> — Garrett

## Template 4: First follow-up to non-responders (24 hours later)

About 60% of recipients won't respond to the first send. Send a single follow-up. After that, move on — repeated follow-ups become spam.

**Email follow-up:**

> Hey [first name] —
>
> Quick bump on this — I'm collecting 30 conversations this week and yours would be one of them if you have 30 minutes.
>
> If the email below got lost, the Calendly link is here: [URL]
>
> If now's a bad week, no problem — just say "later" and I'll circle back.
>
> — Garrett

**DM follow-up (Twitter or Discord):**

> bumping this in case it got buried 🙂
>
> [Calendly URL]

That's the entire follow-up DM. Keep it tiny.

## Template 5: Thank-you (sent within 2 hours of completed call)

> Hey [first name],
>
> Thanks for the 30 minutes today — the conversation was genuinely useful and I'm taking your feedback into where this lands.
>
> A couple of things I committed to following up on:
>
> [List 1–2 things if you mentioned them on the call — e.g., "I'll send the methodology PDF I mentioned," or "I'll tag you when Vault opens"]
>
> [If they're Galaxy already] As promised — I've added 30 days to your subscription.
>
> [If they're not Galaxy already and offered them a trial] I've added a 30-day Elite trial — you'll get a login email shortly.
>
> [If they said yes to being a founding member] You're on the founding-member shortlist. I'll be in touch before the public launch with first-claim details.
>
> Appreciate it.
> Garrett

Send from Garrett's personal email so the thread continues warm.

## Template 6: No-show reschedule (sent within 1 hour of missed slot)

> Hey [first name] —
>
> Looks like we missed each other at [time]. No problem — would another time this week work? I'm flexible Thursday and Friday.
>
> Just hit reply with what works, or grab another slot here: [Calendly URL]
>
> — Garrett

One reschedule attempt is fine. After a second no-show, drop them off the list.

## Template 7: Soft decline ("not this week") response

> No worries at all. If you ever want to revisit, the door's open. I'll keep you on the list for the next round in [3–4 weeks].
>
> Thanks for considering.
> Garrett

Optionally add them to a "research panel" tag in your CRM for future research outreach.

---

## Template 8: Hard decline response

If someone responds with a hard "not interested" or worse:

> Got it — appreciate you taking the time to reply. All best.
>
> — Garrett

End it there. Don't argue. Don't ask why. Don't try to convert. The brand is built on restraint — apply it to outreach too.

## Outreach sequencing rules

1. **Day 1 send:** First-attempt outreach to all 30 (across pools). Done by 11:00 AM Day 1.
2. **Day 2 follow-up:** Template 4 to any Day 1 non-responder. Send by 11:00 AM Day 2.
3. **Day 3 backup batch:** If <12 booked by end-of-Day-2, send 10 more first-attempts to fresh names.
4. **No third follow-up.** Two touches max. Past that, you're spamming.

---

## Compliance and brand-safety reminders

- Do not promise Vault features that may shift in scope post-customer-dev. Use phrases like "the tier I'm thinking about" not "the tier we're launching."
- Do not promise specific pricing in outreach. Save price for the interview itself.
- Do not characterize the offering as guaranteed or risk-free. Galaxy's brand-safety filter applies to outreach too.
- Use the same compliance vocabulary in outreach as on Galaxy surfaces — "research," "rationale," "transparency." Not "edge," "lock," "guaranteed."

---

## Tracking outreach in the recruitment sheet

For each message sent, log:
- `sent_at` — timestamp.
- `source_pool` — galaxy_elite | twitter | discord | referral.
- `template_used` — template ID (1–9).
- `response_received_at` — timestamp.
- `response_type` — booked | declined | maybe_later | no_response_yet | hard_decline.
- `slot_booked` — datetime if booked.
- `interview_completed` — y/n.

This data has value beyond Vault validation — it's your first Galaxy customer-acquisition funnel baseline.
