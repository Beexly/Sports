# Galaxy Sports Edge — /pricing Page Copy

**Route:** `/pricing`
**Audience:** Public. Visitors deciding which subscription tier fits.
**Voice:** Functional. Tighter than /about. No marketing copy.

## Page structure

The /pricing page has 5 sections. Total page reads in 3-4 minutes.

```
Pricing
```

```
Which tier fits you

Use Pro if:
— You want the publications + the public archive.
— You don't need earlier access timing.
— You read Galaxy occasionally rather than daily.

Use Elite if:
— You read Galaxy daily or near-daily.
— Earlier publication access matters to your decision-making.
— You want the market analysis layer alongside the publications.

Use Vault if:
— You read the Loss Room before the Ledger.
— You'd rather see why Galaxy passed than why Galaxy picked.
— You want the founder-led conversation around the publications.
— You're willing to commit annually to a smaller room (1,000 max).

If none of these fit you specifically: don't subscribe. The public surfaces give you most of what Galaxy does. Subscriptions are for readers who want more depth or earlier access, not for readers who want general sports content.
```

The "don't subscribe if none fit" line is restraint-aligned. Galaxy filters subscribers actively rather than maximizing conversion.

## Section 5 — Pricing FAQ

```
Things people have asked

Q. Can I switch tiers?
A. Yes. Upgrade applies the pro-rated credit from your current tier toward the new tier. Downgrade applies on next renewal.

Q. Why does Vault cost more than 12 months of Pro?
A. Vault includes founder time — weekly digests written by the founder, monthly office hours hosted by the founder, quarterly data reviews. Pro and Elite are publication products; Vault is publication + access. The cost reflects that.

Q. Is there a free trial?
A. No. Galaxy doesn't run free trials. The public surfaces (the Board, Loss Room, Pass List, methodology page) function as the trial — they're free and don't expire.

Q. Why are Pro and Elite priced monthly but Vault annual-only?
A. Pro and Elite are publication products that scale linearly. Vault requires the founder's annual time investment (52 weekly digests, 12 office hours, 4 quarterly reviews). The annual commitment matches the operating commitment.

Q. Will pricing change?
A. Possibly. If pricing changes, existing subscribers keep their original rate for the life of their continuous subscription. Vault founding-rate is explicitly locked.

Q. What if I'm a journalist or analyst doing research?
A. Email garrett@galaxysportsedge.com. Galaxy provides press-access subscriptions for legitimate journalism + analysis purposes. Same content; no payment.

Q. Refund policy?
A. Pro: 14 days from first subscription. Elite + Vault: 30 days from first subscription. Refunds outside the window are case-by-case; reach out and we'll evaluate.

Q. What payment methods?
A. Stripe handles all subscriptions. Credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, ACH for US members. International payment methods vary by country (Stripe handles the routing).

Q. Tax invoice?
A. Stripe generates a tax invoice automatically. For business subscribers, your tax invoice is available in your member dashboard.

Q. What happens if I cancel?
A. Access continues through the end of your current billing period. After that: subscription stops, role-gated channels become inaccessible, member dashboard shows your historical activity. We don't delete your data; you can re-subscribe and your history is intact.
```

The FAQ is restraint-aligned. Honest answers. No marketing language. The "is there a free trial?" answer is brand-position-protective.

## Section 6 — Subscription mechanics fine print

```
Subscription mechanics

— Billing cycle: monthly subscriptions renew on the date of subscription; annual subscriptions renew on the date of subscription one year later.

— Renewal reminders: Vault members receive a renewal-pre-warning email 30 days before annual renewal. Pro and Elite members receive a 5-day pre-warning.

— Cancellation: process through your member dashboard or by email. Access continues to the next renewal regardless of cancellation method.

— Refund policy: 14 days (Pro), 30 days (Elite + Vault) from first subscription start, no questions asked. Outside the window: case-by-case per our member support playbook.

— Founding rates: members who join during a publicly-announced founding window (currently: Vault founding-1000) keep the founding rate for the life of continuous subscription.

— Price changes: existing subscribers keep their original rate. Future subscribers pay current rate.

— Subscription transfer: subscriptions are personal to the account they were purchased on. Transfer is not supported; if you need to change accounts, contact garrett@galaxysportsedge.com.

— International: subscriptions are USD-denominated. Stripe handles currency conversion at checkout for international members.
```

The fine print is restrained but complete. Galaxy doesn't bury important mechanics in dense legal language.

## What this page deliberately does NOT include

1. **No "compare features" table.** Galaxy doesn't present pricing as a feature-comparison matrix. The tier blocks describe what each tier IS, not how they differ on N attributes.

2. **No "save 20%" annual-vs-monthly badges.** Annual pricing already discloses the 2-months-free math; no further marketing.

3. **No "most popular" tag on Elite.** Conversion-optimization tactic; brand-position violation.

4. **No fake urgency ("Vault filling up fast! 217 seats left!"). Real seat counter renders on /vault landing, not /pricing.

5. **No testimonials.**

6. **No comparison to competitor pricing.**

7. **No "starting at" pricing language.** Each tier shows the actual price.

8. **No risk-reversal language ("Risk-free!"). Refund terms are stated factually.

9. **No "limited time" framing.** All pricing is current; no implied deadline.

10. **No "founders' edition" / "lifetime access" tiers.** Galaxy doesn't sell lifetime memberships; subscription is the model.
