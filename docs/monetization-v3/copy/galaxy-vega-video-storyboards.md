## Decision log entry

### DEC-NEXT-022 - Prepare five Vega storyboards before visual production

**Decision:** Galaxy will storyboard the first five Vega videos before commissioning or generating visuals.

**Rationale:** Storyboards catch brand drift, pacing issues, weak CTAs, and over-focus on the character before production spend. They also make the designer/vendor handoff more concrete.

**Guardrail:** These are private production drafts. Public posting still requires platform-policy review, Garrett approval, and live proof-surface URLs.

---

## Shared format

Each video:

- 60-90 seconds.
- 9:16 vertical.
- Garrett voice.
- Vega appears as artifact guide.
- Artifact card is the main visual.
- CTA routes to proof surface.
- No direct checkout.
- No hype hook.

Shared visual system:

- Near-black background.
- Off-white artifact cards.
- Muted blue accent for methodology.
- Muted star-gold accent for Vega.
- Captions in high-contrast lower third.
- Vega at lower-right or upper-left, never center-stage for the full clip.
