# Galaxy 12-Month Personal Retrospective Template

**Audience:** Garrett. Private. NOT for any Galaxy public surface or member-facing communication.

**Purpose:** At the end of Year-1, Garrett reviews not just Galaxy's metrics but his own operating experience. This template is the framework for that personal reflection.

**Use:** Last week of December 2026, after the Month-12 KPI decision is made (per `copy/vault-month-12-renewal-decision-memo-template.md`) but before drafting the year-end annual report.

**Duration:** 2-3 hours.

**Storage:** private personal file outside `docs/monetization-v3/` because it is personal-private. Suggested filename: private/year-1-retrospective-2026.md.

## What this retrospective deliberately doesn't do

1. **No member-facing content.** This is private. Public retrospective is in the year-end annual report.

2. **No comparison to other founders' Year-1.** Galaxy operates by Galaxy's compass.

3. **No goal-setting or revenue projections.** Year-2 strategic question framework handles those.

4. **No emotional venting disguised as reflection.** The format is structured for productive self-awareness, not catharsis.

5. **No editing for "how this would sound to others." This is private.**

## How this connects to other Year-end work

The personal retrospective is one of three Year-end documents Garrett produces:

1. **This document** — private/year-1-retrospective-2026.md (personal-private).
2. **Year-2 strategic question framework decisions** — future owner archive under `reviews/` (per `galaxy-year2-strategic-question-framework.md`).
3. **Year-end annual report** — `copy/galaxy-year-end-annual-report-template.md` (public).

All three happen in December 2026, in the order above. The personal retrospective informs the strategic decisions; the strategic decisions inform the annual report.

The template is updated when:
- Year-1 ends + Garrett uses it: the first instance generates lessons about the template itself.
- Year-2 ends: template is refined based on Year-1's experience.

Future-Garrett may rewrite this template entirely. The current version is the starting point.
