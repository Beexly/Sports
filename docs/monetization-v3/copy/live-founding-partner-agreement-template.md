## Section 1 — What Galaxy provides

Galaxy will provide the Partner with the following during the term of this Agreement:

### 1.1 Galaxy Live OBS Plugin (the "Plugin")

- A configurable streaming overlay that displays Galaxy's live Edge Index, factor breakdown, and methodology references during sports content streams.
- Installation support, documentation, and technical onboarding via direct communication with Galaxy founder (Garrett Baxley) or designated technical contact.
- Ongoing software updates throughout the term, at no additional cost.
- A streamer-facing dashboard at galaxysportsedge.com/[partner-slug]/dashboard for overlay configuration.
- Crash-safe failure behavior — Plugin will fail closed (overlay disappears) rather than fail loud (errors visible to viewers or stream).

### 1.2 Partner Attribution

- Galaxy will track sign-ups attributable to Partner via UTM-tagged links unique to Partner.
- Partner attribution URL format: galaxysportsedge.com?ref=[partner-slug]
- Tracking begins on Effective Date.
- Monthly attribution reports delivered to Partner via email by the 5th business day of the following month.

### 1.3 Founding Partner Status

- For the duration of this Agreement, Partner is recognized as one of Galaxy's founding streamer partners (limited to a maximum of five total founding partners in V1).
- Public recognition, if any, requires Partner's prior consent.

## Section 2 — What Partner provides

In exchange for the above, Partner agrees to:

### 2.1 Active overlay use during eligible streams

- Partner will use the Plugin during a minimum of [eight streams per month] containing sports content where Galaxy's overlay is reasonably relevant.
- The Plugin remains visible during a minimum of [30 cumulative minutes] of each qualifying stream.
- Partner retains full editorial control over what content the overlay displays during their stream (Partner may hide overlay at any time, may select which data shows, may select position and opacity).

### 2.2 Verbal acknowledgment

- Partner will verbally acknowledge Galaxy Sports Edge during at least one segment per qualifying stream when the overlay is active.
- Acknowledgment language is at Partner's discretion. Galaxy will not require specific scripted language.
- If Partner is asked by their audience what the overlay is, Partner agrees to provide a true and accurate description (e.g., "Galaxy Sports Edge — sports research platform with public losses and a methodology page").

### 2.3 Brand-aligned representation

- Partner agrees not to publicly characterize Galaxy as: a returns service, a black-box automation product, a tout service, or a platform that promises specific betting outcomes.
- If Partner makes statements that materially misrepresent Galaxy, Galaxy reserves the right to require correction or, if not corrected within seven calendar days, terminate this Agreement.

### 2.4 Reasonable cooperation on technical issues

- If the Plugin causes a technical issue (crash, latency, visual error), Partner will report the issue to Galaxy within 24 hours via the designated technical contact.
- Galaxy will respond to technical reports within one business day.

## Section 3 — Compensation

### 3.1 No fixed fee during initial term

Partner receives no fixed fee or upfront payment from Galaxy for participation as a founding partner during the initial 12-month term.

### 3.2 Revenue share

For the duration of the initial 12-month term AND for 12 months thereafter (24 months total revenue-share window from Effective Date), Galaxy will pay Partner 10% of the first-year subscription revenue from each Galaxy subscriber whose sign-up is attributable to Partner via UTM tracking, subject to the following:

- The attributable subscriber must have completed payment and passed the 14-day refund window.
- The 10% applies to the FIRST 12 MONTHS of the attributable subscriber's subscription only. Renewal years are not commissioned.
- Galaxy's standard subscription tiers: Pro ($19/month), Elite ($49/month), Vault ($200/year). Revenue-share applies to actual subscription revenue collected by Galaxy, before payment processor fees.
- Stripe processing fees (~3%) are borne by Galaxy, not subtracted from Partner's share.

### 3.3 Payment terms

- Revenue-share accrues monthly. Galaxy issues monthly statement and payment by the 15th business day of the following month.
- Payment method: Stripe Connect direct deposit OR via standard ACH; Partner specifies preference at signing.
- For payments aggregating to less than $50 in a given month, Galaxy may accumulate until the next month where total exceeds $50.

### 3.4 Tax responsibility

Partner is solely responsible for taxes on income received under this Agreement. Galaxy will issue a Form 1099-NEC (or applicable equivalent for non-U.S. partners) for any calendar year in which Partner receives more than $600.

### 3.5 Clawback for refunds

If an attributed subscriber refunds within their first 12 months of subscription, the proportionate revenue-share for that subscriber is reversed. If reversals exceed accrued credits in a given month, the deficit carries forward to the next month's accrual.

## Section 4 — Term and termination

### 4.1 Initial term

12 months from Effective Date.

### 4.2 Renewal

After the initial term, this Agreement converts to month-to-month. Either party may terminate by providing 30 days written notice.

### 4.3 Termination for cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if:

- The other party materially breaches any provision and fails to cure within 14 calendar days of written notice of breach.
- The other party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases operations.
- The other party engages in conduct that materially damages the goodwill of the other party.

### 4.4 What happens at termination

Upon termination for any reason:

- The Plugin will stop functioning on Partner's streams within 24 hours.
- Partner attribution tracking continues for any sign-ups that occurred during the active term (revenue-share continues until the standard 12-month window per attributable subscriber expires).
- Partner remains obligated to confidentiality and to non-misrepresentation (Section 8 below) for one year following termination.

---

## Section 5 — Exclusivity and non-compete

### 5.1 No exclusivity required by Galaxy

Galaxy does not require Partner exclusivity. Partner may continue all other partnerships, sponsorships, and promotional relationships during this Agreement.

### 5.2 Limited Galaxy commitment

Galaxy will limit founding partner program to a maximum of FIVE simultaneous partners in V1. Galaxy will not enter into a sixth founding partner agreement during the initial 12-month term of any active founding partner.

### 5.3 No tout-style non-compete

Galaxy will not prevent Partner from promoting other sports research products, sportsbooks, or competing services. Galaxy explicitly recognizes that streamers maintain multiple commercial relationships and that requiring exclusivity would damage Partner's audience trust.

---

## Section 6 — Intellectual property

### 6.1 Galaxy retains all rights to the Plugin

The Galaxy Live OBS Plugin, all Galaxy branding, Galaxy Edge Index data, factor breakdown methodology, and supporting materials are and remain Galaxy's intellectual property.

Partner is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Plugin solely during the term of this Agreement.

### 6.2 Partner retains rights to their content

Partner retains all rights to their stream content, including the audio and video recorded during streams where the Plugin is active. Galaxy claims no ownership interest in Partner's broadcasts, recordings, or audience.

### 6.3 No use of Partner identity without consent

Galaxy will not publicly identify Partner as a founding partner, use Partner's name in marketing materials, or characterize Partner's relationship to Galaxy without Partner's prior written consent. This includes social media, press releases, website content, the annual Galaxy Almanac, and investor materials.

### 6.4 Partner reference to Galaxy

Partner may reference Galaxy Sports Edge on Partner's social media and audience communications consistent with Section 2.3 (brand-aligned representation).

---

## Section 7 — Galaxy commitments to Partner

### 7.1 Plugin reliability

Galaxy commits to maintaining the Plugin at a level of technical reliability such that it does not crash Partner's stream. If the Plugin causes a stream-impacting failure attributable to Galaxy's code, Galaxy will:

1. Acknowledge the issue publicly if Partner requests.
2. Patch the issue within five business days of acknowledgment.
3. Credit Partner one additional month of revenue-share window for material disruptions affecting more than one stream.

### 7.2 Reasonable support response

Galaxy will respond to Partner's technical or business questions within one business day during the initial term.

### 7.3 No coercion on content

Galaxy will not pressure Partner to promote specific Galaxy publications, picks, or marketing campaigns beyond the verbal acknowledgment described in Section 2.2.

### 7.4 Honest representation of Partner data

Galaxy will not publish performance data attributable to Partner's audience without Partner consent. Internal Galaxy analytics use the attribution data for operational purposes only.

---

## Section 8 — Confidentiality

### 8.1 Confidential information

The following are confidential and may not be disclosed by either party without the other's prior written consent:

- The specific revenue-share rates and payment amounts.
- Galaxy's subscriber counts, internal performance data, methodology specifics not published on galaxysportsedge.com.
- Partner's audience analytics and viewership data not otherwise public.
- The commercial terms of this Agreement.

### 8.2 Confidentiality survives termination

The confidentiality obligations of this Agreement survive for one year following termination.

### 8.3 Permitted disclosures

Each party may disclose this Agreement to:
- Their respective legal, financial, and tax advisors who are bound by confidentiality.
- Their business partners (managers, agents) who are bound by confidentiality.
- As required by law, court order, or regulatory inquiry.

### 8.4 Public-facing partnership status

Partner and Galaxy may both publicly identify the Partner as a "founding streamer partner of Galaxy Sports Edge" subject to written consent (Section 6.3). Public framing of the relationship must be consistent and brand-aligned.

Partner has no authority to bind Galaxy to obligations beyond this Agreement. Galaxy has no authority to direct Partner's day-to-day content decisions.

## Section 11 — Limitation of liability

Neither party's liability under this Agreement exceeds the total revenue actually paid or received under this Agreement, except in cases of:

- Material breach of Section 6 (intellectual property).
- Material breach of Section 8 (confidentiality).
- Gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

Galaxy specifically disclaims liability for any betting losses, audience losses, sponsorship-conflict damages, or other consequential losses Partner may incur as a result of Plugin use or termination.

### 12.4 No sports betting recommendations to minors
