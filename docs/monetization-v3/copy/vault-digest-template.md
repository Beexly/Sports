# Vault Digest Template

Status: Draft
Cadence: weekly, Wednesday
Target length: 500-900 words
Writing budget: 45-75 minutes after Claude/cockpit notes are available

## Digest Contract

Each digest covers one publication from the prior week. It can be a win, a loss, or a pass. The selection criterion is not entertainment; it is whether the publication teaches something about the model.

## Standard Structure

```markdown
# Vault Digest - [Week of YYYY-MM-DD]

## The Publication

[Plain statement of the pick, loss, win, or pass.]

## The Factor That Drove It

[The most important factor in the call. Explain why it mattered.]

## The Assumption Underneath

[The assumption the factor relied on. This is where the model could be fragile.]

## What Could Have Flipped It

[What new data, line movement, injury news, market behavior, or model signal would have changed the call.]

## What We Would Do Differently Now

[If rerunning the publication after the result, what would change? If nothing should change, say why.]

## Member Note

[One short note for Vault: a question, chart, or office-hours topic.]
```

## Variant Protocols

### If the Digest Covers a Win

Do not celebrate. Focus on whether the right thing happened for the right reason.

Required sentence:

```text
The result was favorable, but the important question is whether the reasoning survived.
```

### If the Digest Covers a Loss

Lead with the miss. Do not bury it under process language.

Required sentence:

```text
This was wrong, and the useful part is where.
```

### If the Digest Covers a Pass

Explain why non-publication was the discipline.

Required sentence:

```text
This did not make the slate, and that is the point of the digest.
```

## What Not to Include

- New picks.
- Individual betting advice.
- Private market tips.
- Promises of future performance.
- Competitor trash-talk.
- Long caveats that obscure the actual lesson.

## Office-Hours Handoff

Each digest should end with one discussion prompt that can become the week's office-hours or Discord thread.

Examples:

- Which factor in this publication deserves more weight?
- Did the pass discipline hold, or was Galaxy too conservative?
- Was the loss a model issue, a market issue, or acceptable variance?
