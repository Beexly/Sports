## The conference design

### Format

**Annual Vault Conference.** First held: Q4 2027 (target). 1.5 days. Mix of in-person + livestream.

- In-person: 200 attendees max. Founding-cohort members get first invite; remainder fills from Year-2+ Vault members on waitlist.
- Livestream: 1,500 attendees max. Open to all Vault members + select invited industry friends.

### Cadence

Once per year. October or November typically (avoiding NFL season peak + sports gambling peak periods).

### Location

Year-1 (2027): single US city. Travel-friendly hub (NYC, Chicago, Atlanta, or Austin). Specific venue TBD by Q2 2027.

Year-2+ (if conference continues): may rotate cities, may add second event.

### Content arc

```
Day 1 — Morning (3 hours)
- Garrett opens: state of Galaxy (45 min).
- Methodology deep-dive: factor model evolution since Year-1 (30 min).
- Q&A with the room (60 min).
- Coffee break (45 min) — networking.

Day 1 — Afternoon (3 hours)
- Loss Room retrospective: a year of autopsies (60 min).
- Member-led panel: members discuss their own approaches alongside Galaxy's (60 min).
- Q&A (45 min).
- Wrap-up (15 min).

Day 1 — Evening
- Dinner (optional, separate ticket). Founding cohort + invited industry friends.

Day 2 — Morning (3 hours)
- Pass List deep-dive: what we didn't publish + why (45 min).
- Open Q&A (90 min).
- Closing remarks + Year-2 announcement (45 min).
```

Total: ~9 hours of content + 1 dinner. Restrained format; no "after-party" mass event.

### Pricing

- In-person: $500 (Year-1). Includes both days + dinner. Covers venue + AV + food + Galaxy's modest production budget.
- Livestream: $50. Includes all sessions + recordings.

Founding-50 members get first-class treatment:
- First-priority in-person invitation (before public Vault members).
- Dinner included free.
- Optional: signed Galaxy Almanac (if available).

### Revenue model

Year-1 conference target:
- 200 × $500 = $100,000 in-person revenue.
- 1,500 × $50 = $75,000 livestream revenue.
- Total: ~$175,000.
- Costs: ~$70,000 (venue, AV, catering, travel for Garrett, recording infrastructure).
- Net: ~$105,000.

The conference is brand-position-aligned: not a revenue maximizer; not a loss leader. A break-even-plus event that compounds member experience.

### What's NOT in the conference

1. **No keynote speakers from outside Galaxy.** The conference is about Galaxy + members. Inviting external speakers dilutes the brand position.

2. **No paid sponsorships.** No sportsbook logos, no fintech presenting partners. Galaxy's brand-position rejects this.

3. **No celebrity bettors or sports media personalities.** Galaxy is method-led.

4. **No "show floor" with vendors.** Pure conference; no exhibition.

5. **No after-party bottle service.** Galaxy's brand-position rejects this.

6. **No "first-year-special" pricing.** Conference price is conference price.

### Format constraints

- Day 1 ends by 18:00. Members travel back to hotels at reasonable hour.
- No livestream after-party.
- Recording quality matters: professional AV team, multiple cameras, transcript-ready audio.
- Recordings released to Vault members within 7 days post-event (free with their Vault subscription).

## Risks + mitigations

### Risk 1: Conference quality doesn't match Galaxy's brand position

If the venue is wrong, the AV is bad, the format feels "industry conference" rather than "Galaxy room": members notice. Brand-position-damage potential is real.

**Mitigation:** Galaxy hires top-tier production partners (venue, AV, catering). Budget is allocated carefully. Garrett oversees every element.

### Risk 2: Garrett can't deliver content across 9 hours of program

Single-operator hosting a 1.5-day event is intense. Founder burnout risk is real.

**Mitigation:** Member-led panel reduces Garrett's solo airtime. Pre-event prep (1-2 weeks of focused preparation) reduces in-the-moment cognitive load. Day 2 is shorter than Day 1.

### Risk 3: Low attendance

Conference economics depend on filling seats. Vault membership at Year-2 cap-lift may not produce enough committed attendees.

**Mitigation:** Conference doesn't proceed unless Vault Membership > 3,000 at Year-2 mid-point. Smaller cohort → no conference (or conference smaller-scale).

### Risk 4: Member criticism of conference

Some members may feel the conference is "selling more" rather than "deepening the Vault product." If brand-position-aligned criticism surfaces.

**Mitigation:** The conference is communicated as a benefit FOR Vault members, not a separate revenue stream. Founding-50 members get dinner free + first-priority invitation. The brand position holds.

## Conference team

The conference requires more than single-operator + 1099 contractors:

- **Venue + catering vendor.** Contracted via standard 1099 engagement.
- **AV / production company.** Contracted; budget significant ($25-40k).
- **Conference logistics coordinator.** 1099 contractor for Q2-Q4 2027 (~$10-15k).
- **Recording/transcript service.** Per the office-hours archive vendor or equivalent.

Garrett doesn't try to run the conference solo. Hiring the coordinator is the difference between a brand-aligned conference + a brand-damaging one.
