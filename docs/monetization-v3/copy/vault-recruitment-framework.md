# Vault Recruitment Framework

Status: Week-1 execution companion

## Target Sample

30 interviews across 3 pools:

| Source pool | Count | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Galaxy Elite subscribers | 10 | Validates upgrade path |
| Twitter followers, unsubscribed | 15 | Surfaces conversion blockers |
| Sports-betting Discord members | 5 | Tests adjacent audience reach |

## Diversification Criteria

Audit the sample after 12-15 booked calls. Bias new outreach toward under-sampled categories.

| Dimension | Target mix |
|---|---|
| Betting frequency | Daily 8-10 / Weekly 12-15 / Monthly-seasonal 5-8 |
| Annual spend | Under $1k: 3-5 / $1k-$5k: 8-10 / $5k-$25k: 10-12 / $25k+: 3-5 |
| Tenure | New under 2 years: 5-7 / Mid 2-5 years: 12-15 / Veteran 5+ years: 10-13 |
| Sport coverage | NFL 12-15 / NBA 8-10 / MLB 4-6 / Multi-sport 5-8 |
| Paid research history | Currently paid 18-22 / Never paid 8-12 |

## Elite Subscriber Pull

Filter:

- Active Elite subscription for 60+ days.
- Site interaction 5+ times in last 30 days.
- Has engaged with Loss Room or Pass List at least once.

Rank:

1. Most recent login.
2. Frequency of board, ledger, and loss-room visits.
3. Email reply history.

## Twitter Pull

Filter:

- Follows `@GalaxySportsAI`.
- Public account.
- Bio mentions sports, betting, fantasy, handicapping, NFL, NBA, MLB, or adjacent interest.
- Tweeted or retweeted in last 30 days.
- Not currently a Galaxy paid subscriber.

Avoid:

- Other tout services.
- Anonymous accounts with no profile info.
- Accounts that have publicly criticized Galaxy.

Rank:

1. Engagement with Galaxy posts.
2. Profile clarity.
3. Follower count in the 5k-100k range.

## Discord Pull

Filter:

- Posts regularly.
- Mentions research, methodology, restraint, transparency, or similar topics.
- Has had at least one prior interaction with Garrett.

## Mid-Week Sample Audit

After 12-15 booked calls, ask:

- Are daily bettors overrepresented?
- Are never-paid users underrepresented?
- Is NFL crowding out NBA/MLB/multi-sport?
- Is the sample too warm to predict public launch?
- Are high-spend bettors too few to validate the premium tier?

Then adjust outreach for the remaining calls.
