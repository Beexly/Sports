# Vault Renewal Email Sequence

**Audience:** Garrett. Internal.
**Purpose:** The Year-1 → Year-2 renewal moment is Vault's single highest-stakes member touchpoint. The email sequence below covers the 35-day window from Day-330 to Day-365 + the post-renewal follow-up.

**Note:** This sequence works alongside `copy/vault-retention-checkins.md` (the broader retention check-in cadence) and `launch/vault-renewal-period-playbook.md` (the operational playbook). This file specifically covers the renewal-email copy.

---

## Email cadence overview

| Day | Email | Trigger | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 330 | Email 1: "A year ago" | Automated | Heads-up that renewal is coming; soft frame |
| Day 345 | Email 2: "What this year looked like" | Automated | Reflect the year + restate the brand position |
| Day 358 | Email 3: "Renewal — a personal note" | Automated | Final pre-renewal email; honest framing |
| Day 365 | Email 4: "Welcome to Year 2" | Automated (after charge) | Thank-you for those who renewed |
| Day 365 | Email 5: "If you decided not to renew" | Automated (after no-renewal) | Honest goodbye for those who didn't |
| Day 380 | Email 6: "Two weeks into Year 2" | Automated (for renewers) | Renewal landed; check-in |
| Day 380 | Email 7: "Anything we should change?" | Automated (for non-renewers) | Last touch; feedback request |

The cadence is restrained. 7 emails across 50 days. Spam-pressure avoided.

---

## Email 1: Day 330 — "A year ago"

**Subject:** A year ago — and what comes next

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

A year ago today you joined Vault. (Well, a year ago plus or minus a few days; the actual signup was on [date].)

I want to flag something a few weeks out: your subscription renews on [renewal date]. Stripe will process the charge automatically unless you cancel before then.

Two reasons I'm telling you now and not on the day:

1. It's not a surprise this way. You can decide on your own schedule.

2. If something about Vault hasn't been right this year — content, methodology, the pace, the community — I'd rather hear it now than after you've cancelled. Reply to this email and I'll read it.

The renewal is automatic; the decision is yours.

Either way: a real thank-you for being part of Year-1. The founding cohort made this possible.

— Garrett
```

### What this email does

- Sets the renewal expectation 30+ days out.
- Acknowledges the member's specific signup anniversary.
- Invites pre-renewal feedback rather than post-cancellation feedback.
- Doesn't push for renewal; doesn't pressure.

### What this email doesn't do

- Promote V2 features or upcoming products.
- Quote testimonials or "success stories."
- Use urgency-language ("don't miss out").
- Pressure for an answer.

---

## Email 2: Day 345 — "What this year looked like"

**Subject:** What Vault Year-1 looked like

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

A reflection on the year, since renewal is two weeks out.

Quantitatively — the things you can verify:
- Galaxy published [N] calls across [Vault digests / Pass List / Loss Room].
- Calibration held at [actual %] in the [confidence-band] across the year.
- The Loss Room published [N] autopsies. The Pass List published [N] held games.
- The methodology was revised [N] times across the year, all documented at galaxysportsedge.com/methodology.

Qualitatively — the things harder to measure:
- We resisted [specific brand-position decision Galaxy made this year — e.g., declining a sportsbook partnership, holding the digest format despite member requests for more picks, etc.].
- We invested [specific investment Galaxy made — e.g., the Almanac, the methodology page revision, the office hours cadence].
- We mostly didn't apologize for being slow when speed wasn't the point.

I won't claim Year-1 was perfect. I'll claim it was the year I'm proudest of building so far.

Year-2 ahead. Renewal is on [date].

— Garrett
```

### What this email does

- Quantifies the year's output transparently.
- Acknowledges the brand-position investments + restraint.
- Positions the renewal decision as informed, not assumed.
- Stays grounded in the methodology + the year, not the sale.

### What this email doesn't do

- Quote member testimonials.
- Use marketing copy ("we exceeded expectations").
- Hide the calibration metric (calibration is the most-important number; publish it).
- Promise specific Year-2 deliverables.

## Email 3: Day 358 — "Renewal — a personal note"

**Subject:** A week from renewal

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

One more email before renewal on [date].

If you've thought about it and Vault Year-2 doesn't fit — please cancel. The button is on your account page or [cancel link here]. Stripe won't charge you on [date].

If you've thought about it and Year-2 does fit — just do nothing. The renewal lands automatically + your access continues uninterrupted.

If you're on the fence and want to talk it through — reply. I'll respond personally. The 15 minutes is mine to give; the decision is yours.

A few things that wouldn't happen if you cancel:
- Stripe won't charge you again.
- Your Discord access continues to the end of your current term, then revokes.
- You can re-subscribe later at the then-current Year-2 price (if higher than Year-1, you'd pay the new price).

A few things that won't happen even if you stay:
- You won't get a Year-2-only special discount.
- You won't get a "founding-status-extended" deal.
- You won't get more emails about renewal after this one. The decision is yours; my job is to make it as easy as possible.

— Garrett
```

### What this email does

- Provides clear option-A vs option-B framing.
- Removes urgency by removing artificial urgency.
- Surfaces specific consequences of each choice (Discord access, repricing).
- Reinforces that the decision is the member's.

### What this email doesn't do

- Discount for renewal.
- Last-minute offer.
- Guilt for considering cancellation.

---

## Email 4: Day 365 — "Welcome to Year 2" (renewers only)

**Subject:** Renewed — and a quick note

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

The renewal landed. Welcome to Year 2.

A few notes:

1. **Nothing changes today.** Same content, same cadence, same Discord, same office hours. Year-2 is a continuation.

2. **The methodology will continue to evolve.** Updates will land in the digest + the methodology page + the next Model Journal — same channels as Year-1.

3. **Your founding status is permanent.** You're founding-[N]. That's locked in regardless of what Galaxy looks like in Year-3 or Year-5.

4. **What I'd most appreciate:** if there's something you wanted from Year-1 that didn't happen, reply. The Year-2 plan still has flex.

Thanks for the renewal. This is the bet I'm making, and the renewal is the strongest signal that the bet is working.

— Garrett
```

### What this email does

- Acknowledges renewal warmly.
- Reinforces continuity (no shock changes coming).
- Surfaces founding-status as a permanent recognition.
- Opens the door for Year-2 feedback.

### What this email doesn't do

- Push V2 features that weren't promised.
- Use celebratory language.
- Promise specific deliverables.

---

## Email 5: Day 365 — "If you decided not to renew" (non-renewers only)

**Subject:** Year 2 — without you

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

The renewal didn't happen on [date]. Your Vault access continues through the end of your current term ([end-of-current-term date]); after that, Discord roles revoke + your subscription is closed.

I'm not going to ask you to reconsider. The renewal decision is yours, and I'd rather respect it than pressure you.

A few things, before you go:

1. **The methodology page stays public.** galaxysportsedge.com/methodology will continue to be there. If you want to follow Galaxy from the outside, that's the surface.

2. **The Loss Room + Pass List stay public.** You can read both at the website even after Vault access ends.

3. **You can re-subscribe later.** If Year-2 doesn't go the direction you wanted, or Year-3 looks more like what you wanted, the door is open. (You'd pay the then-current price; no special "founder return" deal.)

4. **I'd genuinely value your honest read** on what Vault Year-1 missed for you. Reply with whatever you'd want to say. I read every reply. The feedback doesn't change the cancellation; it shapes what Year-2 becomes.

Thank you for being part of Year-1. The founding-cohort exists because people like you signed up before there was a track record.

— Garrett
```

### What this email does

- Acknowledges the cancellation directly + respectfully.
- Removes friction from the goodbye.
- Surfaces public surfaces (methodology, Loss Room, Pass List) the member can continue to access.
- Asks for feedback explicitly.

### What this email doesn't do

- Beg for reconsideration.
- Promise a "win-back" discount.
- Tag the member for re-engagement marketing.
- Treat the cancellation as a failure on either side.

## Email 6: Day 380 — "Two weeks into Year 2" (renewers)

**Subject:** Two weeks into Year 2

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Two weeks into Year 2. Quick check-in.

If anything about Vault has shifted — pace, format, voice, methodology — let me know. Year-2 is still finding its rhythm; feedback is most actionable now.

If everything's working as expected: just say "all good" and that's enough signal.

— Garrett
```

### What this email does

- Lightweight check-in 2 weeks post-renewal.
- Surfaces any Year-2 drift the member might be noticing.
- Doesn't pressure for a response.

### What this email doesn't do

- Push survey or NPS.
- Ask "would you recommend Vault?" (referral language).
- Promote specific Year-2 deliverables.

---

## Email 7: Day 380 — "Anything we should change?" (non-renewers, optional)

**Subject:** A quick question

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

A few weeks since your Vault cancellation. I won't ask you to reconsider — that's settled.

But I would value a 1-2 sentence reply on what Vault missed for you. No agenda; just honest.

The reason: Year-2 plans are still flexible. Honest reads from people who left have shaped product decisions I wouldn't have made otherwise.

If you don't have a take — totally fine; skip the email. If you have one — reply with whatever you'd want to say. Even a fragment is helpful.

— Garrett
```

### What this email does

- Final, optional touch with non-renewers.
- Frames the request as Garrett's need for input, not the member's obligation.
- Sets up the possibility of re-engagement without pushing.

### What this email doesn't do

- Promise a "we'll fix it" callback.
- Use guilt or scarcity.
- Push for a re-subscription.

### Decision point: include or skip Email 7?

This email is optional. If Galaxy's non-renewer cohort is small (<10% of total) + cancellation feedback has been collected through other means, Email 7 can be skipped. If non-renewer cohort is larger or feedback is sparse, Email 7 fires.

---

## What we don't do across the sequence

1. **Don't send Email 1 only X days before renewal.** The 35-day lead time is non-negotiable.

2. **Don't add an in-app pop-up alongside the emails.** The emails are the channel; multiplying surfaces compounds annoyance.

3. **Don't market V2 features the member won't see.** Year-2 product is still being built.

4. **Don't quote past-year revenue or member-count numbers.** The renewal isn't a sales pitch.

5. **Don't issue last-minute discount codes.** Brand-position violation; permanent posture.

6. **Don't move the renewal date.** Galaxy doesn't extend trial periods or grant grace-period delays at scale.

## Founding-50 special touchpoint (if applicable)

For founding-50 members, replace Email 4 (Day 365 renewer) with a personal DM (Discord or email):

```
Hey [first name],

A founding-[N] renewal landed. Wanted to drop a personal note rather than the standard email.

Year-1 worked because the founding-50 signed up before there was a track record. The renewal is the strongest signal that the trust held.

Year-2 will continue the brand position — methodology, restraint, Loss Room, Pass List — and add the Almanac (shipping January 15) and whatever Galaxy Live looks like by Q3 2027.

Your founding-[N] status stays permanent. The pricing stays $200 for as long as you renew.

Substantively: anything you'd want Year-2 to focus on or fix that Year-1 didn't address? Reply with whatever's on your mind.

— Garrett
```

The personalized DM signals the founding-50 relationship continues with care.

- Email reply rates per email.
- Feedback themes from replies.
