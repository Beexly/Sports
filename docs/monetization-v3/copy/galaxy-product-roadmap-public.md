# Galaxy Product Roadmap (Public-ready)

**Audience:** Garrett primarily; member-facing if Garrett chooses to publish a redacted version.
**Purpose:** Galaxy will be asked "what's next?" by members, press, and inbound. The roadmap below is the canonical answer — Year-1 → Year-2 → Year-3, with restraint about specific commitments.

**Note:** This document is intentionally less detailed than the master plan. It captures the "where are we headed" frame without over-promising deliverables.

---

## How Galaxy talks about the roadmap

Galaxy's roadmap discipline:

1. **We commit to direction, not deadlines.** "Galaxy will publish a hardcover annual" is a direction. "Galaxy will publish the Almanac on January 15" is a deadline-once-committed.

2. **We commit publicly only to what we'll do.** No "we're considering" or "we might add." If it's in the roadmap, we're doing it.

3. **We name what we're explicitly NOT doing.** Restraint is product. The "not doing" list is published.

4. **Year-2 + Year-3 stay loose.** We don't commit to specific features 18+ months out. The frame stays directional.

## Year-1 (2026 — Launch Year)

### What's shipped

By end of 2026:

- **Vault subscription** ($200/year). Wednesday digest, Pass List, Loss Room, monthly office hours, Vault Discord.
- **Methodology page** + Loss Room + Pass List archive publicly accessible.
- **Model Journal** (cadence: ~monthly) published on the public site.
- **Twitter presence** (@GalaxySportsAI) — restrained content discipline.
- **Annual report** — Year-1 retrospective published in December.

### What's NOT shipped in Year-1

- The Almanac (ships January 15, 2027 — Year-2 trigger, technically Year-1 work).
- Galaxy Live (Year-2 product).
- Any tier above $200/year Vault.
- Black-box prediction features.
- Sportsbook partnerships of any kind.
- Galaxy mobile app.
- Public API.
- Community manager or contractor hires (single-operator through Year-1).

### Year-1 product evolution

The methodology improves, the digest cadence holds, the brand position is demonstrated.

What members can expect in Year-1:
- ~50 Wednesday digests.
- ~200-300 Pass List entries.
- ~30-50 Loss Room autopsies.
- 12 monthly office hours sessions.
- 12 Model Journal entries.
- 1 Year-1 retrospective + annual report.

## Year-2 (2027 — Maturation)

### What ships in Year-2 (conditional on Year-1 success per KPI ritual)

- **Galaxy Almanac (Year-1 edition)** — January 15, 2027. Hardcover + digital.
- **Vault continues at $200/year** for founding-50 + Year-1 cohort. Pricing for new Year-2 joiners examined per `galaxy-vault-pricing-evolution-framework.md`.
- **Vault Conference (V2 cap-lift activation)** — conditional on renewal ≥70%. Q4 2027 target.
- **Galaxy Live** — Q1 2027 partnership exploration; Q3 2027 launch target with first founding-partner streamer.
- **Founding-50 advisory channel** — formalized per spec; Month-4-6 activation in Vault Year-2.
- **Methodology v3.x** — model evolution incorporating Year-1 autopsy lessons.

### What's NOT shipped in Year-2

- Vault tier above $200 (or whatever Year-2 base is).
- Galaxy mobile app.
- Public API for member-built integrations.
- Galaxy podcast (deferred indefinitely; the brand position rejects personality-first content).
- Sponsorship of Galaxy content.
- Sportsbook affiliate partnerships.
- Investor fundraising (bootstrap-through-Year-2 holds).

### Year-2 strategic decisions

Per `galaxy-year2-strategic-question-framework.md`, 7 strategic questions get answered in December 2026:
- Question 1: Vault Year-2 trajectory (status quo / cap-lift / sunset).
- Question 2: Almanac annual commitment.
- Question 3: Live track status (proceed / defer / kill).
- Question 4: Hiring plan (community manager / contractor / continue solo).
- Question 5: Capital structure (bootstrap / fundraise / acquisition).
- Question 6: Sport coverage (current scope / NHL playoff add / other expansion).
- Question 7: Brand position (continue / refine / shift).

## Year-3 (2028 — Compounding)

### What ships in Year-3 (highly conditional on Year-2 trajectory)

- **Galaxy Almanac (Year-2 edition)** — January 15, 2028. Second annual.
- **Vault Conference (Year-2)** — if Year-1 Conference activated + executed well, Year-2 Conference proceeds.
- **Galaxy Live (mature partnerships)** — Year-2 founding partners renewed; potentially 1-2 additional streamer partnerships.
- **Vault membership cap-lift consideration** — V2 (5,000-member) cap may extend per renewal trajectory.
- **Potential community manager hire** — if Year-1 + Year-2 demonstrated the brand position holds under operator-multiplied execution.

### What's possibly shipped in Year-3 (lower commitment)

- **Higher-touch Vault tier** ($500-$1000/year)? Open question; depends on member feedback + brand-position calibration.
- **Galaxy Live as standalone product** (not just streamer partnerships)? Open question; depends on Live track maturity.
- **Galaxy Index** (deferred from monetization expansion plan)? Open question; revisited Year-3 if Vault + Almanac + Live are mature.
- **First investor conversation** (if fundraising)? Open question; only if Year-2 ARR is $5M+ and the right partner exists.

### What's NOT shipped in Year-3 (still excluded)

- Sportsbook affiliate revenue.
- Black-box prediction features.
- Galaxy mobile app (unless member-led need is clear).
- Galaxy podcast (continues to be deferred).
- Sponsored content (permanent posture).
- Galaxy Search / Discovery integration (deferred unless partner emerges).

## What Galaxy will NEVER do

The roadmap includes some explicit "never" commitments. These are permanent posture, not "right now" defer:

1. **Never sportsbook affiliate revenue.**
2. **Never sponsored content.**
3. **Never tout-certainty framing.**
4. **Never black-box prediction marketing.**
5. **Never celebrity-bettor partnerships.**
6. **Never outcome-promise language.**
7. **Never sell or share member email lists.**
8. **Never make calls based on tips or non-public information.**
9. **Never compromise Loss Room transparency.**
10. **Never compromise the methodology page transparency.**

These commitments hold across Year-1, Year-2, Year-3 — and beyond.

## How members learn about the roadmap

Galaxy doesn't have a public roadmap page (it would invite the "where's feature X" trap that other platforms struggle with).

Instead:
- **Annual report** (December each year) covers the Year-N retrospective + Year-N+1 directional commitments.
- **Vault digest** (occasional, when relevant) references roadmap items in passing.
- **Office hours** is the live venue for member questions about the roadmap.
- **#vault-feedback** is where members can ask about specific items.

If a member asks "is X on the roadmap?", the response is direction-only:

```
Hey [first name],

Quick answer about [X]:

[Yes — it's planned for Year-Y. Specific timing depends on [prior dependency].]
OR
[No — it's not planned. The reasoning is [brand-position constraint or operational reason].]
OR
[Genuinely open. We haven't decided. The decision will happen [at which gate per the strategic framework].]

Real answers; not marketing language.

— Garrett
```

## How press learns about the roadmap

Galaxy doesn't publish detailed roadmap to press. The press kit references the brand position + the 3 product surfaces; specific roadmap items are not disclosed unless they've already shipped.

If press asks about future plans:

```
Galaxy's focus through Year-2 is the 3 active tracks — Vault, Almanac, Live. Specific feature work + partnership work is deliberately not pre-announced; we ship what we ship.

The brand position is what we promise. The discipline is what we ship.
```

---

## What this roadmap deliberately doesn't do

1. **Doesn't commit to specific dates 18+ months out.** Galaxy commits to "Year-2 Almanac ships" not "Year-2 Almanac ships March 1."

2. **Doesn't quantify member targets publicly.** Member counts are not roadmap deliverables.

3. **Doesn't reference revenue targets.** ARR isn't a roadmap item.

4. **Doesn't list "exploring" or "considering" items.** If it's not committed, it's not in the roadmap.

5. **Doesn't promise partnership-conditional outcomes.** Galaxy doesn't say "Live will have N streamer partners by date." Galaxy says "Live will have founding partners we believe in."

6. **Doesn't apologize for what's not yet on the roadmap.** The list of "not shipping" is intentional restraint, not gap.

---

## When the roadmap gets updated

- **At Year-1 close (December 2026):** Year-2 commitments locked.
- **At Year-2 close (December 2027):** Year-3 commitments locked.
- **Quarterly during the year:** Internal review; rarely public-facing change.
- **At Year-2 strategic framework activation:** 7-question synthesis informs roadmap.

Updates are quiet. No "Roadmap V2.0!" announcements. The annual report is the substantive communication.
