## Corrections Required Before Public Use

- Do not say Outlier raised in November 2025 unless a November-dated primary source is found. Current spot-check supports December 2025.
- Be precise about PFF: Teamworks acquired the enterprise/B2B business, not necessarily the entire consumer-facing company.
- Treat third-party revenue/subscriber estimates as planning comparables unless primary sources confirm them.

## Public Claim Guardrail

Public copy should say only what Galaxy can prove:

- Good: "We publish our losses and the games we pass."
- Good: "Deterministic model outputs refresh on a schedule."
- Avoid unless legally reviewed: "Most transparent platform in sports betting."
- Avoid unless sourced and current: competitor subscriber counts, funding claims, market-size claims.
