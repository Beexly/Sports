# Continuity and Founder Dependency

Galaxy can survive a short founder absence. It cannot survive long founder absence without delegated operators.

Operator-protection companion: [founder-resilience-playbook.md](founder-resilience-playbook.md).

## Founder-Dependency Map

| Track | Founder-dependent function | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vault | Weekly digest, monthly office hours | High | Claude drafts from cockpit logs; Garrett edits; communicate missed office hours transparently |
| Almanac | Year-in-review essay and editorial review | Medium | October content freeze; copyeditor; layout designer; avoid December crunch |
| Live | Partner relationships and brand voice in BD | High | 1099 BD consultant as alternate point of contact; partner agreements survive individual absence |

## 2-Week Absence Test

| Track | Impact | Survival |
|---|---|---|
| Vault | 1 missed digest and possibly 1 missed office hour | Survives with explanation and content credit |
| Almanac | Minor schedule slip except during final production | Survives |
| Live | BD conversations may slow | Survives with consultant support |

## 2-Month Absence Test

| Track | Impact | Survival |
|---|---|---|
| Vault | 8 missed digests and 2 missed office hours | Significant damage |
| Almanac | Recoverable May-October; high risk November-January | Conditional |
| Live | Partner relationships strain | Significant damage |

## Continuity Hires

| Hire | Trigger | Cost |
|---|---|---:|
| Vault community manager, 1099 part-time | Month 6 or 1,000 members | ~$2k/month |
| Editorial assistant, 1099 part-time | Month 9 Almanac crunch | ~$3k/month for 4 months |
| BD consultant, project basis | Live launch prep | $10k-$25k per quarter |

Year-1 continuity-hire budget: $30k-$50k.

## Continuity Rules

- Every recurring customer-facing commitment needs a backup owner or a written missed-service protocol.
- Every partner conversation needs a CRM entry and next step.
- Every Almanac production artifact needs a versioned source and freeze date.
- Every decision gate needs a decision-log entry.
