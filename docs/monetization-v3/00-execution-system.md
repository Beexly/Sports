# Execution System

## Founder Commitments

Garrett commits to three operating constraints before monetization work expands:

1. Pre-commit to the active-track focus determined by runway.
2. Pre-commit to track-level and founder-level kill criteria.
3. Pre-commit to honest runway calibration before code or spend.

If these commitments are not real, the plan becomes theater. If they are real, the business can compound.

## Side-by-Side Workflow With Claude

Claude and Codex can run in parallel if ownership boundaries are explicit.

| Workstream | Primary owner | Codex ownership | Claude ownership |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy source of truth | Garrett | Convert strategy into operating docs, templates, dashboards, and implementation tickets | Challenge assumptions, sharpen messaging, draft high-level strategic copy |
| Customer development | Garrett | Trackers, scripts, interview forms, synthesis templates | Interview question refinement, pattern synthesis |
| Product implementation | Codex | Repo changes, product specs, engineering plans, tests | Copy drafts, rationale digests, editorial drafts |
| Decision discipline | Garrett | KPI gates, dashboards, decision logs | Narrative interpretation and second-opinion review |

Rule: Claude can draft and critique; Codex owns repo artifacts and implementation structure unless Garrett redirects.

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not launch more than runway allows.
- Do not start Vault engineering before customer development unless operating in the 6-month survival scenario.
- Do not activate any deferred track without a written activation gate.
- Do not soften the brand into generic tout language.
- Do not let revenue pressure increase pick volume or weaken restraint.
- Do not remove kill criteria without replacing them.
- Do not override a track kill criterion more than once.
