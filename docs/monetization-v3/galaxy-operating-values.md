# Galaxy Operating Values

**Status:** Single-page codification of Galaxy Sports Edge's operating values. Distinct from `galaxy-brand-voice-canonical.md` (that file is voice rules; this file is values that the voice rules express).

**Purpose:** A document Garrett can hand to a hire, a contractor, a partner, or an acquirer. It explains what Galaxy is in the simplest possible terms — without explaining how to write a sentence.

**Read time:** 4 minutes.

---

## The five values

### 1. Process beats outcome on horizon

A single losing pick is data. A single winning pick is data. Both teach less than the operating discipline that produced them.

Galaxy publishes its losses, its passes, its methodology, and its model versions so that the process is auditable. The outcomes are downstream — calibrated probabilities meeting reality, recorded in the Ledger and the Loss Room.

**What this means in practice:**

- We do not celebrate wins publicly. We do not apologize for losses publicly. We publish both, with the same page count.
- We do not change the model based on individual outcomes. We change the model based on patterns across many outcomes.
- We do not promote subscribers, partners, or members based on whether they bet well. We do not measure ourselves by whether they did.

---

### 2. Restraint is the product

Most sports analytics platforms compete on volume — more picks, more sports, more confidence, more often. Galaxy competes on restraint — fewer picks, no tout-certainty, no win-rate bragging, no urgency manipulation.

The restraint costs reach. The restraint earns the readers we want.

**What this means in practice:**

- We cap daily publication volume on purpose. The Pass List makes the cap visible.
- We refuse marketing vocabulary that promises outcomes ("guaranteed," "lock," "edge as marketing word").
- We turn down promotional opportunities — sponsorships, affiliate deals, partnerships — that would dilute the position.
- We pick smaller audiences over larger ones when the smaller audience is closer to the brand position.

---

### 3. Transparency is demonstrated, not claimed

Words like "trustworthy" and "transparent" are devalued in the sports betting category. Every tout service uses them.

Galaxy doesn't use the words. Galaxy publishes the artifacts that earn them.

**What this means in practice:**

- We do not write copy that says "Galaxy is transparent." We publish the Loss Room, the Pass List, and the methodology page. Readers assess.
- We do not write copy that says "Galaxy is the only honest sports research platform." We publish what we did. Readers compare.
- We publish what we'd be embarrassed to publish, when it's true. The autopsy on a 64% confidence call we got wrong reads worse than the win does. We publish both.
- When we are wrong, we say so plainly and quickly. The apology is short; the autopsy is long.

---

### 4. Method, not personality

Galaxy is built by an individual founder. The brand reflects the operating method, not the founder's personality.

This is the rule most often tempted to break. Founders' faces sell. Galaxy's brand resists this.

**What this means in practice:**

- Public Galaxy surfaces use "we" or "Galaxy" pronouns. "I" is reserved for founder-led editorial surfaces (Vault digests, Almanac essay, founding-member emails, office hours).
- We do not run a founder Twitter persona. The @GalaxySportsAI account speaks for Galaxy, not for Garrett.
- We do not publish "founder's journey" content. The journey is irrelevant; the operating method is the work.
- We do not put a founder photo on the homepage. Galaxy's identity is method, not face.

---

### 5. Decisions get logged

Every meaningful decision Galaxy makes gets an entry in the decision log. The log is append-only, dated, and reasoned.

The discipline is not bureaucratic. It is the operating discipline that makes the rest of the work possible.

**What this means in practice:**

- We do not change a major direction without a decision-log entry explaining why.
- We do not override a pre-committed kill criterion without a decision-log entry naming the new criterion.
- We do not pivot the brand position in response to one piece of critique. We pivot if a pattern emerges in the log.
- We do not pretend a past decision was always the plan. The log shows the evolution.

---

## What the values rule out (the operating discipline as no's)

The five values implicitly reject:

- **Tout-shop operating model.** Selling picks-as-products with selective memory.
- **Volume-led growth.** Maximizing publications to drive engagement.
- **Founder-personality brand-building.** Optimizing for the founder's visibility over the method's visibility.
- **Marketing-vocabulary inflation.** Using language Galaxy can't defend with evidence.
- **Outcome-chasing pivots.** Changing the model after a single bad month or single good month.
- **Member-tier proliferation.** Adding paid tiers to extract more revenue per member without adding more product depth.
- **Affiliate dependency.** Earning revenue from sportsbook traffic in ways that distort what Galaxy publishes.
- **Acquihire optimization.** Designing the company to be sold instead of designed to operate.

These are not "things we won't do." These are "things that would mean Galaxy isn't Galaxy anymore."

## How to use these values

### As a hiring filter (when Galaxy hires anyone)

For any future hire (community manager, editorial assistant, BD consultant, eventual full-time staff):

1. Send this document before the interview.
2. In the interview, ask: "Which of the five values would be hardest for you to operate under?"
3. The honest answer tells you whether the hire is brand-aligned.

The wrong hire: anyone who says "I love all five." That's the wrong-shape person.

The right hire: someone who picks a specific value and explains why it would be hard, then explains how they'd operate under it anyway. That's the brand-aligned person.

### As a contractor filter

For 1099 contractors (cover designer, copyeditor, lawyer, BD consultant):

- Send this document with the engagement scope.
- Ask: "Is anything in here a problem for how you work?"
- The honest answer surfaces friction before it costs Galaxy time.

Specifically: a contractor who doesn't understand value #4 (method, not personality) will produce work that drifts toward founder-celebration. Catch it early.

### As a partner filter

For any partner (streamer, podcast host, affiliate, B2B customer):

- Send this document during the partnership conversation.
- Ask: "Are these values compatible with your audience?"
- A partner whose audience expects volume + hype will struggle to integrate Galaxy. Better to know before a contract is signed.

### As an acquirer filter (Year 2+)

If acquisition conversations begin:

- Send this document to the acquirer's leadership.
- Ask: "Will Galaxy retain these values under your ownership?"
- The honest answer determines whether the acquisition would preserve Galaxy or destroy it. Sometimes Galaxy declines the higher offer.

## What this document is NOT

1. **Not a marketing tagline.** "Process beats outcome on horizon" is not a Twitter post. The values are operational, not promotional.
2. **Not a vision statement.** This is what Galaxy IS, not what Galaxy aspires to become.
3. **Not a company culture deck.** Galaxy is single-operator-plus-contractors V1. Culture-deck framing implies a team that doesn't exist yet.
4. **Not a code of conduct.** The brand-safety scanner is the conduct enforcement. This document is the operational identity behind the conduct.
5. **Not a strategy document.** Strategy lives in the root `CODEX_MONETIZATION_V3_MASTER_BRIEF.md` and `docs/monetization-v3/`. Values are upstream of strategy.

---

## When to revisit this document

Quarterly. As part of the founder-resilience playbook's reflection on whether the brand position still feels right.

If Garrett reads these five values and any one of them no longer feels true — that's a strategic signal. The values are the bet. If the bet changes, this document changes, the brand position changes, the operating discipline changes.

A change to this document is the highest-stakes change Garrett can make. It requires:

1. 7-day cooling-off period after the impulse to change.
2. Decision-log entry explaining the change.
3. Cascade-review: which downstream surfaces (brand voice, landing copy, methodology page, Almanac essay) need updating because of the change.
4. Public communication to Vault founding-50 members explaining the change.

The high friction is by design. The values are the bet.
