## Track 3: Galaxy Live - Sketch Partnership

Phase: Phase 7 launch target, Q1 2027 or later depending on runway and product sequencing

Offer: OBS plugin for Twitch and YouTube stream overlays

V1 pricing: $99 Solo / $499 Team

Core promise: Galaxy's live Edge Index and factor breakdowns become visible inside sports streams.

### V1 Product

- OBS plugin
- Streamer dashboard
- Auth
- Overlay position, opacity, and data controls
- Live Edge Index
- Factor breakdown
- Founding partner attribution links

### Launch Strategy

This is not a generic streamer rollout. It is the Sketch moment plus 4 additional founding partners:

1. Sketch
2. Mid-tier sports streamer
3. YouTube sports analyst
4. Twitch DFS streamer
5. YouTube sports betting podcast

Founding partners get zero-cost access in exchange for promotion and 10% revenue share on attributable subscribers for 12 months.

### Sketch Outreach Path

Sketch is the highest-leverage target, but the plan must avoid fantasy BD.

1. Run a 2-week warm-intro audit across Garrett's network, adjacent creators, streamer managers, sports betting podcasts, and Twitch partnership contacts.
2. If no warm path appears, hire a 1099 BD consultant for 90 days at $10k-$25k to find a credible introduction.
3. If the consultant fails after 90 days, send one direct cold message and one follow-up 14 days later.
4. If Sketch does not respond, recruit 5 mid-tier founding partners and revisit Sketch after case studies exist.

### TAM and Year-3 Ceiling

| Step | Estimate |
|---|---|
| US Twitch sports streamers with 10k+ followers | 5,000 |
| US YouTube sports streamers with 50k+ subscribers | 3,000 |
| Regularly streaming live sports content | 2,400 |
| Willing to pay for overlay | 240 in year 1 |
| Realistic year-3 capture with strong launch | 1,000 |

Year-3 Live streamer-tier ARR ceiling: $3.0M.

Tier A platform deals are upside, not the base case. Two platform deals at $1.5M/year each add $3.0M.
