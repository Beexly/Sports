# Customer Development

Customer development is the integrity layer that prevents expensive product work before demand is validated.

## Vault Interview Sprint

Goal: 30 interviews before Vault engineering in the 12-month and 24-month scenarios.

### Target List

| Source | Target count |
|---|---:|
| Galaxy Elite subscribers | 5-10 |
| Twitter followers of @GalaxySportsAI who are not subscribed | 10-15 |
| Sports betting Discord communities where Galaxy is active | 5-10 |
| Total | 30 |

### Interview Guide

Background:

1. How often do you bet on sports?
2. What is your annual betting spend, roughly?
3. Do you currently subscribe to any sports betting research products? Which ones?

Existing-tool experience:

4. Tell me about your relationship with Outlier, PFF, Action Pro, or any other product you use. What works?
5. What is the most frustrating thing about your current research stack?
6. If you canceled tomorrow, why would you?

Galaxy positioning:

7. First reaction to Galaxy Sports Edge?
8. Galaxy publishes losses with autopsies, every considered-but-unpublished game through the Pass List, and refreshes every 30 minutes. How does that read?
9. What is missing?
10. Compared with your current paid tool, does this feel more or less trustworthy? Why?

Vault offer:

11. Vault is a $200/year premium tier. Members get weekly internal-rationale digests, monthly group office hours, Vault-only Discord, quarterly private data review, and early Model Journal draft access. How do you react to that price?
12. What would make Vault worth $200/year to you?
13. What would make Vault feel overpriced?
14. The first 1,000 members are founding-tier. Does that change anything?
15. Would you join the founding 1,000?
16. If no, what is the blocker: price, time, skepticism, or something else?
17. If yes, what would you tell a friend about why?

Wrap:

18. What should change about how Vault is described?
19. Would you be willing to be one of the first 50 founding members?
20. Who else should Garrett talk to?

### Validation Thresholds

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| 20+ qualified yes responses | GO at $200/year |
| 15-19 qualified yes responses | LIKELY GO with 10-interview retest |
| 10-14 qualified yes responses | PIVOT to $150/year retest |
| 5-9 qualified yes responses | DEEP PIVOT; test alternate offers |
| 0-4 qualified yes responses | NO-GO; reposition Vault benefits as Elite perks |

A qualified yes means `intent_to_join` is definitely or likely, `politeness_suspected = FALSE`, and the reasoning names at least one specific Vault benefit. Use `copy/vault-validation-plans.md` for the full Plan A-E action table and secondary gates.

Day-7 synthesis must produce the decision memo and the voice deck:

- `week-minus-1/04-day-7-decision-memo-template.md`
- `week-minus-1/08-voice-deck-template.md`

The voice deck is the feedback loop from interviews into landing copy, welcome emails, checkout copy, Discord seeding, and future press language. If the vocabulary alignment gate fails, do not smooth it over in copy.

## Almanac Interview Sprint

Goal: 25 interviews before production.

### Target List

| Source | Target count |
|---|---:|
| Galaxy subscribers | 10 |
| Twitter accounts engaged with sports analytics | 10 |
| Substack readers of sports research newsletters | 5 |
| Total | 25 |

### Interview Guide

1. Do you own any sports analytics reference books? Which ones?
2. When researching sports analytics, where do you go first: books, online content, paid platforms, or something else?
3. What is the last sports analytics book you bought? At what price? What did you like?
4. What is missing from existing sports analytics references?
5. Galaxy Almanac would be a 300-page annual with every settled pick from 2026, every loss with autopsy, methodology snapshot, and supporting research essays. Hardcover $99, digital $39, audiobook $29 in V2. How does that fit your buying habits?
6. The standout section is every loss explained. Does that change your interest?
7. Hardcover or digital?
8. What section would you open first?
9. Does $99 hardcover feel high, fair, or low?
10. If $99 feels high, what is your maximum for a sports reference book?
11. Would you buy this if it existed January 2027? What is your confidence level?
12. What would prevent you from buying?
13. Who else should Garrett ask?
14. What should change about the description?
15. If a pre-order email arrives in October, would you respond?

### Validation Thresholds

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| 15+ of 25 say they would buy hardcover at $99 | Proceed at $99 |
| 8-14 say yes | Proceed at $79 |
| Under 8 say yes | Digital-only V1 at $29 |

## Live Founding Partner Development

Goal: validate through commitments, not surveys.

### Target Mix

1. Sketch
2. Mid-tier sports streamer with 100k-500k followers
3. YouTube sports analyst with 50k-500k subscribers
4. Twitch DFS streamer with 50k-200k followers
5. YouTube sports betting podcast with 50k-200k subscribers

### Outreach Script

```text
Hi [name],

I'm Garrett, founder of Galaxy Sports Edge. We're a deterministic sports betting research platform: we publish our model's factor breakdowns, our losses with autopsies, and every game we considered and did not publish. Different from the usual touts.

We're building an OBS plugin that overlays our live Edge Index and factor breakdown directly on streams. We're recruiting 5 founding streamer partners: zero cost in exchange for promotion plus 10% revenue share on attributable subscribers for 12 months.

Would you be open to a 30-minute call to see the demo and consider it?

Galaxy: galaxysportsedge.com. The Public Ledger and Loss Room show the part most platforms hide.

Best,
Garrett
```

### Validation Thresholds

| Result after 90 days | Action |
|---|---|
| 3+ of 5 targets agree | Proceed with Phase 7 founding launch |
| 1-2 agree | Single-partner pilot |
| 0 agree | Defer Live and invest in network relationships |

### Pitch Variants

Warm intro:

```text
Hey [first name],

[Mutual contact] suggested I reach out. Galaxy Sports Edge is a sports research platform that publishes its losses and its passes. Different shape than the usual sports-betting tools.

I'm building a live-stream overlay that shows our Edge Index and factor breakdown during sports content. Recruiting 5 founding streamer partners. Zero cost for the founding cohort; 10% revenue share on subscribers attributable to your audience for the first 12 months.

Not a tout product. Not a tail-my-picks overlay. The pitch is: your audience sees the rationale behind the call as you are watching the game.

Open to 20 minutes this week?
```

### Sketch-Specific Flow

1. Map warm-intro network for 2 weeks.
2. If no credible path appears, hire 1099 BD consultant for 90 days.
3. If no path appears after consultant window, send direct cold message plus one follow-up after 14 days.
4. If Sketch does not respond, recruit 5 mid-tier partners and revisit Sketch after case studies exist.
