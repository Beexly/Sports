# Acquisition Optionality

Status: Operating narrative

This plan should not be optimized for acquisition. Acquisition is upside. The base case is a profitable, durable Galaxy business that can operate without needing a buyer.

## Base Case: Sustainable Business

The most likely good outcome is not an exit. It is Galaxy reaching $5M-$10M ARR with high margins and owner control.

At that stage, Galaxy can be a profitable solo-led or small-team business with:

- Vault as recurring high-margin community/editorial revenue.
- Almanac as annual brand artifact and press cycle.
- Live as creator distribution and optional platform wedge.
- Deferred B2B tracks funded organically.

This outcome is valuable even if nobody buys Galaxy.

## Parent Company Acquisition Shape

Earliest realistic acquisition discussion: year 2-3, after $3M-$10M ARR.

Potential acquirer profile:

- Better Collective-style media and betting ecosystem acquirer.
- Sports data or analytics company seeking direct-to-consumer trust surface.
- Subscription media company seeking high-margin sports intelligence product.

Planning valuation ranges:

| Revenue stage | Possible valuation logic |
|---|---|
| $3M ARR | $24M-$30M at 8-10x ARR if growth and retention support it |
| $5M ARR | $30M-$50M strategic conversation range |
| $10M ARR | $80M-$120M if brand, retention, and multi-track portfolio are credible |

These are planning ranges, not public claims.

## Vision LLC Acquisition Shape

Galaxy Vision, if activated, should remain separate from the parent.

Reason:

- Hardware-adjacent AR has a different capital profile.
- Acquirer set differs from sports media and betting ecosystem buyers.
- Separation preserves multi-acquirer optionality.

Potential shape:

- Raise $2M-$5M seed into Vision LLC.
- Galaxy parent retains 70%-80%.
- Vision becomes acquisition-shape after iOS/AR V1 plus meaningful MAU traction.

Possible acquirer profile:

- Apple-like AR platform buyer.
- Sports broadcast or immersive media platform.
- Device ecosystem company seeking sports data overlay IP.

## Failure Case

If kill criteria fire:

- Galaxy returns to side-project mode.
- Highest-performing track may remain.
- Deferred tracks stay frozen.
- Garrett returns to full-time work or raises only if evidence supports it.

This is not a shame state. It is the plan's honesty working.

## Strategic Rule

Do not make decisions that improve acquisition optics while weakening the trust posture.

Examples:

- Do not increase pick volume to inflate engagement.
- Do not weaken the Loss Room.
- Do not obscure Pass List data.
- Do not rebrand as AI for investor fashion.
- Do not activate Vision or Integrity before capability gates clear.

Galaxy's acquisition value, if it comes, is a derivative of restraint and trust. Protect the source.
