# KPI and Decision Rules

The plan is designed to be self-correcting. Garrett reviews active tracks on the last Friday of every month.

## Monthly Review Questions

For each active track:

1. Are we at a kill threshold? If yes, sunset by Monday.
2. Are we at a doubling-down trigger? If yes, invest within 2 weeks.
3. Are we between thresholds? Apply the mechanical rule below.

## Between-Threshold Rule

| Performance vs target | Decision |
|---|---|
| Below 30% | Kill criterion |
| Below 50% for two consecutive periods | Approaching kill; active intervention required |
| Below 80% | Wait one more period |
| 80%-100% | Maintain |
| 100%-150% | Maintain and extend with 20% more capacity if available |
| 150%+ | Doubling-down trigger |

No qualitative override unless the owner-override protocol is followed.

## Owner-Override Protocol

A pre-committed kill criterion can be overridden once per track only if:

1. A decision-log entry explains the override.
2. A new kill criterion is written.
3. The override is the first override for that track.

A second override is automatic sunset.

## Vault Dashboard

Monthly metrics:

- New paid sign-ups
- Active paid members after cancellations
- Renewal rate, rolling 6-month once available
- MRR or ARR equivalent
- Average time-to-first-engagement
- Digest read rate
- Office hours attendance
- Discord active-member rate

### Vault Decision Gates

| Time | Threshold | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| Month 3 | Under 250 signups | Active intervention: investigate funnel and review referral health without promo-rate changes |
| Month 3 | Under 150 signups | Warning: revise pricing or positioning within 30 days |
| Month 6 | 500 members | On track; begin V2 planning |
| Month 6 | Under 250 members | Active intervention: community manager hire to drive engagement and referrals |
| Month 6 | Under 150 members | Kill criterion: sunset Vault tier and reposition as Elite perk |
| Month 12 | 700-999 members with 80%+ retention | Continue and lift cap to 1,500 |
| Month 12 | Renewal rate below 70% | Kill criterion: product is not sticky |

### Vault Doubling Triggers

- Month 3: 500+ sign-ups means move conference earlier.
- Month 6: 750+ members at 90%+ retention means consider cap to 10,000 and price to $300/year.
- Month 6: NPS over 65 means invest in advocacy/referral program.

Referral interventions must follow `copy/vault-referral-program.md`: no double-rate months, no tiered ambassador structure, and no public referrer leaderboards.

## Almanac Dashboard

Metrics:

- Pre-order count
- Launch-month sales
- 6-month cumulative sales
- Hardcover-to-digital sales ratio
- Post-purchase NPS
- Refund rate
- Press mentions

### Almanac Decision Gates

| Time | Threshold | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| Month 10 | Under 500 pre-orders | Warning: immediate press, community, and paid promotion push |
| Month 13 | Under 1,000 total sales | Active intervention: discount campaign, podcast tour, increased press |
| 6 months post-launch | Under 2,500 hardcover sold | Kill V2 hardcover; make V2 digital-only |
| 6 months post-launch | 6,000+ hardcover sold | Doubling trigger: audiobook and sport-specific editions |

### Almanac Doubling Triggers

- 1,000 pre-orders before launch means invest in V2 audiobook earlier.
- 5,000+ hardcover by 6 months means invest in conference-circuit distribution and per-sport editions.
- NPS over 70 means invest in next-year multi-volume edition.

|---|---:|---|

| 3 months post-launch | Under 10 paid subscribers | Warning: marketing push and 30% price reduction for 90 days |
| 6 months post-launch | Under 25 paid subscribers | Kill V1 paid streamer tier; keep partner integration as brand asset |
| 12 months post-launch | 200+ paid subscribers | Doubling trigger: hire platform BD specialist for Tier A |

## Portfolio Quarterly Review

Every quarter:

1. Review all active tracks for kill, intervention, maintain, or double-down status.
2. Review all 10 deferred tracks against activation gates.
3. Recalculate runway.
4. Decide whether to compress, maintain, or expand.

Quarterly output:

- Updated active track list
- Updated deferred track list
- Runway scenario
- Decision-log entries for all changes
