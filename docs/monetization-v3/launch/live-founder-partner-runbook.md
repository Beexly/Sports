# Live Founding Partner Runbook

Status: operational runbook
Build gate: Scenario C and Live activation decision
Agreement companion: `../copy/live-founding-partner-agreement-template.md` (draft only; lawyer review required before use)

## Activation Gate

Do not start Live engineering until:

- Runway scenario is C.
- DEC-NEXT-007 is written.
- 3+ founding partners commit, or Garrett records a single-partner pilot override.
- Founding-partner agreement draft is ready.
- Lawyer-approved agreement exists before any partner-facing contract use.
- Zero stream crashes attributable to overlay is accepted as launch gate.

## Partner Target Mix

1. Sketch.
2. Mid tier sports streamer.
3. YouTube sports analyst.
4. Twitch DFS streamer.
5. YouTube sports betting podcast.

## Outreach Sequence

### Sketch

1. Run 2 week warm intro audit.
2. If no path, hire 1099 BD consultant for 90 days.
3. If still no path, one cold message and one follow up after 14 days.
4. If no response, move on to mid tier founding partners.

### Mid Tier Partners

1. Personalized first message.
2. Demo call.
3. Fit review.
4. Founding agreement.
5. Closed beta.
6. Launch coordination.

## Demo Call Structure

1. Galaxy posture: Loss Room, Pass List, methodology.
2. Why the overlay exists.
3. What the streamer controls.
4. How attribution and revenue share work.
5. Crash safety expectations.
6. Partner's audience fit concerns.
7. Next step: closed beta or no fit.

## Closed Beta Gate

Minimum 30 days with founding partners.

Required:

  Zero stream crashes attributable to overlay.
  Partner can hide overlay instantly.
  Overlay readable at standard stream resolutions.
  Attribution links work.
  Support process tested.
  Partner confirms launch willingness.

## Public Launch Checklist

  [ ] 3+ partners active.
  [ ] Closed beta gate passed.
  [ ] UTM attribution working.
  [ ] Revenue share terms documented.
  [ ] Support docs complete.
  [ ] Rollback/fail closed behavior tested.
  [ ] Launch announcement approved by each partner.

## Kill / Pivot Signals

  0 partner commitments after 90 days: defer Live.
  1 2 commitments: single partner pilot.
  Under 25 paid subscribers 6 months post launch: kill paid streamer tier; keep partner integration as brand asset.
