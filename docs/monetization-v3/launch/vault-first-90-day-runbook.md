# Vault First-90-Day Operating Runbook

**Audience:** Garrett (sole operator V1).
**Scope:** Days 8–90 of Vault operations. Picks up after Codex's `launch/vault-launch-runbook.md` (Days 0–7).
**Goal:** Get Vault from launch to month-3 KPI gate (250 paid members target) without burning out the operator, drifting from brand position, or letting any of the recurring rhythms slip.

---

## The 90-day arc at a glance

| Week | What's the headline | Stakes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (Days 0–7) | Launch + founding-50 onboarding | Highest — Codex's launch runbook covers |
| 2 (Days 8–14) | Founding-50 window continues + first OH approaches | High — first cohort assessing the product |
| 3 (Days 15–21) | First office hours + month-1 KPI check approaching | High |
| 4 (Days 22–30) | Month-1 KPI gate + Day-30 retention email | Critical signal point |
| 5–8 (Days 31–60) | Steady state + month-2 KPI ritual | Pattern formation |
| 9–13 (Days 61–90) | Month-3 KPI gate decision point | Decision point |

The risk profile inverts over the 90 days: Days 1–14 are about not breaking; Days 31–90 are about not drifting.

## The standing weekly cadence (after Day 7)

Set this as the default. Every week looks like this unless explicitly different.

| 16:00–17:00 | Reply to Vault Discord threads from weekend; surface insights from members | Medium |
| 17:00–18:00 | Hard close. No Galaxy work after this. | Low |

| Time | Action | Energy band |
|---|---|---|

### Saturday — OFF

No Galaxy work. No Discord checking. No email. Reset.

### Sunday — Soft pre-load

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 19:00–19:30 (optional) | 30 minutes Monday pre-load. Identify Monday's digest topic. Open relevant tabs. |

Past 30 minutes on Sunday: the work is dipping into Monday's energy reserve. Stop.

## Week-by-week milestone tracking

### Week 2 (Days 8–14) — Founding-50 window continues

**Goal:** Convert remaining founding-50 invitees + drop the first digest.

**Recurring weekly work:** standard cadence above.

**Specific to this week:**
- Day 8: Check founding-50 conversion against expected 60–80% in first 72 hours. If <50% converted by Day 8, send Day-3 personal follow-up per `copy/vault-retention-checkins.md`.
- Day 11: Day-7 follow-up to any founding-50 non-converters.
- Day 13: Day-13 final-window note.
- Day 14: Founding-50 window closes. Public Vault opens.

**Brand-position watch:**
- If founding-50 members start posting picks or asking for picks → reinforce channel rules in pinned post.
- If founding-50 members ask for win-rate updates → redirect to public Ledger.

### Week 3 (Days 15–21) — First OH + brand-soft-launch

**Goal:** Run first office hours successfully. Land week-2 digest. Open public Vault.

**Specific to this week:**
- Day 15: Public Vault opens. Watch sign-ups closely; calibrate against expectations.
- Day 16–18: Continue daily Discord engagement.
- Day 19 (week of OH): full OH prep per Tuesday block above.
- Day 20: First office hours (8pm Eastern). Run per `copy/vault-office-hours-playbook.md`.
- Day 21: OH recording uploaded; summary post in #vault-lounge.

**Critical:** First OH sets the format. If attendance is low (<20 members), don't shorten the hour. Use the full time. Pattern signals "this is real," not "this didn't work."

### Week 4 (Days 22–30) — Month-1 KPI gate

**Goal:** Hit month-1 milestone. Send Day-30 retention email. First KPI ritual.

**Specific to this week:**
- Day 23–25: Daily check on month-1 paid-member count against trajectory.
- Day 28 (last Friday): First monthly KPI ritual per `audit/kpi-operator-ritual.md`. 09:00–11:00.
- Day 29–30: Day-30 retention email auto-sends to first founding members.

**Month-1 KPI thresholds (per `04-kpi-decision-rules.md`):**

- **Track:** Active paid members.
- **Doubling trigger:** N/A at month 1 (too early).
- **Concern threshold:** <30 paid members by Day 30. Investigate funnel.
- **No kill threshold at month 1.** Monthly KPI gates start firing at month 3.

### Weeks 5–8 (Days 31–60) — Steady-state pattern formation

**Goal:** Establish the rhythm. Members move from "new" to "regular." Three more digests + one more OH ship.

**Pattern observations expected:**
- Day 31–45: Engagement is high (new members are curious). Discord posts/day peaks.
- Day 46–60: Engagement normalizes. Some members go quiet (read-only). Others become regulars.
- Day 50ish: Second monthly KPI ritual (last Friday of month 2).
- Day 60: Day-60 conditional retention email to members showing low engagement (per `copy/vault-retention-checkins.md`).

**Risk this period:** boredom drift. Garrett may start losing motivation if KPIs are mid-band. Reread the founder-resilience playbook + the master plan's "the bet, restated honestly" section.

### Weeks 9–13 (Days 61–90) — Month-3 KPI gate

**Goal:** Hit month-3 milestone (250 paid members target per v3 plan + Codex KPI rules). Make the decision.

**Specific to this period:**
- Day 65: Third office hours (cycle has 3 by now).
- Day 70: Quarterly data review delivered to all members (per Vault PRD). PDF + recorded walkthrough. ~25 hours of Garrett's time to produce.
- Day 75–80: Pre-month-3 KPI gate check. Look at trajectory.
- Day 85 (last Friday of month 3): Third monthly KPI ritual + month-3 KPI gate decision.
- Day 90: Day-90 retention email auto-sends. Includes the "favor to ask" Almanac framing.

**Month-3 KPI thresholds:**

- **Under 250 paid members:** Active intervention. Investigate funnel + relaunch press cycle + consider referral push.
- **Under 150 paid members:** Warning. Revise pricing or repositioning within 30 days.
- **250–500 paid members:** On track. Begin V2 planning.
- **500+ paid members:** Doubling-down trigger. Move V2 conference planning earlier.

**Decision-log entry required at Day 90:** DEC-NEXT-MONTH-3-VAULT capturing the trajectory and decision.

## Mistake-prevention list (the things that go wrong if Garrett doesn't watch)

### Wrong 1: Digest gets late → digest gets sloppy

**Pattern:** Monday-Tuesday writing block gets eaten by reactive work. Wednesday morning Garrett tries to write the whole digest. Quality suffers.

**Prevention:** the Monday-Tuesday peak block is for digest writing ONLY. If urgent reactive work appears, do it in Wednesday afternoon's Lower band, NOT in the Tuesday Peak band. Digest takes priority.

**Recovery:** if Garrett gets to Tuesday afternoon without a clean draft, send a shorter Wednesday digest (500 words, not 700) rather than a sloppy 800-word version. Length is negotiable; brand-voice quality is not.

### Wrong 2: First office hours feels awkward → Garrett dreads next OH

**Pattern:** First OH has thin attendance (15 members). Conversation feels stiff. Garrett interprets as "OH not landing."

**Reality:** first OH almost always feels awkward. Members don't know the format. Don't extrapolate from one session.

**Prevention:** read the office-hours playbook before first session. Have a backup of 3 prepared questions to ask the room if conversation stalls. Don't fill silence with monologue.

**Recovery:** post in #vault-feedback after first OH asking what would make next OH better. Members will surface concrete suggestions.

### Wrong 3: Member criticism → Garrett over-corrects

**Pattern:** A Discord post critiques Vault. Garrett rewrites the landing page in response.

**Prevention:** anti-spiral protocol from founder-resilience playbook. One member's critique is one data point. Three independent members on same issue = pattern. Otherwise, log it.

**Recovery:** decision-log entry if anything got changed in response to one-off critique. Make the over-correction visible to future-Garrett.

### Wrong 4: KPI mid-band for 2 months → motivation drops

**Pattern:** Month-1 KPI hits 200 of 250 target. Month-2 hits 220 of 350 target. Trajectory is positive but slow. Garrett starts losing enthusiasm.

**Prevention:** the founder-resilience playbook covers this. KPIs are inputs, not the operator's mirror. Reread monthly.

**Recovery:** schedule a deliberate week off if mid-band lasts 3+ months. The break compounds better than pushing through.

### Wrong 5: Press cycle eats peak block

**Pattern:** Press inquiry arrives Tuesday morning. Garrett interrupts digest writing to respond. Quality of digest suffers; press response is also rushed.

**Prevention:** press response default is 24 hours, not same-hour. Tuesday digest block stays sacred.

**Recovery:** apologize to press contact if response is slower than they hoped; politely explain that Galaxy operates on a publication-first schedule.

### Wrong 6: Vault Discord moderation needed → Garrett delays

**Pattern:** A member posts a betslip in #vault-lounge. Garrett sees it but doesn't act within hours. Other members see the violation; channel-rule signaling weakens.

**Prevention:** moderation actions per `copy/vault-discord-launch-pack.md`. Same-day response on rule violations.

**Recovery:** explicit post in #vault-lounge restating the rule + private DM to violator. Don't compound the delay with silence.

### Wrong 7: Quarterly data review gets rushed

**Pattern:** Day 75 arrives. Quarterly data review is due Day 70. Garrett produces it in 4 hours instead of the 25 hours required.

**Prevention:** quarterly data review work starts at Day 50, not Day 70. The Codex `product/admin-operations-spec.md` cockpit auto-generates the data layer; Garrett's narrative layer is the time investment.

**Recovery:** delay the quarterly review by 7 days if quality would suffer. Announce in #vault-announcements that it's coming next week instead of skipping or shipping rushed.

### Wrong 8: Referral program creates referrer-confusion

**Pattern:** A member uses the referral link incorrectly (e.g., they post it publicly without context). Galaxy gets attributed sign-ups but the referrer doesn't follow up; some sign-ups churn.

**Prevention:** Day-30 referral email + dashboard documentation per `copy/vault-referral-program.md`. Clear language about how attribution works.

**Recovery:** edge cases get manual Garrett review monthly. Most resolve in <5 min.

## What success looks like at Day 90

If the 90 days went well:

- **Vault paid members:** 250–400 (target band).
- **Founding-50 retention:** 95%+ still active.
- **Digest ship rate:** 13 of 13 Wednesdays.
- **OH attendance:** average 30–60 members live + 60–80% replay watch within 7 days.
- **Member NPS:** ≥60 (asked once in Day-60 conditional email).
- **Refund rate:** <5% of monthly subscriptions.
- **Brand-position health:** zero compliance scanner violations in published surfaces; zero member-facing brand drift logged.
- **Garrett's energy:** consistent. Sleep average ≥7 hours/night. Saturday OFF discipline held.

If the 90 days went poorly:

- **Vault paid members:** <150. Below warning threshold.
- **Founding-50 retention:** <80%. The first cohort is leaving; product-market issue.
- **Garrett's energy:** depleted. Sleep slipped. Saturdays eaten by Galaxy work.

**Plan B vs Plan E decision happens here.** Reread `audit/01-vault-landing-page-critique.md` decision flow + `copy/vault-validation-plans.md` thresholds. Either commit to the V2 conference planning OR begin Vault sunset planning.

## What this runbook deliberately doesn't do

1. **No specific marketing targets per week.** Member acquisition is downstream of brand position + member referrals. Don't optimize against arbitrary weekly numbers.
2. **No content-marketing schedule (blog posts, Twitter threads).** Galaxy doesn't run a content marketing program. The operating discipline is content.
3. **No engagement-hacking tactics.** Tactical "post at 9am for engagement" rules are not Galaxy.
4. **No A/B testing of Vault offer.** The Vault offer is locked V1. A/B testing pricing/cap/benefits is V2 planning, not V1 operations.
5. **No formal team meetings.** Single-operator means no internal meetings to schedule. Eventual community manager hire adds one weekly sync.
