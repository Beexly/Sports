## Pre-Order Gate

Do not open pre-orders until:

- Runway scenario is B or C.
- Almanac customer development is complete.
- Price is recorded in DEC-NEXT-006.
- Hardcover/digital format is confirmed.
- Cover-design budget is authorized if hardcover proceeds.
- Delivery and refund policy are approved.
- Export tooling can produce a credible sample data package.

## Pre-Order Page Requirements

- Hero uses accountability-record frame.
- Page lists what is inside.
- Page says Almanac is not a 2027 prediction guide.
- Page states ship/release date: January 15, 2027.
- Page states hardcover and digital pricing.
- Page states refund/delay policy.
- Page does not claim future betting advantage.

## Pre-Order Launch Sequence

### T-14 days

- Finalize cover direction.
- Prepare sample spread.
- Finalize page copy.
- Build email list segment.
- Draft press note.

### T-7 days

- Soft preview to Vault if Vault is active.
- Ask for objections, not praise.
- Fix unclear copy.

### T

- Open pre-orders.
- Email Galaxy subscribers.
- Post on owned social.
- Send press note to targeted sports analytics writers/podcasts.

### T+7 days

- Review pre-orders vs 500 target.
- If pacing is weak, schedule podcast/press push and community announcement.

## Refund / Delay Policy

Recommended:

- 30-day hardcover refund after delivery.
- If book ships late, refund difference between hardcover and digital and buyer keeps both.

Do not publish this policy until Garrett confirms operational willingness to honor it.

## P0 Issues

Pause pre-orders if:

- Delivery date cannot be supported.
- Price or bundle shown incorrectly.
- Refund policy cannot be honored.
- Export data has serious integrity issues.
- Page accidentally frames Almanac as a future prediction guide.
