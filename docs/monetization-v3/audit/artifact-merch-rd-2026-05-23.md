# Artifact Merch R&D

**Date:** 2026-05-23
**Status:** Internal R&D only
**Related decision:** DEC-NEXT-067

## DEC-NEXT-067 - Define merch as artifact-led R&D, not a near-term revenue track

**Decision:** Keep merch as a deferred R&D lane that can only activate after there is demonstrated demand for Galaxy artifacts, phrases, or annual record objects.

**Why now:** Merch is tempting because it feels easy and visible. It is also a classic distraction: low margin, fulfillment complexity, design churn, and brand drift into creator-store behavior. Galaxy should define what merch could be before it becomes an impulsive store build.

## What Merch Could Be

Allowed in principle:

- Almanac-adjacent annual objects;
- Loss Room or Pass List archive prints;
- methodology/calibration desk objects;
- restrained apparel for founding members only;
- signed Almanac bundles if Almanac activates;
- limited internal/team-of-one operational objects.

Not allowed:

- sportsbook-style slogans;
- outcome-promise phrases;
- daily-pick merch;
- sexually suggestive synthetic-host merch;
- platform-meme merch disconnected from the public record;
- drops that require constant promotion;
- discounts, countdowns, or artificial scarcity.

## Best First Products If Demand Appears

| Product | Why it fits | Activation trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Signed Almanac edition | Tied to annual record, not hype | Almanac preorder clears gate. |
| Loss Room print | Turns accountability into object | 10+ members ask for archive objects. |
| Methodology desk card | Reinforces process and restraint | Vault members cite methodology language repeatedly. |
| Founding-50 private object | Thank-you, not acquisition | Founding-50 conversion hits target. |

## What Not To Build First

- Generic logo shirts.
- Character-first merch.
- Hats or hoodies before demand.
- Anything tied to a single winning pick.
- Anything that makes Galaxy feel like a fan club before the record earns it.

## Demand Signals Required

Merch stays dormant until at least two of these are true:

- 10+ organic member requests for a physical object;
- Almanac preorder demand validates physical-product operations;
- a specific Galaxy phrase appears in customer-dev voice deck and member replies;
- a proof-surface artifact gets shared repeatedly without prompting;
- fulfillment can be handled in under 2 founder hours per month.

## Economics Filter

Minimum before activation:

- gross margin target: 50%+ after production and shipping materials;
- founder time: under 2 hours/month after setup;
- support burden: under 5 tickets/month;
- no inventory risk beyond a small test batch;
- no customer-service promise that competes with Vault support.

If merch cannot meet those constraints, it is not a business. It is a chore.

## Brand Filter

Every merch idea must answer:

1. Does this object deepen the record?
2. Would this still make sense if Galaxy had a losing month?
3. Does this make Galaxy feel more method-led or more influencer-led?
4. Would a Vault member be proud to own it without explaining away the tone?
5. Does it still fit if the synthetic character test is killed?

If any answer is weak, do not build it.

## Test Shape

Only after gates clear:

1. private mockup to Vault members;
2. collect interest, not payment;
3. if 20+ qualified members opt in, run a small preorder;
4. fulfill manually once;
5. write a postmortem;
6. decide whether to repeat annually.

## Guardrail

This R&D lane does not activate merch, create a store, select vendors, generate designs, or authorize public posting. It exists to prevent merch from becoming a distraction before Galaxy has earned demand for physical artifacts.
