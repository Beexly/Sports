# Runway Scenarios

The plan is calibrated to cash runway. Do not make the plan fit the desired story; make the active tracks fit the runway.

## Scenario A: 6-Month Runway

Estimated cash on hand: $100k-$200k

Posture: survival.

Active tracks:

- Vault only

Execution changes:

- Compress Vault launch to 8 weeks.
- Skip or sharply compress customer development only if the runway is truly 6 months.
- Launch with a 500 founding-member cap instead of 1,000.
- Target Vault cash-flow positive by month 4 instead of month 6.
- Do not start Almanac or Live.

Expected outcome:

- $100k-$150k year-1 net margin from Vault alone.

Founder-level kill criterion:

- If Vault has fewer than 200 paid members at month 4, Galaxy becomes a side project and Garrett returns to full-time work.

## Scenario B: 12-Month Runway

Estimated cash on hand: $200k-$400k

Posture: foundation.

Active tracks:

- Vault
- Almanac

Execution changes:

- Run Vault customer development before code.
- Launch Vault around month 3.
- Start Almanac production around month 4.
- Almanac launch falls slightly outside the 12-month window, so pre-order validation matters.
- Do not start Live engineering.

Expected outcome:

- $300k-$500k year-1 net margin combined, depending on Vault conversion and Almanac pre-orders.

Founder-level kill criterion:

- If combined Vault plus Almanac pipeline is under $250k by month 9, raise outside capital or return to full-time work.

## Scenario C: 24+ Month Runway

Estimated cash on hand: $500k+ or signed advance against expected revenue.

Posture: portfolio.

Active tracks:

- Vault
- Almanac
- Live

Execution changes:

- Vault launches around month 3.
- Almanac launches January 15, 2027.
- Live launches around month 19 if the plan is counted from May 2026, or Phase 7 if tied to product-roadmap phases.
- Vault and Almanac margin fund the Live investment year.

Expected outcome:

- $400k-$700k year-1 revenue contribution before Live matures.
- $9.1M year-3 revenue ceiling across the active tracks.

Founder-level kill criterion:

- If combined 3-track ARR run-rate is under $1M at month 18, raise outside capital or consolidate to the highest-performing track and reduce Galaxy to side-project mode.
