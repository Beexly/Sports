## Fundraising Posture

Default recommendation: do not raise outside capital for Galaxy parent until year 2 at earliest.

Reason:

- Year 1 ARR around $500k implies weak valuation and high dilution.
- Year 2 ARR around $3M supports materially better terms.
- Year 3 ARR around $10M gives the strongest optionality.

## Galaxy Parent Fundraise Scenarios

| Timing | ARR | Possible valuation | Raise profile |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Year 1 | ~$500k | $4M-$5M | $1M-$1.5M at 25%-30% dilution |
| Year 2 | ~$3M | $24M-$30M | $5M at ~20% dilution |
| Year 3 | ~$10M | $60M-$80M | $10M-$15M at ~15% dilution |

## Galaxy Vision LLC

Vision should be separate if activated:

- Seed raise: $2M-$5M
- Seed valuation: $10M-$15M
- Galaxy parent retains 70%-80%
- Investors receive 20%-30%

Do not activate Vision until the deferred-track gate clears.

## Acquisition Framing

Base case: profitable, bootstrapped business at $5M-$10M ARR.

Plausible upside: acquisition at $30M-$100M valuation once Galaxy reaches $3M-$10M ARR and strategic acquirers can underwrite the brand, subscriber base, and Live/Almanac surface area.

Long-shot upside: Vision LLC acquisition if AR product traction exists.

Failure case: kill criteria fire and Galaxy returns to side-project mode.
