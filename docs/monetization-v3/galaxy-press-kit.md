## What Galaxy will NOT comment on

These are explicit refusal areas. Politely decline if asked.

- **Win rates and specific accuracy claims.** Galaxy publishes calibrated confidence numbers; aggregate win-rate framing is not a metric Galaxy uses publicly.
- **Specific competitor performance comparisons.** Galaxy can compare brand positions but not metrics ("Galaxy's win rate is X% better than Outlier's" — refused).
- **Acquisition speculation or interest.** Galaxy is not currently positioned for acquisition conversations. "We're focused on operating; the rest is downstream of the work."
- **Funding rounds or capital plans.** Galaxy is bootstrapped through Year 1. Capital structure beyond that is a strategic decision that won't be discussed publicly until decided.
- **Individual customer / subscriber identification.** Vault membership is private by default. Galaxy will not confirm whether any specific named individual is a subscriber unless they self-identify publicly.
- **Predictions of specific game outcomes.** Galaxy publishes probabilities at the time of decision; the brand position rejects future-outcome speculation in interviews.
- **Personal financial details of Garrett or contractors.** No.
- **Anything that would require Galaxy to make a claim it cannot defend with data.** If a question requires speculation that contradicts the methodology page, Galaxy declines.

## Embargoed announcements protocol

If Galaxy plans to share something embargoed (Almanac pre-order opening, Vault V2 cap-lift, etc.), the protocol:

1. **24–72 hours before public announcement:** outreach to selected outlets with embargo terms in the subject line.
2. **Embargo subject line:** "EMBARGOED until [date + time + timezone]: [topic]"
3. **Embargo email body:** includes the full announcement, fact sheet, available quotes, and explicit lift time.
4. **Lift confirmation:** Galaxy publishes simultaneously across owned channels (site, email, Discord) at lift time. Press publishes at or after lift time.
5. **Violation handling:** If an outlet breaks the embargo, Galaxy does not reciprocate retaliation. The outlet gets removed from future embargo outreach but no public statement is made.

## What this press kit is NOT

- **Not a marketing brochure.** No subscription numbers, no growth charts, no breathless claims. Press kit reads like Galaxy itself.
- **Not a sales pitch.** Reporters arrive looking for story, not product. The kit serves story.
- **Not promising specific access.** Press contacts get Garrett's time on Galaxy's schedule, not on demand. Garrett is one person.
- **Not co-branded.** Galaxy doesn't share press kit assets with sportsbooks, affiliate programs, or partner brands. Stand-alone.

## What Garrett does when press cycles get heavy

If multiple press inquiries arrive in the same week:

1. Garrett triages by outlet quality (Tier 1 + 2 from `copy/vault-launch-press-pack.md` get same-day response; Tier 3 + 4 get 48-hour response).
2. For Tier 4 (long-tail) inquiries that don't fit current Galaxy bandwidth, polite decline with "I'd love to revisit when we have more public data points to share — likely Year 2."
3. Garrett blocks calendar 2 hours per week during press cycle months for inquiry response. Past that allocation: next-week response.
4. If Garrett is unavailable (vacation, illness, family): auto-responder directs press to email + sets 7-day response expectation. No urgent press needs Galaxy intervention; Galaxy's brand is built on consistency, not urgency.
