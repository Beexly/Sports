## Activation Checklist

No deferred track can move to active until all are true:

- Current active tracks are at or above maintain thresholds.
- Runway remains in the selected scenario after adding the track.
- Required capability or hire exists.
- The track has a named owner.
- A 90-day validation plan exists.
- A kill criterion exists.
- A decision-log entry records activation.
