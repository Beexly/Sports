# Galaxy Year-2 Strategic Question Framework

**Audience:** Garrett. Internal.
**Purpose:** When Year 1 closes (Dec 31, 2026) with Year 1 data in hand, Galaxy faces strategic decisions about Year 2. This framework names the questions worth asking + the decision rules for each.

**Use:** Read once at end of October 2026 to start framing the Year-2 conversation. Re-read in December 2026 as Year-1 data crystallizes.

**Reading time:** 12 minutes.

---

## The 7 Year-2 strategic questions

### Question 1 — Does Vault scale, or does it cap at the founding-1000 forever?

**Year-1 data needed:**
- Founding-1000 retention rate at month 12 (the 70% kill threshold).
- Founding-1000 NPS or qualitative satisfaction signal.
- Active engagement rate (Discord posts, office hours attendance, digest open rate).

**Decision rule:**
- Retention ≥70% + engagement high + member-led demand for more: scale to V2 cap (5,000 members).
- Retention ≥70% but engagement mid-band: stay at 1,000 cap; deepen V1 product.
- Retention <70%: per `launch/vault-sunset-playbook.md` Scenario C, Vault sunsets.

**The strategic question behind the question:** is Vault's value proposition "depth + founder access" (which scales linearly with member count, eventually breaking founder time) or "depth + founder access + small-room dynamic" (which doesn't scale — the small room IS the product)?

If the latter is true, scaling Vault destroys what made it work. Then V2 might mean a SECOND product (not bigger Vault) — different name, different value proposition, with founding-1000 staying capped.

---

### Question 2 — Does the Almanac become an annual artifact or a one-off?

**Year-1 data needed:**
- Almanac pre-order count by November-December.
- Year-1 sales by March 2027 (60 days post-ship).
- Member feedback specifically on the Almanac.
- Press coverage of the Almanac.

**Decision rule:**
- 2,500+ hardcover sold + brand-aligned press coverage + Vault member referral signal: commit to annual.
- 1,500-2,500 sold + mixed signal: produce 2027 Almanac with smaller print run; revisit before committing to 2028.
- <1,500 sold: deferred-to-digital-only for 2027; reconsider hardcover annually.

**The strategic question behind the question:** is the Almanac Galaxy's most durable brand asset (worth annual commitment regardless of unit economics) or is it a Year-1 experiment to monetize the year's work (worth continuing only if economically viable)?

The brand-asset framing argues for annual even at unit-economic loss. The product framing argues for digital-only if hardcover doesn't work.

---

### Question 3 — Does Live activate, defer, or sunset?

**Year-1 data needed (Scenario C only — Live didn't activate in B):**
- Founding 5 partner activity (active overlay use, audience attribution).
- Sketch outreach outcome (warm intro succeeded, BD consultant delivered, or cold outreach failed).
- Paid streamer-tier subscriber count.

**Decision rule:**
- 3+ founding partners + 25+ paid subscribers + Sketch in or pipeline: continue to V2 with Tier A (platform integration with DAZN/fuboTV) exploration.
- 1-2 partners + <25 subscribers: pause to single-partner pilot; revisit after 90 days.
- 0 partners + no Sketch progress: sunset Live entirely for V2; deferred indefinitely.

**The strategic question behind the question:** is Live a Year-2 priority or a Year-3+ priority? Live engineering consumes 10-12 weeks of Codex time + ongoing operational commitment + partnership maintenance. If Year 1 generates more pressure on Vault expansion or Almanac maturity, Live may be the right thing to defer.

---

### Question 4 — Does Galaxy hire?

**Year-1 data needed:**
- Garrett's actual operating capacity at Year-1 end.
- Vault member count + support volume.
- Almanac production capacity strain.
- Live BD bandwidth (if active).
- Founder-resilience-playbook kill criteria status.

**Decision rule:**
- Vault hits 1,000+ members + support volume exceeds 5 hours/week: hire community manager (part-time 1099 → eventually FTE).
- Almanac sells 5,000+ hardcover: hire editorial assistant for V2 Almanac (1099 part-time).
- Live partnerships at 3+ active: BD consultant becomes ongoing relationship (or convert to small fractional FTE).
- All KPIs green + Garrett at <50% capacity: don't hire. Single-operator is the brand position.

**The strategic question behind the question:** does Galaxy want to scale Galaxy (more people doing more) or scale the work (same operator, deeper output)? The former is conventional business growth; the latter is restraint-aligned but caps the company at founder-bandwidth.

A first hire (community manager) is brand-position-safe if the brand voice protocols hold. A second hire is a different threshold — at that point Galaxy becomes a team, not an operator.

---

### Question 5 — Does Galaxy raise outside capital?

**Year-1 data needed:**
- Cash runway at Year-1 end.
- Revenue (Vault + Almanac + Live combined).
- Member growth trajectory.
- Inbound investor interest (probably some by end of Year 1 if metrics are strong).

**Decision rule:**
- Cash runway >12 months + revenue >$1M ARR + organic growth: don't raise. Bootstrap continues.
- Cash runway <6 months + revenue trajectory positive: raise minimal capital (~$500k-$1M) to extend runway without diluting heavily.
- Cash runway acceptable + acquisition shape emerges: don't raise; explore acquisition optionality per `12-acquisition-optionality.md`.

**The strategic question behind the question:** does outside capital change what Galaxy is? Raising capital introduces investor expectations that may misalign with restraint discipline. The "scale subscribers fast" pressure that Outlier's $10.7M raise creates is exactly what Galaxy's brand position avoids.

Galaxy's default: don't raise. Raise only if cash runway is short AND no acquisition path exists AND the investor genuinely aligns with restraint discipline.

---

### Question 6 — Does Galaxy expand sport coverage?

**Year-1 data needed:**
- Member requests for additional sports (tracked in `week-minus-1/08-voice-deck-template.md`, `templates/vault-feedback-themes.csv`, and Vault Discord).
- Factor model maturity per sport.
- Calibration data per sport.

**Decision rule:**
- Multiple sports (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college) all show stable calibration: expand to 1 new sport in Year 2 (e.g., college football specifically, MMA, soccer).
- 1-2 sports show calibration drift: don't expand; stabilize existing coverage.
- Member requests cluster around 1 specific sport not yet covered: investigate; consider adding only if factor coverage is achievable in 6 months of validation.

**The strategic question behind the question:** does Galaxy expand by covering more sports (breadth) or by going deeper on existing sports (depth)? Breadth scales reach; depth compounds brand position. The brand-aligned answer is usually depth, but specific member demand can shift the calculus.

If Galaxy adds a sport in Year 2: it ships under a "experimental" designation for 6 months while calibration data accumulates. Members are told this explicitly.

---

### Question 7 — Does the brand position evolve, or stay locked?

**Year-1 data needed:**
- Brand-position-aligned actions taken throughout the year (decision-log review).
- Brand-position drift incidents (per the qualitative success markers).
- Member feedback specifically about brand position.
- Competitive landscape changes (Outlier, PFF post-acquisition, any new entrants).

**Decision rule:**
- Brand position holds across Year 1 + member feedback positive + competitors haven't matched: lock in for Year 2.
- Brand position drifted in some ways + member feedback mixed: course-correct on specific dimensions; don't change the core position.
- Brand position is being squeezed (competitors copied, market shifted, restraint became commodity): rare. If genuinely happens, evolve thoughtfully via decision-log entry, not reflexively.

**The strategic question behind the question:** is restraint still the brand position that wins? In Year 1, the bet is that restraint compounds. By end of Year 1, the bet either has empirical support or doesn't. If it does: lock in. If it doesn't: investigate why before pivoting.

The brand position is not the company. Changing the brand position is the same kind of decision as sunsetting Vault — a structural change that needs the highest discipline + a one-time override-style protocol.

## How to use these 7 questions

### October 2026 (Year-1 end approach)

- Read all 7 questions.
- Note which questions Galaxy has enough data to start answering.
- Note which questions need additional data (specific KPIs, specific member feedback) before Year-1 close.

### November 2026

- Begin gathering data for the questions that need it.
- Schedule a 90-minute Year-2 strategic review for the last week of December.

### December 2026

- Run the Year-2 strategic review. Two hours.
- For each of the 7 questions: write a paragraph answering it based on Year-1 data.
- For each answer: write the specific Year-2 actions that flow from the decision.

### January 2027

- Year-2 plan locked.
- DEC-NEXT-Y2 series of decision-log entries written:
  - DEC-NEXT-Y2-001 — Vault Year-2 trajectory (scale to V2 cap / stay at 1,000 / sunset).
  - DEC-NEXT-Y2-002 — Almanac Year-2 commitment (annual / V1+V2 incremental / digital-only).
  - DEC-NEXT-Y2-003 — Live Year-2 status (activate further / pause to pilot / sunset).
  - DEC-NEXT-Y2-004 — Year-2 hiring plan (yes specific role / no).
  - DEC-NEXT-Y2-005 — Year-2 capital structure (bootstrap continues / raise / acquisition explore).
  - DEC-NEXT-Y2-006 — Year-2 sport coverage (expand / stabilize / new vertical).
  - DEC-NEXT-Y2-007 — Year-2 brand position (locked / specific course-corrections / structural evolution).

The 7 entries together comprise Galaxy's Year-2 strategic plan.

---

## What Galaxy does NOT do in the Year-2 strategic review

1. **No goal-setting based on hopes vs data.** The 7 questions are answered with Year-1 data, not Garrett's optimism.

2. **No "should we be more aggressive?" framing.** Galaxy's brand position rejects optimization-driven aggressiveness.

3. **No comparing to Outlier's growth rate.** Galaxy and Outlier operate at different scales for different reasons.

4. **No "rebrand" consideration.** Galaxy's brand voice and operating values are locked artifacts. Year-2 doesn't pivot voice; voice evolves continuously.

5. **No emotional override of data signals.** If Year-1 data says sunset Vault, sunset Vault. Don't override because Vault has been emotionally meaningful.

6. **No "what would competitors do?" framing.** Galaxy operates by Galaxy's compass.
