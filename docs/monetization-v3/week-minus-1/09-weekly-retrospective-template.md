# Galaxy Weekly Retrospective Template

**Audience:** Garrett.
**Cadence:** Every Friday 16:00–17:00.
**Pairs with:** `founder-resilience-playbook.md` § "Weekly retrospective" + `galaxy-daily-operations-checklist.md`.

**Use:** Save weekly under `reviews/` with a dated weekly filename. The weekly archive becomes annual retrospective input.

---

## How to use this template

### Friday 16:00-17:00

Open the template. Fill it out honestly. ~45 minutes typical.

Don't optimize for it looking good. The retrospective is private; the data is what matters.

### Friday 17:00

Hard close. Saturday is OFF.

### Monday morning (Step 1 of daily ops)

Re-read last Friday's retrospective as part of the morning routine. The continuity from one week to the next is part of the discipline.

---

## What this template deliberately does NOT do

1. **No goal-setting beyond next-week's three priorities.** The annual + Year-2 planning happens at different cadences.
2. **No comparison to other founders' weeks.** Galaxy operates by Galaxy's pace.
3. **No emotional padding or self-criticism beyond what's useful.** The retrospective is functional, not therapy.
4. **No outward-facing communication generated.** Internal only.
