## Common failure modes + responses

### Outreach response rate <20%

**Cause:** Subject line, opener, or channel is wrong.
**Response:** Iterate on the email template (per `week-minus-1/11-customer-dev-outreach-emails.md`). Don't grind through unchanged.

### Interview slots booking but no-shows >30%

**Cause:** Calendly confirmation flow weak, or low candidate seriousness.
**Response:** Add a 24-hour-before reminder email. If no-shows persist, screen candidates with a 2-question pre-call check.

### Interviews are shallow / generic

**Cause:** Sticking too rigidly to the 30-question script.
**Response:** Lean into open-ended follow-ups. The questions are a frame, not a script.

### All interviews feel like enthusiastic yes signals

**Cause:** Self-selection bias — only the eager candidates booked.
**Response:** Add 5-10 outreach to skeptics (Twitter accounts that publicly critique paid sports content).

### All interviews feel like skeptical no signals

**Cause:** Sample is overweighted toward existing burned-out paid sports content customers.
**Response:** Add 5-10 outreach to candidates without prior paid-sports-content history.

## What this sprint deliberately doesn't do

1. **No A/B testing of pricing.** $200 is the price for the customer dev phase; pricing reactions are signal, not experiments.

2. **No promise of founding-50 status during the interview.** Interview is for listening, not selling.

3. **No multi-interviewer setup.** Garrett runs every interview personally.

4. **No interview compensation.** $20 Amazon gift cards or equivalent are off the table. The interview is a peer conversation.

5. **No recording without explicit consent.** Per legal + interview protocol, recordings require verbal opt-in.

6. **No marketing during the interview window.** Twitter goes quiet on Vault content; the Vault landing page stays in pre-launch state.
