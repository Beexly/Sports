# Founding-50 Outreach — By Candidate Source

**Audience:** Garrett. Internal.
**Purpose:** Founding-50 invitations go to a curated, hand-picked list. The 50 invitations are scarce — fewer than 50 candidates will get them. The outreach templates below cover the 5 main sources the founding-50 cohort will come from.

**Status:** Pre-launch artifact. Activates only after Day-7 decision memo confirms GO + founding-50 selection framework is run per `week-minus-1/07-founding-50-selection-framework.md`.

---

## What founding-50 invitation IS

A personal, single-recipient email from Garrett offering one of 50 founding-cohort spots in Vault. The invitation:

- Is sent before Vault opens to broader audience.
- References the specific reason this candidate is being invited.
- Includes a 7-day reservation window (founding spot held; payment due within 7 days).
- Carries a permanent founding number (1-50) once accepted.
- Reaches founding-cohort advisory channel access after Month 4-6 (per spec).

Founding-50 spots are not "first 50 to subscribe." They are 50 invitations to specific people.

---

## Universal invitation constraints

Across all source templates:

- Subject line ≤55 characters.
- Opening sentence is hand-personalized — references their specific Galaxy-relevant context.
- Founding number is offered but not yet assigned (assigned at payment).
- 7-day reservation window stated clearly.
- Payment link is included.
- No marketing language. No urgency tactics.
- Recipient is given a clear "no" path that doesn't burn the relationship.

## Template 1: Customer-dev interviewee who GO-signaled

**Candidate signal:** During the customer-dev sprint, this person signaled clear interest in Vault at $200/year.

**Subject:** Founding-50 spot — for you, if you want it

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Following up on our conversation a few weeks ago.

You said something during our call that stuck with me: [exact quote or paraphrase]. That's the kind of read that's rare + it's the reason I'm reaching out now rather than waiting for Vault to open broadly.

I want to offer you a founding-50 spot in Vault.

The deal:
- $200/year subscription (same as everyone else).
- You get a permanent founding number (1-50) — that doesn't change regardless of what Galaxy looks like in Year-3 or Year-5.
- Founding-cohort advisory channel access after Month 4 (more on that closer to the time).
- 7-day reservation window from this email; payment via [link].

If founding doesn't feel right for whatever reason — timing, money, just not ready — no problem. Vault opens to the broader audience [N days later]. You're welcome there too.

If you want in: payment link is above. Reply once payment lands + I'll lock in your founding number.

— Garrett
```

### Why this works

- The exact quote from the customer-dev call demonstrates Garrett listened.
- The "rare read" framing honors the candidate without flattery.
- The 7-day reservation window creates structure without false urgency.
- The "no" path is gentle.

## Template 2: Twitter substantive engager (pre-customer-dev)

**Candidate signal:** Person who has engaged substantively with Galaxy on Twitter — not a customer-dev interviewee, but a known signal from public engagement.

**Subject:** Founding spot in Galaxy Vault

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

I've been reading your replies on Twitter for a while + the consistent through-line is that you actually think about how sports forecasting gets built. Most readers don't. You do.

I'm opening Vault — Galaxy Sports Edge's subscription product. Before opening to the broader audience, I'm inviting 50 people who I think will hold the brand position alongside me.

The deal:
- $200/year.
- Permanent founding number (1-50) — yours regardless of what Galaxy looks like in Year-3.
- Wednesday digests, Pass List, Loss Room, monthly office hours, private Discord.
- Founding-cohort advisory channel access after Month 4.

Why founding-50 specifically: the founding cohort signs up before there's a track record. That trust is what makes Galaxy work; the founding status is the permanent recognition of that trust.

7-day reservation window from this email. Payment link: [link].

If founding-50 doesn't fit — timing, money, anything — no problem. Vault opens broadly [N days after this window closes].

— Garrett
```

### Why this works

- Specific reference to their actual Twitter posture.
- Explains *why* founding-50 specifically without making it feel like a sales pitch.
- Frames the trust dynamic honestly.

## Template 5: Known sportsbook critic / methodology rigorist

**Candidate signal:** Someone publicly known for substantive critique of how sports forecasting is sold. Hasn't necessarily engaged with Galaxy yet but is a signal-aligned voice.

**Subject:** Founding spot — methodology over marketing

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Your [specific public work / post / podcast / argument] about [their substantive critique of the industry] is the kind of clarity I want Galaxy to live up to.

I'm opening Vault — my Galaxy Sports Edge subscription product. Before broader launch, I'm offering 50 founding spots to people whose public read on the industry aligns with what Galaxy is trying to do: methodology over marketing, restraint over engagement-bait, transparency over hype.

You've been making that argument publicly for [N years/time]. Wanted to offer you one of the spots.

The deal:
- $200/year.
- Permanent founding number (1-50).
- Wednesday digests + Pass List + Loss Room + monthly office hours + private Discord.
- Founding-cohort advisory channel access after Month 4.

7-day reservation window. Payment: [link].

You may decide Galaxy doesn't live up to the brand position over time. Founding-50 isn't a permanent endorsement; it's a place at the table. If you join + later think we drifted, I'd actually rather hear that than have you stay quietly disappointed.
```

### Why this works

- Honors their public position substantively.
- Frames the founding-50 relationship as accountability, not endorsement.
- Pre-commits Galaxy to accepting critique from the cohort.
- Skeptics who accept tend to become highest-quality founding-50 members.

## What we do AFTER they accept

When a founding-50 invitation is accepted (payment lands):

1. **Assign the founding number.** First-paid-first-served within the founding-50 cohort (1-50 in payment order).
2. **Personal welcome DM** (not the templated welcome email — that comes later).

```
Hey [first name],

You're founding-[N]. Payment received + the number is locked in.

What happens next:
- Standard Vault welcome email arrives within 24h (orientation, methodology page, Discord invite, etc.).
- Vault Discord invite arrives separately.
- You'll see the founding-50 designation in Discord roles once you join.

A few founding-specific things:
- After Month 4-6, you'll get an opt-in invitation to a private founding-50 advisory channel.
- Your $200/year price is locked in for as long as you renew. New Year-2 cohort may pay differently; you don't.

I take founding-50 trust seriously. Reply if anything's confusing or if you want to talk methodology before the digest hits.

— Garrett
```

3. **Update the founding-50 tracker** with name, payment date, founding number, and source.

---

## What we do if they DECLINE

If a founding-50 invitation is declined or doesn't respond within 7 days:

1. **Don't follow up aggressively.** One light reminder at Day 5 of the window:

```
Hey [first name],

Quick check-in on the founding-50 spot. Window closes [date] (a couple days from now).

If founding-50 isn't right, no problem — Vault opens broadly [N days later] + you're welcome there too. If you want in, payment link's still the same: [link].

— Garrett
```

2. **At Day 7 window close:** mark the founding-50 spot as unfilled. Don't offer it to a new candidate. The cohort can finish below 50.

3. **No long-tail follow-up.** No "checking in again 3 weeks later." Respect the no.

---

## What we do if founding-50 doesn't fill

If only 35 founding spots are accepted: the cohort is 35. Founding-50 is the title; the actual number is what gets paid.

- Update the press kit + landing page to reflect the actual founding-cohort size.
- Don't artificially fill the remaining 15 spots from broader Vault sign-ups.
- The founding numbers 36-50 are permanently held — never reassigned, even if Vault Year-3 needs another founding-style cohort.

This is brand-position discipline. The founding-50 spots earned their permanence.

---

## What this outreach does NOT do

1. **No urgency tactics.** "Only 50 spots left" framing is gimmicky; founding-50 spots are by invitation.

2. **No price discount.** Founding pays $200, same as broader cohort.

3. **No "exclusive content" promise.** Founding-50 gets the same digest, Pass List, Loss Room as all members. Founding status is recognition, not tiered content.

4. **No mass-send.** Each invitation is hand-crafted with at least one personalized reference.

5. **No "limited-time founding price."** $200 is the price; founding status is the recognition.

6. **No subscribed-publication marketing.** Founding members aren't asked to share on Twitter or post about their founding-50 status.

---

## Founding-50 source mix target

Aim for a balanced cohort:

- ~15 from customer-dev GO-signal interviewees (Template 1).
- ~10 from Twitter substantive engagement (Template 2).
- ~10 from warm-intro suggestions (Template 3).
- ~5 from existing professional relationships (Template 4).
- ~10 from public methodology-rigorists (Template 5).

Total: 50. Plus 10-15 reserved for "we'll send to whoever organically converts" if the curated list under-fills.
