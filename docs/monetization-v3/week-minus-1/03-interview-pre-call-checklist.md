# Interview Pre-Call Checklist

**Print this. Have it in front of you for every call.**

---

## 5 minutes before call

- [ ] Calendar entry open. Confirm respondent name, source pool, scheduled time.
- [ ] Tracking sheet row created with `respondent_id` assigned (R001–R030).
- [ ] Reviewed respondent's intake: their email signature (if Elite), Twitter profile (if Twitter source), Discord handle (if Discord source). Note one observable detail.
- [ ] Recording tool ready: Zoom cloud-record toggle on, or Otter / Fathom open.
- [ ] Interview guide (`copy/vault-interview-field-guide.md` or the version in `CODEX_MONETIZATION_V3_MASTER_BRIEF.md` Part 4.4) in second tab.
- [ ] galaxysportsedge.com open in third tab (for Q7 screen-share).
- [ ] Loss Room, Pass List, and Methodology page pre-loaded so screen-share is fast.
- [ ] Glass of water nearby.
- [ ] Phone on do-not-disturb.
- [ ] Quiet room. Door closed.
- [ ] Camera on. Lighting good.
- [ ] No distracting background. Galaxy mark visible is fine; logos of other brands are not.

## During the call

- [ ] Open with the 2-minute consent script from interview guide.
- [ ] Ask consent for recording.
- [ ] Stay quiet during Q11 (Vault pitch) — let respondent speak first.
- [ ] Capture verbatim quotes for Q5 (frustration), Q8 (transparency reaction), Q15 (intent reasoning), Q16 (blocker), Q17 (referral message).
- [ ] If respondent's energy shifts (leans forward / leans back), note it in `energy_signal`.
- [ ] If respondent uses vocabulary that's distinct or quotable, capture in `vocabulary_log` sheet.
- [ ] End on time at 30 minutes. Don't run over.
- [ ] Confirm thank-you action: 30-day Galaxy subscription extension (for Elite) or 30-day Elite trial (for non-subscribers).

## Within 1 hour after call

- [ ] Save recording to local drive: `/Galaxy/customer-dev/vault/recordings/R0XX-<first-name>-2026-05-XX.mp4`.
- [ ] Update tracking sheet row with ALL tagged fields per interview guide.
- [ ] Tag `politeness_suspected` honestly. If gut says yes, mark TRUE.
- [ ] Capture 3 most quotable lines in `quote_1`, `quote_2`, `quote_3` columns.
- [ ] If respondent answered `early_commit = yes`, add to `founding_prospects` sheet.
- [ ] If respondent offered referrals (`referrals_offered` non-empty), send referral outreach (Template 9 from CODEX_MONETIZATION_V3_MASTER_BRIEF Part 4.3) by end of day.
- [ ] Send thank-you email (Template 5) within 2 hours.

## Same evening — quick synthesis

- [ ] Re-read the row. Anything missed in the moment?
- [ ] Mid-week pattern check: after every 4th interview, audit diversification (frequency tiers / spend tiers / tenure tiers / sport coverage / subscription state). Course-correct next outreach if any category is over- or under-sampled.

## Red flags to watch for during the call

- **Respondent talks past the questions** — they have an agenda (pitching their own service, asking for free advice). Politely redirect. If continues, end early at 15-min mark.
- **Respondent gets upset** — about past tout service. Listen for 5 extra minutes. Their anger is goldmine for understanding what NOT to do.
- **Respondent obviously doesn't fit profile** — wrong sports, wrong betting frequency, wrong audience. Finish interview anyway. Mark `outside_target`. Negative signal is signal.
- **Call quality is poor** — audio dropping, video freezing. Reschedule. Don't push through bad audio.

## What to do if you're emotionally drained mid-week

After 4 interviews in a day, the 5th interview will be lower-quality. Reschedule to next day. Interview fatigue distorts read more than scheduling slip does.
