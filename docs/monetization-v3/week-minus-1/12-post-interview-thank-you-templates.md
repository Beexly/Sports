# Post-Interview Thank-You + Follow-up Templates

**Audience:** Garrett. Internal.
**Purpose:** Every interview gets a thank-you email within 24 hours. Some get follow-ups based on signal. The templates below cover the main shapes.

---

## Template 1: Standard thank-you (sent within 24h, all interviewees)

**Subject:** Thanks for the time

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Quick note to say thanks for the 30 minutes earlier today. The conversation was substantive — specifically the part about [reference 1 specific thing they said that mattered]. I'm logging it in my notes + your read will shape what Vault becomes.

Two things:

1. If you have follow-up thoughts in the next few days that didn't surface in the call, just reply to this email. I read every one.

2. When Vault launches in [N weeks], I'll send you a heads-up — no pressure to subscribe; just so you know the thing you contributed to is real.

Thanks again,
Garrett
```

### Why this works

- Specific reference proves Garrett was listening.
- Opens a low-friction follow-up channel.
- Signals future contact without committing to a sales push.

---

## Template 2: GO-signal interviewee (high-conviction Vault fit)

**When:** Interviewee said something equivalent to "yes I'd subscribe at $200" without prompting, or was deeply substantive about brand-position alignment.

**Subject:** Thanks + a thought

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Thanks for the 30 minutes. The thing that surprised me most was [specific point they made] — I'd been thinking about [topic] differently, and your framing is sharper.

You mentioned during the call that Vault sounds like something you'd want to subscribe to. Wanted to be straight with you on what comes next:

- Vault opens [target date] at $200/year.
- Founding-50 members get permanent founding number recognition + (later) access to a private advisory channel for substantive input.
- I'll send you a heads-up when subscription opens. No pressure if the timing is wrong.

Genuinely appreciate the conversation.

— Garrett
```

### Why this works

- Doesn't oversell.
- Surfaces founding-50 status only because the interviewee already signaled interest.
- "No pressure if the timing is wrong" preserves the relationship.

---

## Template 3: SKEPTIC interviewee (substantive critique)

**When:** Interviewee pushed back on Vault concept, brand position, or pricing in substantive ways.

**Subject:** Thanks for the honest read

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Thanks for the 30 minutes + the honest pushback. Specifically the point about [their critique]: I've been sitting with it since the call ended.

Two reactions:

1. The brand-position critique [or whichever specific critique] is real. I'm not sure I have a complete answer; it'll shape how I think about Vault Year-2 if not Year-1.

2. The thing I disagree with is [specific point]. My reasoning is [brief, non-defensive]. I might be wrong; the call gave me something to test.

If you want to follow up after Vault launches + see whether the critique held — drop me a line. I'd value the comparison.

— Garrett
```

### Why this works

- Honors the critique without capitulating.
- Acknowledges what's real + names where Garrett still disagrees.
- Frames the relationship as ongoing, not transactional.
- Skeptics who feel heard often become later-cohort members.

---

## Template 4: NO-signal interviewee (Vault isn't a fit)

**When:** Interviewee was substantive but clearly Vault isn't the right product for them.

**Subject:** Thanks — and good luck

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Thanks for the 30 minutes. The conversation made clear that Vault isn't really aimed at someone in your spot — [brief honest reason: e.g., "you don't engage with sports content the way Vault assumes" or "your needs are closer to general analytics than narrow forecasting"].

That's useful to know. It helps me sharpen who Vault is for + not waste your time with marketing later.

If you ever want to point me to people who *would* be a good fit, no pressure but I'd appreciate.

Thanks again,
Garrett
```

### Why this works

- Honest acknowledgment that the fit isn't there.
- Doesn't pressure for a subscription that doesn't make sense.
- Opens the door for referrals without demanding them.

---

## Template 5: PRICING-RESISTANT interviewee

**When:** Interviewee was interested in Vault concept but pushed back on $200/year as too expensive.

**Subject:** Thanks + a note on pricing

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Thanks for the 30 minutes. The pricing pushback is heard.

A quick note on my thinking, not as a defense but as transparency:

- $200/year ($16.67/month equivalent) is where Vault economics actually work — between premium sports newsletters ($100-150/year) and active analytical subscriptions like Outlier ($600/year tier).
- The price is also a filter: members who value the methodology + transparency + restraint enough to pay $200 tend to engage substantively. Members who'd only pay $50 tend to bounce when the content is restrained rather than hot-take-y.

You may still think the price is wrong. That's a real signal.

If Vault Year-2 ends up at a different price for a different cohort, I'll send a note. For now: thanks again.

— Garrett
```

### Why this works

- Honors the price feedback as real signal.
- Explains the reasoning without being defensive.
- Opens future-state possibility (Year-2 pricing experiment) without committing.

## Template 6: REFERRAL interviewee

**When:** Interviewee was so substantive they offered to introduce Galaxy to other potential candidates.

**Subject:** Thanks — and yes to intros

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

Thanks for the 30 minutes + for offering to point me toward folks in your network. Yes please.

If you can send a brief intro email to [specific person they mentioned, if applicable], or just text me names + I'll send blind cold emails — either way works.

Also: I'm logging the conversation as one of the most substantive interviews so far. Specifically [reference one thing they said]. Wanted you to know.

Thanks,
Garrett
```

### Why this works

- Accepts the offered help without forcing it into a specific shape.
- Closes the loop on a substantive interview with a real compliment.
- Sets up the next round of warm-intro outreach.

---

## When to send each template

The sorting decision happens during the interview-notes review (same day as interview):

| Interviewee signal | Template |
|---|---|
| Standard / neutral engagement | Template 1 |
| Explicit GO signal during call | Template 2 |
| Substantive skeptical critique | Template 3 |
| Clear Vault non-fit | Template 4 |
| Pricing pushback as primary objection | Template 5 |
| Offered referrals | Template 6 |

Some interviews fit multiple templates (e.g., GO signal + referral offer). Combine, prioritizing the GO signal.

---

## What this doesn't include

1. **No automated mass-send.** Each thank-you is written within the template but personalized with at least one specific interview reference.

2. **No PDF or attachment.** No "here's the Vault one-pager" included.

3. **No mass mailing-list opt-in.** Galaxy doesn't have a marketing list at this stage; thank-you doesn't subscribe them to anything.

4. **No referral incentive offer.** No "$50 if you refer someone." Per `copy/vault-referral-program.md` discipline.

5. **No celebrity reference.** Garrett doesn't drop names of who else he's talked to.

## Synthesis-share follow-up (Day +14 to +21)

About 2-3 weeks after the customer dev sprint completes, send a synthesis-share email to all interviewees:

**Subject:** Following up on our conversation

**Body:**

```
Hey [first name],

A quick follow-up from our customer-dev conversation a few weeks ago.

I ran 30 of those interviews + wanted to share what surfaced — the patterns, the surprises, the things that shaped what Vault becomes.

[Brief 3-paragraph summary of:
- The most common positive theme across interviews
- The most common skeptical theme across interviews  
- The decision that emerged + the reasoning]

If you have a few minutes to read, here's the longer write-up: [link to public-ready synthesis post or PDF]

If anything in there strikes you wrong or you want to push back, just reply.

Vault opens [target date]. I'll send a separate heads-up around then.

Thanks again for the time + the input,
Garrett
```

### Why this matters

The synthesis-share email:
- Closes the loop on the interview experience.
- Demonstrates Galaxy's transparency operationally (we share what we heard).
- Builds the relationship into something durable beyond the one call.
- Filters: interviewees who reply substantively are highest-likelihood Vault founding-50 candidates.
