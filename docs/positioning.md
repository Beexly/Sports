# Galaxy Sports Edge Positioning

Last updated: 2026-05-22

## Primary Position

Galaxy Sports Edge is transparent sportsbook research built on deterministic scoring.

Primary line:

> We're not AI. We're math you can read.

Use this as the consumer-facing wedge. Do not reposition the product as a general intelligence platform in public marketing copy.

## What To Say

- Deterministic scoring
- Factor model
- Factor breakdown
- Math you can read
- We post when the model finds edge
- Most days, fewer than five picks
- Thin slate? We post less. Sometimes nothing.

## What Not To Say

- AI-powered, AI-driven, or powered by AI
- Mission Control
- ecosystem
- transform, unlock your, level up, your edge starts here
- first-person algorithm voice
- personified board or model language
- pick card or VIP card
- certainty language around outcomes

## Tier Narrative

- Free: See it. One pick a day plus public surfaces.
- Pro: Bet it. Every pick, confidence, reasoning, factor breakdown, and alerts.
- Elite: Master it. Full analysis, advanced tools, weekly learning digest, and early access.

## Public Methodology Rule

Publish the framework: factors, conceptual approach, gating philosophy, and changelog.

Withhold the implementation: weights, constants, and exact aggregation formula.
