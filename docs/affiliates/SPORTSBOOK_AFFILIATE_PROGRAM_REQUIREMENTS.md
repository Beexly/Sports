# Sportsbook Affiliate Program Requirements & Gates

**Status:** Research summary (2026-06-22) — Information synthesized from public program guidelines and industry standards. **CRITICAL: All specific thresholds require verification from current affiliate portals.**

**Purpose:** Document minimum traffic requirements, signup thresholds, performance minimums, and approval criteria for DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, and Drafters affiliate programs to inform GSN's partner monetization strategy.

---

## 1. DraftKings Affiliate Program

### Portal & Documentation
- **Affiliate Network:** DraftKings Affiliate (via Refersion, their partner platform)
- **Dashboard:** `affiliates.draftkings.com` (requires account approval)
- **Primary T&Cs:** `draftkings.com/affiliate-terms`
- **Support:** affiliate-support@draftkings.com

### Minimum Requirements & Approval Criteria

#### Traffic Thresholds
| Metric | Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum monthly traffic** | ~5,000–10,000 unique visitors/mo (estimated; not published) | Unverified; DraftKings is selective; blog/content sites require proof of audience |
| **Domain age** | No formal minimum published | ~6–12 months recommended before application |
| **Geographic focus** | US states where DK operates (single-game sports betting states minimum) | Some states disqualify you; international domains face scrutiny |

#### Promotional Allowances
- **Affiliate links:** Permitted on sports content/blog/prediction pages
- **Email campaigns:** Permitted to opted-in subscribers only
- **Paid search:** Restricted—must register keywords; branded terms require approval
- **Branded domain:** Cannot use "DraftKings" in domain; `[site]-draftkings-affiliate` is forbidden
- **Cookie duration:** 30–90 days (verify current)

#### Content Requirements
- **Editorial independence:** Affiliate link must not appear in "picks" or "predictions" content (hard rule for sports predictor affiliates)
- **Disclosure:** FTC-compliant affiliate disclosure required above the fold
- **Responsible gaming:** RG messaging + NCPG helpline must be displayed
- **No guaranteed picks:** Cannot claim affiliate link + pick = "sure profit"

#### Commission Structure
| Commission Type | Rate | Holds & Holdback |
|---|---|---|
| **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)** | $100–$200 per sign-up deposit (varies by state) | Held 90 days pending reverify + no chargeback |
| **Revenue Share** | 20–30% of net gaming revenue (NGR), state-dependent | Held 30–90 days |
| **Hybrid** | CPA for first deposit + 15–25% NGR thereafter | Rare; must negotiate |

**Payment:** Net-30 or Net-45 (state-dependent); minimum payout threshold typically $100

## 2. FanDuel Affiliate Program

### Portal & Documentation
- **Affiliate Network:** FanDuel Affiliate (direct program + Refersion subagent)
- **Dashboard:** `affiliate.fanduel.com`
- **Primary T&Cs:** `fanduel.com/affiliate-terms` (revised frequently; check for state-by-state addenda)
- **Support:** partners@fanduel.com

### Minimum Requirements & Approval Criteria

#### Traffic Thresholds
| Metric | Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum monthly traffic** | ~3,000–8,000 unique visitors/mo (estimated) | FanDuel is more permissive than DK; accepts smaller content sites |
| **Domain age** | 3–6 months minimum (some states less strict) | New domains face extended review (~4 weeks) |
| **Audience quality** | US-based; <5% traffic from restricted states | Geo-filtering compliance required before approval |

#### Promotional Allowances
- **Affiliate links:** Permitted; embedded in sports content is acceptable with proper disclosure
- **Email:** Permitted; FanDuel monitors click-through velocity for sign-up patterns
- **SMS:** Not permitted without explicit opt-in (state-by-state; IL/MA/NY more restrictive)
- **Paid search:** Restricted; must request approval for Google Ads / branded keywords
- **Coupon/bonus stacking:** Prohibited; cannot link to FD promotions not authorized by FD

#### Content Requirements
- **Editorial separation:** Best practice—separate affiliate promo content from picks/predictions
- **Disclosure:** Full FTC disclosure required; placement in header or immediately above link preferred
- **State compliance:** Must suppress affiliate content in non-licensed states (IL, MA, NY, VT, others)
- **Responsible gaming:** RG messaging + 1-800-GAMBLER on affiliate landing pages

#### Commission Structure
| Commission Type | Rate | Holds & Holdback |
|---|---|---|
| **CPA** | $100–$150 per sign-up with deposit | Held 45–60 days for chargeback reversal |
| **Revenue Share** | 25–35% of NGR (tiered by volume) | Held 30 days; $500+ monthly minimum to qualify |
| **Hybrid** | CPA for first 90 days, then NGR | Negotiable for proven publishers |

**Payment:** Net-45; minimum payout $50; 1099 required (US affiliate status)

## 3. BetMGM Affiliate Program

### Portal & Documentation
- **Affiliate Network:** BetMGM Affiliate (via Impact or direct)
- **Dashboard:** `affiliates.betmgm.com` or `mgmresorts-affiliates.impact.com`
- **Primary T&Cs:** `betmgm.com/en/affiliate-terms`
- **Support:** affiliatemanager@betmgm.com

### Minimum Requirements & Approval Criteria

#### Traffic Thresholds
| Metric | Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum monthly traffic** | ~2,000–5,000 unique visitors/mo | BetMGM is most accessible; newer programs attract smaller publishers |
| **Domain age** | 3 months minimum | Newer domains approved if content quality is high |
| **Content volume** | ≥20 pieces published (estimated from acceptance patterns) | Blog-based sites have higher acceptance rate |

#### Promotional Allowances
- **Affiliate links:** Permitted in sports/betting content
- **Email:** Permitted; monitoring for list-scraping compliance
- **Paid media:** Google Ads restricted; must request keyword list approval
- **Social media:** Permitted; TikTok/Instagram less monitored than Facebook
- **Comparison tools:** Permitted (sportsbook comparison grids with BetMGM affiliate link)

#### Content Requirements
- **Transparency:** FTC disclosure required; "As an affiliate" language acceptable
- **No misleading claims:** Cannot claim picks are "verified" or "guaranteed"
- **Responsible gaming:** RG helpline must appear on landing page or terms
- **No affiliate-hiding:** Cannot mask affiliate relationship in URL shorteners or cloaked links

#### Commission Structure
| Commission Type | Rate | Holds & Holdback |
|---|---|---|
| **CPA** | $50–$150 per sign-up deposit (state & promo tier-dependent) | Held 30 days |
| **Revenue Share** | 20–30% of NGR | Requires $500+/month ongoing revenue |
| **Sliding scale** | % increases with monthly volume (e.g., 22% at $5K, 25% at $10K) | Tiered; BetMGM most transparent on tiers |

**Payment:** Net-30; minimum payout $25; fastest payout cycle of the three

## 5. Approval Process & Timeline

### Standard Application Flow

| Step | Typical Timeline | Criteria Checked |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Initial application** | 1–2 hours (submission time) | Legal entity, domain ownership, traffic proof |
| **2. Traffic verification** | 2–7 days | Google Analytics export or similar; audience geo-check |
| **3. Content review** | 3–10 days | Manual review of site for brand safety, responsible gaming, no gambling guarantees |
| **4. Approval/rejection/conditional** | ~10–14 days total | Final decision + vendor contract delivery |
| **5. Account setup & link generation** | 1–3 days | Dashboard access, tracking code, first affiliate link available |

**Expedited paths:**
- **Premium publisher fast-track** (DraftKings/FanDuel): Proven sports media / brand sites can compress to 3–5 days
- **State-limited pilot:** Some programs allow limited-state launch while national approval is pending

## 6. Renewal & Ongoing Compliance Requirements

### Performance Gates

| Metric | Threshold | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum monthly performance** | $0 (no minimum, but inactivity triggers review) | Accounts inactive 90+ days reviewed for termination |
| **Sign-up quality** | <5% chargeback rate on new players | Elevated chargebacks trigger account review or suspension |
| **Traffic attestation** | Annual Google Analytics export | Failure to submit → account suspended pending review |
| **Responsible gaming compliance** | Ongoing traffic audit for RG messaging | Non-compliant pages → link removal until fixed |

### Annual / Semi-Annual Reviews

All programs conduct **6–12 month compliance audits**:
- **Traffic audit:** Verify claimed audience still present
- **Content audit:** Confirm no banned phrases, proper disclosures
- **Chargeback/fraud review:** Flag high-chargeback affiliates
- **State compliance:** Confirm geo-fencing on restricted-state traffic
- **Commission reconciliation:** Full revenue ledger audit

**Failure outcomes:** Suspension (30 days, then re-audit), or termination with 30-day wind-down.

## 7. Specific Restrictions & Disqualifications

### Automatic Rejection Criteria (All Programs)

| Trigger | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Domain contains gambling keywords** (`casino-affiliate.com`) | Evades disclosure requirements |
| **Underage audience evident** (college student site, TikTok-first) | Violates AGA/NCPG standards |
| **High-risk traffic source** (adult, piracy, malware-associated) | Brand safety + legal liability |
| **No SSL certificate / outdated security** | Compliance gate |
| **Existing sportsbook license** | Conflict of interest (you can't be both operator and affiliate) |
| **Crypto/USDT-only monetization** | Regulatory mismatch; not recognized by programs |
| **Published predictions without disclaimer** | FTC violation; brand liability |

### State-Level Disqualifications (DraftKings Example)

| Restriction | State(s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **No affiliate marketing permitted** | Utah, Hawaii, Idaho (and others without legal sports betting) | Automatic disqualification for traffic from these states |
| **Licensed operator only** | Nevada, California (tribal), Oregon | Independent affiliates face extra scrutiny; must include NV disclaimer |
| **Geo-fence required** | Illinois (Chicago), Massachusetts (Boston), New York | Affiliate must suppress content in these metros |
| **Approval requires state license** | Tennessee, Louisiana | Applicant must hold their own gambling advertising license |

## 8. Key Operational Notes for GSN

### Compliance Posture (from `COMPLIANCE_AND_RESPONSIBLE_GAMING.md`)

**GSN's position:**
- ✅ Editorial independence: Affiliate links segregated from picks content
- ✅ FTC disclosure: `affiliateDisclosureIncluded` flag in `ContentDraft` model
- ✅ Responsible gaming: NCPG helpline + RG messaging on all surfaces
- ✅ State gating: Geo-aware rendering of promo content

**Gaps requiring action before affiliate launch:**
1. **Formal affiliate disclosure SOP** — document where/when affiliate links appear relative to picks
2. **State-by-state legal review** — confirm which states allow affiliate marketing of picks/predictions
3. **Vendor agreement review** — ensure `Promotion` record captures all contractual gates (minimum traffic, renewal audit, etc.)
4. **Domain age requirement** — GSN must be live ≥3–6 months before most affiliates approve
5. **Traffic proof submission workflow** — export GA data for approval cycles

### Recommended Affiliate Application Strategy

**Tier 1 (easiest approval):**
- BetMGM (most accessible; ~2,000 UV/mo; 30-day payout)
- Drafters (smallest audience required; DFS-focused)

**Tier 2 (moderate approval):**
- FanDuel (mid-tier gate; ~3,000–5,000 UV/mo; 45-day payout)

**Tier 3 (hardest approval):**
- DraftKings (highest bar; ~5,000–10,000 UV/mo; 90-day hold on earnings)

**Recommended timing:**
- **Q1/Q2:** Launch site, build traffic to 3K+/month organic
- **Q3:** Apply to BetMGM + Drafters (faster approval paths)
- **Q4:** Once BetMGM approved + 60+ days live, apply to FanDuel
- **Q1+1:** Once FanDuel proven (90+ days, no red flags), apply to DraftKings

### Risk Mitigations

1. **Start with revenue-share (not CPA).** Lower immediate cashout friction; affiliates scrutinize CPA affiliates more heavily for fraud.
2. **Segregate affiliate content.** Keep affiliate promo pages on separate URL path (`/promotions/*`) separate from `/picks` or `/prediction/*`.
3. **Maintain RG messaging parity.** Every affiliate landing page must have the same RG messaging as core product.
4. **Document state compliance.** Maintain a state-by-state matrix of where affiliate content is suppressed + where it's live.
5. **Pre-audit for banned phrases.** Run `trust-claims.ts` / banned-phrase scanner on all affiliate landing pages before submission.
