## 1. Thesis verdict: "Week 1 teams revert to identity"

**Overall: MIXED — the run-identity half is supported, the rotation half is refuted.**

| Sub-claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Run teams run more in Week 1 | SUPPORTED | 2024 Week 1: only 2 QBs topped 300 yds (vs 6 in 2023 W1); 8 QBs hit 250+ (vs 13 in 2023). Running was more efficient than passing. Two-high shells forcing teams to run. (Dawgs By Nature, via SB Nation's JP Acosta) |
| Run-heavy teams lean run early in games | SUPPORTED | PFF 2021-25 opening-play data: most run-heavy teams on 1st play — Chiefs 73.0%, Patriots 66.7%, Giants 62.5%, Lions 60.4%, Falcons 60.0%. "Offenses are noticeably more likely to run the ball to begin games." |
| Pass teams stay pass-heavy | SUPPORTED (by stability) | 2025 neutral pass rates are structural: ARI 65.3%, KC 63.1%, CIN 61.9%, LAC 61.2% vs BAL 50.1%, NYJ 49.9%. These don't flip week to week. |
| "Use two running backs when they run" | REFUTED for Week 1 | Week 1 actually CONCENTRATES backfields: 2025 W1 — Chase Brown 91.3% of team rush attempts, Tony Pollard 85.7%, Kyren Williams 91% snap share (PFF). Coaches trust veterans/lead backs in openers; committees settle later. |
| "Spread it around when passing" | UNVERIFIED | No Week-1-specific target-concentration data found in free sources. Treat as unproven. |
| "Heavy-TE teams lean on TEs" | PLAUSIBLE, unverified | No Week-1 TE-target-share study found free. Directionally consistent with run-identity logic (play-action + TE usage correlate), but mark as hypothesis. |

**Week 1 usage is predictive (Fantasy Footballers):** RBs with 10+ opportunities in Week 1 average 100+ season points; single-digit Week 1 opportunities is a huge red flag. TEs need 6+ targets to hit the relevance breakeven. Implication: Week 1 touch leaders are NOT noise — they signal real roles.

**Identity caveat — new schemes on this slate:** BAL (Jesse Minter era begins), ATL (Kevin Stefanski era), LAC (Mike McDaniel now OC). "Revert to identity" is weakest for these three — their 2025 pace numbers describe the OLD regime.
