## FLAGGED: injury / split / depth-chart news (all 2026, date-verified)

1. **Josh Jacobs is OUT — Commissioner's Exempt List.** PackersWire (2026-09-08): "Without Josh Jacobs available, the Green Bay Packers are planning to use a committee at running back to start the 2026 season... for a little while." MarShawn Lloyd presumed starter; Chris Brooks (passing downs), Kaleb Johnson (via PIT trade), Pierre Strong Jr. in the mix. (Jacobs also had a groin injury in camp and a May domestic-abuse arrest.) **He must not be rostered.** Lloyd is the cheap committee play (DK touches table: 12.2; RotoBaller RB31).
2. **David Montgomery is on the HOUSTON Texans, not Detroit.** RotoBaller (Sept 10): "With RB David Montgomery being traded to the Houston Texans, Gibbs now owns this backfield virtually to himself." DK touch table lists Montgomery HOU vs BUF (11.4 proj touches, 9.6 car) — *fewer* than Woody Marks (14.9), which the DK writer disputes. Big positive for Gibbs' workload case; downgrades the HOU backfield to a committee.
3. **Backfield injuries affecting Week 1 touches:** Jeremiyah Love (ARI) — ankle sprain, questionable, and listed *behind* Tyler Allgeier on the depth chart (Allgeier 16.2 vs Love 17.5 proj touches; Love's edge is 6.7 projected targets); Ashton Jeanty (LV) — ankle sprain, questionable; Alvin Kamara (NO) — sprained MCL, (Q), backing up Etienne (Kamara 11.0 proj touches at 75% volume); Jonathon Brooks (CAR) — groin injury, limited, speculative; Kyle Monangai (CHI) — hyperextended knee Aug 16, practiced Monday but may be snap-counted or inactive; D'Andre Swift (abdomen) is fine.
4. **Depth-chart/movement notes:** Rachaad White moved to Washington (new pass-catching threat in WAS backfield; leaves Irving's TB receiving role uncontested); Travis Etienne signed with New Orleans (4yr/$47.4M) and is the Saints' lead back; David Montgomery's exit makes Gibbs the only Tier-1 Detroit back; Chargers OC Mike McDaniel may mix in Kimani Vidal and Keaton Mitchell behind Hampton; Jaguars have an "unproven duo" at RB with Trevor Lawrence expected to keep goal-line work (DK: Lawrence had 9 rush TDs last year).
