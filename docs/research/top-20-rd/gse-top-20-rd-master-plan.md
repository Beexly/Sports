## Executive Direction

The highest-leverage path is not to become another sports website, picks page, fantasy blog or DFS optimizer. The path is to become an NFL decision operating system: source-provenanced, scenario-aware, personalized, fast, visually excellent and honest about uncertainty.

The core product loop should be:

1. Ingest and govern source-backed football state.
2. Detect what changed.
3. Explain why it matters.
4. Let the user simulate or personalize the impact.
5. Convert the result into a saved watchlist, optimizer action, scenario, briefing or autopsy.
6. Learn from outcomes and source quality.

## Build Logic

- Do source provenance, trust UX, entitlement projection and website quality before public feature expansion.
- Build What Changed before personalized notifications.
- Build scenario contracts before optimizer scenario integration.
- Build player cards before best-ball and advanced fantasy personalization.
- Build autopsy and calibration before public performance claims.
- Keep founder-only formulas and source-risk controls out of public routes.

## Non-Negotiable Guardrails

- No scraping private contest rooms, sportsbook lines, pick'em projections, paid rankings or paid articles.
- No raw provider redistribution unless the contract permits it.
- No guaranteed-win, sure-thing, Kelly, staking or medical diagnosis language.
- No public shadow-factor leaks.
- No LLM-generated numbers or claims without source-backed input.
- No migration or schema change without an ADR and explicit approval.
