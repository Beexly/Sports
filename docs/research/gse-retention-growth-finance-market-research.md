## Retention Strategy

- Weekly NFL rhythm: Monday autopsy, Tuesday waiver/context, Wednesday injury/weather watch, Thursday slate primer, Sunday live scenario, Monday settlement.
- Personal watchlists: teams, players, injuries, weather, market movement, fantasy formats.
- Source freshness badges: users return because information changes and the product shows exactly what changed.
- Scenario saves: let users save "what if X sits" or "wind jumps to 20 mph" views.
- Explain deltas: every major model change should answer "what changed since yesterday?"
- Community without chaos: expert rooms, moderated challenge boards, and claim cards, not open rumor feeds by default.

## Engagement Loops

- Free: daily public context capsule, quick optimizer, limited watchlist, weekly recap.
- PRO: more scenario toggles, player cards, weather/injury detail, saved lineups, notifications.
- ELITE: full scenario lab, portfolio/contest simulator, source history, late-swap alerts, analog ratings.
- Founder-only: source war room, formula/weights, provider budgets, competitor watch, alpha notebook.

## Sales and Pricing Notes

- Avoid competing with sportsbooks on deposit bonuses.
- Use trust, explainability, source quality, and repeat workflow as sales language.
- Lead magnet: free NFL quick optimizer plus source-freshness cards.
- Paid conversion: scenario lab, late-swap, saved watchlists, export, backtests and elite analog cards.
- Enterprise/B2B option: source-provenanced projections/API, content widgets, or white-label tools.

## Funding and Finance Watchpoints

- Track CAC, activation rate, free-to-paid conversion, monthly churn, gross margin after data costs, support load, refund/chargeback risk, and source/provider cost per active user.
- Provider contracts can become the largest marginal cost after engineering.
- Subscription products need retention before paid acquisition.
- Grants are unlikely for gambling/fantasy consumer promotion, but SBIR/STTR-style funding could fit explainable AI/data infrastructure if scoped carefully.
- Angel/VC story should be "trusted sports decision infrastructure" rather than "another pick seller."

## Brand Voice

GSE should sound sharp, evidence-led, calm, and source-aware. Avoid guaranteed-win language, hype-only "AI picks", gambling bravado, medical speculation, or hidden black-box claims. The strongest voice is: "Here is what changed, why it matters, how confident we are, and what source could prove us wrong."

## Promotion Ideas

- Weekly "what changed" NFL world-state card.
- Public weather/injury watch with source badges.
- Free "optimizer autopsy" after slates showing why projection-only lineups failed.
- Founder letter / research notebook for serious users.
- Partner with high-stakes fantasy creators for format-specific strategy, not generic tout content.
- Creator affiliate program only after compliance/disclosure policy exists.

## Forecasting Inputs To Track

- Audience: email list, Discord/community, returning weekly active users, free optimizer users.
- Conversion: free account creation, first saved watchlist, first imported CSV, first scenario save, first paid upgrade.
- Retention: D1/D7/D30, weekly active during NFL season, offseason best-ball engagement, churn by source freshness.
- Revenue: ARPU, ARPPU, plan mix, annual prepay rate, refund rate, data cost per subscriber.
- Marketing: CAC by channel, creator CPA, content SEO conversion, newsletter conversion, paid ad payback.
