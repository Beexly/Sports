**Estimand (all compounds):** the game-level home-field probability *p* that the home side wins,
evaluated against the closing line's own devigged probability `qClose`, i.e. we ask whether a
compound identifies games where the *realized* home-win rate departs from `qClose` in a
pre-registered direction, and whether a logistic model containing the compound beats a
`logit(qClose)`-only baseline out of sample.

**Baseline:** `qClose` = devigged (proportional de-margin) home moneyline; where a moneyline is
absent, `qClose` from `spread_line` with σ=13.5 is the fallback (not expected to be needed for 2015+).
**Primary metric:** out-of-sample Δlog-loss (nats/game) via expanding-window temporal CV
(train seasons ≤ k−1, test season k, k = 2018…2025) + flagged-subset calibration slope.
**Secondary metric:** flagged-subset flat ROI at the closing moneyline (no line shopping).
**Significance:** 2,000-draw bootstrap (resample *games*, refit) 90% CI for the compound coefficient.

**Universal kill line (pre-registered):** a compound is KILLED if any of
(1) coefficient sign opposite the pre-registered direction,
(2) out-of-sample Δlog-loss < 0.0015 nats/game,
(3) 90% bootstrap CI for the coefficient contains 0 in the pre-registered direction,
(4) flagged n < 150 games,
(5) the effect is present only in the middle tertile of its continuous driver (boundary fail).
