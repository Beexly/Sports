# Shared Risk Register

## Excluded from operational use
- 12ft.io: paywall bypass. Do not use in research or production workflows.
- LibGen / Sci-Hub / PDFDrive: copyright-sensitive shadow-library risk. Use lawful open sources instead: publisher pages, public domain sources, Open Culture, Project Gutenberg, archive pages where lawful.
- SMS24 / temporary SMS services: do not use for account creation, verification, evasion, or platform abuse.
- FakeDetail: do not use for fake identities, fake customers, fake reviewers, or platform evasion.
- Scraping/bypass/security gists: do not operationalize. Use only as threat model inputs.

## Quarantine / research-only
- OSINT directories, username checkers, email lookup tools, breach tools: use only for owned brand protection, impersonation checks, source validation, and fraud-awareness. Do not use to harass, stalk, dox, or collect private data.
- AI image/video tools: use only with owned/authorized assets and explicit provenance.
- Voice cloning: use only with explicit informed consent from the voice owner, clear AI disclosure, and stored voice-asset controls.

## GSN-specific risks
- Unlicensed or stale odds/data.
- Publishing rumors.
- Treating public/sharp splits as predictive certainty.
- Guaranteed-outcome language.
- Auto-published picks.
- No-Bet treated as failure rather than quality control.
- AI voice implying Garrett personally spoke live without approving the script.

## Lumera-specific risks
- Fake product proof.
- Fake scarcity.
- Stolen product images.
- Watermark removal without rights.
- Unverified product claims.
- Hiding shipping/returns until late checkout.
- Uploading customer/supplier data into random tools.
- AI-generated product copy without human review.
