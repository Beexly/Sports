# Lumera Product Page System

## Purpose
The product page must close the confidence gap. It should answer: What is this, why does it matter, can I trust it, what happens after I buy, and what is uncertain?

## Above the fold
- Product name
- Price
- Primary image/gallery
- Variant selector
- Availability truth, not fake scarcity
- Shipping estimate or processing range
- Returns clarity link
- Add to cart / buy button
- Truth Receipt badge

## Mid-page
- Product story
- Key facts
- Comparison/fit/context block
- Image details
- Supplier/provenance note if useful
- FAQ

## Bottom-page
- Related products
- Recently viewed
- Watchlist CTA
- Support/returns reminder

## Mobile rules
- Sticky CTA.
- No giant hero that hides product decision data.
- Thumb-safe variant controls.
- Receipt available but not intrusive.
- Collapse long text into scannable sections.

## Acceptance criteria
- Shipping/returns visible before cart.
- No unsupported claim is shown.
- LCP image optimized.
- Layout reserves image space to avoid shift.
- Product card and PDP both expose truth state.
