# GSE Product Signal Map

## Product Surfaces

| Surface | What It Can Show | What It Must Hide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FREE | Schedule, public-safe weather, source freshness, high-level matchup context, limited confidence labels | Formulas, weights, risky source names, market detail, injury speculation |
| PRO | Richer matchup context, rest/travel, weather sensitivity, stable historical trends, attribution-backed explainers | Founder formulas, raw provider data, high-risk claims |
| ELITE | Scenario toggles, injury/availability impact, market-aware context, player-role and analog cards | Private source-risk details, proprietary weights, contract-sensitive feed details |
| Founder-only | Source war room, formulas, weight registry, blocked-source ledger, alpha notebook, provider budget, weird experiments | Nothing public by default |
| Admin/operator | Source health, freshness, ingestion runs, blocked feeds, manual review queues | Consumer-facing claims until approved |

## Signal-to-Product Rule

Every signal should carry tier, source, confidence, freshness, and legal status. Product rendering should be a projection of those properties, not a duplicate hand-written permission system.
