## Patterns To Steal Without Copying

- Free content creates trust before tools sell.
- League sync, saved preferences, alerts and watchlists turn generic analysis into personal workflow.
- Expert personalities sell judgment, but GSE can sell repeatable evidence with a clear operator voice.
- Betting products use immediacy, odds movement and alerts; GSE needs licensed feeds and compliance boundaries before touching that surface.
- Paid tools rarely win on one feature. They win by bundling rankings, projections, optimizer, alerts, Discord/community, app UX and cadence.

## GSE Position

GSE should avoid becoming a generic tout, affiliate odds site or rankings clone. The defensible position is: a source-provenanced NFL decision OS with enough free context to build habit, enough Pro tooling to save time, and enough Elite/world-state depth to justify serious subscription spend.
