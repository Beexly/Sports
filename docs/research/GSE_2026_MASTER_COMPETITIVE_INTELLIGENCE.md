**Purpose:** Authoritative competitive landscape document for Galaxy Sports Edge (GSE) product and strategy decisions.
**Scope:** 50 competitors across fantasy football, DFS, sports prediction, and betting analytics.
**Date:** June 2026
**Methodology:** Based on publicly available product pages, marketing copy, pricing pages, feature documentation, and app store listings known as of training cutoff. All specifics labeled by confidence level. Revenue/user figures not fabricated — labeled as "source gap" where unknown.

---

## How to Read This Document

- **Confidence: HIGH** = product features are well-documented and stable; author has high confidence in accuracy
- **Confidence: MEDIUM** = features are known but pricing/details may have shifted; verify before citing
- **Confidence: LOW** = product exists and category is known but specific feature claims are inferred or sparse

## 1. FantasyPros

**URL/Source:** fantasypros.com
**Category:** Draft assistant / fantasy hub / consensus rankings aggregator
**Core Promise:** The single authoritative source for expert consensus rankings — takes the cognitive overhead of evaluating 100+ experts off the player's plate.

**Key Features:**
- Expert Consensus Rankings (ECR) — aggregated from 100+ experts weighted by accuracy score
- Cheat Sheet builder with custom scoring support
- Draft Wizard (live draft assistant) syncs to Yahoo, ESPN, CBS, Sleeper, NFL.com, and others
- Mock Draft Lobby with real opponents (the largest mock lobby in the industry — source gap on exact volume)
- Draft Analyzer / grade after draft completes
- Perfect Draft simulator (find the statistically optimal draft)
- My Playbook — personalized weekly waiver/start-sit recommendations
- Salary Cap tool for auction leagues
- Trade Analyzer
- Injury and news feed with expert commentary
- Dynasty rankings, rookie rankings, IDP support

**Draft Features:** Draft Wizard overlays on hosted platforms, real-time ADP, tier-based drafting, projected availability, post-draft grade, queue management, auction support
**DFS Features:** Limited — not a DFS-first product; some DFS projections exist
**Prediction/Betting Features:** Minimal; some picks content exists but not a core feature
**Monetization:** Freemium. Free tier has full ECR access. FantasyPros Premium ~$7–$9/month (source gap — verify current pricing). Seasonal bundles common. Draft Kit packages for ~$30–$50/season (source gap).
**Strongest Feature:** Expert Consensus Rankings — the aggregation model creates a defensible moat because it requires sustained relationships with 100+ analysts
**Weakest/Missing:** Prediction/betting layer is shallow; no real-time alerts; content is aggregated not original; weak dynasty depth vs. specialists
**Mechanic GSE Learns:** ECR model shows users will trust aggregated expert consensus over single-source picks — social proof at scale reduces anxiety
**How GSE Exceeds It:** GSE's confidence scores are model-generated and calibrated against real outcomes; FantasyPros aggregates human experts who are not held to the same accuracy accountability. GSE can show verified hit rate; FantasyPros cannot easily do this at the individual expert level.
**Confidence:** HIGH

## RARE / FIRST-OF-KIND — GSE WHITE SPACE
*Where no competitor has built deeply; genuine differentiation opportunities:*

1. **Verified, published accuracy calibration** — showing predicted confidence vs. actual win rate on a public calibration curve; no competitor in the consumer space does this rigorously
2. **Cross-domain intelligence** — unified confidence engine that spans fantasy starts, DFS selections, and sports bets in one product; competitors are siloed by category
3. **Pick-to-action notification** — alerts that go from "news event" to "specific pick impact" to "recommended action" in one push notification
4. **Portfolio correlation view across bets AND fantasy** — no tool shows how your weekly DFS lineups correlate with your betting positions
5. **GM Ledger / season-long decision history** — a searchable, filterable personal history of every pick, grade, and outcome across seasons
6. **Confidence interval communication** — showing floor, median, and ceiling as a distribution rather than a point estimate with uncertainty ranges
7. **Tier-gated pick access with transparent reasoning** — showing paying users WHY a pick earned its confidence score (factor trail), not just the score
8. **Founding member guarantee with grandfathered pricing** — a commitment mechanism that no competitor offers; converts early adopters with long-term value
9. **Calibration curve as a public proof artifact** — marketing the accuracy calibration publicly as social proof; no competitor uses this as a primary marketing mechanism
10. **Regression signal as a consumer-facing pick factor** — translating advanced concepts (regression to mean, luck-adjusted metrics) into plain-English pick explanations
11. **Context-aware in-season decision loop** — weekly cadence that matches how users think about decisions (Thursday injury report → lineup decisions → game-day alerts → result tracking)
12. **Real-time pick adjustment after injury news** — when a player is ruled out, immediately surface the pick implication and the replacement recommendation
13. **Multi-season calibration comparison** — show users how model accuracy trends over seasons (improving, stable, degrading) to build long-run trust
14. **Pick rationale in plain English** — every pick accompanied by 2-3 sentences explaining the key factors, not just a confidence number
15. **Defined upgrade moments with proof gates** — the pricing ladder is public and the milestones to reach next tier are verifiable; no competitor makes their pricing evolution explicit and trustworthy
