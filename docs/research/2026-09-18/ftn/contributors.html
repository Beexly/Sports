Meet our amazing team

Jeff Ratcliffe

Jeff Ratcliffe is the co-CEO of FTN and one of the most accomplished analysts in the fantasy sports industry. He hosts The Jeff Ratcliffe Show weekdays on SiriusXM Fantasy Sports Radio and The Rant podcast.

A nine-time FSWA award winner, Ratcliffe previously spent a decade at Pro Football Focus, where he served as Director of Fantasy, and nine years as an analyst on CBS Sports Network. His career has also included work with NBC Sports Rotoworld and contributions to ESPN. He has won six FSWA awards for Best Radio Show, along with honors for Best Article, Best Short Form Video and Best Publication, as well as an FSGA award for Best Radio Show.

Before entering the fantasy sports industry, Ratcliffe was a college professor. He holds a PhD in cultural anthropology from Temple University.
Aaron Schatz

Aaron Schatz has been doing NFL analytics for over 20 years and is the creator of advanced stats including DVOA, DYAR, and Adjusted Line Yards. He also writes for ESPN+ and ESPN Bet and hosts the FTN Fantasy Schatz and Tanier NFL Podcast. He is also one of the 50 members of the Associated Press NFL voting panel for regular-season awards and the All-Pro team.
Tyler Orginski

Tyler has been playing fantasy football for over 20 years and been creating FF content for seven. When not fighting for his life in the spreadsheets, he enjoys spending time with his family and playing video games. Tyler won the Fantasy Pros 2024 Accuracy Contest. The secret? Many are saying he’s never watched a game.
Vlad Sedler

Vlad is the head of fantasy baseball content at FTN Fantasy. He also writes for FanGraphs, is a Tout Wars Expert League champ, member of the CDM Fantasy Sports Hall of Fame and has been nominated for FSWA writing awards six times. Vlad has been playing fantasy baseball since 1995, winning 42 NFBC leagues since 2012 and ranking in the top percentile in NFBC’s Online Championship contest (33% win rate, 52% cash rate; 64 leagues). Much to the chagrin of his colleagues and most baseball aficionados, Vlad is a lifelong Dodgers fan who claims his first gut call at age 9 was Kirk Gibson’s 1988 World Series home run. Some, but not all, call him the FAAB Whisperer.
C.H. Herms

Herms is a fantasy football analyst and writer at FTN. He finished as the No. 1 ECR D/ST draft ranker for the 2023 season and provides analysis, rankings and strategy to help fantasy managers make smarter decisions throughout the year. Follow Herms on X at @CH_Herms and on Bluesky at @herms.bsky.social.
Tyler Loechner

Tyler is a 20-year fantasy football vet specializing in redraft, DFS, best ball, and dynasty. He has earned multiple FSWA nominations and won the Best Football Series award in 2017. Tyler previously wrote for PFF Fantasy, RotoViz, and Bleacher Report.
Bryan Knowles

Bryan has been writing about the NFL for nearly 20 years. He has previously written for Football Outsiders, PlaySheet, Bleacher Report, Fansided and more. The resident expert on all things Dayton Triangle. You can follow him on Twitter at @BryKno
Frank Brank

Frank Brank is a former college baseball pitcher and professional sports bettor who brings a data-driven, market-focused approach to sports analysis. With years of experience building predictive betting models and consistently beating the market across all major sports, Frank specializes in identifying value, interpreting line movement, and turning advanced analytics into actionable betting insights. His background as an athlete gives him a unique understanding of game flow, player performance, and competitive dynamics, while his success as a professional gambler allows him to break down betting markets with a sharp, numbers-first perspective.
Matt Okada

Matt is nearing a decade of experience in the fantasy football content space, with bylines across the industry and several seasons working for NFL Media in Los Angeles. He’s most known for presenting hard-hitting, analytical, sometimes fiery takes in a fun and entertaining package. While he participates in a few long-running redraft leagues (and the Scott Fish Bowl), Matt’s true passion is dynasty (with a healthy garnish of best ball). You can find him stuck on an LA freeway, cooking up a fancy data analyzer in Excel, or @MattOkada on socials.
Colin McTamany

Colin has been playing fantasy football since 2007 and creating fantasy football content since 2021. He plays in all types of formats, but especially loves any involving an auction/salary cap draft. Colin loves the year-round investment it takes to win a fantasy championship including evaluating rookies, offseason mock draft analysis and working the waiver wire. Colin is a die-hard Philadelphia sports fan who loves traveling to see the Eagles.
Laquan Jones

Laquan started working for the NFL in Los Angeles, California, in May 2022. As part of the NFL Research Department, he worked on broadcasting programming shows such as GMFB NFL Total Access, NFL Now, NFL Insiders, NFL Fantasy Live, HBCU Legacy Bowl, Path to the Draft, and many more digital programming shows as an editorial researcher. Worked personally with such on-air talent as Chief National Reporter Steve Wyche, Cynthia Frelund, Andrew Siciliano, Scott Hanson, Brian Baldinger, and many more NFL Media talent.
Adam Strangis

Adam has been playing fantasy sports for well over a decade and writing across various sites for the past five years. He wears his sports on his sleeve with a Pittsburgh based tattoo that covers a good portion of his right arm and is a loving husband living with a great doggo named Meeko. When not writing about betting and DFS, he can be found watching true crime documentaries and with his nose in a book.
Zac Graham

Zac Graham (aka Kawhisenberg) joined FTN in July 2020 as an NBA DFS specialist and has been a prominent name on 20-max entry NBA GPP leaderboards since 2014. Zac’s DFS hoops success isn’t limited to NBA regular and postseason contests though, as he has been notably profitable in contest offerings for NBA Summer League, NBA Preseason, WNBA, TBT, FIBA, Euroleague and the Olympics. A 20-year fantasy football veteran, Zac also contributes NFL content at FTN and loves to boast about the 53-man roster dynasty league he created and has run for the last eight seasons.
MLB Dream

MLBDream is the head of betting content for FTN. He is FTN’s lead MLB handicapper, and also provides NFL content. He’s from the DC area and is a huge O’s fan. He’s been betting on baseball since 2012 and Oriole Park at Camden Yards is one of his favorite places on earth. Follow him on Twitter @MLBdream
Walter Waddell

Walter Waddell is a college football and college basketball analyst at FTN. Nobody knows niche sports like Walt, and you can find all of his content at ftnfantasy.com You can catch him on the college supershow every week!
Dan Fornek

A lifelong Chicago sports fan who provides offensive line analysis and general fantasy football advice for FTN. Dan utilizes his experiences as a coach and player to break down the game from a unique perspective.
