## Recurring graphic formats (templates we can mirror with public data)

1. **Pass Chart** — field-view pass-location map. Always: player / season+opponent / #+position / team; legend COMPLETE (green), TOUCHDOWN (blue), INCOMPLETE (white), INTERCEPTION (red), LOS (blue line), yard markers; stat boxes COMP/ATT, YARDS, TD-INT + one efficiency metric (CPOE or EPA/dropback).
2. **Carry Chart** — field-view carry-path map. Always: player / matchup / #+position / team; legend TACKLED FOR LOSS (red), 0-5 YDS GAINED (yellow), 5+ YDS GAINED/TD (green), TOUCHDOWN (blue target), LOS (blue line), FUMBLE LOST (red target); stat boxes CARRIES, YARDS, TD + one metric (MTF, RYOE, or SUCCESS RATE).
3. **Route Chart** — field-view route map. Always: player / matchup / #+position / team; legend INCOMPLETE ROUTE (gray), ROUTE (white), AFTER CATCH (green), TOUCHDOWN (blue target), LOS (blue line); stat boxes TARGETS-REC, YARDS, TD, RECYOE.
4. **Pass Rush Chart** — pass-rush path map. Always: player / matchup / #+position / team; legend NO PRESSURE (gray), PRESSURE (green), SACK (blue circle), LOS (blue line); stat boxes PASS RUSHES, PRESSURES (%), SACKS + GET OFF (seconds) or BLITZ RATE.
5. **Coverage comparison table** — one instance: "BY COVERAGE DEFENDER" (GONZALEZ vs ALL OTHERS): coverage matchups, receptions-targets, yards, TDs, man coverage %; footer takeaway line.
6. **AWS/NFL Draft infographics** — bar-chart rankings of NGS overall/athleticism draft scores; axis labeled < AVG / AVERAGE / GOOD / ELITE; plus the 32-team "Projected First-Round Big Boards" infographic (NFL IQ, powered by AWS).

- Situational splits carry the insight: blitz vs. not, under center, on/off field, man vs zone, by coverage defender.
- Video posts pair one tracking number (top speed, YAC over expected) with the clip.
- Quote-tweet commentary on @NFLPlus/@NFL_Researcher/@RapSheet posts extends reach without new graphics.
- pro.nfl.com and NFL Pro are the recurring product surfaces; NFL IQ (powered by AWS) is the draft-season surface.

## Published methodology details (2026-09-21 backend report — full text in ngs-methodology-backend-2026-09-21.md)

The backend report found actual public calculation descriptions for several metrics whose definitions were previously inferred-only above. These are NGS-published facts with sources — not our models:

- **Completion Probability (upgrades #14; CPOE #3):** XGBoost model hosted on Amazon SageMaker (amazon.science, Feb 2026). Trained on 36,000+ pass attempts back to 2016; validated on a random 10% holdout; actual completion % vs completion probability correlation r-squared = 0.98. Only ~6 of 10+ factors named publicly: air distance, target separation, sideline separation, pass rush separation (QB-to-nearest-rusher distance), passer speed, time to throw. CPOE (nflreadr data dictionary, verbatim): expected_completion_percentage = "Using a passer's Completion Probability on every play, determine what a passer's completion percentage is expected to be"; completion_percentage_above_expectation = "A passer's actual completion percentage compared to their Expected Completion Percentage". Hyperparameters, full feature list, and calibration are NOT published.
- **RYOE / xRY (upgrades #2):** expected rushing yards from a 2D convolutional neural network by Philipp Singer & Dmitry Gordeev ("The Zoo"), winners of the 2020 Big Data Bowl (2,000+ entrants, scored with CRPS). Inputs: only five vector features for all 22 players at the moment of handoff — X, Y, S (speed), A (acceleration), Dir (direction). Outputs a probability distribution over rushing outcomes; xRY = sum of (outcome × probability). NGS implemented the architecture in SageMaker. Also yields First Down Probability and Touchdown Probability at handoff. Companion defined terms (nfl.com, Jul 20 2020, verbatim): ROE = "the percentage of runs where a ball-carrier gained more yards than expected"; RYOE/Att per attempt.
- **YAC over expected (upgrades #11):** original 2018 model superseded by the same modeling structure as the xRY model. nflreadr definitions: avg_expected_yac = "Average expected yards after catch, based on numerous factors using tracking data such as how open the receiver is, how fast they're traveling, how many defenders/blockers are in space, etc".
- **Pressure (upgrades #5, #8, #9):** NGS-published 75% threshold — "A pass rush becomes a pressure when pressure probability exceeds 75 percent" (nfl.com, Sep 21, 2023). Three ML models: (1) GNN identifying blockers vs route-runners and rusher vs coverage defenders in real time; (2) random forest gauging each defender's pressure likelihood in tenths of a second, plus separate per-rusher models and a team composite; (3) blocking-matchup model (who blocked who). Derived terms now defined: pressure rate (avg rusher 10.3%); time to pressure = time from snap to first moment of pressure (avg 2.9s); quick pressures = within first 2.5s; pressure time = duration with PP over threshold; pressure probability at snap (from alignment/locations); average/peak pressure probability; pressure rate over expected = avg PP − PP at snap; positive/negative rushes; OL metrics (matchup frequency, pressures/sacks allowed, pressure rate allowed, double teams). This replaced the old rule (defender within 2 yards of QB at pass / 1.5 yards at any point). Whitepaper: "Feeling the Pressure" (Abdoo co-author, MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference, 25-page PDF).
- **Tackle probability (upgrades #1, MTF):** "estimates the likelihood of a defender completing a tackle at the moment of contact, factoring in speed, angle, distance, leverage, and pursuit" (amazon.science, Feb 2026) — the basis for identifying missed tackles and tackle yards saved. Pipeline (AWS blog, Sep 10, 2024, Abdoo/Band): >2M data points; frame-level probability every 10th of a second for all 11 defenders (~20 features/frame; ~20K datapoints per 10-second play); trained 2018–2022, tested 2023; 15 ML models tested on >1M datapoints in 4 months; production inference through "hundreds of thousands of data points every second." Derivative metrics: missed tackles by defender, tackle efficiency, missed tackles forced by ball carrier, group tackles, open-field attempts, tackled out of bounds, primary tackler, downhill/upfield/chase-down attempts, missed-tackle yards lost, tackle yards saved. Winning model type undisclosed; 20 features named by count only.
- **QB Passing Score:** TCN encoding per-player time series (176 columns = 22 players × 8 features: x/y position, x/y speed, x/y acceleration, direction, body orientation; variable rows snap→release) + static features → MLP → spliced binned-Pareto distribution output (binned base with Pareto tails beyond 5th/95th quantiles). Play score = rank of actual yards in predictive CDF. ~50K passing plays 2018–2020. AWS released PyTorch code + demo notebook (Aug 2022 article). Correlates better with win/playoff % than prior QB metrics.
- **Defensive Alerts:** acceleration patterns + presnap shifts + situational context (down, distance, game state), "applies generative AI to predict likely rushers." No architecture/equations published.
- **Defender ghosting (trajectory prediction):** CNN-LSTM (AWS ML Solutions Lab, Lin Lee Cheong); tracking accurate within 6 inches in 99.9% of cases; predicts next 10 positions from 3 input measurements. A component of stats under development, not a public stat.
- **Data pipeline (public facts):** RFID chips in shoulder pads + inside the football; 20+ ultrawideband receivers per stadium; players sampled 10x/second, ball 25x/second; "accurate to a few inches" (2026). AWS official technology provider since 2017. ~300M data points/season (2021). 75+ ML models on AWS, results in under a second; 500–1,000 stats produced per play. Optical pose tracking: 4K cameras, 16 angles → x,y,z of 29 body parts per player at 60 Hz; capture-to-analysis under 1 second; 2026 season is the first with full league-wide installation (data internal while validated).
- **Public data/code inventory:** Kaggle NFL Big Data Bowl datasets (2020 rushing theme won by The Zoo; 2022 kickoff returns; 2023 pressure; 2024 tackling — weeks 1–9 2022 tracking with schema; 2026: predict 22-player positions for 4 frames post-throw, Euclidean-distance eval); nflverse `load_nextgen_stats()` (player weekly NGS stats from 2016, full data dictionary at nflreadr.nflverse.com); github.com/SumerSports/SportsTrackingTransformer (transformer on raw tracking data predicting tackle location, ADE 4.61 vs 5.78 for the 2D-CNN baseline — directly relevant to NGS's 2026 run models); github.com/imathur1/nfl-big-data-bowl (expected kickoff return yards, 2D CNN); AWS-released SBP PyTorch code. API dead-ends: api.nfl.com → 401; nextgenstats.nfl.com → 401; pro.nfl.com requires NFL Pro auth (not pursued).
- **New product, not a metric:** "Ask NFL IQ" AI assistant inside NFL IQ (Mar–Apr 2026 posts) — instant data-driven answers on team needs, draft prospects, free agency, NGS Draft Scores.
