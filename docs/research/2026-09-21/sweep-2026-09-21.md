## New metrics / data cuts
1. **CORE (CollegeFootballData.com)** — "Points above FBS average per 100 plays", 25% preseason / 75% observed blend. Offense vs Defense scatter, 2026 through Week 3. Standouts: Mississippi State chart-topping offensive CORE (~+15, defense ~−5); Alabama/Notre Dame/Ohio State firmly "stronger on both sides" (defenses ~−13); LSU best defensive CORE (~−14, offense ~+2); USC/Ole Miss strong offense (~+13) with weak defense. CFB-side team-strength metric.
2. **Age-curve derivatives (Barlowe ANALYTICS)** — derivative of natural-cubic-spline fitted EPA age contributions, 94% pointwise intervals. Samples: RB 149,694 carries/533 RBs; WR 134,254 targets/807 WRs; QB 200,377 dropbacks/188 QBs (2013–2025 / 2016–2025 NFL play-by-play). Peak ages (zero of derivative): RB 24.53 yr, WR 25.33 yr, QB 26.67 yr. Author caveat: "not a total EPA difference or a causal aging effect."
3. **Pressure-to-sack rate (SumerSports, via @benlinsey_)** — "Share of pressured dropbacks that end in a sack." Bryce Young: 23.3% (30 of 30, 2023) → 15.8% (10 of 31, 2024) → 12.7% (8 of 29, 2025) → 10.5% (2026, unranked, 2 games/38 pressured dropbacks). League average ~18% flat. Ranks among QBs with 300+ dropbacks.
4. **NGS pass-rush chart (official)** — Lukas Van Ness vs NYJ Week 2: 30 rushes, 9 pressures (30.0%), 1.5 sacks, 0.70s get-off; five pressures under 2.5s (tied 3rd-most by a Packers defender since 2020). Green/gray arcs, blue sack markers, LOS line. Canonical public source for pressure % / get-off / quick-pressure splits.
