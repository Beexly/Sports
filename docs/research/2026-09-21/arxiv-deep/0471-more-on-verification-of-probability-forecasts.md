# [0471] More on verification of probability forecasts for football outcomes: score decompositions, reliability, and discrimination analyses (arXiv:2106.14345v2)

**Citation:** Jean-Louis Foulley (2021). *More on verification of probability forecasts for football outcomes: score decompositions, reliability, and discrimination analyses*. arXiv:2106.14345v2. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.14345v2
**Ledger completed:** 2026-09-21. **Read:** full text (PDF text extract, 1057 lines).
**Verdict:** ADAPT — adopt the full Murphy/Yates/LB Brier-decomposition + logistic-calibration verification suite as the standard GSE-vs-market diagnostic; the Poisson-ELO scoreline model itself duplicates Dixon-Coles already in the repo inventory.

## 1. Research question
How can the quality of football WDL (Home Win / Draw / Away Win) probability forecasts be diagnosed beyond mean Brier score? The paper reviews and applies Brier-score decompositions (Murphy calibration-refinement, likelihood-base, Yates) with reliability diagrams and discrimination analysis, illustrated on 4 seasons of UEFA Champions League (C1) group-stage matches forecasted by a Poisson-ELO model vs bookmaker odds — pedagogical verification toolbox for probability forecasts.

## 2. Dataset / schema
UEFA Champions League group-stage matches, seasons 2017–2020, N=384 matches (377 unique probability profiles). Model training: ex-ante — e.g., 2020 group stage forecasted from 2017–2019 scorelines, etc. Covariates: team ELO ratings (standardized r_i = (ELO_i − 1800)/150), taken as close as possible before kickoff. Benchmark: bookmaker 3-way odds implied probabilities from OddsPortal (average of 10–12 well-known bookmakers), de-vigged as p_{m,j} = o_{m,j}⁻¹ / Σ_k o_{m,k}⁻¹. Bayesian fitting via Win/OpenBUGS, noninformative priors η0=0, σ_η²=10⁴; h0=0, σ_h²=10⁴; bk=0, σ_β²=10³. No download URL stated.

## 3. Method / model
**Forecasting model (App. A):** Poisson loglinear on scorelines y_{ij,k}^(t) | λ ~ P(λ_{ij(t,k)}), log λ_{ij(t,1)} = η + h + β1∆r_{ij}(t′) + β2 r̄_{ij}(t′), log λ_{ij(t,2)} = η + β1∆r_{ji}(t′) + β2 r̄_{ij}(t′), where η intercept, h home effect, ∆r strength difference, r̄ mean strength. Posterior-mean predictive scoreline probabilities summed to WDL: Pr(home win) = Σ_{u,v<u} P(u,v|y), Pr(draw) = Σ_u P(u,u|y), away win = complement. Posterior means (Table A-1, 2014–2019 sample): intercept 0.117 (SD 0.034, 95% CI [0.049,0.183]); home effect 0.329 (0.043, [0.246,0.413]); team difference 0.303 (0.016, [0.272,0.335]); team average 0.045 (0.032, [−0.017,0.106], ns, deviance p=0.157). **Verification machinery:** (i) Murphy CR decomposition (eq. 1) with Siegert (2017) binning estimator REL = S(p) − S(x̂), RES = S(x1_N) − S(x̂), UNC = S(x1_N) (eq. 7); binning via fixed intervals, quantile intervals, isotonic regression via PAV; (ii) likelihood-base decomposition (eq. 2): REF = Var(P), DIS = Var_X[E_P(P|X)], CB2 = E_X[(E_P(P|X) − X)²]; (iii) Yates decomposition (eq. 4): BRS = UNC − 2COV + VPB + VPW + RIL, where VPB = VarX[E(P|X)] ("VarPmin", beneficial = DIS), VPW = within-class scattered variance (detrimental), RIL = marginal bias² ("reliability-in-large"); (iv) Brier skill score BSS = 1 − BS/BS_ref = (RES − REL)/UNC (eq. 3); (v) logistic calibration logit[Pr(X_i=1)] = α + β logit(p_i) with Wald/LR tests of α=0, β=1 (Cox 1958); patterns: (α>0,β=1) concave under-forecasting, (α<0,β=1) convex over-forecasting, (α=0,β>1) sigmoid, (α=0,β<1) inverse-sigmoid; (vi) discrimination diagnostics: Wilcoxon/KS tests and Harrell C-statistic (AUC) on forecast distributions conditional on X=0 vs X=1; Brier-score test for departure from perfect calibration (Spiegelhalter 1986).

## 4. Equations & assumptions
Murphy CR: E[S(P,X)] = Var(X) − E_P[(E_X(X|P) − E_X(X))²] + E_P[(E_X(X|P) − P)²] (1) = UNC − RES + REL. Likelihood-base: E[S(P,X)] = Var(P) − Var_X[E_P(P|X)] + E_X[(E_P(P|X) − X)²] (2). Yates: E[S(P,X)] = Var(X) − 2Cov(P,X) + Var_X[E_P(P|X)] + E_X[Var_P(P|X)] + [E(P) − E(X)]² (4). Brier skill: BSS = 1 − BS/BS_ref = (RES − REL)/UNC (3). Empirical CR: REL = S(p) − S(x̂), RES = S(x1_N) − S(x̂), UNC = S(x1_N) (7). Discrete-case estimators (eq. 6): REL = N⁻¹Σ_k n_k(x_k − p_k)², RES = N⁻¹Σ_k n_k(x_k − x̄)², UNC = x̄(1−x̄). Logistic calibration: logit[Pr(X_i=1)] = α + β logit(p_i). Ignorance score: L(P,X) = −X log(P) − (1−X) log(1−P), with 2N[L(p) − L(q̂_p)] asymptotically χ². Poisson model equations in §3 above; induced goal correlation: Var(y_{m,k}) = μ_k{1 + μ_k[exp(β²γ) − 1]}; Cov(y_{m,1},y_{m,2}) = μ1μ2[exp(−β²γ) − 1]; ρ12 = μ1μ2[exp(−β²γ) − 1] / sqrt({1+μ1[exp(β²γ)−1]}{1+μ2[exp(β²γ)−1]}), always negative for β≠0. Assumptions: properness of Brier/log scores; binning consistency for CR decomposition (Siegert estimator); for the model: conditional independence of home/away goals given rates, Δr_m iid N(0,γ), isotonicity of PAV recalibration function.

## 5. Features / target
Features: per-match standardized ELO difference and ELO mean (team-strength covariates), plus estimated intercept and home effect. Target: binary event indicators for each of Home Win / Draw / Away Win (three one-vs-all binary verifications); model predicts full scoreline then aggregates to WDL probabilities. Horizon: pre-match forecast of group-stage outcomes.

## 6. Validation design
Ex-ante rolling training: 3 prior seasons train → next season's 96 group-stage matches forecasted (e.g., 2017–2019 → 2020). No cross-validation; evaluation is prequential on the 384 matches. Baselines: climatological forecast (UNC as reference), bookmaker odds implied probabilities (ODD) as the strong benchmark. Metrics: mean (half-)Brier score, Murphy REL/RES under 3 binning schemes, Yates components, LB components, logistic-calibration α/β Wald tests + deviance LR test, Wilcoxon/KS discrimination tests, Harrell C, Brier-score calibration test p-values, reliability diagrams with 95% consistency bars.

## 9. Leakage & limitations
Adversarial notes: (a) only 384 matches (96/season × 4) — thin for reliability estimation; ISO/PAV binning on ~384 points with 377 unique profiles is fragile; (b) ELO ratings taken "as close as possible before play" — if ELO incorporates matches from the same season window being forecasted there is subtle lookahead; not audited; (c) Bayesian posterior-mean probabilities vs odds is not apples-to-apples: the POI model is a 4-parameter toy vs full bookmaker information sets (injuries, lineups) — the 5% skill gap is expected, not a model indictment; (d) the draw conclusion (1.4–3.0% skill) may be an artifact of group-stage UCL matches (asymmetric motivation: dead rubbers) rather than a general property of draws; (e) the logistic-calibration α/β tests ignore serial dependence across matches (teams appear repeatedly); (f) NFL transfer: NFL has no draws to speak of (ties ~0.5% of games); the directly transferable finding is the verification machinery itself, and the lesson that low-RES outcome categories (e.g., NFL pushes, or rare tail events like exact-score props) will show the same near-climatological signature.

## 10. GSE overlap
Extension on verification; duplicate on modeling. The existing map: Dixon-Coles, Skellam, Poisson, Harville, Elo all inventoried as metrics/models; calibration stack (isotonic regression, ECE-by-slice, grouping loss 2210.16315, LRD 2207.13770) covered; NFL-specific: gse-lab computed team metrics from nflverse. What is NEW here: the three-way Brier decomposition suite (Murphy REL/RES + Yates COV/VPB/VPW/RIL + LB REF/DIS/CB2) as a *comparative* diagnostic of GSE probabilities vs de-vigged market probabilities, plus the logistic-calibration α/β Wald/LR testing protocol and the C-statistic/AUC discrimination check on conditional forecast distributions. The repo's calibration work (ECE by slice, Clopper-Pearson) does not include Brier decomposition or model-vs-odds discrimination comparisons. The Poisson-ELO scoreline model itself duplicates the inventoried Dixon-Coles/Poisson family — no modeling value. Cite: 2026-09-17 dossiers + gse-lab (calibration), market-microstructure entries (de-vigged consensus, beat-the-close).

## 11. GSE implementation spec
1. **Standard verification suite** for GSE's published probabilities (win prob, over prob, ATS cover prob): implement the three decompositions exactly as eqs. (1)/(2)/(4) with Siegert binning (eq. 7) + PAV isotonic binning, computed on every backtest window (2020–2026 nflverse games, ~1,900 games): report BRS, REL, RES, DIS, VPB, VPW, RIL, COV, skill vs climatology, side-by-side for GSE vs de-vigged Pinnacle/OddsAPI consensus. Effort: ~2 days Python.
2. **Logistic calibration monitor**: weekly job fitting logit(cover) = α + β logit(GSE prob) per market (spread/total/moneyline); alert if Wald rejects α=0 (systematic over/under-forecasting) or β≠1 (over/under-confidence). This is the paper's Table 2 protocol.
3. **Discrimination dashboard**: per market, report C-statistic (AUC) of GSE vs market probabilities on X=0/X=1 conditional distributions, plus mean forecast gap (X=1 minus X=0) — Table 3 protocol; publish as internal QC.
4. NFL adaptation: no draw lane; apply to cover/no-cover, over/under, win/lose. Add push handling (void) per market rules.

## 12. Reproducible test
Dataset: nflverse 2021–2025 regular-season games with closing spread/total from the odds API archive (The Odds API historical or oddsportal-style capture): GSE engine's pregame win probability + over probability vs de-vigged closing market. Baseline: current GSE evaluation (Brier score only). Metric: full Table-5-style comparison — BRS, skill%, REL%, RES%, DIS% (VPB), ΔVarP%, 2Cov% for GSE vs market on win and over events; logistic-calibration Wald tests for GSE probs. Test protocol: rolling 2-season train / 1-season test (2021–22 → 2023, etc.); require all three binning schemes (fixed, quantile, isotonic) reported.

## 13. Acceptance / rejection gate
Adopt the suite as the standard diagnostic if: on the 2025 test window, the decomposition reveals an actionable deficiency the current Brier-only reporting misses — e.g., RES_GSE < RES_market − 0.03 (market has ≥3 pts more resolution as fraction of UNC) on either event, or logistic test rejects α=0 at p<0.01 for a GSE market. Reject if: GSE and market decompositions are statistically indistinguishable on both events (all component differences < 1% of UNC, calibration tests p>0.10) — i.e., the suite adds no diagnostic signal beyond current reporting.

## 14. Improvement experiment
Go beyond the paper: the paper bins each binary event separately. For NFL, build the **joint** calibration check — decompose the Brier score on the 2D outcome (win × over) via a copula-style joint reliability diagram: bin games by (win-prob decile, over-prob decile) and test whether conditional joint frequencies factorize. The paper's machinery is univariate; NFL edges often live in the joint tail (e.g., favorite-wins-and-covers). Additionally, replace the paper's static reliability with the CORP/T-PAV estimator from paper 0469 (Gneiting & Resin) for the binning step — the paper cites Dimitriadis et al. (2021) as "promising" but uses basic isotonic; T-PAV gives consistent optimally-binned diagrams with nonnegativity guarantees, unifying papers 0469 and 0471 into one GSE verification library.
