# [1475] Auxiliary Likelihood-Based Approximate Bayesian Computation in State Space Models (arXiv:1604.07949v3)

**Citation:** Gael M. Martin, Brendan P. M. McCabe, David T. Frazier, Worapree Maneesoonthorn, Christian P. Robert (2018, v3). *Auxiliary Likelihood-Based Approximate Bayesian Computation in State Space Models*. arXiv:1604.07949v3. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.07949
**Ledger completed:** 2026-09-21. **Read:** full text (PDF via arxiv.org/pdf, 34 pages, §§1–7 + Table 1–3 + Fig. 1 read in full; proofs in supplementary appendix acknowledged but not line-verified).
**Verdict:** ADAPT
**Verdict rationale:** score-based auxiliary-likelihood ABC with integrated-likelihood dimension reduction is a principled, empirically-validated way to fit state-space models with intractable likelihoods; port to NFL latent team-strength / scoring-dynamics models where exact likelihoods (Poisson-binary mixtures, non-central transitions) are unavailable.

## 1. Research question
In state-space models (SSMs) where the measurement density p(y_t|x_t,φ) or transition density p(x_t|x_{t-1},φ) is analytically unavailable or computationally intractable, can approximate Bayesian computation (ABC) still deliver statistically valid inference? The paper proposes using the MLE of a tractable *misspecified* auxiliary SSM as the ABC summary statistic (exploiting asymptotic sufficiency of the auxiliary MLE), proves Bayesian consistency for both the MLE-based and a far cheaper score-based variant, and proposes an integrated-likelihood trick to defeat ABC's curse of dimensionality in multi-parameter settings. Illustrated on stochastic-volatility models where exact inference is hard or impossible.

## 2. Dataset / schema
- **Simulation study (SV-SQ):** one 'empirical' sample of size T (not explicitly stated in text) simulated from the square-root (Heston) SV model with φ1=0.004, φ2=0.1, φ3=0.062 — calibrated to match daily S&P500 returns and realized-volatility characteristics over 2003–2004 (a calm period, avoiding jumps). Observations: de-meaned log returns r_t; latent variance x_t with non-central χ² transitions. Exact posterior benchmark via Ng et al. (2013) deterministic nonlinear filtering + fine-grid numerical integration.
- **Empirical illustration (§6):** S&P500 daily open-to-close returns, 2 Jan 2013 – 7 Feb 2017, n=1033 observations, standardized by sample SD; model = conditionally α-stable returns with SV (24)–(25), φ1=0 fixed, estimating (φ2, φ3, φ4) under U(0.7,0.999)×U(0.001,0.5)×U(1.2,2) priors.
- Large-sample consistency numerics (supplementary appendix): all three SV model classes, not in main text.

## 3. Method / model
**Core idea:** SSM (3)–(6): y_t=b(x_t,w_t,φ), x_t=G_φ(x_{t-1})+Σ_φ(x_{t-1})v_t, φ∈Φ⊂ℝ^{dφ} compact. Choose a parsimonious tractable auxiliary SSM (7)–(8) with parameters β∈B⊂ℝ^{dβ}, dβ≥dφ, yielding known q_β, g_β and auxiliary likelihood ℓ(y;β) = ∫···∫ χ(dx_0) g_β(y_0,x_0) ∏ Q_β(x_{p-1},dx_p) g_β(y_p,x_p). ABC summary: β̂(y)=argmax_β L_a(y;β), L_a=log ℓ. Distance (10): d{η(y),η(z(φ^i))} = [β̂(y)−β̂(z(φ^i))]' Ω [β̂(y)−β̂(z(φ^i))]^{1/2}, Ω positive-definite (or sandwich/Hessian weighting).
**Score-based variant (§3.3.1):** avoid re-optimizing per ABC draw (reported ~60× slower); use distance (14): [S(z(φ^i);β̂(y))]' Σ [S(z(φ^i);β̂(y))]^{1/2} with average score S(z;β)=T^{−1} ∂L_a(z;β)/∂β (15), S(y;β̂(y))=0. Requires maximizing the auxiliary likelihood only once.
**Dimension reduction (§3.3.2):** integrated auxiliary likelihood L_I^a(y;β_j)=∫_{B_{−j}} L_a(y;β) w(β_{−j}|β_j) dβ_{−j} (16); use the scalar integrated score S^I(z;β̂_j)=∂log L_I^a/∂β_j|_{β̂_j} as the selection statistic for each structural parameter φ_j — one (dφ−1)-dim numerical integration per parameter per ABC draw; natural 1:1 mapping when the auxiliary model is a discretization of the true model.
**Theory:** Theorem 1 — Bayesian consistency of p_ε(φ|η(y)) as T→∞, ε_T→0 (posterior concentrates on φ_0) under Assumptions A (compactness, stationarity/ergodicity, continuity/boundedness of q_β, g_β, unique limit L_∞) and I (prior mass at truth, identification φ↦b(φ) continuous with β_0=b(φ)⟺φ=φ_0, deviation control (I3)). Theorem 2 — same for score-based ABC under (A5'), (I2'), (I3'). Informal result: MLE- and score-based selection yield asymptotically equivalent draws. Authors note identification (I2) is analytically unverifiable in practice; consistency verified numerically for all three SV classes (supplementary).

## 4. Equations & assumptions
- ABC joint: p_ε(θ,z|η(y)) ∝ p(θ)p(z|θ) I[d{η(y),η(z)}≤ε] (Alg. 1, Eq. 1–2).
- SSM: (3) y_t=b(x_t,w_t,φ); (4) x_t=G_φ(x_{t-1})+Σ_φ(x_{t-1})v_t; densities (5)–(6).
- Auxiliary: (7)–(8); auxiliary likelihood ℓ(y;β); β̂(y)=argmax L_a(y;β) (9).
- Distance: (10) Wald-form on auxiliary MLEs; (14)–(15) score form; (16) integrated likelihood.
- Consistency target: ∫_{A_δ(φ_0)} p_ε(φ|η(y)) dφ = o_P(1), A_δ={φ: d(φ,φ_0)>δ} (13), for ε_T=o(1) with ε_T^{d_β+κ} v_T^κ→∞.
- SV-SQ: r_t=x_t^{1/2}η_t (17); dx_t=(φ_1−φ_2 x_t)dt+φ_3√x_t dW_t (18), transition p(x_t|x_{t-1},φ)=c exp(−u−v)(v/u)^{q/2} I_q(2√(uv)) (19) — non-central χ² with modified Bessel I_q; auxiliary: y_t=ln(x_t)+ε_t (22), x_t=β_1+β_2 x_{t-1}+β_3√x_{t-1} e_t (23) via Euler discretization + Gaussian wt, evaluated with augmented unscented Kalman filter (AUKF); α-stable models (24)–(31) with GARCH(1,1)/TARCH-N/T auxiliary models (Table 1).
- Assumptions: listed A1–A5 (compact Φ,B; stationary-ergodic simulation; positive continuous bounded q_β; g_β integrability/tail decay (A4.1–4.4); unique limit maximizer b(φ)) and I1–I3 (prior support at truth; continuous injective-in-limit identification map; polynomial deviation control). Explicitly: correctness of the auxiliary model is NOT assumed (P∉G allowed).

## 5. Features / target
ABC summary features = auxiliary-model parameters: score vector S(z;β̂(y)) or integrated scalar scores per parameter — dimension = dβ (or 1 per parameter with integration). Target = marginal posterior densities p(φ_j|y) of structural SSM parameters (volatility persistence, vol-of-vol, tail index). Conventional-ABC comparators use hand summaries (32): s1=Σy_t, s2=Σy_t², s3=Σy_t y_{t-1}, s4=y_1+y_T, s5=y_1²+y_T² on transformed y_t=ln(r_t²) or raw returns.

## 6. Validation design
Time-ordered simulation: exact marginal posteriors for SV-SQ from deterministic nonlinear filtering (Ng et al. 2013) on a very fine grid — a genuine exact benchmark, no simulation error. 18 competitors (Table 2): auxiliary-score ABC variants (AUKF joint score ± linear regression adjustment (Beaumont 2002), integrated score), GARCH/TARCH auxiliary scores × linear/nonlinear (Blum–François NN) adjustment, pooled scores, ABC-MCMC (Marjoram 2003) without dimension reduction, FP semi-automatic ABC (Fearnhead–Prangle 2012, 4th-order polynomial) on scores or on (32) raw/transformed, PMMH-BPF ('exact' up to simulation error; 10k draws after 5k burn-in, 3000 particles) and PMMH-ABCF. Each ABC: N=50,000 prior draws, retain 0.5% quantile distances. Metric: RMSE of kernel-density ordinates vs exact posterior at G grid points (33), reported as ratio to best method per parameter; overall rank = mean ratio across 3 parameters.

## 7. Numerical results / baselines
- **Table 3 (relative RMSE, bold = best per parameter; overall rank):** AUKF-AR-IN (integrated auxiliary score) rank 1: ratios 1.0000 (φ1), 2.2004 (1−φ2), 1.3307 (φ3). FP-ABC-TRANS rank 2 (1.0759, 1.0000, 1.9254). PMMH-BPF rank 3 (1.3584, 2.8303, 1.6552) — the integrated-score ABC beats the 'exact' particle method. AUKF-AR-LL rank 5, AUKF-AR rank 6; GARCH-T-AR-LL rank 7 (best φ3 ratio 1.0000). All ABC-MCMC variants rank 15–18 (lowest third) — MCMC sophistication does not compensate for missing dimension reduction. PMMH-ABC (no summaries) rank 11.
- Key findings (authors'): (i) integrated-score on a discretized-true auxiliary model most accurate overall, beating exact PMMH-BPF; (ii) FP semi-automatic reduction is strong only with good base statistics (FP-TRANS ≫ FP-RAW); (iii) dimension reduction is decisive — ABC-MCMC worst; (iv) nonlinear NN adjustment ≈ linear adjustment (negligible gain); (v) GARCH auxiliary scores + reduction beat no-summary ABC.
- **Empirical S&P500 α-stable SV:** 500 ABC draws from 331,882 replications; posterior modes φ2≈0.95, φ3≈0.25, φ4∈(1.96,1.98) — concentrated and plausible; PMMH-ABCF posterior for φ2 flat (mimics prior), φ3 peaks at lower bound (non-convergence) — auxiliary-score ABC 4h vs PMMH-ABCF 12.5h (3× faster, more reliable).
- MLE-as-summary was ~60× slower than score-based in pilot timings.

## 9. Leakage & limitations
Adversarial notes: (1) Exact benchmark exists only for SV-SQ (a model where the likelihood is actually tractable) — the headline "beats PMMH-BPF" is on the friendliest case; for the α-stable models there is no exact comparator, so accuracy there rests on plausibility. (2) Identification condition (I2) and deviation control (I3) are analytically unverifiable — the consistency theorem's hardest assumptions are asserted, numerically checked only in supplement. (3) Auxiliary model choice is doing heavy lifting: AUKF on a discretized true model ≈ the true model; in NFL settings where we don't know the true DGP, a poorly chosen auxiliary SSM could bias posteriors while looking precise (FP-TRANS vs FP-RAW gap shows statistic choice dominates). (4) 50k ABC draws at 0.5% retention = 250 draws for density estimation — thin for tail inference; RMSE metric is on ordinates, forgiving in tails. (5) Empirical φ2 mode 0.95 vs prior U(0.7,0.999): concentration could partly reflect prior truncation. (6) Finance domain: stationary ergodic assumptions (A2) are more defensible for daily returns than for NFL team strength with regime changes (coaching turnover); the theory needs the DGP to be stable.

## 10. GSE overlap

The map's ML brief lists "state-space team strength" as commissioned-not-yet-delivered. Nothing covers **likelihood-free Bayesian inference for intractable SSMs** or ABC at all. This is a new capability: GSE's latent team-strength models with realistic observation models (e.g., Poisson-binary scoring mixtures, heavy-tailed margin processes, α-stable-like upset dynamics) have intractable likelihoods — exactly the paper's setting. Adjacent: CEPT (Baxley Causal E-Process Theory) in the corpus is ensemble theory, not Bayesian SSM inference — no duplication.

## 11. GSE implementation spec
Build an ABC-SSM lane for dynamic team-strength estimation: true model = latent team offense/defense strengths with AR(1) dynamics + scoring observation model with heavy-tailed margin noise (intractable joint likelihood); auxiliary model = linear-Gaussian dynamic Bradley-Terry/Elo SSM with Kalman-filter-evaluable likelihood (the "discretized-true" analogue); summary = auxiliary score at β̂(y) via analytic/numerical differentiation; per-parameter integrated scores for the 4–6 structural params (persistence, innovation SD, home effect, tail index); accept/reject ABC with 0.5% quantile retention over ~50k simulated seasons; weighting Σ = inverse Hessian at β̂(y). Data: nflverse pbp 2009–2026 (public). Deliverable: full marginal posteriors on strength-dynamics parameters for the benchmark-lane rating system, with consistency-diagnostic (supplementary-style) concentration checks on simulated seasons. Effort: ~1 week (score evaluation + ABC loop in Python/JAX; UKF/AUKF for auxiliary likelihood via filterpy/JAX).

## 12. Reproducible test
Dataset: simulated seasons from a known heavy-tailed dynamic-strength DGP calibrated to NFL margins (2009–2024 seasons as T), truth φ_0 known. Metric: RMSE of ABC marginal-posterior density ordinates vs exact posterior (computable via particle filter on the true DGP) at grid points — same protocol as the paper's Table 3. Baselines: (a) exact PMMH-BPF on the true DGP; (b) plain ABC on hand summaries (season means/variances/autocorrelations of margins, the NFL analogue of (32)) with FP semi-automatic reduction.

## 13. Acceptance / rejection gate
ADAPT-accept iff the integrated-score ABC's mean relative RMSE across structural parameters ≤ 1.5 (i.e., within 50% of the best comparator) on the simulated-season benchmark AND ranks above the hand-summary FP-ABC baseline; otherwise reject (the auxiliary-model choice isn't informative enough for NFL dynamics).

## 14. Improvement experiment
Hybrid ABC–particle exactness check: use the ABC posterior draws as the proposal/initialization for a short PMMH chain on the true NFL DGP, testing whether score-based ABC can serve as a warm-start that cuts PMMH burn-in by ≥50% while recovering the same marginals — this would give GSE both the speed of ABC and the exactness guarantee of PMMH, beyond what the paper tested (they compared the two as rivals, never combined them).
