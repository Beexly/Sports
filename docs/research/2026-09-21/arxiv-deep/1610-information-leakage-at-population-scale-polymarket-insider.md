# [1610] Information Leakage at Population Scale: An Evaluation of the Polymarket Insider-Relevant Subpopulation, 2020–2026 (arXiv:2605.00459)

**Citation:** M. Nechepurenko (2026). *Information Leakage at Population Scale: An Evaluation of the Polymarket Insider-Relevant Subpopulation, 2020–2026*. arXiv:2605.00459. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00459
**Ledger completed:** 2026-09-21. **Read:** full text (ar5iv HTML converted to text, 22,272 words).
**Verdict:** ADAPT — the Information Leakage Score (ILS) framework, the hazard-decay baseline correction, and the category-conditional informed-flow argument are portable to an NFL sharp-flow monitor, but only under the paper's own cautions: treat market-level ILS as one weak complementary signal to wallet-level detection, never as a standalone detector, and always anchor-robustness-screen it.

## 1. Research question
The paper asks whether the deadline-resolved Information Leakage Score (ILS^dl) — which measures the share of a prediction market's terminal price move accomplished *before* public observation of the underlying event — remains usable when scaled from a single-case demonstration to a population of 12,708 Polymarket markets (military/geopolitics, regulatory, corporate-disclosure categories, Oct 2020–Apr 2026). It is explicitly framed as a scope-discovery study: does population-scale deployment reveal broad insider-flow signal, or does it reveal that the method's effective domain is structurally narrow? Three companion aims: (i) position the market-level ILS lens against the wallet-level (Mitts–Ofir 2026) and account-level (Gómez-Cram et al. 2026) informed-trading lenses; (ii) argue that pooled detectors suffer a selection penalty and that detection should condition on insider-relevant categories; (iii) validate the T_event recovery pipeline and hazard-rate specification at scale.

## 2. Dataset / schema
- **Master extract:** 911,237-market Polymarket-Gamma corpus; 865,725 resolved markets; 99,919 markets above the $50K cumulative-volume CLOB-coverage cutoff.
- **Insider-relevant subpopulation:** 127,403 resolved markets across three categories (military/geopolitics, regulatory decisions, corporate disclosures); 11,263 above the $50K cutoff.
- **Candidate sample:** 12,708 markets entering the ILS pipeline; temporal span Oct 2020–Apr 2026; 33 pre-2024-cycle, 55 post-2024-cycle in the in-scope subset.
- **In-scope sample:** 88 markets (0.7%) produce computable ILS^dl (mean volume ≈ $597K, median ≈ $179K, total ≈ $53.7M); 12 of the 88 (13.6%) are anchor-robust.
- **FFIC inventory (validation):** 32 markets across 8 publicly documented insider-trading episodes (CFTC default-judgment Maduro contract, Oct 2024 Iran strike, 2026 U.S.–Iran cluster, 2026 U.S.–Venezuela/Maduro cluster, Bitcoin ETF SEC approval, Google Year-in-Search, FTX/SBF, Romanian election). Only 1 of 32 is in scope.
- **CLOB data:** per-trade history via Polymarket subgraph from T_open onward; continuous collection pipeline operational since April 26, 2026 (operational subset reserved for follow-up paper 3b).
- Access: public. Dataset `polymarket-deadline-ils-v3` at https://github.com/ForesightFlow/datasets (CC-BY-4.0). Companion releases: `polymarket-resolution-typology-v1`, `foresightflow-insider-cases`. Code: https://github.com/ForesightFlow/platform, tag v1.1-paper3a-rev1 (MIT).

## 3. Method / model
Four sequential pipeline filters then scoring: (i) resolution-typology classification (drop unclassifiable; binary discrete-event vs. deadline-resolved); (ii) exclude deadline-resolved markets that resolve NO (by construction no recoverable underlying event); (iii) LLM-cascade T_event recovery with confidence ≥ 0.7 (Tier 1 Gemini 2.5 Flash + Search grounding; Tier 2 GPT-4o-mini; Tier 3 Claude Sonnet 4.6; escalate on low confidence; Iran-Apr30 reference value hard-assert before batch); (iv) full CLOB price coverage from T_open. Then compute ILS^dl under scope conditions (τ > 0 lead time, edge-effect |p(T_open)−0.5| ≤ 0.4, non-trivial move |Δ_total^dl| ≥ ε = 0.05). Population pipeline cost ≈ $1.20 in LLM spend. Distribution analyzed in six (sub-bucket × period) cells: regulatory_announcement (scheduled events, public timing), regulatory_formal (endogenous deliberation timing), milgeo_corporate (pooled, finer labels deferred). Detection thresholds at top-decile/top-5%/top-1% with B=500 market-level bootstrap CIs. Anchor-sensitivity check: recompute ILS under 30-min/2h/6h/24h anchor offsets; pass requires no sign change and shift ≤ 0.3. Hazard-rate fit (exponential vs. Weibull vs. lognormal) on time-to-event τ with parametric-bootstrap KS goodness-of-fit.

## 4. Equations & assumptions
- Base score: ILS(M) = Δ_pre / Δ_total, Δ_pre = p(T_news) − p(T_open), Δ_total = p_T_resolve − p(T_open).
- Deadline extension: ILS^dl(M) = (p(T_event^−) − p(T_open)) / (p_T_resolve − p(T_open)).
- Hazard-decay-adjusted: ILS^{dl,adj}(M) = (p(T_event^−) − E[p(T_event^−) | T_open, D, λ̂]) / (p_T_resolve − p(T_open)), subtracting the survival-probability-implied baseline for deadline contracts.
- Murphy-decomposition reading: ILS quantifies the share of the Brier-score resolution component accumulated before the public-observation anchor.
- Interpretable when |Δ_total| ≥ ε (ε = 0.05), |p(T_open) − 0.5| ≤ 0.4 (edge-effect), anchor-robust.
- Hazard: pooled post-2024 exponential λ̂ = 0.042 (half-life 16.4 days); corporate-disclosure-only reference from prior work λ̂ = 0.156, half-life 4.5 days. Weibull used as default production prior pending per-category re-estimation; shape parameter k̂ explicitly not interpreted.
- Assumptions: share price = implied probability; recovered T_event is the true price-discovery moment; hazard-decay model captures rational pre-deadline price drift; news citation coupling and flagged wallet sets are independent evidence; FFIC inventory is a valid (incomplete) ground truth.

## 5. Features / target
The score itself is the feature; no ML model is trained in this paper (a gradient-boosted-tree detector combining ILS^dl with wallet-level features — PIN, VPIN, Kyle's lambda, wallet novelty score, two-sidedness, variance ratio, trade-size kurtosis, Hawkes self-excitation — is sequenced for the follow-up paper 3b; the detector is *not* built here). Target variable in the validation design: documented insider cases from the FFIC inventory. Category-conditional reporting is used as the prior-shaping device: conditioning on insider-relevant categories up-weights the prior probability of flagged trades reflecting MNPI by ≈7.9× (population shares 11.3% vs. 88.7%).

## 6. Validation design
- Independent-second-pass T_event validation on a 50-market stratified sample (criterion ex ante: exact-date agreement ≥ 90%).
- Anchor-sensitivity scope condition on all 88 computed markets (no sign change, shift ≤ 0.3 across short-window anchor offsets).
- Iran-Apr30 hard-assert: recovery pipeline must reproduce the ground-truth event date from the single-case paper before batch execution.
- FFIC disposition audit: all 32 documented insider markets run through the pipeline to measure coverage.
- LLM-assisted tail diagnostic on the top-10 and bottom-10 ILS^dl markets (Claude Haiku classifier, author review of disagreements).
- Hazard functional-form comparison with parametric-bootstrap KS (B=999), seed 20260430, snapshot cutoff 2026-04-27.
- Cross-method positioning against Mitts–Ofir (210,718 flagged wallet–market pairs, $143M flagged profit, 60σ deviation) and Gómez-Cram et al. (1,950 informed accounts of 1.72M) as comparative positioning, not a confusion-matrix analysis (their flagged sets are not public).

## 7. Numerical results / baselines
- **Coverage:** 88 of 12,708 candidate markets (0.7%) yield computable ILS^dl; 12 of 88 (13.6%) satisfy anchor-sensitivity. Effective interpretable domain ≈ 12/12,708 markets.
- **FFIC:** only 1 of 32 documented insider markets in scope (Bitcoin ETF approval, fficd-005, ILS^dl = 0.012, at the regulatory_announcement pre-2024 cell median, not the right tail). Dominant exclusion: 14 of 32 (43.8%) unclassifiable by resolution-typology; 8 (25.0%) deadline-resolved NO; 2 (6.25%) edge-effect (p_0 ≥ 0.9); 1 low T_event confidence; 1 price-data failure; 1 outside categories.
- **T_event validation:** 57.8% exact-date agreement overall — below the ≥90% ex-ante criterion. By sub-bucket: milgeo_corporate 78.9%, regulatory_formal 35.7%, regulatory_announcement 50.0%. Of 24 date disagreements: 9 minor (≤7 days), 10 major (>7 days), 5 second-pass no usable timestamp.
- **Distribution:** raw ILS^dl medians negative across all six cells (range −0.88 to −0.16). Hazard-decay adjustment: regulatory_formal post-2024 −0.21 → −0.02; milgeo_corporate post-2024 −0.55 → −0.34; regulatory_announcement post-2024 −0.80 → −0.84, bootstrap CI entirely below zero [−1.45, −0.24].
- **Anchor robustness:** Spearman rank correlation 0.30 between article-derived anchor and 24h variant; 86% anchor-fragility rate; fragility concentrates in markets where independent recovery disagrees on date.
- **Hazard:** pooled post-2024 (n=57) Weibull AIC 454.66 vs. exponential 476.59 (Δ=21.9, well above the 4-unit threshold); parametric-bootstrap KS rejects exponential (p<0.01), accepts Weibull (p>0.50). Per-subcategory check: post-2024 regulatory_formal alone (n=22) prefers exponential (AIC 179.45 vs. Weibull 180.50) and rejects Weibull (KS p=0.043) — pooled preference reflects category mixture, not within-cell duration dependence.
- **Detection thresholds:** right tail is "candidate-flag region, not established positive mean": post-2024 90th percentiles 0.62 (regulatory_formal), −0.01 (milgeo_corporate — right tail doesn't cross zero), 0.11 (regulatory_announcement). 9 of 10 right-tail markets classify as qualitatively leakage-shaped after hazard adjustment; left tail median −1.85 raw (interpretations: anti-information flow, T_event error, or outcome-dependent selection).
- **Selection effect:** insider-relevant subpopulation is 15.3% of resolved markets / 11.3% of ≥$50K-vol markets; pooled detectors bucket signal at 1:7.9 against a structurally-irrelevant control (sports, crypto price, weather).

## 9. Leakage & limitations
- The method's own headline is a warning: 12/12,708 effective domain, 57.8% T_event agreement vs. a ≥90% criterion, and a single in-scope ground-truth case (Bitcoin ETF, mid-distribution, not right tail). Any adaptation must inherit these caveats, not cite the framework as proven.
- Anchor-fragility is the dominant measurement error; the anchor-robustness condition functions empirically as a T_event-localizability filter. In NFL adaptation the analog (injury-news anchor, line-move anchor) needs the same 30min/24h stability test or the score is noise.
- The regulatory_announcement post-2024 cell — the one with the clean negative signal — rests on n=19 markets and a 50% T_event validation rate; treat as suggestive.
- Outcome-dependent selection biases the tails: left-tail "anti-information" markets are YES-resolving markets that collapsed pre-event; the matched NO-resolved control is not assembled (deferred to 3b).
- Unclassifiability is structural in documented insider cases (43.8%): ambiguous resolution predicates ("strike", "custody", "win"). In NFL terms, exotic/derivative markets with ambiguous settlement language are exactly the ones where informed flow concentrates — and exactly the ones a market-level score cannot timestamp.
- FFIC ground truth is incomplete (32 markets) and the flagged wallet/account sets from the two companion methods are not public, so the cross-method confluence claim is positioning, not evidence.
- NFL external validity: the paper covers political/regulatory/geopolitical Polymarket contracts. NFL moneyline/spread markets resolve on observable field outcomes, not news events, so the "event vs. deadline" structure differs — GSE's analog is scheduled-information markets (coach firings, trade deadlines) and pre-kickoff line discovery.

## 11. GSE implementation spec
1. **NFL ILS analog ("pre-close drift share"):** for each NFL game-week, compute line movement share before vs. after a public anchor. Define anchors: (a) scheduled news (injury report release Wednesday, inactive list 90 min before kickoff); (b) market events (a 2+ point spread move or 5+ cent moneyline move). Score = (price at anchor⁻ − open) / (close − open), for spreads normalized in probability space. This mirrors ILS^dl with kickoff as the deadline and injury news as T_event.
2. **Hazard-decay correction:** fit the natural drift of NFL lines toward close (the "rational decay" baseline) by time-to-kickoff, then report hazard-adjusted drift — exactly the paper's ILS^{dl,adj} idea — so that late-breaking-but-legitimate injury moves aren't scored as informed flow.
3. **Anchor-robustness gate:** recompute the score under anchor perturbations (±1h injury-news anchor, ±6h steam anchor); drop any game where the sign flips or the score shifts >0.3 — the paper's rule, imported verbatim. Expect most games to fail this gate; that's the point.
4. **Wallet/side proxy:** GSE lacks per-bettor flow, so proxy informed flow with consensus-vs-sharp-book divergence (Pinnacle vs. market-average drift) and bet-split asymmetry where available.
5. **Ensemble with 1608/1609 signals:** feed the anchor-robust drift score, the 1609-style dislocation detector, and the 1608-style arb/inefficiency monitor into a small GBM; calibrate against whether the pre-close drift side covered (this mirrors the paper's 3b roadmap of ILS + wallet features → GBM).
6. **Effort:** ~2–3 days for the NFL drift-score + anchor gate on one season of line history; the GBM ensemble is a second sprint once 1608/1609 pipelines exist.

## 12. Reproducible test
Dataset: GSE's historical NFL odds DB (The Odds API captures), 2024–2025 seasons: open, kickoff-close, and a dated injury-news anchor per game. Metric: compute the anchor-robust drift-share score and correlate with whether the early-drift side covered ATS. Baseline to beat: the paper's own expectation that unadjusted scores are noise — a Spearman correlation significantly > 0 (p < 0.05) between the anchor-robust hazard-adjusted drift share and cover outcome, computed only on the anchor-robust subset. Secondary: replicate the coverage finding — report the fraction of NFL game-weeks passing the anchor-robustness gate; if it exceeds 25% the market is cleaner than Polymarket's 13.6%, if below, the gate is the value (a noise filter).

## 13. Acceptance / rejection gate
**Adapt** the ILS framework into GSE's steam/informed-flow suite if: (i) the anchor-robust subset (however small) shows a drift-share/cover correlation significant at p < 0.05 on 2024–2025 NFL; and (ii) the anchor-robustness gate filters out ≥50% of raw flagged games (proving it's doing the noise-filtering job the paper documents). **Reject** (do not deploy) if the anchor-robust subset shows no significant correlation or the anchor gate passes nearly everything — in which case the score is uncalibrated noise in NFL markets and GSE stays with the 1608/1609 direct market-efficiency monitors instead.

## 14. Improvement experiment
The paper's biggest structural gap is the unclassifiable bucket — documented insider cases concentrate in ambiguous-predicate markets. GSE's improvement: build the *multi-anchor* extension the paper defers to 3b, but for NFL derivative markets (first-TD scorer, player props, quarter lines): compute the drift score under every candidate news anchor (each injury report, each beat-reporter tweet timestamp within the window) and take the distribution of scores rather than a point estimate. Markets whose score distribution is right-shifted under *all* anchors are the robust informed-flow candidates. This directly attacks the paper's 57.8% T_event-agreement failure by refusing to commit to a single anchor — and it tests the paper's own hypothesis (ambiguity attracts informed flow) in a market where GSE can measure settlement cleanly.
