## 1. Research question
Can a deep network blindly recover physical optical aberration parameters (Zernike coefficients Z2–Z37) from a single blurred smartphone image, so that the recovered coefficients satisfy Fourier-optics constraints and enable stable downstream non-blind deconvolution — rather than having a "blackbox" network hallucinate sharpened pixels? (Paper: Abstract + §1.)

## 2. Dataset / schema
- **IDMxS Mobile Camera Lens Database** (proprietary/patented, access "available at https://idmxs.org/research/mobilephonecameralensdatabase/" per Data Availability Statement; internally described as "Propr. Dataset (2025)" in ref [7]). 109 discrete smartphone lens designs supplied as Zemax OpticStudio files; ground-truth Zernike coefficients (Z2–Z37, Noll sequential indexing) extracted per lens.
- Clean images sourced from a **public smartphone microscopy dataset** [8] ("P. Source, Smartphone microscopy dataset, 2025"), used as high-frequency biological-structure patches (256×256 px).
- **Training corpus:** 110,090 synthetic blurred images generated by convolving clean patches with PSFs computed from lens coefficients via a Fourier-optics model. No public row-level access; lens designs are proprietary.
- Split: 5-fold cross-validation; train/test strictly separated **by lens design** (evaluated on unseen lenses from the same database). Exact fold sizes not stated.

## 3. Method / model
**Lens2Zernike**: ResNet-18 backbone modified to regress a 36-dimensional Zernike coefficient vector, with a three-part physics-consistent supervision strategy (§2B):
1. **Coefficient loss (Lcoeff):** standard MSE in normalized Zernike coefficient space (z).
2. **Physics loss (Lphysics):** a differentiable optics layer maps predicted coefficients → wavefront phase map (φ) → PSF via Fourier transform; MSE minimized between derived quantities and ground truths. Added in two granular variants: wavefront-only (pW) and PSF-only (pP), then combined (p).
3. **Multitask map loss (Lmap):** auxiliary decoder heads explicitly predict high-resolution wavefront and PSF maps (m), giving dense spatial supervision.
Optimization uses MSE to penalize large outliers during training; hyperparameters (learning rate, optimizer, epochs, λ weights) **not stated in paper**. Baselines for comparison: adapted DLWFS (Xception-based) [9] and DLAO (LAPANet) [10].

## 4. Equations & assumptions
Equation (1), exactly as stated in §2B (PDF extraction garbled spacing; reconstruction uncertain):
`L_total = λz·Lcoeff + λp·Lphysics + λm·Lmap`  — weighted sum of the three loss terms.
Physics loss: MSE between the wavefront/PSF derived from predicted Zernike coefficients (via differentiable optics layer) and ground-truth quantities. No further equations stated (no explicit Zernike polynomial expansion, no PSF formula, no Wiener-filter equation given in text).
**Assumptions:** (i) lens aberrations are adequately modeled by Zernike polynomials Z2–Z37 at the pupil plane (Noll indexing); (ii) the Fourier-optics forward simulation (PSF from coefficients) is physically faithful; (iii) evaluation in units of λ (reference optical wavelength); (iv) 5-fold lens-level splits generalize to unseen in-domain lens designs; (v) training on synthetic forward-simulated blur transfers to real captures (future work — not validated in this paper).

## 5. Features / target
- **Input features:** single blurred 256×256 image patch (raw pixels).
- **Target:** 36-dimensional Zernike coefficient vector (Z2–Z37) in normalized coefficient space; evaluation in unnormalized physical wave space (λ).
- No prediction horizon; this is a single-frame inverse problem, not forecasting.

## 6. Validation design
5-fold cross-validation with strict **lens-design-level** separation (train on some lenses, test on unseen lenses from the same proprietary database). Primary metrics: MAE and MSE of Zernike coefficients in unnormalized λ space; downstream evaluation via mean PSNR of Wiener deconvolution using predicted PSFs vs. oracle PSFs. Baselines: DLWFS (Xception) and DLAO (LAPANet) adapted to the task. Fold-level variance/confidence intervals **not reported**. Train/validation/test split not time-ordered (not applicable — no temporal dimension).

## 7. Numerical results / baselines
(§3, Tables 1–2; quoted exactly:)
- Ablation (ResNet-18), MAE (λ) / MSE (λ²):
  - z (baseline, coeff-only): 0.00197 / 5.82×10⁻⁵
  - z + Lwave (pW only): 0.00156 / 5.56×10⁻⁵
  - z + Lpsf (pP only): 0.00152 / 5.19×10⁻⁵
  - z + p (combined physics): 0.00137 / 4.73×10⁻⁵
  - z + p + m (full multitask): **0.00128 / 4.20×10⁻⁵** — "an approximate 35% improvement over the baseline" (MAE: (0.00197−0.00128)/0.00197 ≈ 35%).
- Literature baselines: DLWFS (Xception) MAE 0.00173λ; DLAO (LAPANet) MAE 0.00324λ; ours (z+p+m) MAE 0.00128λ.
- Downstream restoration: mean PSNR 24.66 dB (predicted PSFs) vs. oracle 25.02 dB; "Oracle Gap" −0.36 dB.
- Note: MAE of 0.00128λ reported to 3 significant figures; Table 1 shows MSE values with inconsistent spacing (5.82 × 10⁻⁵ etc.). All numbers are paper claims; no independent verification possible (proprietary data).

## 9. Leakage & limitations
- **Proprietary, irreproducible core asset:** the 109 lens designs are patented; no external replication possible.
- **Synthetic training data:** network inverts the paper's own forward model (PSF computed from the same Zernike representation it regresses) — circularity risk; real-hardware validation is explicitly future work.
- No fold-level variance or significance testing; a 35% relative gain on MAE at λ-scale absolute errors (0.00128λ) may be within simulation noise — untested.
- Hyperparameters (λz, λp, λm, optimizer, epochs) unstated → not reimplementable exactly.
- **External validity to NFL: none.** Mobile-optics deconvolution has no transfer path to sports prediction. The only faint methodological motif — adding physics-consistency auxiliary losses to regularize a regressor — is a generic ML idea with no GSE application.
