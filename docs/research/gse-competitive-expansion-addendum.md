# GSE Competitive Expansion Addendum

## Scope

This is the continuation requested after the initial NFL world-model packet. It adds:

- High-stakes fantasy and contest-source watchlist.
- User-mentioned platform coverage: FFPC, NFFC/NFPC correction, RTS/RTSports, Run The Sims, Dimers, Ballers, Drafters, Rebet, DraftKings, DK Pick6 and adjacent products.
- 46 optimizer or optimizer-adjacent competitor records.
- 15 media, betting-intelligence and fantasy-tool competitor records including FTN, Fantasy Guru, I Sell Winners, Draft Sharks, FantasyPros, Footballguys, Underdog Network, Action Network, Covers, VegasInsider, OddsJam and Outlier.
- Retention, engagement, sales, marketing, finance and brand research.

## Main Conclusion

Competitors are converging on the same commodity primitives: projections, salaries, ownership, stacks, simulations, locks, excludes, CSV export, Discord/community and premium subscriptions. GSE should not chase them feature-for-feature blindly. GSE should build a source-provenanced football world model, then expose optimizer/scenario/retention products from that shared evidence layer.
