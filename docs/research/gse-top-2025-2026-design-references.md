## Design Direction For GSE

1. Cockpit screens should stay quiet, dense, and fast: tables, panels, keyboardable controls, clear health states, and very limited motion.
2. Public story pages can be more premium: full-bleed sports media, controlled scroll narrative, source-world visualizations, and luxury-level card detail.
3. Motion should explain state changes. It should never hide latency, hijack scroll, or make a user wait to inspect data.
4. Every data card should have a trust affordance: source, freshness, fallback, conflict, and what changed.
5. The brand should avoid generic sports-betting neon and generic dark SaaS. Use measured contrast, live-event energy, and evidence-first surfaces.
