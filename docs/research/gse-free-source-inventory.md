## Best Free Source Families

- nflverse/nflreadr/nflreadpy/nflfastR: best historical NFL modeling spine.
- NWS API: best official U.S. weather source for stadium forecasts and alerts.
- Wikidata/Wikimedia: low-risk entity and attention baselines when validated.
- Sleeper API: useful fantasy attention proxy, not truth.
- GDELT: useful news-volume and narrative-pressure proxy.
- Big Data Bowl datasets: useful tracking-model R&D slices, not live data.

## Best Low-Limit or Free-Key Families

- The Odds API: already integrated; verify current plan and NFL access before expanding.
- CollegeFootballData: free key with low monthly quota; useful for CFB/prospect priors.
- YouTube Data API: quota-based metadata only; useful for video attention.
- SportsGameOdds/API-Sports/SportsDataIO trials: evaluate behind provider abstraction, never as hidden permanent dependencies.
