## PART 2: THEORIES & INNOVATION KERNELS

Every attributed theory, composite, twist, or presentation kernel from the sweeps. No verdicts, no implementation recommendations, no build orders.

### September 18

- **Ten-facet EPA/WPA decomposition** (@SamHoppen): orienting nflfastR EPA/WPA to the team and assigning each play to one of ten facets (turnovers > penalties > pass/run off > pass/run def > ST > other), then summing — fully replicable from public nflfastR data (per the Sept 18 reverse-engineering pass).
- **Market-implied neutral strength** (@benbbaldwin): regressing point spreads on a team-incidence matrix to an implied-SRS (intercept = home-field advantage; 2021 market HFA ≈ 0.62), then blending with de-vigged futures — the strongest-prior family in the Sept 18 reverse-engineering ranking; blend weights undisclosed.
- **PROE+** (@MagicSportsGuy/StatRankings): joining pass-rate expectation and neutral pace into one number.
- **StatRankings CB/Receiver Matchup Report** (@MagicSportsGuy): alignment-overlap matchup mapping crossed with man/zone splits, coverage-shell splits, and positional team target share — 2025 and 2026 never blended.
- **Similarity Finder** (@FantasyPtsData): historical similarity scoring on usage/efficiency shape for one-game samples (SIM score on 9 of 144 usage stats).
- **Bellcow Report** (@FantasyPtsData): backfield dominance measured via expected fantasy points rather than touches.
- **Y-Aware PCA Tendency Rating** (@PattonAnalytics): a single composite play-caller process score built from tendencies (personnel diversification, sequencing) rather than outcomes.
- **NFL SNAP backtest** (@EaglesXsandOs): the "which categories tie to winning" backtest framing; EPA/db explains results better than ANY/A (83.5% vs 80.9% same-game; 62.6% vs 62.1% predictive).

- **Under-center efficiency gap** (@tejfbanalytics, Sept 21 but seeded here): SumerSports charting crossed with formation splits.
