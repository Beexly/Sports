Best DFS Lineup Optimizers (2026): An Honest Comparison

By the FantasySportsEdge TeamA small team of data and AI engineers who play DFSUpdated October 2, 2026

Six tools show up when you search for a DFS lineup optimizer: SaberSim, Stokastic, RotoWire, FantasyLabs, Daily Fantasy Fuel, and us. We built FantasySportsEdge, so weigh our angle accordingly. What follows is what each one actually does, checked against their own feature pages on 2026-09-25.

If you want a free multi-sport builder across NBA, WNBA, MLB, and NFL with no card on file, that's us, with Pro for bigger builds and props. If you want tournament simulations from a team built around GPP modeling, SaberSim or Stokastic do that. If you only play NFL and want one free lineup, RotoWire has that too.

What does the Fantasy Fuel optimizer include?

Daily Fantasy Fuel's free tier is a DraftKings and FanDuel optimizer capped at 2 lineups per build. Its Premium plan adds ownership projections, a Prop Explorer, Analytics/Trends, and dedicated PrizePicks and Underdog optimizers. We don't publish a price for it here — see their pricing page for current plans.

Is FantasySportsEdge actually free?

Yes, up to a point. Sign in with a free account and every lineup builder is open: NBA, WNBA, MLB, and NFL, classic and single-game slates, 5 lineups per build, no card required. Without an account it is 2. Pro raises that to 50 lineups per build and opens the full props board for NFL and MLB. It starts with a7-day trial that asks for a card. What we don't have: no tournament simulations and no model-building tools. If you're chasing those, one of the five tools below does it better than we do.

What does SaberSim offer, and who's it for?

SaberSim covers 17 sports and esports categories including WNBA, and its pitch is built on tournament simulations rather than single-point projections. That's a real edge if you play large-field GPPs and want a diverse pool of lineups built fast. It offers a paid trial, not a free tier, so it's not built for someone who wants to check one lineup for free.

What does Stokastic offer?

Stokastic covers NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, WNBA, PGA, college football, NASCAR, and MMA, and its own copy positions it around projections, ownership, and simulations, the same category as SaberSim. It doesn't advertise a free tier. We don't list a price for it here; check their signup flow directly for current plans.

Is RotoWire's DFS optimizer free?

The NFL optimizer is; RotoWire says so on the page itself. Everything past that sits behind a subscription that also covers MLB, NBA, NHL, soccer, golf, college football, college basketball, and WNBA. RotoWire's angle is breadth: season-long tools, DFS, and betting content in one account.

Is FantasyLabs still around, and what does it do?

Yes, and it covers ten sports including WNBA, NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, PGA, MMA, NASCAR, college football, and college basketball. It's sold sport by sport through a signup flow. What it's built for: Player Models and SimLabs backtesting, so you build and test your own projections instead of trusting someone else's. We couldn't find a genuinely free tool; the trends page we checked pushes a paid trial with no free path.

The honest bottom line

All five competitors have WNBA pages or coverage, although Daily Fantasy Fuel does not list it on the Premium plan card, so that filter alone won't narrow the field. What actually separates them is whether the free version does anything real and what each one is built for. SaberSim and Stokastic are built for GPP simulation. RotoWire gives away one free lineup and charges for scale or betting content. FantasyLabs charges for the ability to build your own model. Daily Fantasy Fuel offers a capped free optimizer and bundles ownership and prop tools with Premium. We give away a capped multi-sport builder (5 lineups per build), sell Pro for bigger builds and the props board, and don't have the sims or model-building tools the others do.

Pick based on what you actually need, not which page ranked first.
