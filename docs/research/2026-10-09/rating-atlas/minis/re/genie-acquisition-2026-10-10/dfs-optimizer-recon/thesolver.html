How the tools fit together

You can use any one tool on its own. Most DFS players use them in this order.

1

Build your lineups

Start in the Optimizer. Pick a projection set, set the rules you want (stacks, exposure, how many lineups), and it builds the lineups for you.

2

Test them before you enter

Send those lineups to the Simulator. It runs them through tens of thousands of simulated contests and tells you which ones are worth the entry fee.

3

Review and track

After the contest, the Post-Lock Sim Dashboard shows what the winning builds did differently, and the Bankroll Tracker keeps a running record of your profit and ROI.

Play best ball instead? The Draft Assistant helps you pick while you draft, and the free Best Ball Explorer helps you manage your teams after. Both work with Underdog and DraftKings.
