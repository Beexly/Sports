What is a DFS Lineup Optimizer?

A DFS lineup optimizer streamlines the process of lineup generation. On sites like DraftKings and FanDuel, you can enter up to 150 lineups in a given contest, and even more in a single day if you’re playing multiple sports or slates.

Operators have different constraints and rules, such as salary caps, roster requirements, fantasy scoring and player pricing. Lineup optimizers take these constraints into account to ensure your lineups adhere to each site’s rules for a given sport and DFS game style (Classic vs. Showdown).

If you’re entering more than 20 lineups on a slate, you’re likely spending a lot of time simply entering your lineups and monitoring your player exposure. Handbuilding is also susceptible to mistakes such as duplicate lineups.

In giving users the ability to lock, filter, and exclude players and teams, lineup optimizers save countless hours that can be spent elsewhere, on leisure or more DFS research, whatever you prefer!

LineupHQ is a one-stop shop for your lineup-building needs. Within the lineup optimizer, you will find expert projections (or you can upload your own), projected ownership, and our Opto%.

Additionally, you get expert picks and analysis from DFS pros like John Breslin (Squirrelpatrol), Dave Potts (CheeseIsGood), Stephen Young (Stevietpfl), Derek Farnsworth (Notorious), Justin Van Zuiden (stlcardinals84), and more! You can also sort by floor or ceiling projection, not to mention a dozen different statistical categories.

That’s not all…

Use the lineup optimizer to create groups within your player pool, along with complex stacking rules to go deeper with your optimizing strategy than ever before.

Build and Save Stacks

Stacking is a pivotal part of building successful, winning tournament lineups. The lineup optimizer’s Stack Builder feature allows you to build multiple team stacks utilizing your player pool. Want to stack a QB with a WR from their team and an opposing WR? You can do that here. Want to go with a minimum number of onslaught stacks? You can do that as well. Generate the number of different stacks you want and the Stack Builder will generate the top projected stacks based on the criteria you enter. From there, you can go save the ones that stand out for you and allocate percentages to those stacks, and the lineup optimizer will follow your lead when you generate lineups.

Player Groups

Groups provide the functionality to include or exclude players based on definitions of your desired criteria. Think of this as player pools within your player pool, creating groups to force an allocated percentage of your lineups to use specific player sets.
