# Saturday 2026-10-10 — college slate, not scored

Saturday 2026-10-10 is a college football slate. No NFL games. This packet
does not score it.

## Why not scored

- The research branch has no college close file. The close is the bar, and
  without it nothing here can be priced or graded.
- College is an additional model, not a config flag on the NFL engine.
  See CFB_MODEL_DECISION.md for the decision and the build order.
- packages/prediction-engine has no college code, and none was added.
- NFL sigma 13.45, home-field 1.56, and NFL_EPA_HFA 0.025 EPA/play are NFL
  constants. None of them is ported to college. College sigma and home field
  must be fit on college residuals against a real close.

## What the intake may do

`packages/data-ingestion/src/cfbfastr-intake.ts` on main is data intake only:
env-gated (`CFBFASTR_INTAKE_ENABLED`), fail-closed, null-on-missing, as-of
discipline. It validates plays and aggregates EPA. It does not price a game.

## What a college card would need

A card, if one is ever produced, carries the market, the residual against
the close, and a situational read. No number alone. No live-bet language.

## Standing order

Do not score the Saturday slate from this packet. If college work starts,
it starts from the CFB_MODEL_DECISION.md build order: FBS games table and a
real close join, fit sigma and home field on college residuals against that
close, seal last season, score against the close.
