# GSE FREE / PRO / ELITE / Founder-Only Signal Split

## FREE

- Schedule state, game status, team names, venue, kickoff, public weather badge, source freshness badge.
- Low-detail matchup summary with confidence labels only.
- Public-safe explanation text with attribution.
- No formulas, raw lines, medical language, or source-risk details.

## PRO

- Rest/travel, historical form, weather sensitivity, stable team/player context, source-backed trend cards.
- Limited non-wager market context if compliance-approved.
- Player role and matchup cards with confidence bands.

## ELITE

- Scenario toggles for inactive players, roof/weather changes, line movement, and role-share shifts.
- Injury/availability impact with official-status precedence and no diagnosis.
- Video-game analog cards and matchup lab views.
- Higher-frequency alerts if source contracts and quotas allow.

## Founder-Only

- Formula weights, source-risk ledger, blocked feeds, provider choices, contract notes, weird experiments, alpha hypotheses, calibration failures, and moat analysis.
- Founder kill switches for source families, products, and model outputs.

## Public Safety Defaults

If a source is blocked, stale, contract-expired, or legally ambiguous, the default product behavior is silence or degraded confidence, not hidden substitution.
