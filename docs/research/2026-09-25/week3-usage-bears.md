### The "Keenum guy" — best-supported read

From the existing study (`docs/research/2026-09-24/keenum-target-splits.md`, nflverse 2013–2023, 2,270 targets): Keenum shows **no positional bias** (career WR 59.6% / TE 20.4% / RB 20.0% vs. league 59.3% / 20.9% / 19.7%); after long layoffs he skewed slightly *more* WR-heavy (62.2% / 20.1% / 17.6%). His "safety blanket" is **player- and scheme-specific**, not position-specific: Chris Thompson (2019 Wk1: 10 of 43 targets), Jarvis Landry (2021 Wk7: 8 of 32; 2022 Wk18: 8 of 24), Noah Brown (2023 Wk15: 11 of 34). The archetype: a **trusted short-area target — slot receiver or receiving back**.

Applied to Chicago's two games:

1. **Kalif Raymond (strongest evidence).** Led Bears WRs in production **both weeks** (8/9/84 in Wk1; 5/5/40 in Wk2), 92.9% catch rate, ~60% snaps, Ben Johnson's trusted receiver from Detroit, quick-game role that NBC likens to the old D.J. Moore role. He is the closest thing to a Landry-type comfort target on this roster.
2. **D'Andre Swift (second).** Clear receiving-back role: 5/5/54 in Week 2 (team-high receiving yards), plus 1/1/10 in Wk1. Fits the Chris Thompson archetype if Johnson scripts checkdowns for a rusty 38-year-old QB.
3. **Cole Kmet (speculative).** Every-down snaps (60/61) and Johnson publicly promised more TE involvement — but the targets haven't followed (4 total through two weeks). Narrative only, not role evidence.
4. **Rome Odunze / Luther Burden (weak for Keenum).** Odunze is the downfield/mossed-defender threat on limited snaps; Burden's Week 2 targets were garbage-time production on the final drive. Neither fits a first-start safety-blanket profile.

**Uncertainty (explicit):** only two games of 2026 Bears data; the offense is run-heavy (291 rushing yards in Wk1); Keenum has never taken a snap in Johnson's scheme; Philadelphia's secondary is the strongest unit he'll have faced in this offense's short history — which itself argues for the quick game (Raymond, Swift) over downfield shots. No first-read share data is publicly available for any Bear.

**Week 3 role changes to monitor:** Monangai's practice status (Thu report), Williams/Bagent official designations, and whether Johnson's promised TE involvement materializes.
