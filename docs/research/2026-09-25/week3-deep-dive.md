## 7. MNF: Bears + Keenum

**Situation:** Keenum (38) took first-team reps Wed 9/23 and is expected to start vs PHI. He hasn't attempted a regular-season pass since December 2023 (2 starts: 34/53, 291 yds, 1 TD, 3 INT). Projections disagree sharply: FantasyPros 200.3 yds / 1.0 TD; PFF 240.8 yds / 1.2 TD. Don't split the difference — treat both as "nobody knows."

**The "Keenum guy" (from the nflverse study, 2,270 targets):** Keenum shows zero positional bias (career WR 59.6% / TE 20.4% / RB 20.0% — dead even with league). His safety blanket is a *person*, not a position: Landry (slot), Chris Thompson (receiving back), Noah Brown. The archetype is a **trusted short-area target**.

Applied to two games of Bears data:
1. **Kalif Raymond $3,800 (strongest evidence).** Led Bears WRs in production both weeks (8/84 Wk1, 5/40 Wk2), 13/14 catches (92.9%), ~60% snaps, Johnson's trusted guy from Detroit, quick-game role NBC likens to the old D.J. Moore role. Prop: 24.5 yards / 3.5 receptions. The Landry archetype.
2. **D'Andre Swift $6,200 (second).** 5/5/54 in Week 2 — team-high receiving yards. The Chris Thompson archetype if Johnson scripts checkdowns for a rusty QB. (Monangai $5,200 [Q] missed Wed practice — Swift's role is secure.)
3. **Cole Kmet $3,000 (speculative).** Every-down snaps (60/61), and Johnson publicly promised more TE involvement — but 4 targets in two weeks is narrative, not role. Punt only.
4. **Odunze $5,300 / Burden $5,600 (weak for Keenum).** Odunze is the downfield threat on limited snaps; Burden's Week 2 targets were garbage-time. Neither fits a first-start blanket profile.

**PHI side:** Hurts $6,800 / Barkley $7,000 / D. Smith $7,100 / Wicks $4,100 (2.8% main-slate proxy — GPP dart) / Ertz $3,100 (Goedert out, expected elevation). CHI +4.5, Hochuli under lean, calm weather.
