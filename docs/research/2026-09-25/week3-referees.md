## DFS synthesis — crews that matter most

1. **Alan Eck (TEN@NYG):** fewest flags in football (significant). Fewer free first downs; cleaner, less penalty-extended game. Pairs with rain — game-flow risk, not a player call.
2. **Clay Martin (CIN@PIT):** offensive-holding crew. Bad for OL-dependent run games (PIT), mildly good for pass-catchers on checkdowns and for CIN D/ST sack/holding upside.
3. **Adrian Hill (LV@NO):** false-start crew + strongest under lean. Drive-killer penalties; downgrade long-drive touchdown equity slightly.
4. **John Hussey (BAL@DAL Rio):** 61% home ATS — Dallas +3 gets the best home-crew edge on the slate (neutral-site caveat: no real crowd).
5. **Bill Vinovich (LAR@DEN):** 62% away ATS — strongest road-crew lean; conflicts with LAR as road favorite (favorites only 41%).
6. **Alex Moore (KC@MIA):** no data — first-year lead ref. No edge either way.
