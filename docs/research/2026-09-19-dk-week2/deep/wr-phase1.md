## CRITICAL SLATE-SCOPE FLAG (for parent/synthesis)
DK Network's Week 2 WR rankings carry this footnote (dknetwork.draftkings.com, 9/15/26):
"*Salary from a non-Sunday DraftKings slate. Thursday, Sunday Night and Monday Night players are on
separate contest slates with different salary pools than the Sunday Main.*"
Asterisked players (Rashee Rice $6,400, Xavier Worthy $4,500, Hollywood Brown KC $3,200, Tyquan Thornton
$3,400, Josh Downs $5,400, Alec Pierce $5,600, Keenan Allen $4,600 — all KC/IND) are on the **Sunday Night
Showdown slate, NOT the Sunday Main slate**, with a different salary pool. The lane briefing lists IND@KC
as game 14 of a "14-game main slate," but DraftKings' actual Sunday Main contest pool excludes SNF.
**KC/IND WRs should be treated as OFF the main slate** (or flagged as SNF-only). Their salaries below are
Showdown-slate salaries, not Main-slate salaries.
