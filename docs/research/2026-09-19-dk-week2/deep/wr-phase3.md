## 3. Adversarial challenges — verdicts

### (a) Parker Washington's one-game similarity comps (2023 Tyreek / 2025 Puka / 2025 JSN)
CHALLENGE: One game; 62.5% routes is a real red flag; comps are descriptive, not predictive.
SECOND SOURCES: fantasydata.com 9/18 — "Only Parker Washington earned more than three targets" on
JAX in Wk1. Market: Athlon 9/17 ranks him WR22; FanDuel 9/17 WR15; RotoBaller 9/17 WR14; SI 9/17 WR21.
The entire market independently converged on WR2 territory for him.
VERDICT: **HOLD as a top-8 DK WR play.** The route% is the known risk (game script at DEN could
squeeze him), but four independent rankers plus the target-share data corroborate. Size the risk via
the salary ($5,900), not by fading.

### (b) Week 1 CB passer-rating-allowed "targets" (Stevenson +158.3, Lassiter +153.3, Sainristil +149.3, Ward +147.9, DeJean +143.2)
CHALLENGE: One-game samples with massive opponent-quality confounds (e.g., Stevenson faced a MIN
offense on its backup QB; DeJean faced TEN's backups). CB passer rating allowed is also scheme- and
safety-help-dependent.
VERDICT: **DOWNGRADE from "matchup edge" to "tiebreak only."** Do not roster a WR *because* of these
numbers. Keep only as a nudge between two otherwise-equal options (e.g., Higgins vs. a peer).

### (c) Watson/Golden target concentration via Love's 78.6% first-read rate
CHALLENGE: One-game QB stat; GB could regress to a spread attack; Watson's 32.7-DK-pt Wk1 could be
the ceiling game.
SECOND SOURCES: Athlon 9/17 WR13, SI 9/17 WR10, RotoBaller 9/17 WR18, FanDuel 9/17 WR25 for Watson;
SI 9/18 calls Golden "the exact archetype of value receiver cash-game players should target";
Fitzpatrick OUT removes NYJ's deep safety.
VERDICT: **HOLD — Watson stays the #1 value on the slate; Golden stays top-10.** The first-read
concentration is corroborated by the actual target distribution (Watson 8, Golden 12, Reed 7 in Wk1).
Fitzpatrick's absence is a fresh bullish increment neither Phase 1 nor 2 had.

### (d) Kayshon Boutte as the HOU beneficiary without Collins
CHALLENGE: Beat-writer buzz (Aaron Wilson) vs. hard data: the trio combined for 6 receptions in Wk1
(fantasynerds 9/17); DraftSharks 9/18: "wouldn't trust any of them in fantasy lineups"; NBC Sports
9/18: Schultz benefits most (TE1 ranks vs inside-funnel Bengals).
VERDICT: **DOWNGRADE to GPP-only dart.** Remove from the core cash-value list. The committee risk is
real and a second credible source (NBC) points the targets at TE instead. Keep Boutte/Hutchinson as
tournament darts at $4,000/$3,500 only.

### (e) JSN under Drew Lock
CHALLENGE: Backup-QB downgrade is real; Lock's career efficiency is poor.
SECOND SOURCES: Lock's 64.0% first-read rate (corpus) is top-5 — concentrated QBs feed alphas;
JSN's 3.79 YPRR leads the slate's YPRR list (scottbarrett csv); The Huddle 9/19 names JSN a top-3
"Weekly strategy" WR1 option alongside Jefferson and Lamb.
VERDICT: **HOLD as the top premium play.** The first-read concentration specifically insulates
alpha-WR1s from backup-QB downgrades. $8,100 is fair, not a discount.

### (f) Coker over McMillan
CHALLENGE: The market disagrees — Athlon 9/17: McMillan WR10, Coker unranked; SI 9/17: McMillan WR18,
Coker WR25; RotoBaller: McMillan WR27, Coker WR29. McMillan has Round-1 draft capital; Coker is the
undrafted second-year man.
REBUTTAL: Fitzmaurice 9/18 — Coker has more catches/yards/TDs than McMillan over the last 9 games
(43/600/6 since Wk11 2025); Coker's Wk1 was 8-138-2; Coker is now designation-free while ATL's slot
CB is OUT; $1,000 cheaper ($5,100 vs $6,100).
VERDICT: **HOLD as the leverage pivot precisely because the market prefers McMillan.** In large
fields, Coker carries lower ownership with a comparable-or-better median. In cash, McMillan's
safety is defensible — note the split.

### (g) Olave at $7,200 while Q (hamstring)
CHALLENGE: Hamstring re-injury risk; multi-source optimism could be gamesmanship; BAL's Humphrey/
Wiggins are a real coverage test.
VERDICT: **HOLD with the Vele hedge.** 83% play probability (FantasyPros model 9/19), "right side of
questionable" (RotoBaller), Wk1 yardage leader. If inactive, Vele $4,200 is the direct pivot
(second-sourced by The Huddle 9/19).
