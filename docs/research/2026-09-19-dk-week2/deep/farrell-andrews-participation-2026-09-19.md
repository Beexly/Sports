### Role diagnosis: GAME-SCRIPT NOISE, not an emerging role

1. **Snap share is a blocking artifact.** 45–46 snaps produced only 16–19 routes (44% route rate) and 2 targets. Kittle out-targeted him 5-to-2 on 16 fewer snaps, with 3x the TPRR (38.5% vs 12.5%). Snaps came from run-heavy personnel: SF ran 21-personnel 42%, 12-personnel 19%, 22-personnel 9% with only 36 dropbacks (56%) across 64 plays in a 27–7 blowout (RotoWire, 2026-09-15).
2. **Career archetype is "pseudo-sixth offensive lineman."** Signed March 2025: 3yr/$20.25M ($11M guaranteed) specifically as a blocking TE2 (Niners Nation, 2025-03-10). 2024 JAX: 238 run-blocking snaps vs 103 routes; 363 inline vs 16 slot. Never topped 160 receiving yards in a season before 2026 (TWSN, 2026-09-15). The 90% special-teams share confirms the profile.
3. **The role shrinks, not grows, in Week 2.** Kittle (8 months post-Achilles tear of 2026-01-11 vs PHI; off PUP August 2026 — TWSN/NBC, 2026-09-15/16) is ramping, not fading; his schemed Week 1 usage says the staff still designs for him. Jake Tonges's absence (IR 2026-09-16; **out for the season**, MCL surgery — Field Level Media, 2026-09-18) protects Farrell's *snap* floor but Kittle absorbs the *routes*. Behind them only practice-squad TEs remain (Brayden Willis; Jack Stoll signed to PS — FLM, 2026-09-18; grabsomepine, 2026-09-14).

His 2 targets are scheme-dictated checkdowns (0.9 aDOT) independent of QB; and SF is a **-12.5 home favorite** vs MIA (total 46.5 — Fox Sports, 2026-09-14), so a backup-QB blowout script likely leans *harder* into the run game that inflates Farrell's blocking snaps without adding routes. The QB change helps Kittle (security-blanket schemed looks), not Farrell.

Tonges's season-ending injury secures Farrell's snaps, not his routes. Not a DFS consideration at any plausible salary.

### 2025 baseline — is 24–25% target share an outlier?
**Yes as a number, no as a direction.** 2025: 48 rec on 65–70 targets, 422 yds, 5 TD across all 17 games; career-low 8.8 YPR; TE23 finish; 17.2% target share (16th among TEs, PlayerProfiler via Athlon 2026-09-14) vs 15.3% in 2024 vs 1st/1st/4th in 2021–23; PFF 64.4 overall (26th/37 TEs), 61.6 receiving (31st), 3 drops, 8.0 aDOT, 1.3 Y/RR (PFF; PFN, Aug 2026). The preseason 2026 bull case (PFN, 2026-08) was purely situational: **Likely and Charlie Kolar gone → Andrews should beat 2025's ~63% snap share**, and "behind Zay Flowers, Baltimore does not have another dependable pass catcher." Week 1 delivered the snap/route boost (70–72% / 68% — RotoWire: "only tiny boosts compared to last year") while the 25% share was juiced by Flowers's early exit and Lane's injury. Camp reports were negative on separation and drops (PFN, Aug 2026 — label: preseason), which aligns with the Week 1 drop data rather than contradicting the role.

### Target-competition map (BAL) — corrected
- **Isaiah Likely: NOT ON THE ROSTER.** Giants, 3yr/$40M, March 2026 (Giants Wire, 2026-09-17; Russell Street Report, 2026-09-14). Zero Ravens target competition from him — the "pivot to Likely" thesis in the task brief and in-repo deep dive is void.
- **In-team TE competition: minimal.** Durham Smythe: 40–42 snaps (62%) but only 10 routes and 1 target — a run-game TE who steals snaps, not routes (RotoWire; RavensWire, 2026-09-14). Rookie Matthew Hibner: 21–23 snaps, 5 routes, 1 target. Josh Cuevas: ~5–6 snaps. Andrews ran **2.1x Smythe's routes**. Andrews is the undisputed receiving TE (PFN preseason TE depth note; FPTrack, 2026-09-13: "remains the starter atop Baltimore's tight end depth chart").
- **WR room (verified current):** Zay Flowers **DOUBTFUL** (left hamstring, aggravated Week 1; DNP Wed/Thu, limited Fri; "not expected to be available" — Jeff Zrebiec via RavensWire, 2026-09-18; ESPN's Jamison Hensley, 2026-09-19). Ja'Kobi Lane to **IR** (fractured wrist surgery, out 4+ weeks — Heavy.com, 2026-09-18). Devontez Walker (groin) practiced fully, good to go (FantasyPros, 2026-09-18). Available: Rashod Bateman (51 snaps/80%, every-down WR — RotoWire), promoted Chris Moore, LaJohntay Wester, Walker, rookie Elijah Sarratt (possible debut) (RavensWire, 2026-09-19). Hensley's stat that matters: **over the last 18 games Flowers has 91 catches for 1,361 yards; all other Ravens WRs combined: 55 for 814** (ESPN, 2026-09-19). The condensed pie genuinely flows through Bateman + Andrews + Henry. Head coach Jesse Minter (Friday): no scheme change, confidence in the room's preparation (RavensWire, 2026-09-19).

### Game script: NO @ BAL
- **BAL -8.5, total 46.5–47.5** (SI via DK Sportsbook, 2026-09-16; Fox Sports, 2026-09-14). Russell Street Report (2026-09-17): the 46.5 total "strongly indicates oddsmakers anticipate a **grueling, run-heavy grind** rather than an explosive shootout."
- Week 1 template: 64 plays, **31 dropbacks (48%)**, -2.5% PROE; Henry 24-144-3; personnel 11p 42% / 12p 31% / 13p 11% / 6OL 11% (RotoWire). Saints just surrendered **156 rush yards to Jahmyr Gibbs** (USA Today, 2026-09-16) — BAL's path of least resistance is Henry, which caps pass volume (~25 attempts). **This is the main ceiling risk.**
- Counterweights: Tyler Shough threw for 325+ in 2H/OT vs DET (USA Today, 2026-09-16) — if NO scores, BAL must throw; and Andrews's 79%/64%/75% personnel splits keep him on the field in every grouping regardless.

### VERDICT: PLAY (and there is no Likely pivot — he is a Giant)
1. **Elite-for-position Week 1 participation:** tied for the team lead in targets (6), 25% share, ~29% TPRR, 68% route share, 70%+ snaps, +1.7 receiving EPA — the receiving TE in all personnel groupings (79%/64%/75%) with no in-room competition (Smythe: 10 routes).
2. **The pie condenses in his favor:** Flowers doubtful + Lane on IR leaves Bateman, Andrews, and Henry as the only proven touches; RotoWire ("Stock ⬆: Mark Andrews") and RotoBaller's contingency stack both explicitly boost him on Flowers-out.
3. **The "Likely threat" does not exist.** The real risks are the -8.5 home-favorite run-heavy script (Henry) and the hands (20% drop rate; 2nd-and-goal drop; August camp drop concerns — PFN). At **$4,400** [2P] as DK's TE22 by projection (7.8 proj PPR), the discount more than prices the warts. Andrews is a legitimate value TE play, not a fade.
