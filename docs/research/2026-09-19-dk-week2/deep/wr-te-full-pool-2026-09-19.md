## Method

- **Qualifying filter:** Week 1 route participation **>50% OR target share >15%** (strict; exactly 50% routes or exactly 15% share does not qualify on that metric).
- **Week 1 usage source:** RotoWire "NFL Box Score Breakdown Week 1" (article updated **2026-09-15**; observed **2026-09-19**). RotoWire states its tables use NFL Next Gen data and include routes, route share, targets, target share, TPRR, aDOT, air yards, and air-yard share. YPRR is calculated as receiving yards ÷ routes run. Where RotoWire commentary conflicts with its table, the table is used.
- **Qualifying pool:** 101 players across 30 teams after applying the strict filter and the explicit exclusions below. Percentiles are computed against **only this 101-player qualifying pool** using mean-rank scoring: (number below + 0.5 × number tied) ÷ valid observations × 100. aDOT and air-yard share use valid-observation denominators (Calvin Ridley has neither). These are qualifying-pool percentiles — not league-wide percentiles and not PFF percentiles.
- **Flags:** `[ELITE]` = 90th percentile or higher of the qualifying pool. `[WEAK]` = 25th percentile or lower of the qualifying pool. Thresholds are documented here and applied uniformly.
- **Salaries:** allowed sources only — The Huddle (published **2026-09-19**) and DK Network (dated **2026-09-15**, primetime). Every surfaced salary carries `[2P]`. Where neither source supplies a salary the exact phrase `not publicly surfaced from allowed sources` is used.
- **Ancillary stats** (slot rate, first-read share, contested catches/drops, red-zone/inside-10 targets, man/zone splits) appear only where a dated public source exists; otherwise the exact phrase `not publicly surfaced` is used.
- Explicit player exclusions applied before writing: Puka Nacua, Davante Adams, Garrett Wilson, Xavier Hutchinson, Jaylin Noel, Dalton Schultz, Luther Burden III, Rome Odunze, Cole Kmet, Parker Washington, Brian Thomas Jr., Rashod Bateman, Michael Mayer. No sections exist for excluded players.
- OUT and Doubtful qualifiers receive complete sections but are not framed as playable.
