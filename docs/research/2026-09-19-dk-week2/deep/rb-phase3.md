## 1. THE ETIENNE THESIS IS DEAD — Alvin Kamara returns, three-back committee risk

**Prior thesis (Phase 1/2):** Etienne $6,000, dknetwork proj 15.1, 8.4 projected targets (highest pass-catching projection among mid-tier), 71% BELLCOW XFP share, second-best points/$1K value tier (2.52), "clean pass-catching value."

**New facts (Friday 2026-09-18):**
- Kamara practiced FULL all week (Wed–Fri) with no injury designation and WILL play Week 2, his first game of the season (sprained MCL, Aug 18). Saints Wire 9/18: "We'll see how his usage changes with Travis Etienne Jr. on top of the depth chart." Heavy.com 9/18: "expected to make his 2026 debut." SI 9/17: Kamara activated off IR.
- NO backfield context: Week 1 usage was Etienne 13 touches/88 scrimmage yds/TD with Kendre Miller mixing in for 7 touches/40 yds (Jacksonville.com 9/18). Kamara now re-enters the NO backfield on top of that rotation.

**Adversarial verdict:** The 8.4-target projection and 20.7-touch workload projection were made BEFORE Kamara was confirmed active (dknetwork 9/17 table; projections dated pre-Friday). Those numbers assumed a two-back split at best. With three viable backs and Kamara's historical receiving role, Etienne's target share is at real risk of dropping to the 4–6 range and touches to 12–15. Etienne goes from **mid-tier value lock → volatile usage risk**. He stays playable in GPPs (Saints run-heavy identity, BAL allows work) but is no longer a "clean" anything. Downgrade ~30-40% from Phase 1 projection confidence.

**Corollary — Kamara as the pivot:** Kamara at $4,600, dknetwork proj 8.3, is the new speculative lever in the NO backfield. If he reclaims the receiving role immediately, he outscores his price by 2x. But first-game-back usage after a month out with an MCL sprain is genuinely unknown — Saints Wire literally wrote "we'll see." Treat as tournament-only speculation, not a cash value.

**Second source:** Heavy.com (9/18) confirms full participation + expected debut; SI (9/17) confirms activation. Kamara's readiness is triple-sourced. Usage split is the unknown, not readiness.

## 3. CMC: THE WORKLOAD THESIS WEAKENS — but Black's groin is the swing factor

**Prior thesis (Phase 2):** CMC 66% BELLCOW XFP — lowest among pay-ups; Black led SF in Week 1 carries; CMC is leverage, not lock.

**New facts:**
- RotoWire Yahoo DFS (9/18): "McCaffrey handled a light workload by his standards with just 15 touches in Australia against the Rams last Thursday... dealt with cramps... 49ers nursed a comfortable lead throughout the second half, which contributed to rookie third-round pick Kaelon Black matching McCaffrey's touch total."
- 49ers.com final report (9/18): **Black limited Thursday and Friday, QUESTIONABLE with groin.** Shanahan called the limitation "precautionary" but gave only "a questionable amount of optimism" (NBC Bay Area 9/18). CMC: full Thursday/Friday after a Wednesday rest day — healthy.
- Black is still rostered as questionable, not out.

**Adversarial test of the fade:** The Week 1 committee look had extenuating circumstances (cramps + blowout + Thursday game in Australia = short week travel). If Black sits, CMC's projected 20.7 touches could jump to 23-25 against Miami, and the $8,000 price with 20.2 proj becomes a smash in a game SF should control. If Black plays, the 66% share concern stands.

**Verdict:** CMC moves from "leverage fade" to **genuinely binary on Black's status**. This is the highest-leverage injury news on the slate for the pay-up tier. Inactives decide it. Note the touch-count discrepancy in sources (15 per RotoWire Yahoo vs 21 per FantasyPros) — see §10.

## 4. IRVING: CONFIRMED ROLE, NEW RISK — Sean Tucker is active and the Y1 sample is thin

**For the bullish thesis:** RotoWire Yahoo DFS (9/18) confirms "Fears of Irving ceding significant work to offseason signing Kenny Gainwell appear to have been overblown... 8 carries and 7 catches for 93 total yards and a TD in Week 1... averaged a robust 5.6 yards per carry... game script likely to flip in Tampa Bay's favor in the home opener against a hapless Browns team." @ffdataroma (9/18): 80%+ of TB XFP, 15-to-6 touch edge. @statyxio (9/18): 62.5% interior run path vs CLE ranked 31st/32 defending it; 0.63 evaded tackles/attempt (99th %ile); 62.5% rush success (99th %ile).

**Adversarial attacks:**
1. **Sean Tucker practiced FULL all week with no designation** (Sharp 9/18) and preseason reporting described a potential short-yardage/goal-line role. A healthy Tucker can steal exactly the TD equity Irving's price needs.
2. **The @statyxio percentiles are built on EIGHT mapped carries.** 99th-percentile anything on n=8 is noise with a costume on.
3. **Week 1 stat conflict:** dknetwork recap (9/17) says 15 carries/81 yds/3 rec vs BUF; RotoWire Yahoo (9/18) says 8 carries/7 catches/93 yds/TD in a "loss to Cincinnati." These cannot both be the Week 1 game. One of them is mislabeled (possible preseason/2025 contamination or opponent error). Flagged in verify/rb-verify.md — do not build a game-script argument on the wrong baseline.
4. Game script: TB is a home favorite; if they lead big, Gainwell/Tucker close. Irving's pass-catching role (21 routes, .33 TPRR per @MagicSportsGuy 9/18) protects him, but the ceiling needs the TD.

**Verdict:** Irving stays a core mid-tier play but is not the "free square" the efficiency table suggests. The Tucker goal-line risk is the specific thing to watch in inactives/snap splits.

## 5. SWIFT: TOP OF THE VALUE BOARD, BUT THE REGRESSION FLAG IS REAL

**For:** dknetwork proj 22.0 (highest projection under $7K), 3.49 pts/$1K — best on slate. Full Friday practice, no designation (NBC Sports 9/18). PFN RB rank 13, USA Today flex 10.

**Adversarial attacks:**
1. Chicago overperformed expected rushing YPA by +2.0 in Week 1 (Paganetti internal table, 9/18) — the single largest positive rushing regression signal. Swift's 35.4 dknetwork Week 1 score (32.4 per FantasyLabs — source conflict, use 32–35 range) included 3 TDs; TD rate will not repeat weekly.
2. Kyle Monangai is fully healthy too and $5,300 — the rookie keeps a small role; any Swift in-game issue and the split shifts.
3. MIN run D: the Vikings just lost Jordan Mason (IR) and start Aaron Jones — but MIN's defensive front is unchanged.

**Verdict:** Swift stays the best points-per-dollar projection on the slate, but the right mental model is "22-point median with a fat tail," not "35-point repeat." He's a cash-core piece, not a GPP differentiator — everyone sees the same number.

**Verdict:** Stays a Phase 2 upgrade — core cash play, GPP-fade candidate only if ownership goes nuclear. No injury or role news contradicts the workload.

## 7. HENRY: THE CLEANEST PAY-UP — stress-test the blowout risk

**For:** 38.3 Week 1 DK pts, 144 rush yds/3 TD (RavensWire/Jacksonville.com 9/18), 80% XFP, $7,200, 24.0 proj, 3.33 pts/$1K — second-best value on slate. Ravens 41-23 over IND. NO Saints coming off... wait — BAL hosts NO in Week 2 (FantasyPros cheat sheet: BAL vs NO core play Derrick Henry).

**Adversarial attacks:**
1. **Blowout script:** BAL is a heavy home favorite vs a Kamara-debut Saints team. Henry's Week 1 line came in a 41-23 win. If BAL leads 28-3 at half again, Henry sits the 4th quarter and 24.0 proj becomes 16 on 14 carries.
2. **Keaton Mitchell factor:** Mitchell's 69-yard TD run (ssbcrack 9/18) shows a live big-play back who eats garbage-time carries.
3. Henry's 3-TD Week 1 will not repeat; the projection already knows this (24.0, not 38).

**Verdict:** The projection is fair and the role is clean. The risk is game script, not workload — which is the same risk every favorite's RB carries. Stays top pay-up. Pair with the reality that BAL team total is the slate's engine.

## 8. BIJAN: THE EXPENSIVE CONSENSUS — find the hole

**For:** $8,200, 25.2 proj (highest), 84% XFP, 8.0 projected targets, home vs CAR, FantasyPros CORE play, RotoWire cash list, PFN RB2, Athlon flex 2, USA Today flex 2.

**Adversarial attacks:**
1. **He is the consensus RB1 and priced like it.** 25.2 proj at $8,200 = 3.07 pts/$1K — good, not slate-breaking. You're paying full freight for a number everyone has.
2. **Brian Robinson is in ATL now** ($5,000-ish DK per Huddle table — verify). If BRob has a real early-down role, Bijan's 84% XFP share is a Week 1 number that can decay.
3. CAR is bad, which invites the blowout script (Allgeier... wait, Allgeier tagged ARI now; Tyler Allgeier $4,900 in Huddle table) — ATL's backup situation is the garbage-time risk.

**Verdict:** No hole in the role; the hole is in the price. He's the "pay up and be right with everyone" play. True leverage vs Bijan is Henry (better pts/$1K, similar ceiling) or the $6K tier.
