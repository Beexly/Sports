### Reading the penalty-rate column for DFS

- **Fewer flags (Eck 10.9, Allen 11.1, Novak 11.1)** → fewer free first downs via DPI/holding; drives must be earned. Marginal drag on total plays.
- **More flags (Blakeman 13.2, Rogers 13.1, Hochuli 13.0, Hill 13.0)** → more drive extension, especially Hill (No. 1 in DPI/roughing auto-first-downs since 2021) and Blakeman (top-5 flag volume + yardage).
- **Pre-snap heavy (Hussey 74% W1; Hill No. 1 in false starts)** → drive-killers that offset the extension effect; consistent with both crews' career under leans.

## Bottom line — officiating boost vs. drag on pace/total plays

1. **Biggest pace/total boost: MIA @ SF (Shawn Smith).** Smith owns the dataset's largest over-lean (+1.7 pts vs closing total, 108 games), and the game already has the slate's highest total environment (SF -13.5, 16.0 implied against Miami). Caveat: RotoWire explicitly flags totals splits as the least trustworthy referee metric — none clear significance.
2. **Second boost: CLE @ TB (Brad Rogers).** Career 58-50 to the over with a +1.3 pts lean and a high flag rate (13.1/gm) that extends drives. Fits the game script of Tampa passing volume against Cleveland's league-worst pass offense playing catch-up.
3. **Biggest drag: CIN @ HOU (Adrian Hill).** The single strongest under-lean of any Week 2 crew (62-50-1 career under, -1.0 pt vs total). Hill's flag profile cuts both ways — most offensive holding in 2025 and most false starts in Week 1 (drive killers) against the most DPI/roughing first downs since 2021 (drive extenders) — but the net across 113 games is fewer points than the market expects.
4. **MNF-specific drag: NYG @ LAR (Brad Allen).** Allen is 83-60-1 to the under since 2016, MNF itself goes over only ~43% of the time historically, and big MNF favorites are 40% ATS since 2012. The one counter-tension: the Giants committed the most DPI in Week 1 and the Rams draw it efficiently, so penalty yardage could still pad receiver/return-man production even in a lower-scoring game.

**Caveats:** All crew totals/ATS splits except Eck's penalty rate fail RotoWire's simultaneous-significance testing — treat them as suggestive priors, not edges. Alex Moore (CAR @ ATL) has no usable history. 2026 Week 1 flag counts are single-game samples (e.g., Allen's 22-flag game is flagged by Sharp as due for sharp regression).
