### IND@KC stack and leverage analysis

- **Mahomes ($5,500, 23.9 proj) is the slate's standout QB value on paper** — but the Week 1 film says his ceiling is currently Walker-capped: 27 pass attempts, zero completions beyond 6 air yards, and a run-first script that produced 95th-percentile rushing efficiency. A Mahomes stack bets on game script forcing KC to throw (Colts hanging around) or on positive TD regression (Grueling Truth's top props: Mahomes over 1.5 pass TDs). DERIVED: in a 75-entry winner-take-all, Mahomes will carry the highest QB ownership of the two primetime games.
- **Stack partners, in order of evidence:** Kelce (concentrated 5 targets, 71 yards, red-zone role intact) > Worthy ($4,500, co-team-high 6 targets, off the injury report, cheapest path to Mahomes passing production) > Rice ($6,400, better real-life player than Week 1 fantasy role; 7% TPRR is the buy-low/avoid fulcrum — 4for4 expects it to rise). Walker is not a correlated Mahomes stack piece; he is the **game-script alternative**: if KC leads throughout, Walker (23/173/1 baseline, "over 84.5 rushing yards" is Grueling Truth's top prop of the game) outscores the passing game.
- **Bring-backs:** Taylor ($7,500 — elite usage: 89% snaps, 19 carries, goal-line role; the single best Colt and viable as an uncorrelated one-off) > Downs ($5,400 — route leader at 89% snaps/91% of 2-TE sets with only 4–6 targets; the positive-regression bring-back) > Pierce ($5,600 — 4/61 on a Week 1 snap count, now practicing in full; deep threat vs. a KC secondary missing Conner and possibly Delane) > Allen ($4,600 — 6/6 safety valve) > Warren (93% snaps and the TD, but TE salary unknown; FanDuel Research ranks him the **#1 TE of the entire week**).
- **Defensive angle:** Jones was pressured on 53% of dropbacks (week-high) behind an offense that ranked dead-last in passing success; KC's defense just smothered Denver. KC DST was B/R's #4 weekly DST (9/16). DERIVED: KC DST is a legitimate tournament DST in this contest, likely low-owned relative to Sunday options.
- **Game-total signal:** the total falling from 48.5 to 46.5 with two Under-leaning sharp columns (RotoWire, Grueling Truth) argues this is more likely a **game-script/under game than a shootout** — stack KC passing with awareness that the most likely KC blowout path runs through Walker, not Mahomes' arm.
