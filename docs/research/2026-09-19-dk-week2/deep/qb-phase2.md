## What Phase 1 missed or under-leveraged
1. **Trevor Lawrence is the slate's best efficiency+leverage combo and P1 buried him in Tier 2.** +0.79 EPA/play (GridironInfo_ W1 leader), 75.0% comp / 0 INT / 4.2% sacks (dropback-outcome CSV), ANY/A 13.1 (#1, Patton), composite QB #2 (98.5) with CPOE +16.2 (#2) and 11.2 air yds/rec (#1). 13% deep rate with 39.1% intermediate. DEN generates 39.4% pressure but Lawrence's pressure EPA/db was +0.81 (Tej) — he punished pressure in W1. DEN is 4th vs QBs (DKN) and altitude is real, but the internal numbers say the field will underweight him behind Caleb/Purdy/Dak chalk.
2. **Geno Smith's efficiency is completely disconnected from his 9.3 DK-point W1.** 79.2% comp, 0.0% sacks, 0.0% INT, composite #4 (98.0), 0.0% bad-throw... vs GB at $4,800 with a ~20.5 implied total. If TDs normalize, he's the cheapest credible efficiency play. Risk: 26 dropbacks W1 = low volume.
3. **Baker Mayfield's accuracy counters the "checkdown" label.** Led the NFL in completion % (82.1%, dynatyze), CPOE +15.4 (2nd), 63.9% comp with 11.1% scramble. TB uses play action at a slate-high 25.5%. CLE's Ward allowed a 147.9 rating W1 and CLE's O-line allowed 50% pressure → positive script. The Huddle (9/19) already used him in a sample DK lineup, so he's not fully sneaky.
4. **C.J. Stroud's 21% aggressiveness + concentrated targets is a GPP lever, not just a "tournament option."** With Collins OUT and Higgins (ACL) done for the season, the air-yards profile (42.1% of throws in 0-5, but 34.2% in 11-20, 3rd-highest intermediate share) concentrates on Boutte/Hutchinson/Noel/Schultz. Schultz saw 8 targets W1 and CIN allowed 20.9 FPG to TEs in 2025 (BettorGreen 9/18). CIN's blitz is elite (-1.49 EPA/play allowed when blitzing, @GridironInfo_) — Stroud's 7.1% sack rate vs the blitz is the swing factor.
5. **Drew Lock > Wentz as the cheap-QB pivot few will play.** Lock: 72.7% comp, 0 INT, +0.28 EPA/db in relief; SEA -4/-4.5 favorites indoors; JSN's 45.8% target share (led NFL, SumerSports via briefing). Wentz will absorb the salary-relief ownership; Lock at $300 more is the leverage pivot in a cleaner environment (no weather).
6. **Purdy's first-read rate (27.0%, lowest) + MIA's 6.5% pressure generated** = the cleanest pocket on the slate for a distributor, and his 70.6% sub-5-air-yard rate means the ball comes out before SF's -13.5 script matters. The blowout risk is real, but the efficiency path (24.7 DKN proj) doesn't need 4 quarters.
7. **Hurts' rushing floor is matchup-proof but the total (38.5) caps ceiling.** +2.0 rush EPA/gm with 25% scramble and PHI's 13.7% RPO vs a TEN D that generated 11.5% pressure (lowest). If PHI leads, they run — Hurts' TD equity is via his legs, which survives blowouts better than pocket passers'.
8. **Mahomes' W1 was genuinely bad (CPOE -12.3, rank 31; 45.5% comp) — the "value" case is price-only.** P1's 3.45 pts/$1K uses FantasyPros' 19.0, which is 80%-anchored to 2025. If W1 signal is real (new brace? new backfield w/ Kenneth Walker III), 19.0 is high. The leverage angle: he's value chalk, not a secret.
9. **Dak vs Daniels is really a referendum on WAS's secondary.** WAS: Sainristil 149.3 rating allowed; gave up 3 TDs + three 30+ yard completions on 25 attempts to PHI (SI 9/18); 30th vs QBs (DKN). Both sides are playable; Daniels at "moderate" rostership (FantasyPros) is the GPP side, Dak the cash/chalk side. DAL allowed +0.48 motion-pass EPA (2nd worst) — Daniels' 14.7% RPO + 20.6% scramble attacks exactly that.
10. **Burrow's composite was bad (EPA/play 0.03, rank 19) and he's Q with a back.** Taylor says "good to go," but backs + full practice Friday is fine — the risk is in-game re-aggravation, which no projection captures. He's priced at $6,600 (5th-highest) on reputation. P1 correctly did not feature him; P2's add: he's a fade at salary, not a value.
11. **Maye's 27.5% scramble + PIT's 92.3% no-blitz + -0.85 motion-pass EPA allowed** = PIT sits back and contains; Maye's legs are the path, and his WR1 (A.J. Brown) is on IR. Rain 2nd half. Downgrade vs P1's neutral read.
12. **Willis's 13.6 aDOT (led NFL) + 3.21s time-to-throw vs SF's 31% pressure** = sacks or explosives. 5 sacks W1 already. He's a legs/garbage-time punt, not an efficiency play — DKN's 11.3 proj is fair.
