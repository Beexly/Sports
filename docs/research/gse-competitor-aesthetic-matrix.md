# GSE Competitor Aesthetic Matrix

Generated: 2026-06-09

This matrix translates the previously researched competitor universe into website and product-design patterns. It covers platform/watchlist competitors, media and betting intelligence competitors, and the 40+ NFL optimizer inventory.

Important limitation: this is a public-page R&D pass. Logged-in optimizer rooms, paid projections, private drafts, and state-gated app flows were not accessed.

## Pattern Map

## High-Signal Public Evidence

- FFPC exposes a prize-first contest lobby with many tournament cards, live/slow draft lists, entry tiers, app links, testimonials, and responsible-play links.
- DraftKings Pick6 exposes a pick-slip-first layout with league chips, live sport tabs, filters/search, game/player cards, and a hard login-to-submit gate.
- Rebet sells a social/mobile-first experience through free-to-play casino games, feed mechanics, challenges, rewards, simplified lines, reviews, and legal/sweepstakes framing.
- FantasyPros and Footballguys frame optimizer value through lock/exclude controls, exposure limits, custom projections, GPP optimization, stack controls, chart/report management, and export to DK/FD/Yahoo-style destinations.
- Action Network and OddsJam sell betting utility with live odds, bet tracking/syncing, alerting, +EV/arbitrage feeds, calculators, and education pages that lead into paid tools.

## Non-Duplication Rule

GSE should not copy competitor screenshots, colors, projections, rankings, ownership, line feeds, gated app flows, or premium data. The design target is a better intelligence cockpit: source health, fallback status, reasoning, and user authorization visible at every important decision point.
