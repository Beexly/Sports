# First 90 Days Media Plan

Updated: 2026-07-04

Status: operating plan only. No platform accounts, provider integrations, auto-posting, or sponsor claims are created by this document.

## First 30 Days

Prompt label: First 30 days.

Minimum weekly cadence:

- 2 long YouTube videos/week
- 10+ short-form clips/week
- 1 newsletter/week
- 10 partner outreaches/day
- 1 founder build-log/week
- 1 GSE Board Meeting/week
- build media kit page
- build partners page
- build newsletter page
- build content lab page

Daily workflow:

1. Review sports calendar and GSE board/model events.
2. Score content ideas using `scoreContentIdea`.
3. Draft one short and one long-form outline.
4. Run claim safety scan.
5. Queue partner outreach.
6. Log shipped content and blockers.

Content batch workflow:

1. Pick one flagship theme.
2. Draft long-form outline.
3. Create 5-8 short hooks.
4. Build SEO pack.
5. Run compliance review.
6. Manually publish only after approval.

Partner workflow:

1. Identify 10 companies/day.
2. Score fit.
3. Send tailored outreach.
4. Log response and compliance risk.
5. Do not add links until approved.

First week exact content:

- Long video 1: "Confidence Is Not Probability - Why Most Sports Prediction Sites Mislead You"
- Long video 2: "I am Building a Sports AI That Is Allowed to Say No"
- Shorts:
  1. "No bet is a position."
  2. "Why stale odds break trust."
  3. "The line moved. That does not mean edge."
  4. "Box score lied: targets are not role."
  5. "Most models fail because they cannot say I do not know."
  6. "What GSE refuses to do."
  7. "Why I am building this in public."
  8. "Market Mirage in 60 seconds."
  9. "Loss autopsy over fake win-rate screenshots."
  10. "The sports data business in one sentence."
- Newsletter: "The First GSE Board Meeting."
- Partner outreach: 10 creator/data/fantasy/tool companies.

Success in first 30 days:

- cadence established
- public pages live-safe
- first lead magnet drafts scoped
- repeatable claim review habit
- partner pipeline started

Avoid:

- fake traction
- automated posting
- copyrighted footage
- sportsbook offers without metadata
- performance claims without proof

## Days 31-60

Success means:

- a visible weekly rhythm
- first inbound partner conversations
- clearer top pillars by actual engagement
- no compliance regression

- package sponsor inventory
- build production queue

Success means:
