# Sponsorship Rate Card

Updated: 2026-07-04

Status: founding package definitions only. These are not proof of sold inventory, audience size, or sponsor performance.

## Packages

| Package | Price | Who it is for | Deliverables | Boundaries | Compliance requirements |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founding Supporter | $150/month | Local businesses, early supporters | Newsletter mention, static partner listing after approval, manual-review sponsor note | No editorial control | Disclosure near mention, no fake audience or ROI claims |
| GSE Builder Sponsor | $300/month | Creator tools, AI/dev tools, workflow products | GSE Lab sponsor mention, workflow note, partner page listing | No model or editorial influence | Disclosure, tool review honesty |
| Board Meeting Sponsor | $750/month | Newsletter, podcast-ready, sports data tools | Board Meeting sponsor slot, newsletter sponsor block, partner CTA | No control over what shipped/broke/was passed on | Disclosure and separated sponsor copy |
| Category Sponsor | $1,500/month | Category leaders, data/API, cloud/AI partners | Category presence, monthly deep dive, media kit inclusion, partner feature | Category fit does not imply exclusivity unless approved | Disclosure and claim approval |
| Affiliate-only | No fixed fee | Creator tools, fantasy tools, collectibles | Disclosed affiliate link placeholder after approval, tool review if warranted | No guaranteed placement | Disclosure, no fake endorsement |

## Sponsors Cannot Control

- picks
- model outputs
- no-bet decisions
- loss autopsies
- calibration claims
- editorial conclusions

## Rate Card Rules

- Do not claim audience size until real analytics exist.
- Do not claim sponsor ROI.
- Do not claim YouTube monetization status.
- Do not promise sportsbook/DFS placement without compliance metadata.
- Do not accept sponsor edits that alter evidence, model accountability, or conclusions.
