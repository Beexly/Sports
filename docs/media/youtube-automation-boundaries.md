## Purpose

YouTube is the highest-reach video platform and a critical long-term
distribution channel for Galaxy Sports Edge methodology content,
Galaxy Almanac companions, and brand awareness.

This document defines what Sports OS may and may not automate with
respect to YouTube — drawing on YouTube's Terms of Service, their
policies on automated and AI-generated content, and the Sports OS
brand safety doctrine.

This document also directly addresses the "Gemini YouTube Automation"
and "MoneyPrinterTurbo" patterns referenced in Prompt 4 as R&D references —
these patterns are analyzed here and declared FORBIDDEN for Sports OS use.

## Section 1 — YouTube's Policies Relevant to Sports OS

### Repetitive and Auto-Generated Content Policy

YouTube's policy prohibits:
- Channels where content is predominantly auto-generated by AI without
  meaningful human creative input
- Repetitive content produced by automating video creation
- Scraped and auto-assembled content from third-party sources
- Content that lacks meaningful original commentary or editorial voice

**Sports OS implication**: YouTube is a platform for original operator-created
content — methodology explainers, Galaxy Almanac companions, and investor demos.
It is NOT a platform for auto-generated sports content or AI-assembled pick videos.

### Spam, Deceptive Practices, and Scams Policy

YouTube prohibits:
- Content that offers financial gain in exchange for a fee, including
  sports betting tipster services presented as investment opportunities
- False claims about performance, accuracy, or guaranteed outcomes
- Deceptive thumbnails or titles

**Sports OS implication**: Pick performance claims in video titles, thumbnails,
or descriptions must satisfy full claim governance requirements. No "guaranteed
picks" or "lock of the week" framing in any YouTube content, ever.

### Copyright and Content ID

YouTube's Content ID system automatically identifies and claims revenue
from videos containing copyrighted music, sports footage, or broadcast clips.

**Sports OS implication**: See `docs/media/audio-voice-policy.md` for music
licensing rules. No sports broadcast footage may be used without a license.

### AI-Generated Content Disclosure

YouTube requires creators to disclose when content contains AI-generated
material that could be mistaken for real footage of people, places, or events.

**Sports OS implication**: Any use of AI-generated imagery or AI voice must
be disclosed. Sports OS goes beyond YouTube's minimum requirement — ALL
AI-generated content is disclosed, not just content that could be mistaken
for real.

## Section 2 — Gemini YouTube Automation (Reference Analysis)

The "Gemini YouTube Automation" pattern referenced in R&D materials is a
class of tool that uses Google's Gemini API to:
- Generate video scripts from prompts
- Create slide-based videos automatically
- Synthesize AI voiceover
- Auto-upload to YouTube via the YouTube Data API

**Why this pattern is FORBIDDEN for Sports OS**:

1. **Claim governance bypass**: A Gemini-generated sports pick script is not
   reviewed by the Sports OS claim governance scanner. It can and will produce
   forbidden language (locks, sharp money claims, win guarantees).

2. **Auto-upload violates Sports OS policy**: No content may auto-publish
   to any external platform without operator review. This is non-negotiable.

3. **YouTube ToS risk**: YouTube's policy on repetitive auto-generated content
   applies directly to AI-assembled video channels. A Sports OS channel using
   Gemini automation could be demonetized or suspended.

4. **Brand safety risk**: Gemini-generated content that claims pick performance
   data, uses sports footage, or implies guaranteed outcomes creates immediate
   brand and legal risk.

**Conclusion**: The Gemini YouTube automation pattern, and any equivalent
AI-auto-generate-and-upload pattern, is permanently FORBIDDEN for Sports OS.

## Section 3 — MoneyPrinter / MoneyPrinterTurbo (Reference Analysis)

The MoneyPrinter pattern is a broader AI video automation pipeline that:
- Generates a niche video topic
- Writes a script using AI
- Selects background footage (often scraped)
- Synthesizes AI voice
- Assembles the video
- Auto-uploads to YouTube

**Why this pattern is FORBIDDEN for Sports OS**:

1. **Copyright**: The footage selection step commonly uses scraped video from
   unlicensed sources. Sports OS has zero tolerance for copyright violation.

2. **Deceptive content**: This pattern produces high-volume, low-quality,
   often misleading content. Sports intelligence requires the opposite: low
   volume, high quality, transparent sourcing.

3. **YouTube ToS violation**: This is precisely the "auto-generated repetitive
   content" pattern YouTube prohibits.

4. **Claim integrity**: Auto-generated sports pick content is by definition
   not evidence-backed. It is Tier 6 fabrication. Sports OS builds on
   Tier 1/2 evidence. These are incompatible.

**Conclusion**: MoneyPrinter, MoneyPrinterTurbo, and all equivalent patterns
are permanently FORBIDDEN for Sports OS.

## Section 4 — What Sports OS May Automate on YouTube

The following automation is conditionally permitted:

| Automation | Status | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-generation of video titles from brief keywords | Permitted with review | Operator reviews and approves all titles before upload |
| Auto-generation of video descriptions from transcript | Permitted with review | Operator reviews and edits description before upload |
| Scheduled publishing of pre-approved videos | Permitted with approval | Owner approves scheduling capability; operator approves each video |
| Auto-generation of closed caption files from transcript | Permitted | No claim governance risk in transcription |
| Auto-thumbnail generation from still frames | Permitted with review | Operator selects the frame; no AI-generated imagery without disclosure |

**What requires operator upload (no automation)**:
- The video file itself must be uploaded by the operator
- No automated upload of video files via YouTube Data API without per-video operator approval

## Section 5 — YouTube Channel Configuration Rules

When a Galaxy Sports Edge YouTube channel is established:

**Channel description must include**:
- "For entertainment purposes only"
- A link to the `/methodology` page
- A clear statement about what the channel does (sports intelligence education,
  not betting advice)

**Channel tags must not include**:
- "picks", "locks", "guaranteed", "sure bets", or equivalent gambling-adjacent terms
- These terms trigger YouTube's gambling-adjacent content policies

**Videos are classified as**:
- "Not made for kids" — sports intelligence content is for adults
- "Contains paid promotion" — only if the video is sponsored (requires disclosure)
- "AI-generated content" — if the video contains AI-generated imagery or AI narration
