# Founder Media Strategy

Updated: 2026-07-04

Garrett/Beex should post alongside GSE because people trust people before they trust a new sports intelligence brand. Founder-led content should make the work legible, not make pressure the product.

## Purpose

- Build founder credibility.
- Show real work, shipped artifacts, blockers, and lessons.
- Make GSE more human without weakening the official evidence posture.
- Create partner and hiring surface area.
- Turn pressure into disciplined context, not desperation.

## How To Talk About Unemployment

Allowed:

- "I am unemployed, building GSE, and this is what actually moved today."
- "The pressure is real, so the proof has to be real."
- "I cannot afford a fake picks brand, so I am building the audit trail first."

Avoid:

- pity framing
- chaotic venting
- implying the audience owes support
- replacing proof with intensity
- posting private financial or family details

## Personal Channel vs GSE Official

| Topic | Founder personal | GSE official |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human pressure | Yes, disciplined | Rare |
| Product proof | Yes, show shipped artifact | Yes, polished and evidence-focused |
| Model claims | No unsupported claims | Only approved evidence language |
| Partner asks | Yes, direct and human | Yes, formal standards |
| Sponsor copy | Disclosed and reviewed | Disclosed and reviewed |
| Personal lessons | Yes | Only if tied to brand thesis |

## Example Founder Content

- "I am building a sports AI that is allowed to say no."
- "I am unemployed, building GSE, and this is what actually moved today."
- "The hardest part of a sports prediction company is not the model. It is proof."
- "Why I refuse to build a fake picks brand."

## Tone

Human, direct, disciplined, intelligent, not chaotic, not pity-driven.

## Weekly Founder Pattern

1. What shipped.
2. What broke.
3. What the repo now proves.
4. What remains blocked.
5. What partner or collaborator would help.

## Boundaries

- No secrets.
- No private dashboards unless safe.
- No paid-tool automation.
- No fake audience or sponsor claims.
- No performance claims without existing evidence.
