# Content Compliance Policy

Updated: 2026-07-04

This policy governs GSE, GSN, founder-led posts, sponsor mentions, affiliate mentions, newsletters, scripts, thumbnails, pages, and partner content.

## Blocked Claim Patterns

Explicit prompt checks:

- No "lock".
- No "guaranteed".
- No "risk free" or "risk-free".
- No copyrighted footage assumption.

Do not use:

- "lock"
- "guaranteed"
- "risk free" or "risk-free"
- "free money"
- "can't lose" or "cant lose"
- "100% winner"
- "sure thing"
- "easy money"
- "mortgage play"
- "max bet"
- "all in"
- "hammer this"
- "printing money"
- "can't miss" or "cant miss"

## Evidence-Required Patterns

These require evidence, sample window, source reference, and approval before public use:

- win rate
- ROI
- profit or profitable
- verified
- proven
- calibrated
- beats market
- closing line value or CLV
- positive expected value or +EV

If "calibrated probability" appears, the author must attach calibration-threshold evidence and public-claim approval.

## Sponsorship And Affiliate Rules

- Every affiliate or sponsor mention requires disclosure near the mention.
- YouTube paid promotion controls must be used for sponsored videos.
- Sponsor copy must be separated from editorial conclusions.
- Sponsors cannot control picks, model outputs, no-bet decisions, loss autopsies, calibration claims, or editorial conclusions.

## Betting Content Rules

- Betting content must be educational or analytical, not guaranteed-outcome content.
- Sportsbook/DFS offers require disclosure, terms URL, eligible states, restricted states, responsible-gaming text, and approved compliance status.
- No automated betting.
- No personalized financial advice.
- Responsible-play language is required where betting context is present.

## Rights Rules

- No copyrighted broadcast footage assumption.
- Use original graphics, app screenshots, diagrams, cleared assets, or commentary-first formats.
- No unlicensed player images, team marks, music, or broadcast audio.
- AI-generated imagery or AI narration requires disclosure and operator review.

## Review Workflow

1. Draft only.
2. Claim scan.
3. Source and evidence review.
4. Disclosure review.
5. Rights and visual review.
6. Operator approval.
7. Manual publish by a human.

No auto-publish path is approved.
