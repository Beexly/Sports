# Partnership Revenue Playbook

Updated: 2026-07-04

This playbook is a manual outreach and review framework. It does not create live affiliate links, sponsor contracts, or regulated offers.

## Partner Categories

Prompt label aliases:

- Sports data/API tools are the Sports data / API tools category.
- Sportsbook/DFS/betting partners are the Sportsbook / DFS / betting partners category.
- Cloud/AI/dev tools are the Cloud / AI / dev tools category.
- Sports cards/collectibles are the Sports cards / collectibles category.
- Local/regional sponsors are the Local / regional sponsors category.

| Category | Why it fits GSE | Content angle | Revenue model | Compliance risk | First outreach angle | Example offer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creator tools | Helps content workflow | "Tools used to turn one idea into ten assets" | affiliate, sponsor | Low to medium | workflow proof and honest review | disclosed tool review |
| Sports data/API tools | Core to GSE thesis | "Raw data vs intelligence" | sponsor, API beta, referral | Medium | source reliability and rights discipline | data workflow segment |
| Fantasy tools | Player Signal Lab audience | "Role signal beyond box score" | affiliate, sponsor | Medium | fantasy decision discipline | watchlist sponsor |
| Sports cards/collectibles | Sports audience overlap | "Data-informed collector education" | affiliate, sponsor | Medium | education and community | collectibles framework |
| Sportsbook/DFS/betting partners | Monetization fit but high-risk | responsible, compliance-gated education | affiliate, sponsor | High | only after metadata review | regulated offer after approval |
| Cloud/AI/dev tools | GSE Lab and build-in-public | "How GSE is built" | sponsor, credits, partner content | Low to medium | technical proof and governance | builder sponsor |
| Local/regional sponsors | Community credibility | "Founding local supporter" | sponsor | Low | early-stage supporter angle | local supporter slot |

Sportsbook/DFS is not first-lane unless disclosure, terms URL, eligible states, restricted states, responsible-gaming text, and approved compliance status are complete.

## Daily Outreach Target

10 messages/day:

- 4 creator/tool partners
- 2 sports data/API partners
- 2 fantasy/card partners
- 1 local sponsor
- 1 podcast/creator collaboration

## Outreach Templates

### General Partner

Subject: GSE partner fit - evidence-first sports intelligence

Hi [Name],

I am building Galaxy Sports Edge, an evidence-first sports intelligence platform and media brand. I am opening a small founding partner lane for tools and companies that fit source reliability, decision quality, and sports audience education.

No fake audience claims, no forced endorsement, and no sponsor control over model or editorial conclusions. If that standard fits your brand, I would like to explore a manual-review content or sponsor test.

Garrett

### Local Sponsor

Subject: Founding local sponsor slot for GSE

Hi [Name],

I am building GSE from [region] as a sports intelligence and media company. I am opening a small founding supporter package for local brands that want to be near disciplined sports analysis without tout-style claims.

The first package is simple: disclosed sponsor support, manual-review mentions, and no influence over picks, model outputs, or editorial conclusions.

Would you be open to a short conversation?

### Tool Affiliate

Subject: Evidence-first tool review opportunity

Hi [Name],

GSE is building content around the real workflow behind sports intelligence: source reliability, content packaging, AI-assisted drafting, and review gates. Your tool may fit a practical review if it helps that workflow.

Any affiliate relationship would be clearly disclosed, and the review would remain honest.

### Sports Data/API Partner

Subject: Sports data workflow and rights-first content idea

Hi [Name],

GSE's thesis is that raw sports data is not intelligence unless freshness, rights, and decision context are governed. I would like to explore a content or partner piece about what high-quality sports data infrastructure should actually prove.

This would not resell raw data or imply rights that do not exist.

### Podcast Guest/Collab

Subject: GSE Board Meeting guest idea

Hi [Name],

I am developing the GSE Board Meeting format: what shipped, what broke, what the model learned, what we passed on, and which tools or partners matter. You may be a strong fit for a future guest segment or collaboration.

The format is educational, not a tout show.
