# 1. North star

Galaxy Sports Edge is the public doorway into a living sports-science civilization that can:

1. observe sports reality through authorized sources, sensors, humans, markets, media, and software systems;
2. preserve what happened, what was reported, what Galaxy observed, and what became decision-effective;
3. represent uncertainty without forcing false consensus;
4. decide what missing information or capability would matter next;
5. discover, acquire, compose, evaluate, and retire capabilities from ecosystems the founder may never have named;
6. formulate hypotheses, create experiments, generate counterexamples, and preserve negative knowledge;
7. simulate alternate, intervention, adversarial, and synthetic worlds;
8. distinguish description, prediction, causal support, and mechanism;
9. compile validated scientific discoveries into permanent product instruments;
10. place computation across browser, mobile, edge, local, cloud, federated, and confidential environments;
11. evolve models, agents, interfaces, and research programs without allowing production to mutate itself;
12. prove which evidence, code, model, assumptions, policy, and temporal cutoff produced every important output;
13. improve human judgment, not merely increase machine output.

Its defining capability is **recursive instrument creation**:

```text
unknown question
  → explicit capability gap
  → cross-domain discovery
  → candidate capabilities and assumptions
  → typed intelligence contract
  → policy-valid alternative plans
  → most informative affordable experiment
  → temporal, adversarial and counterfactual challenge
  → calibrated scientific instrument
  → API, interface, Academy, agent and simulation projections
  → continuous monitoring, correction, retirement or resurrection
```

## 4.2 Galaxy Metacortex

The Metacortex is a semantic, policy-aware, multi-objective meta-compiler. It chooses among **methods of knowing**, not merely models.

Candidate methods include:

- acquire another observation;
- retrieve historical analogs;
- apply a deterministic instrument;
- run a statistical model;

4. eliminates hard-policy failures;
5. compares valid plans by information value, robustness, cost, latency, privacy, energy, resilience, and operational complexity;
6. preserves rejected alternatives and reasons;
7. emits a deterministic Plan Receipt;

# 15. Implementation laws

1. **Improve, do not remove.** New architecture coordinates accepted systems unless an explicit supersession decision preserves lineage and justification.
2. **Recover before recreating.** Query the Codebase Twin, Git history, open PRs, stranded branches, tests and current production evidence.
3. **One semantic kernel.** No competing canonical entity, evidence, time, branch, capability, assumption, measurement, policy or proof models.
4. **One bounded workstream.** Agents do not build the civilization in one session.
5. **Hard constraints precede optimization.** Rights, privacy, time, trust, entitlement, authority, security and public-claim policy cannot be offset by score.
6. **Scientific status is typed.** Description, association, prediction, causal hypothesis, causal support and mechanism are distinct.
7. **Measurement credibility precedes product promotion.** Computability is not validity.
8. **Production never self-promotes.** Automated systems may research, evaluate, plan, code and open PRs; governed actions remain governed.
9. **Every important output carries lineage.** Data, assumptions, models, code, policy, time and proof remain inspectable.
10. **Failure is retained.** Negative knowledge and stepping stones are assets.
11. **Clouds and hubs are capabilities, not owners.** Galaxy semantics and policies remain provider-neutral.
12. **Human comprehension is part of correctness.** A technically correct output that predictably misleads users is not a successful product instrument.
