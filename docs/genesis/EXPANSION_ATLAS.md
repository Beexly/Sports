# Galaxy Genesis — Capability Expansion Atlas

This atlas widens the frontier beyond GitHub, Hugging Face, agents and workflows without turning Galaxy into a random technology catalog.

Every addition must satisfy three tests:

1. It changes what Galaxy can know, prove, simulate, protect or productize.
2. It connects to an accepted Genesis system through a precise contract.
3. It produces a measurable user, scientific, operational or strategic consequence.

The atlas is modular so coding and research agents load only the region needed for the active workstream.

## Governing rule

These modules are research and destination architecture. They do not authorize implementation out of dependency order. `docs/genesis/WORK_QUEUE.md` is the execution authority, and `GX-000` remains the only current workstream.
