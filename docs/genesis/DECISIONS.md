# Galaxy Genesis — Binding R&D Decisions

These decisions preserve the entire prior frontier program while adding a unifying control plane. They are implementation constraints, not marketing copy.

## D-001 — Improve, do not remove

All accepted systems remain:

- SportsIR
- bitemporal reality and epistemic playback
- Intelligence Contracts
- Evidence Mesh / Evidence Vault / Source Graph
- Branching Reality and Galaxy Multiverse
- Reality Receipts and Proof Fabric
- Model Ecology
- Capability Foundry
- Galaxy Neural Foundry and Hugging Face workstreams
- Research Cortex and Unknown-Unknown Radar
- Red Queen Science Engine
- Failure Atlas / Negative Knowledge
- Product Twin and Decision Twin
- Galaxy Twin
- Galaxy Dynasty / Dynasty Studio
- Agent Foundry and Agent Civilization
- Private Federation
- Edge Cortex / Edge Swarm
- Galaxy Commons and benchmark network

The Metacortex coordinates them. It does not replace them.

## D-002 — The public brand does not change

Galaxy Sports Edge remains the public product. `Galaxy Genesis`, `Metacortex`, `Neural Foundry`, and related names are internal architecture unless the founder later approves public use.

## D-003 — One semantic kernel

SportsIR is the shared semantic language. New systems may extend it through versioned types and capabilities, but may not establish competing canonical entity, evidence, time, branch, or proof models.

## D-004 — Four clocks remain mandatory

Meaningful observations distinguish:

- `occurredAt`
- `publishedAt`
- `observedAt`
- `effectiveAt`

Historical replay may never use information unavailable at the replayed knowledge time.

## D-005 — Model outputs are typed, not flattened

Separate:

1. source observations;
2. model-derived measurements tied to source observations;
3. representation transformations;
4. forecasts;
5. synthetic outputs.

A model-derived measurement is not an independent source. Generated prose and simulation remain synthetic.

## D-006 — The Metacortex plans before anything executes

An Intelligence Contract compiles into a typed, inspectable execution plan before external work begins. The plan records:

- required outputs;
- candidate capabilities;
- rejected alternatives;
- hard constraints;
- uncertainty;
- cost and latency;
- temporal cutoff;
- proof obligations;
- fallback and abstention behavior.

## D-007 — Hard constraints dominate optimization

A cheaper, faster or more accurate candidate is ineligible when it violates:

- source rights;
- data purpose;
- privacy;
- temporal integrity;
- evidence projection;
- entitlement;
- security;
- public-claim policy;
- owner-gated authority.

No weighted score may compensate for a hard-policy failure.

## D-008 — Precise probability is not forced

When evidence cannot justify one probability distribution, Galaxy may retain:

- probability intervals;
- credal or ambiguity sets;
- conformal prediction sets;
- multiple world branches;
- explicit abstention.

`No-Bet` or `Watch` may represent a decision that is not robust across admissible worlds, not merely a low confidence number.

## D-009 — Science and prediction remain distinct

A predictive association does not become a causal claim. Every generated instrument records its causal status:

```text
DESCRIPTIVE
ASSOCIATIONAL
PREDICTIVE
CAUSAL_HYPOTHESIS
CAUSALLY_SUPPORTED
MECHANISTICALLY_SUPPORTED
```

Causal promotion requires explicit assumptions, falsification, sensitivity analysis and independent temporal validation.

## D-010 — Every scientific instrument has measurement semantics

A promoted metric must define:

- construct;
- units or scale;
- valid range;
- measurement process;
- uncertainty;
- reliability;
- validity evidence;
- supported and unsupported regimes;
- missing-data behavior;
- version.

A composite score may never become an unexplained sealed number.

## D-011 — Incremental recomputation is the destination

When one source, injury state, line, model, policy or user contract changes, the system should invalidate and recompute only affected downstream objects. Full-world recomputation is a fallback, not the target architecture.

## D-012 — External capabilities run without ambient authority

Future plugins, models, tools, Spaces, MCP servers and generated components execute through explicit capability contracts. File, network, secret, database and external-action access are denied unless declared and approved.

## D-013 — Formal verification is targeted, not theatrical

Protected algorithms should gain machine-checkable specifications where practical, especially:

- settlement;
- side and market identity;
- CLV population and sign conventions;
- bitemporal cutoff enforcement;
- rights and entitlement projection;
- branch-update invariants;
- proof commitment/opening;
- plan-policy enforcement.

Formal verification supplements testing. It does not justify weak or incorrect specifications.

## D-014 — Training data and evidence have measurable marginal value

Galaxy should eventually estimate which sources, examples, modalities and models materially improve decisions. Popularity, volume and repetition are not value. Negative or harmful contributions remain visible.

## D-015 — Weak labels never become hidden truth

Programmatic labels, agent labels, distant supervision and synthetic labels retain their origin and uncertainty. They may accelerate dataset creation but cannot be silently promoted to authoritative ground truth.

## D-016 — The Codebase Twin is a control instrument

The repository is treated as a living system containing:

- code;
- schemas;
- routes;
- tests;
- feature flags;
- policies;
- documentation claims;
- PR and branch assets;
- runtime and deployment evidence.

Future coding work should query that twin before creating a new subsystem.

## D-017 — Local-first private intelligence is preferred when it changes the privacy equation

Personal journals, Decision Twin state, private media indexing, watchlists and selected Academy data should be capable of local ownership and offline use where feasible. Server centralization must be justified by capability need rather than convenience alone.

## D-018 — Human Performance Twin is broader than betting

Athlete and human-performance capabilities may eventually include biomechanics, movement, recovery, workload, skill development and rehabilitation. These systems require privacy, consent, measurement validity and medical-boundary governance. They are not activated through the consumer picks path.

## D-019 — No autonomous production promotion

Research, discovery, benchmarking, plan generation, code generation and PR preparation may be automated. Production deployment, public publication, source-rights expansion, model promotion, live billing mutation and production migrations remain governed actions.

## D-020 — First implementation is shadow-only

`GX-000` must not alter any production consumer. It emits plans and reports only. No model, source, agent or public route may branch on its recommendation.

## D-021 — The complete canon is preserved once and loaded selectively

`COMPLETE_CANON.md` is the archival completeness authority. `CANON_MANIFEST.json` is the machine-readable lookup layer. Coding agents do not reread the entire canon by default; they query the manifest and active contract.

## D-022 — Assumptions are first-class objects

Every material empirical, causal, measurement, operational, legal, economic, simulation and human-behavior assumption should be versioned and linked to its dependent evidence, models, instruments, plans, branches, interfaces and claims.

Galaxy must be able to compute an assumption’s blast radius and identify shared premises across apparently independent capabilities.

## D-023 — Instrument credibility precedes promotion

Computability, predictive correlation and benchmark performance are insufficient to establish a scientific instrument.

Promotion requires an Instrument Credibility Record covering construct or measurand, units, traceability, measurement procedure, calibration, reliability, validity, invariance, uncertainty, software implementation, supported regimes, V&V, UQ, expiry and recalibration.

## D-024 — Context is compiled, versioned and budgeted

Agent context must be generated from the active contract, Codebase Twin, Canon Manifest, decisions, protected zones, tests and semantic changes.

Context packages carry provenance, semantic hashes, token budgets, expiry and invalidation triggers. Lossy prompt compression requires task-specific evaluation before admission.

## D-025 — Equivalent plans are preserved before selection

The destination Metacortex maintains multiple semantically equivalent candidate plans. Rewrite systems, constraint solving, Pareto analysis and optimization may improve selection, but every elimination and rewrite remains explainable in the Plan Receipt.

Hard constraints are resolved before utility optimization.

## D-026 — Execution placement is compiled, not hard-coded

A valid logical plan may compile to server, SQL, incremental dataflow, CPU, GPU, TPU, NPU, browser, mobile, edge, federated or confidential execution.

Conversion, quantization, sparsity, distillation, kernel or provider changes produce new capability revisions requiring equivalence, calibration, security, rights, performance and rollback evidence.

## D-027 — Resilience is empirically tested

Trustworthiness requires controlled evidence of behavior under source outages, schema drift, stale data, provider failure, queue faults, partial storage failure, policy denial, license revocation, corrupted artifacts, clock skew and observability loss.

Galaxy must prefer honest degradation, abstention and recoverability over apparently continuous but fabricated service.

## D-028 — Rights, artifacts and policies are living dependencies

Licenses, terms, model cards, dataset cards, dependencies, vulnerabilities, standards, regulations, permissions and cloud services may change after admission.

Detected drift must traverse provenance, identify affected capabilities and outputs, and trigger quarantine, restriction, re-evaluation, retraining, retraction, rollback or owner/legal review.

## D-029 — Cloud and platform providers are execution profiles

AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Hugging Face, local and future providers may supply capabilities, but they do not own Galaxy semantics, evidence, policy, assumptions, receipts, evaluation or workstream state.

Provider choice follows contract eligibility, measured capability fit, resilience, cost, latency, energy, residency, portability and rollback.

## D-030 — Cognitive observability is semantic and privacy-aware

Galaxy traces the path from contract through plan, evidence, assumptions, capabilities, policy, output, interface, user decision, outcome and autopsy.

Raw prompts, licensed data, personal data and sensitive evidence are minimized, redacted, hashed, aggregated, kept local or separated according to purpose.

## D-031 — Diversity and stepping stones are preserved

Model, agent, hypothesis, plan, interface and world selection must not collapse every population onto one average winner.

Galaxy maintains quality-diversity archives across regimes, modalities, evidence states, cost, latency, privacy, human comprehension and failure independence.

## D-032 — Capability recombination is systematic but not novelty-driven

Galaxy may search combinations of existing capabilities using typed affordances, constraints, economics and unresolved gaps.

A novel combination earns only a sandbox experiment. Promotion still requires rights, security, evaluation, scientific credibility, user value and operational evidence.

## D-033 — Closed-loop planning is a research destination

The Metacortex may evolve into a cybernetic controller that maintains beliefs, seeks information, acts, observes consequences and replans under partial observability.

Active inference, POMDP, model-predictive and adaptive-control methods are candidates, not doctrine. They must outperform simpler planners under Galaxy-specific evaluation.

## D-034 — Local consistency does not guarantee global truth

Galaxy may research local-to-global consistency and obstruction methods for multimodal data, sources, policies, rosters, model outputs and world states.

Advanced mathematical methods, including sheaf-inspired approaches, remain research-only until they beat simpler constraint, graph and probabilistic baselines on concrete Galaxy tasks.

## D-035 — Institutional and economic systems are part of sports reality

Galaxy may model league rules, roster markets, contracts, incentives, media, fan behavior, governance, regulation and organizational decisions when they materially affect sports understanding.

These systems do not become pick factors merely because they can be represented.

## D-036 — Package completeness is testable

Every accepted system from the founding conversation must appear in at least one of:

- `COMPLETE_CANON.md`;
- `CANON_MANIFEST.json`;
- `ORIGIN_SOURCE_MAP.md`;
- the dependency-ordered work queue.

A future package-completeness test should fail when a canonical manifest entry lacks documentation, a workstream, or an explicit research/owner-gate status.
