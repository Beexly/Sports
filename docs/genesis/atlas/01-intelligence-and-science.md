# Galaxy Genesis Atlas — Intelligence and Science

# 1. Metacortex / Cognitive Query Optimizer

## Improvement

Unifies every existing compiler and router behind one semantic plan. It generates multiple equivalent approaches to the same intelligence problem and selects among them using hard policy constraints, expected information gain, robust decision value, cost and latency.

## Why it is not “another router”

Existing routers choose among models or providers. The Metacortex chooses among entire **methods of knowing**:

- acquire evidence;
- retrieve analogs;
- use a deterministic formula;
- run a model;
- run causal analysis;
- simulate;
- wait;
- ask a human;
- abstain.

## Product consequence

A user asks a question once. Galaxy decides the right combination of evidence, models, computation and interface rather than forcing the question through a predetermined page or model.

# 2. Incremental Reality Kernel

## Improvement

Compiles batch world-state computations into change-aware updates. A single injury, odds snapshot, source correction or model revision invalidates only affected downstream objects.

## Connection

- SportsIR supplies semantics.
- bitemporal worldlines supply time.
- Reality Receipts identify dependencies.
- the Metacortex identifies affected plan regions.

## Product consequence

Galaxy Twin, Game Rooms, contracts and model states update quickly and consistently without repeatedly recomputing the entire universe.

## Research consequence

The system can ask exactly which conclusions changed because of one new observation.

# 3. Semantic Policy and Provenance Fabric

## Improvement

Turns provenance, rights and permitted purpose into machine-evaluable objects rather than prose scattered across docs and registries.

The fabric combines concepts equivalent to:

- provenance entities, activities and agents;
- permissions, prohibitions, duties and constraints;
- shape validation for semantic objects;
- runtime policy decisions.

## Product consequence

Galaxy can answer:

```text
May this observation be stored?
May it train a model?
May it influence a private analysis?
May its derived measurement appear publicly?
What attribution or deletion duty follows?
```

The answer travels with the object and survives transformation.

# 4. Verified Intelligence Kernel

## Improvement

Protected algorithms gain formal specifications and verifier-guided implementations.

Priority targets:

- settlement side identity and totality;
- CLV sign and population invariants;
- temporal cutoff;
- audience projection;
- rights propagation;
- branch normalization;
- plan-policy enforcement;
- commitment opening.

## Product consequence

Trust-critical code can provide proof that it satisfies declared invariants, not only a collection of examples.

## Boundary

Formal methods are applied selectively. A weak specification that verifies is still a weak system; mutation tests challenge the specification itself.

# 5. Credal and Conformal Decision Layer

## Improvement

Replaces false precision with valid sets of possibilities.

Galaxy may express:

- outcome set;
- probability interval;
- conformal interval;
- ambiguity set;
- branch-dependent decision;
- risk-controlled abstention.

## Product consequence

`No-Bet` becomes mathematically meaningful:

> The decision is not stable across the set of worlds still compatible with the evidence.

## Scientific consequence

Galaxy can distinguish uncertainty caused by limited data from uncertainty inherent in the event.

# 6. Scientific Law Foundry

## Improvement

Moves beyond feature discovery toward interpretable dynamics and mechanisms.

Methods may include:

- symbolic regression;
- sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics;
- causal discovery for time series;
- point-process models;
- process mining;
- topological data analysis;
- optimal transport;
- dimensional and unit checks.

## Product consequence

Galaxy can create instruments that users can inspect and reason about, rather than only opaque model scores.

## Promotion rule

A discovered equation or pattern remains a hypothesis until it survives temporal holdouts, simpler baselines, falsification, regime tests and measurement-validity review.

# 7. Model Mechanism Lab

## Improvement

Explains why models disagree or fail by inspecting internal features, training-data influence and shared ancestry.

Potential research tools include:

- sparse feature dictionaries;
- model-difference representations;
- activation comparison;
- training-example influence;
- counterfactual activation tests;
- causal interventions on representations.

## Product consequence

Model Autopsy can say more than:

> Model A and Model B disagree.

It can investigate:

> Both models depend on the same market-history shortcut, while the video species responds to pass-protection structure absent from their representations.

## Boundary

Mechanistic interpretability is still an active research field. Findings are treated as evidence about model behavior, not perfect mind reading.

# 8. Evidence and Data Economics

## Improvement

Measures the marginal value of:

- one source;
- one historical sample;
- one modality;
- one model;
- one human correction;
- one simulation family;
- one expensive API call.

## Connection

- Value-of-Information Router determines future acquisitions.
- Contribution Market allocates reputation and rewards.
- Failure Atlas identifies harmful data.
- Private Federation can compensate contributors without centralizing raw records.

## Product consequence

Galaxy spends money and attention on evidence that actually changes decisions, not evidence that merely increases volume.
