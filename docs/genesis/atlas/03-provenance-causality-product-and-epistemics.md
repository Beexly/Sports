# Galaxy Genesis Atlas — Provenance, Causality, Product and Epistemics

# 17. Media Authenticity and Transformation Ledger

## Improvement

Every media object can retain:

- origin;
- capture device or publisher when available;
- edits;
- generated regions;
- model transformations;
- clip boundaries;
- captions and translations;
- source rights;
- hashes and attestations.

## Product consequence

Film Intelligence can distinguish original evidence, transcoding, enhancement, annotation and synthetic reconstruction.

# 18. Causal Transportability and Regime Transfer

## Improvement

A result learned in one season, league, sport, population or simulation does not automatically transfer.

Every instrument records:

- source domain;
- target domain;
- variables whose mechanisms are assumed stable;
- variables whose distributions changed;
- transport evidence;
- recalibration requirements;
- failure conditions.

## Product consequence

Galaxy can say:

> This mechanism is validated for NFL regular-season passing situations and is not yet supported for college football or postseason regimes.

That is more useful than a universal score built on hidden extrapolation.

# 19. Measurement and Construct Validity System

## Improvement

Galaxy distinguishes a useful operational metric from the underlying construct it attempts to measure.

For every instrument:

```text
construct
operational definition
units
reliability
convergent validity
discriminant validity
criterion validity
sensitivity
specificity
measurement invariance
version
```

## Product consequence

Composite metrics become scientific instruments with known limitations rather than branded numbers that slowly drift in meaning.

# 20. Living Evidence Synthesis

## Improvement

Research reviews become continuously updated evidence graphs rather than static memos.

Galaxy tracks:

- original claims;
- methods;
- datasets;
- replications;
- failed replications;
- critiques;
- code and model revisions;
- Galaxy experiments;
- current confidence in each claim.

## Product consequence

A research-dependent instrument can automatically enter review when a new study challenges its foundation.

# 21. Anytime-Valid Research Ledger

## Improvement

Continuous experimentation no longer relies on repeatedly peeking at ordinary fixed-horizon p-values.

Galaxy should support sequentially valid evidence accumulation, preregistered stopping rules, multiple-testing control and trial history.

## Connection

The existing Edge Lab trials registry and FDR work become part of a broader scientific ledger.

## Product consequence

The system can run long-lived shadow experiments without quietly changing the standard after seeing favorable results.

# 22. Interface Genome and Comprehension Evals

## Improvement

The Interface Compiler evaluates not only clicks or conversion, but whether users correctly understand:

- uncertainty;
- source independence;
- branch sensitivity;
- causal status;
- model disagreement;
- what would change the decision.

## Product consequence

A visually impressive surface is rejected when it causes systematic misunderstanding.

# 23. Organizational and Incentive Twin

## Improvement

Models the incentives created by Galaxy itself:

- what contributors optimize for;
- how leaderboards can be gamed;
- whether agents hide uncertainty to appear productive;
- whether users overact on alerts;
- whether revenue metrics pressure trust rules;
- whether benchmarks reward shortcuts.

## Product consequence

Second- and third-order effects become part of product design rather than postmortem surprises.

# 24. Epistemic Kernel and Argument Graph

## Improvement

Branching Reality gains a formal truth-maintenance mechanism. Every proposition retains assumptions, minimal support sets, attacks, source-origin dependence, temporal scope and admissible environments.

The Signal Courtroom becomes a projection of a machine-readable argument graph rather than a narrative metaphor.

## Genuine leverage

- new evidence invalidates only affected conclusions;
- copied reports do not create false confirmation;
- unresolved contradiction remains visible without disabling unrelated intelligence;
- every decision can state what would flip it;
- historical replay reconstructs the exact justification structure available then.

## Product consequence

Users can inspect not only evidence, but the actual structure of why a conclusion survives, remains contested or is withheld.
