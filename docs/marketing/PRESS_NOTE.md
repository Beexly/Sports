### Galaxy Sports Edge launches as a calibrated, agent-native sports-intelligence platform

**[City, if desired] —** Galaxy Sports Edge (GSE), a sports-intelligence
operating system built by Galaxy Sports Network, today opened its Founding
membership lane at galaxysportsedge.com.

Galaxy Sports Edge is a calibrated, agent-native sports-intelligence platform:
probability claims are gated behind a published calibration contract, pick
records are hash-chained and publicly verifiable, and the engine's default
output is disciplined No-Bet until evidence clears the bar.

The platform's methodology is public — the pipeline that reads market data,
removes bookmaker margin with a seven-method de-vig approach, and checks
independent estimators against the market rather than letting the market grade
itself is documented in full at galaxysportsedge.com/methodology. The exact
weights and aggregation formula stay proprietary; the framework and every gate
around it do not.

Every published pick is written into a hash-chained record before kickoff, so
a reader can verify — without asking GSE to vouch for itself — that history was
not edited after the fact. Anyone can check a record at
galaxysportsedge.com/how-to-verify-a-record.

Free access includes the methodology, a glossary, and the record-verification
path, permanently. Paid tiers — Fantasy ($4.99/mo), Pro ($14.99/mo), and Elite
($24.99/mo) — unlock the working surfaces: the full signal board, factor trail,
and (Elite) real-time alerts and a closing-line-value ledger. Founding members
keep their rate for the life of their subscription; the price only rises on
published proof milestones, never on a calendar.

"We are honest" is what every product in this category says. Galaxy Sports
