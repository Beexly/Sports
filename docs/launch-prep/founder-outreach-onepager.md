## Common objections — how to handle them

**"Why should I trust a brand-new sports service?"**
> You shouldn't, fully. That's why the Calibration Report stays empty
> until there's a defensible number to publish. In the meantime, what I'm
> giving you is the methodology, the gate model, and the factor trail on
> every signal. You can audit my logic before you trust my track record.
> Most services flip that order. I won't.

**"How is this different from {{Insert Service}}?"**
> Two things: every signal exposes its full factor trail — you see the
> inputs, not just the output. And the public win-rate stays gated
> until enough canonical settled signals exist to back it. Most services
> publish a number from day one. I refuse to.

**"What's your win rate?"**
> Refusing to answer right now is the answer. A defensible win-rate
> takes at least 100 settled signals. Once I have that, the Calibration
> Report opens. Until then, the page says "Collecting." If I quoted a
> number today, I'd be lying to you.

**"Is $19/mo a lot for one person and a model?"**
> Pro is $19/mo and gets every signal with its full factor trail. The
> comparable tout service is $99/mo and gives you vibes. The math
> favors me. There's also a 7-day refund window — if it's not earning
> its price, you don't owe me anything.

**"I just want today's lock."**
> Then I'm not your service. I refuse to publish a "lock" because that
> word is a lie in a market with variance. Galaxy IQ ships when the
> evidence supports it — and stays quiet when it doesn't. If you want
> a daily premium pick regardless of evidence, the rest of the
> industry will sell you that. Not me.
