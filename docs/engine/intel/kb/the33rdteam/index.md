<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The 2025 NFL Draft is in the books! After 257 draft choices, countless additional undrafted free agent deals were made during the last 48 hours. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">And while the story of the 2025 season is only just beginning to be written, the work to prepare for three days of chaos for next year's draft starts now.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A big part of every journey is taking the first step. We'll do that here, with a way-too-early snapshot of some of the top names projected to be a part of the 2026 class.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The draft order is set vs. the latest 2025 Super Bowl odds, whereas the picks are made with one eye towards the perceived long-term needs of the teams and the other to the perceived growth and development of some of college football's standouts.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-way-too-early-2026-mock-draft">Way too Early 2026 Mock Draft</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_25091545-1024x682.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82392"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">South Carolina Gamecocks quarterback LaNorris Sellers (16) runs the ball against Illinois Fighting Illini linebacker Joe Barna (43) in the first quarter at Camping World Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">1. Cleveland Browns</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-selection-lanorris-sellers-qb-south-carolina">Selection: LaNorris Sellers, QB, South Carolina</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Cleveland will undoubtedly be thrilled to take the next step in its journey to move on from the Deshaun Watson farce at quarterback. If the Browns finish with the top spot after spending two mid-round picks and acquiring Kenny Pickett this offseason, look for them to go for a toolsy quarterback. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There's plenty of buzz over the latest addition to one of the NFL's royal families, but Arch doesn't have anywhere near the tools or upside you'll get with Sellers.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/USATSI_24937888-1024x739.jpg" alt="Penn State Nittany Lions quarterback Drew Allar (15) throws downfield during the first quarter against the Oregon Ducks in the 2024 Big Ten Championship game at Lucas Oil Stadium." class="wp-image-78286"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Penn State Nittany Lions quarterback Drew Allar (15) throws downfield during the first quarter against the Oregon Ducks in the 2024 Big Ten Championship game at Lucas Oil Stadium. Jordan Prather-Imagn Images.</figcaption></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">2. New Orleans Saints</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Drew Allar, QB, Penn State</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Allar flirted with the idea of declaring for the 2025 NFL Draft after making significant leaps in his play in 2024. His return for another season should have him more polished and closer to being ready to fulfill his potential as a big-armed pocket passer with subtle mobility. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">His pairing with New Orleans would come in part thanks to the Saints' second-round investment in Tyler Shough — it's not a big enough marriage to prevent a marquee pick.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24122874-1024x683.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82393"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Clemson Tigers defensive lineman Peter Woods (11) shown on the field pregame prior to the game against the Georgia Bulldogs at Mercedes-Benz Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">3. New York Giants</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Peter Woods, IDL, Clemson</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">New York added Darius Alexander to play inside next to Dexter Lawrence, but barring a monster rookie season, there should still be plenty of reps to go around inside. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Woods is a disruptive, powerful presence that could help the Giants combat the powerful lines building in the NFC East.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24939429-1024x691.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82394"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Clemson Tigers defensive end T.J. Parker (3) reacts after a play during the second quarter against the Southern Methodist Mustangs at Bank of America Stadium. Bob Donnan-Imagn Images</figcaption></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">4. Tennessee Titans</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: T.J. Parker, EDGE, Clemson</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Titans bid farewell to Harold Landry and replaced him in the pass rush room with second-round pick Femi Oladejo. However, there's a lot of opportunity to be found with this group, and Parker is a powerful player who led Power Four pass rushers in sacks in 2024. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">His pairing with Oladejo would give the Titans a pair of dense, intense rushers off the edge to work with long-term.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24890439-1024x689.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82395"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Arizona State Sun Devils wide receiver Jordyn Tyson (0) celebrates a touchdown against the Arizona Wildcats in the first half during the Territorial Cup at Arizona Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">5. New York Jets </h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Jordyn Tyson, WR, Arizona State</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Tyson was a revelation in 2024. He's a Colorado transfer who became a matchup problem down the stretch for the Sun Devils amid their push to the College Football Playoff. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">He was sorely missed in ASU's upset bid against Texas due to a shoulder injury, but his encore opportunity in 2025 could see him as a top pass-catching prospect.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24451012-1024x683.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82396"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Miami Hurricanes offensive lineman Francis Mauigoa (61) blocks against California Golden Bears linebacker David Reese (7) during the first quarter at California Memorial Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">6. Carolina Panthers</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Francis Mauigoa, OT, Miami</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Carolina exercised its fifth-year option on OT Ikem Ekwonu, but the long-term status of the other tackle position is very much up in the air. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Mauigoa is a prolific mauler with rare agility for a player of his size. If not for quarterback needs elsewhere in the top-5 of this scenario, he'd be a shoo-in to be gone.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_25244059-1024x719.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82397"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Ohio State Buckeyes safety Caleb Downs (2) against the Notre Dame Fighting Irish during the CFP National Championship college football game at Mercedes-Benz Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">7. Las Vegas Raiders</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Caleb Downs, SAF, Ohio State</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Downs might be the best available player in the country based on his 2024 performance. The problem, of course, is that he plays safety, which teams don't typically target early. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Downs is worth the exception, as he can do nearly anything you could need in coverage and is a tremendous run defender.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_25163720-1024x695.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82398"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Texas Longhorns quarterback Arch Manning (16) runs against Ohio State Buckeyes cornerback Jordan Hancock (7) and safety Caleb Downs (2) during the second quarter of the College Football Playoff semifinal in the Cotton Bowl. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">8. Indianapolis Colts </h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Arch Manning, QB, Texas</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Print the jerseys. Go ahead! The Colts are entering 2025 with what has been described as an open competition between Anthony Richardson and Daniel Jones at quarterback. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Richardson will need a big year to take the pressure and negative perception off his status with the team. Jim Irsay would not be mad about landing (another) Manning.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24473991-1024x711.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82400"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Alabama Crimson Tide quarterback Jalen Milroe follows Alabama Crimson Tide offensive lineman Parker Brailsford and Alabama Crimson Tide offensive lineman Kadyn Proctor. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">9. Cleveland Browns </h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Kadyn Proctor, OT, Alabama</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Browns traded away from a potential generational talent in Travis Hunter this weekend. But if the result a year from now is a long-term quarterback and a new staple for an aging offensive line, it would be well worth the late pivot this year.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_25050563-1024x760.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82401"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Texas Longhorns linebacker Anthony Hill Jr. (0) against the Clemson Tigers during the CFP National playoff first round at Darrell K Royal-Texas Memorial Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">10. New England Patriots </h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Anthony Hill Jr., LB, Texas</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">New England is currently in the process of overhauling the middle of its defense amid the arrival of Mike Vrabel this offseason. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">LBs Jack Gibbens and Robert Spillane have the potential to be a sufficient duo, but how long is Spillane's runway? He'll be 30 this season. Hill Jr. is a disruptive force in the middle.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24546860-1024x708.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82402"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Arizona Wildcats cornerback Tacario Davis (1) against the Colorado Buffaloes at Arizona Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">11. Seattle Seahawks</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-selection-tacario-davis-cb-washington">Selection: Tacario Davis, CB, Washington</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Seahawks added Nick Emmanwori to their secondary this year. He's a potent addition alongside Devon Witherspoon and Riq Woolen. But putting another supersized corner like Davis opposite Woolen will fill this secondary out with their fair share of physical freaks.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24879862-1024x756.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82403"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Tennessee defensive back Jermod McCoy (3) reacts after gaining control of a fumble during the second quarter at FirstBank Stadium in Nashville</figcaption></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">12. Atlanta Falcons</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Jermod McCoy, CB, Tennessee</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Atlanta entered the draft with a need at corner opposite A.J. Terrell. Surprisingly, they didn't do much with that spot. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Instead, they opted to double-dip with pass rushers via Jalon Walker and James Pearce Jr., McCoy, an athletic, disruptive corner who would be an awesome addition and solution.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24780046-1024x683.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82404"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Florida Gators defensive lineman Caleb Banks (88) celebrates with the ball after a fumble recovery against the LSU Tigers during the second half at Ben Hill Griffin Stadium. Matt Pendleton-Imagn Images</figcaption></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">13. Dallas Cowboys</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Caleb Banks, DL, Florida</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Banks scorned a jump to the NFL this offseason to return to Florida for his senior season. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Louisville transfer has size and disruption in the heart of the line, with room for more production if he can cut down on some of his missed tackle challenges once he's created backfield havoc.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24738407-1024x683.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82405"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">South Carolina Gamecocks wide receiver Nyck Harbor (8) runs the ball after a made catch against the Vanderbilt Commodores during the first half at FirstBank Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">14. Miami Dolphins</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Nyck Harbor, WR, South Carolina</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Tyreek Hill is on borrowed time in Miami. He's due more than $35 million in 2026 after quitting on the team in the 2024 season finale and a parade of questionable decisions off the field. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Miami will need another marquee passing game weapon to go opposite Jaylen Waddle, and Harbor is an absolute freak athlete with the kind of speed the Dolphins will undoubtedly love.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24341892-1024x683.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82406"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Washington Huskies wide receiver Denzel Boston (12) catches the ball a touchdown pass against Rutgers Scarlet Knights defensive back Robert Longerbeam (7) at SHI Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">15. Arizona Cardinals </h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Denzel Boston, WR, Washington</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Boston is a big-bodied target who thrives on the outside. He appears due for a breakout season in 2025 after appearing on the scene in 2024. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Boston had just 66 career receiving yards in his first two seasons before an 834-yard and nine-touchdown season. He would pair quite well with Marvin Harrison Jr. to give the Cardinals a pair of physical receivers outside.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/USATSI_24599552-1024x683.jpg" alt="LSU Tigers quarterback Garrett Nussmeier looks to pass the ball in the fourth quarter against the Texas A&amp;M Aggies at Kyle Field." class="wp-image-76917"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">LSU Tigers quarterback Garrett Nussmeier (13) looks to pass the ball in the fourth quarter against the Texas A&amp;M Aggies at Kyle Field. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">16. Pittsburgh Steelers</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Garrett Nussmeier, QB, LSU</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Pittsburgh appears to be angling for the Aaron Rodgers band-aid. The team bypassed quarterbacks early before securing Will Howard on Day 3. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Howard is a good add at that stage of the game, but Nussmeier was trending towards a potential first-round ticket last season before falling apart down the stretch.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24420071-1024x683.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82407"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Miami Hurricanes defensive lineman Rueben Bain Jr. (4) reacts after a tackle against the California Golden Bears during the first quarter at California Memorial Stadium. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">17. Chicago Bears</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-selection-rueben-bain-jr-edge-miami">Selection: Rueben Bain Jr., EDGE, Miami</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bain Jr. wasn't quite the same version of himself in 2024 as a freshman star in 2023. But he's a powerful, explosive presence that would be a welcome addition to the Bears' front. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There's no such thing as too many good pass rushers, and Bain Jr. showcased just how disruptive he could be with nearly 50 pressures as a freshman.</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_21880593-1024x683.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82408"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">A Mississippi Rebels wide receiver Jordan Watkins (11) is tackled by Georgia Bulldogs linebacker C.J. <h3 class="wp-block-heading">18. Denver Broncos</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: C.J. Allen, LB, Georgia</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Allen is a rangy, sure-fire tackler on the second level. The Broncos may have other needs, but they'd be wise to protect themselves from the durability questions that may come with Dre Greenlaw, and Allen would be an incredible partner next to the former 49er when both are healthy and on the field.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_24892169-1024x682.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82409"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Auburn Tigers defensive lineman Keldric Faulk (15) celebrates a stop as Auburn Tigers take on California Golden Bears at Jordan-Hare Stadium in Auburn, Ala.</figcaption></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">19. Tampa Bay Buccaneers</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Keldric Faulk, EDGE, Auburn</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Tampa's pass rush help for 2025 comes in the form of Haason Reddick on a one-year contract and Day 3 rookies Elijah Roberts and David Walker. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Faulk is a supersized rusher, not dissimilar to the build of Elijah Roberts. However, his production in the SEC is hard to ignore — he posted 45 pressures on just more than 300 rush opportunities in 2024.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" src="https://thirtythirdv2.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/USATSI_25098344-1024x785.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-82410"/><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Penn State Nittany Lions defensive end Dani Dennis-Sutton (33) against the Boise State Broncos during the Fiesta Bowl at State Farm Stadium. Mark J. Rebilas-Imagn Images</figcaption></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">20. Houston Texans</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Dani Dennis-Sutton, EDGE, Penn State</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You can rest assured that DeMeco Ryans will ensure his defense has all the pressure players they need to keep the heat on. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Dennis-Sutton has "got next" as the marquee Penn State pass rusher and should be in contention for an early draft choice.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">21. Minnesota Vikings</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Mansoor Delane, CB, LSU</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Delane decided to forego the NFL Draft and instead transferred to LSU. One would imagine the extra experience in the SEC will help elevate his profile and boost his resume even more. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">He nearly doubled his career ball production in 2024, but will need to make sure he plays disciplined this upcoming season. If he does, he could be a sturdy answer for the Vikings' secondary.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">22. Los Angeles Chargers</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-selection-david-bailey-edge-texas-tech">Selection: David Bailey, EDGE, Texas Tech</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Khalil Mack is here for a good time but not a long time. Mack will play his age-34 season on a one-year deal with the Chargers, leaving room for a new face long-term. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bailey will likely find monster production after transferring from Stanford to Texas Tech. He posted more than a 20 percent pressure rate for the Cardinal in 2023 and 2024.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">23. San Francisco 49ers</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Spencer Fano, OT, Utah </h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">San Francisco's long-term offensive line outlook could best be described as shaky. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">How much longer can Trent Williams go at the level that he does? With no draft picks this year on the line, the stage is set for new blood next year.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">24. Green Bay Packers</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Daylen Everette, CB, Georgia</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The Packers needed a cornerback in 2025 before the draft, before anything happened with Jaire Alexander. Now, on the other side? </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The only corner the Packers drafted was seventh-rounder Micah Robinson. Everette is the next in line among a talented pipeline of Georgia corners and boasts good size on the outside.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">25. Washington Commanders</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: LT Overton, DL, Alabama</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Washington has been committed to rebuilding the trenches this offseason under Dan Quinn after its 2024 breakout. But playing in the NFC East means your line can't just be "good" — or you may fall off the pace. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Adding the supersized Overton to the Commanders' defensive front gives them the power and depth they'll need to go toe to toe with Philly and Dallas on the line.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">26. Cincinnati Bengals </h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Jeremiyah Love, RB, Notre Dame</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Cincinnati will try to make it work in 2025 with its wide receiver duo and some mid-round guards as its marquee additions on offense.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"> Next year, adding a new element to the group can be something they can aspire for — and a running back like Love would be one heck of an extra layer to have to defend.</p>

<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Cade Klubnik, QB, Clemson</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The annual Matthew Stafford saga figures to continue after this season. At some point, the Rams will be on the clock and determine that a talent fits and can give them the ceiling they need to continue to contend. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The CFB Playoff version of Klubnik we saw last season may have a chance of being that guy.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">28. Kansas City Chiefs</h3>



<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Christen Miller, DL, Georgia </h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We don't know enough about Miller to know that he will be ready to make this kind of leap. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">However, the Georgia kids usually do, especially when they're highly coveted recruits. Miller is a redshirt junior in 2025, coming off a sturdy and productive season as a member of Georgia's rotation.</p>

<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Jake Slaughter, IOL, Florida</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Bills center Connor McGovern is in a contract year in 2025. Is Sedrick Van Pran-Granger going to earn the Bills' trust enough from the bench to claim the long-term job if Buffalo doesn't re-sign McGovern? </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If not, Slaughter is a sturdy blocker in the middle with less than 20 pressures allowed in the last two seasons (730 pass protection reps).</p>

<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Nic Anderson, WR, LSU</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If Detroit moves on from Jameson Williams, having a big-play field stretcher could be on the shopping list for 2026. After transferring to LSU, Anderson is looking to recapture his 2023 magic this year. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At Oklahoma in 2023, Anderson posted nearly 21 yards per catch and 10 scores — he's a supersized receiver with enough juice to get down the field.</p>

<h6 class="wp-block-heading">Selection: Darrell Jackson Jr., DL, Florida State</h6>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Baltimore will miss Michael Pierce, even if Travis Jones is ready to be "the man" in the middle. Still, the Ravens always aspire to be deep in the trenches. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Jackson Jr. is a 330-pound defender in the middle who enjoyed a breakout campaign with Florida State in 2024, posting 30 pressures. If he takes another leap, he could be in line as a rising member of the class.</p>
