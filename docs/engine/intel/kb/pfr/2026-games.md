### How many regular season games in NFL?

Every NFL team plays 17 regular season games over the course of 18 weeks, with a bye week for each team

### When does regular season NFL start?

The 2025 NFL regular season begins on September 4, with the defending champion Philadelphia Eagles facing off against the Dallas Cowboys.

### How many weeks are in the NFL regular season?

There are 18 weeks in the NFL regular season. Each team plays 17 games and then has one bye week.

### How long is the NFL regular season?

The NFL regular season lasts 18 weeks. This year, it will run from September 4, 2025, through Sunday, January 4, 2026
