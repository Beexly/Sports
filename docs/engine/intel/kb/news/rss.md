The Eagles rank 28th offensively under new OC <strong>Sean Mannion</strong>, and after cutting <strong><a target="_blank" href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/D/DaltAn00.htm">Andy Dalton</a></strong>, the team has fourth-year backup/oft-mentioned trade chip <strong><a target="_blank" href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/M/McKeTa01.htm">Tanner McKee</a></strong> set for an extended audition.</p>

<p>The Cowboys added <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/G/GaryRa00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Rashan Gary</a></strong> and <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/W/WintDe00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Dee Winters</a></strong> via offseason trades while signing <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/T/ThomJa04.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Jalen Thompson</a></strong> and using first-round picks on <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/D/DownCa00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Caleb Downs</a></strong> and <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/L/LawrMa01.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Malachi Lawrence</a></strong>. <p>Pickens is off to a respectable start, but fellow 2025 addition <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/W/WillJa10.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Javonte Williams</a></strong> &#8212; who signed a three-year, $24MM deal to stay this offseason &#8212; is averaging 3.7 yards per carry (he finished his bounce-back 2025 season at 4.8). <p>Early struggles aside, Dallas&#8217; status could be interpreted as a good thing moving forward. But the Cowboys are a long way from feeling comfortable even in a weak division down two starting QBs.</p>

With <a target="_blank" href="https://www.profootballrumors.com/2026/10/colts-fire-gm-chris-ballard">Ballard now out</a>, some of his remaining Colts cornerstones are coming up before the trade deadline.</p>
<p><strong><a target="_blank" href="https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-scaled.jpg"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-233x300.jpg" alt="" width="233" height="300" class="size-medium wp-image-409768 alignright" srcset="https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-233x300.jpg 233w, https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-797x1024.jpg 797w, https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-768x987.jpg 768w, https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-1195x1536.jpg 1195w, https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-1594x2048.jpg 1594w, https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-900x1157.jpg 900w, https://www.profootballrumors.com/files/2026/06/USATSI_27894649-scaled.jpg 1992w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 233px) 100vw, 233px" /></a><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/T/TaylJo02.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Jonathan Taylor</a></strong> will be a name to watch. Teams are monitoring this situation, with <a target="_blank" href="https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50252635/several-teams-interested-trade-colts-jonathan-taylor">ESPN.com&#8217;s Stephen Holder indicating</a> multiple teams are open to acquiring the All-Pro running back. Taylor signed a two-year, $44MM extension this summer, but that deal carries some flexibility after 2026. Though, the Colts would be in for a hefty dead money hit if they moved on this year.</p>
<p>The Taylor extension includes an escape hatch. His $15MM 2027 base salary <a target="_blank" href="https://www.profootballrumors.com/2026/08/recent-extensions-settle-debate-about-rb-value-will-2026-deals-pay-off">does not become fully guaranteed until Day 5 of</a> the 2027 waiver period (Feb. 19). If the Colts are willing to take on more than $20MM in dead money this year by moving the recently finalized contract, the deal could appeal to other teams even as it sits third in RB AAV presently.</p>
<p>Taylor, perennial All-Pro guard <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/N/NelsQu00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Quenton Nelson</a></strong> and All-Pro defensive tackle <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/B/BuckDe00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">DeForest Buckner</a></strong> <a target="_blank" href="https://www.profootballrumors.com/2026/10/fallout-from-chris-ballards-firing-steichen-jones-trade-possibilities">emerged as names to watch ahead</a> of the November 10 deadline. Ballard gave both Taylor and Buckner multiple extensions, while Nelson became the NFL&#8217;s first $20MM-per-year guard in 2022. Nelson and Buckner are in contract years; Taylor is signed through 2028.</p>
<p>Despite <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/M/McCaCh01.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Christian McCaffrey</a></strong>, <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/K/KamaAl00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Alvin Kamara</a></strong> and <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/J/JoneAa00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Aaron Jones</a></strong> entering the NFL three years before Taylor, the Colts RB ranks behind only <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/H/HenrDe00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Derrick Henry</a></strong> and <strong><a href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/players/B/BarkSa00.htm?utm_medium=linker&amp;utm_source=www.profootballrumors.com&amp;utm_campaign=2026-10-22_pfr" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener">Saquon Barkley</a></strong> in rushing yards (8,090) <a target="_blank" href="https://www.pro-football-reference.com/leaders/rush_yds_career.htm">among active backs</a>. Barkley (2018) beat Taylor into the league by two years, but the 2020 second-round pick &#8212; save for a bumpy stretch from 2022-23 that included his first run of injury trouble and a de facto hold-in &#8212; has been the more reliable player.
