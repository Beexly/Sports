### 1. Old-QB RB dump rate → **CONFIRM, QUALIFY on causality**
`r22/nflverse-data-catalog.brief.md`. Numbers check out internally: 20.9% vs 18.2% → (20.9−18.2)/18.2 = 14.8% relative (≈14.7% reported), z=8.0, p=1.3e-15, n=4,936 team-weeks, 2016–2024, concentrated 37+. ### 2. Coverage-conditional QB tendencies → **QUALIFY, borderline WEAK**

The file's own ✅/❌/⚠️ ritual is the real durable asset here, not the numbers.

### 5. QB is the most volatile position → **CONFIRM, with a measurement caveat**
`d10/variance-model-and-mccaffrey-falsifier.brief.md`. Measured within-player CV 2020–2024 (n=771, ≥8 games): QB 0.993 > TE 0.936 > WR 0.876 > RB 0.872; pooled-per-game CVs also above spec everywhere. The McCaffrey label-leak proof is airtight arithmetic (313+584=897 ≠ 2×417; posted 417 = round(416.6) realized total).
**Caveat:** CV is σ/μ — a relative-to-mean measure on fantasy PPR points. Cross-position CV comparisons inherit mean differences (QBs score ~2× RBs per week). For band construction (`floor=proj×(1−CV)`) CV is the right relative unit, but "swings ±99%" should not be read as an absolute-point claim, and the ordering is scoring-format-specific. Also the 6-week half-life's post-injury pessimism (McCaffrey fitted 14.59/gm vs realized 24.51) is flagged in-file as an unshipped known defect — honest, not hidden.

### 7. Market-implied team rating recipe → **QUALIFY — blend is inference, not Baldwin's method**

### 8. Bridge-model calibration bar → **CONFIRM on Brier; CHALLENGE on the rest-days leg**
`d22/bridge-fit.brief.md` + `d22/environment-calibration.brief.md`. The sealed evaluation is clean: 6,955 pre-2025 fit games, 285 sealed 2025 games, bridge Brier 0.2237 vs spread-bucket 0.2120 — honestly reported as a loss. **Challenge:** the map bundles in "Rest-days slope +0.411 (se 0.254) used in engine" — that slope is **1.62σ (p≈0.10 two-sided)**, while wind (−0.135/0.162 = 0.83σ) and temp (+0.029/0.039 = 0.74σ) are zeroed as "too noisy." A 1.6σ estimate is itself weak evidence, and the noise bar is applied inconsistently: rest clears a bar that wind/temp fail, but no principled threshold (e.g. 2σ) is stated. The brief itself computes the 1.62σ figure as INFERENCE — the engine wires a marginally-significant slope while claiming a noise discipline. **Recommendation:** either state the bar (e.g. ≥1.5σ) and apply it uniformly, or hold rest at 0 pending multi-season confirmation. The 12-of-18 referee-crew minimum-12-games rule is sound.

### 9. Structural model adds zero over the close → **CONFIRM as paper result; QUALIFY transfer**
`r06/0670-does-a-structural-model-add.brief.md`. ŵ=0.000 boundary is genuine (log-loss monotone increasing in w on validation AND test; paired RPS diff +0.0067, 95% CI [0.0046, 0.0088]; market wins all 7 test seasons). **Qualification:** one sport (Serie A), one model (Dixon–Coles), one benchmark (Shin de-vigged Pinnacle close). ### 10. Hierarchical BT early-season shrinkage → **CONFIRM numbers; QUALIFY NFL transfer**
`r06/0611-hierarchical-bayesian-bradleyterry-for-applications-in.brief.md`. Apr-15 mean absolute rest-of-season win error 8.82 vs 24.65 (Bayes vs MLE) confirmed; convergence by June confirmed. **Transfer caveat:** the parity hyperprior's σ̂ is estimated from 162-game MLB seasons; with 17-game NFL seasons the previous-season σ̂² is far noisier, so the shrinkage prior is weaker exactly when NFL needs it most (weeks 1–6). The brief's own gate (beat dynamic Elo on 2015–2025 walk-forward) handles this — keep the gate, do not port on faith. Note also the error metric is rest-of-*season* wins remaining, so the 24.65→8.82 gap is flattered by MLB season length; the proportional gain is the portable part.
