**Status key:**
- `VERIFIED` — re-opened the cited file; the numbers/equations/gates are there as written.
- `VERIFIED*` — re-opened the arXiv **ledger** (deep-read), not the underlying paper; the
  ledger reports these numbers. Paper-level confirmation not performed.
- `PARTIAL` — numbers confirmed present in source (auto-sweep) but not manually re-opened,
  or the claim carries material caveats noted in `notes`.
- `UNVERIFIABLE` — could not be confirmed from the cited file.
- `CONTRADICTED` — the source contradicts the claim, or contradicts itself.
- `NO-CLAIM` — brief is governance/status/meta with no numeric or method claim to verify.

**Standing caveat:** arXiv-ledger claims are only as good as the ledger author's read. Where the
ledger itself contains adversarial notes, those are quoted in `notes` — they are part of the
verification, not decoration.
