## Notes for the build phase

- **No TESTED-REJECTED coaching components exist in c04.** The only rejected coaching-adjacent idea noted is from a different lane (tracking-only DPI classification, F1 0.164 — CV lane, not coaching). ADAPT verdicts (0207, 0247, 0448, 0678, 1684) mean "port the method", not "rejected".
- **The coaching-tendencies pipeline is the single biggest non-duplication risk**: `go4th_rate` and `two_pt_rate` are already computed and profiled (verified tenures, YoY deltas). Rebuilding those descriptive metrics would duplicate; the unbuilt work is the *decision* layer (recommend vs punt/FG/go, optimal vs actual WP deltas).
- **0207's acceptance gate is the most concrete unbuilt spec**: "≥5% Brier improvement over raw MLE on held-out 2026 drives and ≥80% agreement with 4th-down-bot on 200-play audit" — a ready-made build contract for the 4th-down/2-pt/timeout situational WP engine.
- **Gaps explicitly named in the slice**: OL continuity proxy "needs data / unavailable" (micro-edge ledger; also `coaching-tendencies/DATA_GAPS.md`: blitz rate, man/zone, shells, personnel, motion all UNAVAILABLE in nflverse — honesty constraints on what any tendency profile can claim); "there is no coach-decision audit dataset" (map gap #5).
- **Timeout logic**: 1684's NFL analog spec (propensity GAM/GBM + genetic matching, outcome = integrated centered WP over rest-of-half, 2–3 weeks) is the only concrete unbuilt timeout spec; the NBA estimand itself is explicitly "not a GSE product input".
