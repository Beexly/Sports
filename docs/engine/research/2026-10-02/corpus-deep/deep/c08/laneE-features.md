## INFLATION WATCH — overstated brief claims, in plain language

1. **"Pressure EPA is the single most predictive situational split in football."** It's a line of chart direction in a design brief, not a finding. Nobody measured it. Using it as a feature *choice* is fine; repeating the superlative as fact is inflation.
2. **"FPOE/xFP stack = highest-ROI reproduction build."** True as a buildability ranking, false if read as "works best." The corpus then ran the obvious test and it failed. Keep the reproduction path, drop the ROI adjective until a narrow thesis passes preregistration.
3. **"Ready-to-implement OL matchup feature" (ARBY).** Only the weighting is ready; ARBY itself is a paywalled/closed product. Presenting the board's scores as house-replicable understates the rebuild work.
4. **"Physiological-load signal class" as if discovered.** The *existence* of load state is the paper's implicit claim (plausible, untested in football); the *actionability* (five proxies, spike test) is the brief author's invention. Neither has run. "Unpriced by books" is INFERENCE, not measured.
5. **"~95% of HGB's lift" for the drive linear feature.** The report's phrasing; the ablation tested form-vs-linearization, not feature-vs-production-baseline. Its incremental value over the existing feature set is unknown.
6. **Peak ages quoted to two decimals.** 24.53/25.33/26.67 are derivative zeros of one spline fit with 94% pointwise intervals — quoting them bare, without the author's anti-causal caveat or the sample description, inflates a social-media model into a scientific constant. In priors they belong; in prose they need the caveat attached.
7. **"15% exposure miss swamps 2% rate miss" as a measured fact.** It's an illustrative prior in a doc whose header says every magnitude is a prior. The ordering (exposure first) is well-reasoned; the numbers are not measured.
8. **The Dirichlet-multinomial "single biggest coherence win."** Coherence, yes — it fixes genuinely incoherent independent-NB team totals. But "biggest win" is unmeasured against alternatives (copulas, joint GLMs) and no accuracy delta is claimed. It's the most *structurally correct* core proposed in the slice, not the most *validated*.

**Bottom line for the adversary:** lane E's feature families are strong on structure (compositional shares, exposure-first, linearization gates, guarded negative results) and weak on measurement (most magnitudes are priors, most superlatives are asserted, the flagship efficiency stack failed its own holdout). The honest posture: wire the structures, preregister the magnitudes, cite the FAILs out loud.
