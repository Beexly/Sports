## Challenges (weak claims, method problems, INFERENCE-marked speculation)

**Method/evidence problems (source-verified, not vibes):**

1. **1617 adaptive liquidity — pure theory + author's own simulation.** No empirical data; equilibrium under strategic traders explicitly unstudied; the ledger itself REJECTS the full online-learning loop as production pricing. The exposure-monitor and Hessian-identity applications are the ledger's suggestions, not paper results. Treat as a design sketch with a backtest gate (liability↔drawdown rank correlation ≥ 0.3), not an adopted method.

2. **2062 TabDDPM — internal count inconsistency + circularity.** The source says 15 benchmark datasets but "all 16 sets" in the DCR table (brief §4 repeats both); cite per-table. ML efficiency is tuned w.r.t. CatBoost and evaluated w.r.t. CatBoost — mildly circular (source admits; Appendix A mitigates via MLP transfer). No time-ordered splits: temporal leakage, the core NFL risk, is entirely untested. IN (0.868) membership-inference outlier unexplained. The fidelity gate (TVD ≤5% on ≥90% of features) is necessary but not sufficient — fidelity on marginals doesn't certify joint temporal structure.

3. **2090 SHARP — equity results, short window, brittle arm.** 2025–2026 window is thin on regimes; GPT-4.1-mini's −27.3% max DD on the same method shows the evolved policy is backbone-sensitive; free-form reflection being worse than static is evidence for constraint, not evidence the rubric transfers to discrete sports bets. The ε walk-forward tolerance is hand-set. Gate on the A2 replication + attribution-precision check the ledger names.

4. **2046 AutoAlpha — finance backtest, selection risk.** 90% AR / 3.39 SR is a mined-backtest number on CSI 300 with 0.3% costs; "diverse" means PCA-first-component correlation < 0.9, which does not rule out shared tail exposure. The ~12× diversity multiple is vs gplearn, a weak baseline. The NFL grammar port is entirely INFERENCE — no sports result exists.

5. **1630 Kelly — known-p assumption, no miscalibration analysis.** The .659 fraction is derived under known p; with estimated p the inflation factor (1+w/g) multiplies estimation error too. The cap (max 2×) is a hand-set rail. Must run downstream of the δ/σ gate (1748), never standalone.

6. **1676 soft-global — post-break degradation + correlated errors.** Authors' own finding: post-structural-break, soft-global struggles and EW is recommended. NFL seasons are break-rich (injuries, QB changes). LOO-CV γ tuning assumes uncorrelated combination errors (Bergmeir 2018) — sports ensembles share features and errors, violating the assumption. The 0.921 MSFE/EW is pre-break ECB data, not sports.

7. **2031 Pathway — vendor benchmarks, upper-bound margins.** All authors are Pathway.com staff; the 40–48× streaming margins are self-reported and should be treated as upper bounds (the source itself says this). Zero football relevance — adopt only the policy (batch/stream parity, frozen-prefix hash CI), not the numbers.

8. **1605 TimeSoccer — chunked variant beats the headline.** The 3-min×15 variant outperforms end-to-end on every reported metric (P@0.3 22.5 vs 17.0), weakening the "single forward pass" selling point. Absolute precision is modest (P@0.9 = 3.4%), no code released, caption quality LLM-judged not human, soccer→NFL transfer unvalidated. The genuinely reusable piece is MoFA-Select, not the commentary model.

9. **1701 decathlon — baseline wins on pure prediction.** Tail CV 0.358 baseline vs 0.362 compositional; the ADAPT verdict rests on interpretability + the weather-scaffold INFERENCE, not a predictive win. The paper contains no weather data at all — the NFL weather adaptation is entirely speculative.

10. **1711 sprint — simulated only, small effect.** 0.02 s temperature effect is within timing noise; drag-only pathway ignores athlete physiology (which dominates for endurance, and plausibly for late-game fatigue); no empirical validation. The ledger's own gate (significance in NGS top-speed regression) is appropriately humble.

11. **2142 AC-RAC — wrong domains, discrete-action limit.** Medical + recommender experiments, not sports; finite discrete actions only — continuous stake sizing needs discretization (information loss); the discrete-outcome upper-bound condition fails for W/L (ledger flags); conditional coverage at 2.72% selection is hard to estimate; FDR 0.699 means the certified sets are wide. Certificate ≠ profitability — utility must be defined as realized profit and backtested.

The convergence theorem (weights → truth a.s.) assumes a stable data-generating measure — directly contradicted by the corpus's own regime-shift findings. Dynamic inconsistency is discussed but unresolved; the ledger's "resolute choice" guard (season plan fixed, MWER only vetoes weeks) is INFERENCE beyond the paper.
