	4. Automated Parlay Market Maker (APMM / LOPMM arXiv:2607.18299) and SGP Joint Pricing
4.1 Hierarchical Parameterization and Low-Order Dominance (arXiv:2607.18299)
A foundational mathematical mechanism integrated into the GSE SGP pricing engine is the Automated Parlay Market Maker (APMM) and Low-Order Parlay Market Maker (LOPMM) formalism of Moshrefi, Rana, and Viswanath (2026, arXiv:2607.18299v1/v2/v3). Parlays—joint contingent contracts over a subset of base events S \subseteq [M] of order m = |S| with outcome space \Omega_S = \{0, 1\}^S (\vert{}\Omega_S\vert{} = 2^{\vert{}S\vert{}})—account for roughly 27\% of traditional sportsbook handle and surged from under 3\% to 22\% of total Kalshi exchange volume within six months of launch. On the 2026 Kalshi NBA Same-Game Parlay corpus (April–June 2026: 92 games, 29,781 trades across 7,372 distinct parlay markets over 2,907 base markets), empirical order-flow analysis establishes that belief updates are dominated by low-order interactions: 2-leg parlays account for 95.7\% of all trades, with trade counts decaying monotonically in leg count (k = 7, \rho = 0.32, \nu = 6.7 \times 10^{-3}).
In a standard Logarithmic Market Scoring Rule (LMSR) with liquidity parameter b > 0 and share state vector q \in \mathbb{R}^\Omega, the cost function and instantaneous prices are C(q) = b \ln\left(\sum_{\omega \in \Omega} e^{q_\omega / b}\right) and \pi_\omega(q) = \frac{\partial C(q)}{\partial q_\omega} = \frac{e^{q_\omega / b}}{\sum_{\omega'} e^{q_{\omega'} / b}}, where the operator's expected loss weighted by closing prices \pi(q) equals the Kullback–Leibler divergence b \, \text{KL}(\pi(q) \,\|\, \pi_0). However, across M base markets there exist 2^M - 1 non-empty leg-sets S \subseteq [M] and \sum_{k=1}^M \binom{M}{k} 2^k = 3^M - 1 distinct parlay contracts. Running 2^M - 1 independent LMSR books quotes mutually contradictory prices and forces the market maker to subsidize the same base-market information exponentially many times, whereas ParlayMarket (arXiv:2603.22596) fits a global pairwise Ising model via belief propagation that bounds expected loss at O(M^2) rather than worst-case exposure.
APMM and LOPMM (arXiv:2607.18299) resolve this structural bottleneck through a Hierarchical Parameterization across the combinatorial lattice of parlays. For each non-empty leg-set S \subseteq [M] (\vert{}S\vert{} \le k) and outcome pattern \omega_S \in \Omega_S, APMM stores a dedicated parameter block r^{(S)}_{\omega_S} \in \mathbb{R}, and defines the effective share count Q^{(S)}_{\omega_S} on book S as the sum of its own parameter block and every consistent lower-order parameter block S' \subseteq S (where \omega_S|_{S'} = \omega_{S'}):
Q^{(S)}_{\omega_S} = \sum_{\emptyset \neq S' \subseteq S} r^{(S')}_{\omega_S|_{S'}}, \qquad \pi^{(S)}_{\omega_S}(Q^{(S)}) = \frac{\exp\left(Q^{(S)}_{\omega_S} / b_S\right)}{\sum_{\tilde{\omega}_S \in \Omega_S} \exp\left(Q^{(S)}_{\tilde{\omega}_S} / b_S\right)}
When a causal belief update or informed trader reports a target marginal distribution \tau^{\text{new}}_S \in \Delta(\Omega_S) on leg-set S (with induced marginal \tau^{\text{new}}_{S'} on every subset S[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span)[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span)[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span)' \subseteq S), the mechanism routes a collection of sub-trades bottom-up in ascending order of |S'| from the base markets (\vert{}S'\vert{} = 1) up to S itself. Because every parameter block r^{(S'')} for strictly smaller subsets |S''| < |S'| is already fixed when book S' is reached, the operator sets the single order-|S'| interaction block r^{(S')} to drive book S' to \tau^{\text{new}}_{S'}, applying a uniform additive gauge shift r^{(S')} \leftarrow r^{(S')} + c_{S'}\mathbf{1} (which shifts Q^{(S'')} uniformly for all S'' \supseteq S' and leaves prices \pi^{(S')} and net resolution loss L \propto b \ln(\pi^{(S)}_{\omega_S} / \pi^{(S)}_{0,\omega_S}) completely invariant) so that every sub-trade is executed as a buy-only realization. Under low-order dominance, APMM bounds expected market-maker loss linearly at O(M)—matching the order of cost of running the M base markets alone—and worst-case loss quadratically at [span_35](start_span)[span_35](end_span)[span_36](start_span)[span_36](end_span)[span_37](start_span)[span_37](end_span)O(M^2), while LOPMM executes per-parlay quotes in \Theta(4^m) operations and full trades in \Theta(5^m) operations, independent of the total number of base events M.
