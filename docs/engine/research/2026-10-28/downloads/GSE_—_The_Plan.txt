## CONTEXT — the five facts that decide everything

1. **The NFL game market is closed.** ~190 specifications tested out of sample. The closing line
   is efficient (home covers 49.05% on n=7,081, 27 seasons). No defensive trait is mispriced
   (14 tests, none reach |z|=1). Published edges decay after publication (wind: real for 20
   years, dead by 2025). Our own team model hit 49.83% ATS on 895 held-out games.
   **Nobody spends another hour trying to beat NFL sides or totals with public data.**

2. **Our engine has negative standalone skill and a real orthogonal signal.** Head-to-head on
   540 identical picks: model Brier 0.251, market 0.220, a constant 0.248. But corr(model,
   outcome) = +0.146 while corr(model, market) = +0.128. **At 10% weight blended toward the
   market it beats the market out of sample** (0.20818 vs 0.20905; football 0.18825 vs 0.18946).
   That is the one lever on the engine, and it is a calibration gain, not an edge.

3. **The product loses money and the record is baseball.** Only computable market: 180–44,
   80.36%, **−2.88% ROI** (break-even at −373 is 82.12%). Record is **72.5% MLB, 3.1% NFL**;
   **86% of the NFL record is preseason**; regular-season NFL = **11 picks**. Spreads/totals:
   1,459 settled, **zero** stored prices, ~46.5% win rate.

4. **The only validated NFL model is props.** Brier skill 4.2% over climatology, 0 decile
   inversions, 12,571 out-of-sample predictions. Blocked by: `PickType` has no prop value and
   `@@unique([gameId,pickType])`; ingest fetches 2 of ~13 markets at a credit cap of 8; the
   prop archive has **zero readers**; `/fantasy/props` is 12 fictional players on a normal CDF.

5. **Zero customers.** 2 users (one is a Stripe test), 2 checkout attempts ever (last 08-19,
   three weeks *before* the 503 began), 1 waitlist lead, 0 product events. The constraint is
   demand. And **the repo's problem is not missing capability** — 22 of 25 "advanced" techniques
   already exist; `pfr_adv_stats`, fixture tombstoning, in-play disclosure, the Pinnacle
   archive were all *built and never wired*.

## PRODUCT DEFINITION — locked, so nobody re-derives it

**Galaxy Sports Edge is an NFL-first product that publishes three things:**

1. **NFL player props** — the validated model, graded against posted lines.
2. **Market-anchored NFL game probabilities** — the engine shrunk 90% toward the market, with
   honest calibration and a vig-free exchange reference. Sold as *calibration*, never as edge.
3. **The public record of what does not work** — the reliability table, the 190 dead
   hypotheses, the decay curves. The only position in this market that cannot be copied by a
   competitor with something to hide.

**It stops publishing:** MLB spreads and totals (45.5%/45.4%), NFL preseason, soccer
moneylines, and any row with no book price. Volume that loses is not volume.
