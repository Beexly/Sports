ABSTRACT
A data logger for a monitoring sports which includes an
accelerometer, a GPS unit to sense position and velocity, a
heart rate monitor, a controller programmed to manipulate the
data and provide a display of the heart rate, speed, and other
sport parameters. The data can be stored or transmitted to a
remote computer for use by the coach. The device is useful in
football codes, athletics, snow sports and cycling.
