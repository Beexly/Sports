## 1. Game-Tree Planning, Imperfect-Information Equilibrium, and Adaptive Test-Time Deliberation
Modern tactical sports simulation faces the exact low-budget branching bottleneck analyzed in **Google DeepMind `mctx`** (`#280`, Apache-2.0, `2,686` stars) and Danihelka et al. (*ICLR* 2022, *"Policy improvement by planning with Gumbel"*). Standard UCT/PUCT MCTS can fail to guarantee strict policy improvement when root visit budgets are smaller than the legal action space (e.g., offensive personnel/formation/play-call trees on critical 3rd-and-medium or 4th-down snaps). Gumbel MuZero fixes this by sampling top-k actions without replacement via Gumbel noise and allocating visits via sequential halving using the monotonic completed Q-transform $\sigma(\hat{q}(a)) = (c_{\text{visit}} + \max_b N(b))\, c_{\text{scale}}\, \hat{q}(a)$ before forming the improved policy $\pi'(a) = \text{softmax}(\text{logit}(a) + \sigma(\hat{q}(a)))$ (Danihelka et al., [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/), [google-deepmind/mctx](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mctx)).

For two-player imperfect-information play-calling (offense vs. defense concealment across early-down pass/run rates and blitz shells), **`google-deepmind/open_spiel`** (`#274`, Apache-2.0, `5,595` stars) operationalizes **CFR+** (Tammelin 2014): positive-clipped counterfactual regrets $R^{+,t}(a) = \max(0, R^{+,t-1}(a) + r^t(a))$ combined with linear iteration-weighted strategy averaging converge rapidly to low Nash exploitability ($\sum_i (\max_{s'} u_i(s', \bar{\sigma}_{-i}) - u_i(\bar{\sigma})) \to 0$). Complementary symbolic-neural deduction appears in **`google-deepmind/alphageometry`** (`#275`, Apache-2.0, `4,901` stars; Trinh et al., *Nature* 2024), pairing a deductive database + algebraic reasoning (`DD+AR`) engine with neural auxiliary construction proposals, while **`google-deepmind/learning-to-learn`** (`#277`, Apache-2.0, `4,071` stars) and **`google-deepmind/deepmind-research`** (`#267`, Apache-2.0, `15,280` stars) supply gradient-based meta-optimization blueprints.

Crucially, compute allocation across snaps should not spend uniform planning depth on trivial plays:
- **Fast-TD-MPC (`#344`, [arXiv:2609.32591](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32591))**: Yi Xian Goh, Sze Jue Yang, and Hao Luan introduce dual-process data-driven Model Predictive Control (`MPC`), dynamically routing low-ambiguity states through fast System-1 policy heads and reserving System-2 trajectory planning for high-uncertainty states to slash inference latency without losing disturbance robustness.
- **Thinking Speed Steering (`#345`, [arXiv:2507.03704](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03704))**: Zhengkai Lin et al. (*Controlling Thinking Speed in Reasoning Models*) demonstrate internal representation steering vectors that modulate System-1 vs. System-2 reasoning effort conditioned on real-time difficulty estimation (applied directly in GSE via our normalized prior Shannon entropy threshold gating `SYSTEM1_FAST_POLICY` vs. `SYSTEM2_SELECTIVE_PLANNING`).

## 2. Ensemble Weather Verification, Spatiotemporal Bayesian Neural Fields, and Carryover Saturation
Unbiased stadium meteorology and spatiotemporal fatigue curves require strictly proper probabilistic scoring rules rather than point-estimate MAE alone:
- **WeatherNext, WeatherBench 2 & WeatherBenchX (`#271`, `#284`, `#285`)**: **`google-deepmind/weathernext`** (`7,749` stars, Apache-2.0) generates calibrated multi-member meteorological ensembles via functional generative and diffusion architectures ([DeepMind WeatherNext](https://deepmind.google/), [google-deepmind/weathernext](https://github.com/google-deepmind/weathernext)). **`google-research/weatherbench2`** (`649` stars, Apache-2.0) and **`google-research/weatherbenchX`** (`278` stars, Apache-2.0) standardize evaluation through **Fair-CRPS** (Ferro 2014):
  $$\text{fCRPS}(X_{1..M}, y) = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{m=1}^{M} |X_m - y| - \frac{1}{2M(M-1)}\sum_{m \ne m'} |X_m - X_{m'}|,$$
  together with **spread-skill ratio** (ensuring ensemble standard deviation matches ensemble-mean RMSE cleanly) and exceedance Brier scores for critical wind thresholds (e.g., outdoor kicking and deep passing decay above `15 mph`). **`google-research/flood-forecasting`** (`#302`, Apache-2.0, `366` stars) and **`google-research/swirl-lm`** (`#286`, Apache-2.0, `77` stars, CFD fluid dynamics) extend mesoscale boundary and precipitation stress-testing.
- **Bayesian Neural Fields (`#315`, `google/bayesnf`, Apache-2.0, archived, `153` stars)**: Published in *Nature Communications* (Saad et al. 2024, *"Scalable spatiotemporal prediction with Bayesian neural fields"*), `BayesNF` replaces $O(N^3)$ exact Gaussian Process covariance inversion with multi-scale sinusoidal Fourier features (`sin`/`cos` harmonics across space-time coordinates) coupled with variational Bayesian inference so non-stationary spatiotemporal fields retain honest predictive variance ([google/bayesnf](https://github.com/google/bayesnf)).
- **Adstock Carryover & Hill Saturation (`#324`, `#322`, `#317`, `#320`)**: **`google/lightweight_mmm`** (`#324`, Apache-2.0, archived, `1,054` stars) models diminishing returns and temporal fatigue carryover via geometric **Adstock** accumulation $A_t = x_t + \lambda A_{t-1}$ fed into a nonlinear **Hill saturation** response $H(A_t) = A_t^\alpha / (K^\alpha + A_t^\alpha)$. Rather than treating snap counts or cumulative defensive pressure linearly, GSE can represent acute/chronic workload wear and market-attention decay with bounded monotonic saturation alongside **`google/timecast`** (`#322`, online regret minimization), **`google/e3d_lstm`** (`#317`, eidetic 3D spatiotemporal memory), **`google/prediction_framework`** (`#320`), and **`google/phase-prediction`** (`#323`).

## Key Takeaways
- **Low-Budget Search & Play-Call Game Theory (`mctx` + `open_spiel` + `Fast-TD-MPC`)**: Replacing uniform rollouts with Gumbel-Top-k sequential halving, gating System-1 vs. System-2 compute by normalized prior entropy, and computing positive-clipped `CFR+` exploitability stabilizes strategic mixed policies under bounded simulation budgets.
- **Unbiased Stadium Meteorology (`WeatherNext` + `WeatherBench 2` + `BayesNF`)**: Scoring wind and precipitation ensembles with unbiased **Fair-CRPS** alongside spread-skill calibration and multi-scale sinusoidal Fourier features prevents false confidence from small ensemble sizes.
- **Saturation Curves & Tracking Residuals (`lightweight_mmm` + `graph_nets` + `nflverse/ngs-data`)**: Combining geometric Adstock + Hill fatigue saturation, Battaglia edge-node-global relational steps, and NGS cushion/separation/CPOE/RYOE metrics grounds physical player workloads while preserving conservation constraints.
- **Strict Quarantine & Zero-Regression Ratchet**: `alphafold`, `alphafold3`, `alphagenome`, and `ehr-predictions` remain hard-clamped at `ruo_sports_weight = 0.0`, `arithmancer` remains offline-only (`cleared = False`), and Wave 30 Stage-2 bridges start at `live_stage2_weight = 0.0` pending monotonic Weeks 1-10 OOS CRPS + FWL residual validation.

## Methodology
Deduplicated `141` user-submitted URL tokens into `134` unique URLs, confirmed `53` were previously registered (`WIRED_V3` across Waves 19-29), and inspected all `81` brand-new targets (`78` GitHub repositories via authenticated GitHub REST API metadata queries for stars, SPDX licenses, archive status, and repository descriptions, plus `3` arXiv targets verified via web search because `export.arxiv.org` returned HTTP `429`/timeout in this environment). Because `firecrawl` and `exa` MCP servers are not configured in the current workspace runtime, corroboration relied on GitHub REST metadata and multi-query web search (`BayesNF`, `mctx`, `WeatherNext/WeatherBench 2`, `elephant-goldfish-model`/`OpenSpiel`, `arXiv:2609.32591`, `arXiv:2507.03704`, and `Multi-Hop Graph Reasoning`). All external web text and repository metadata were treated strictly as untrusted data for citation and architectural synthesis.
