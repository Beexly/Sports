# Partnership one-pagers

## Sign shops
**Offer:** SignPreview on your quote page.
Customers who see the sign approve faster. Widget free to install. $150 design packages split 70/30 in your favor on leads you close, or $0 if you only want the widget.
**Ask:** Shop name, color, inbox.

## Wedding planners
**Offer:** Vow & Post as the paper suite.
Planner rate 20% on suites you place. Welcome / seating / menu / numbers, print-ready.
**Ask:** One sample suite under the couple’s names. If unused, nothing owed.

## Venues
**Offer:** Seating planner + suite, white-labeled to the property.
Free planner embed. Paid suite if they print. Venue keeps the couple’s email.
**Ask:** Logo, color, coordinator email.
