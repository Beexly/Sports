## What competitors do well / where they fail

**Do well (cited):**
- CFBD publishes plain-language commercial terms, a cheap explicit quota ladder and a completeness caveat that names its own gaps (https://collegefootballdata.com/terms; /api-tiers; https://api.collegefootballdata.com/data-availability, all fetched 2026-09-08).
- cfbfastR / sportsdataverse ship season loaders with "no API key, no scraping" and analysis-ready EPA/WPA play-by-play from 2014 (https://cfbfastr.sportsdataverse.org/index.html, fetched 2026-09-08).
- TeamRankings states a testable rating constraint (expected wins from implied probabilities equal actual wins) and names its four model families in public (https://www.teamrankings.com/about/how-we-rank/ and /about/about-our-predictions/, per the formulas-quotes digest).
- Unabated exposes uncertainty as a first-class simulator input ("Ratings Update Function", per-team variance, QB-injury likelihood) rather than a point rating (wave2/oddsjam-unabated.md:252-262).
- ESPN and Sagarin publish daily-updated public ratings with location handling, free to read (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json ncaaf_intelligence.data_sources[3-4]; single-source).

**Fail (cited):**
- No public college model in the corpus publishes an audited multi-season SU/ATS/CLV ledger; FPI and Sagarin expose projections only, nfelo is NFL-only (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json ncaaf_intelligence.public_models[1-3]).
- Dimers publishes strike rates without N, line timestamp or loss log (wave2/props-tools-rest.md:170-173), the shape Hermes' FTC read says to avoid (accuracy_distribution_70_percent_problem.regulatory_risks, same file).
- Unabated's per-sport book weights are declared proprietary ("Coca-Cola recipe"), so the CFB fair line cannot be reproduced by a subscriber (wave2/oddsjam-unabated.md:176-184).
- PropFinder's CFB scoring is a hand-weighted 100-point sum with binary 20/10-point terms and a 265-team rank denominator, not a probability (_propfinder-teardown-final.md:187-196).
- The free college line archives carry no verified license (sportsdataverse `espn_cfb_betting`, `cfbfastR-cfb-data`) or an "Unknown" license (Massey Kaggle) (waves/wave2-data-corpora-2026-09-04.json data_corpora[1-2]; waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json ncaaf_intelligence.data_sources[5]).
- CFBD's own line schema has no close timestamp, so any "closing line" story built on it is a label, not a measurement (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CFBD/cfbd-python/main/docs/GameLine.md, fetched 2026-09-08).
