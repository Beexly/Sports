## Key insights

1. **The doctrine is one rule: fire and rank on calibrated edge `e = p - q`, never on confidence `kappa = max(p, 1-p)`.** The handoff states it verbatim: "A 90%-confident favorite has ~0 edge because the line already prices it. Confidence-gating = betting chalk at -EV = scores24's fatal error. This is the single most important modeling rule." (_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:18; restated _MASTER-gse-strategic-dossier.md:33). The edge lab derives the same thing: "a 0.90 favorite has kappa=0.90 and ZERO edge because the line already prices it; selecting on kappa fires on chalk at negative EV" (_gse-edge-lab-final.md:9). The scores24 evidence behind the phrase is a Feb-20-2026 listing where a "100%" confidence tip was priced at -111 (52.6% implied) and a "93%" tip at -104 (51.0%), with 100%/-200 (66.7%), 90%/-141 (58.5%) and 96%/-133 (57.1%) on the same table, i.e. the badge is a streak hit-rate ("covered +1.5 in 36 of last 37"), structurally trapped at 80-100%, and decoupled from the pick's own market probability (_scores24-teardown-final.md:171, :184). scores24 also publishes zero own track record across every capture Feb-July 2026 (_scores24-teardown-final.md:184). The repo's own edge-lab note carries the closed-form version: a published probability `(1-alpha)*p + alpha*q` leaves residual edge `(1-alpha)(p-q)`, so alpha = 0.90 keeps 10% of the independent gap, which is why GSE fires on `e = p - q` and never on a re-anchored probability (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/edge-lab/market-consensus-q.ts:4-11). The doctrine-correct gate exists in code, headed "Fire on calibrated edge e = pLo - q, never confidence" (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/honesty/no-bet-gate.ts:1-5).

2. **The board violated the doctrine until 2026-09-08, and the fix is in code on PR #720, not on this checkout.** Measured at the start of the launch-decision session: `selectiveRecommended.edge = null`, so public selection reduced to `|p - 0.5| >= 0.12`, and 387 of 428 published moneyline picks carried no market probability at all, making `rawEdge` literally `trueProb - 0.5` (data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §1a). 232 of those 387 had a real bookmaker quoting both sides in GSE's own `odds` table at or before `generatedAt`; C-253 wired the calibration loader's resolver into the publish path, lifting the measured ceiling on the moneyline board from 41/428 to 273/428 (data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §1a). On this checkout the runtime still builds thresholds with `edge: plan?.selectiveRecommended?.edge ?? null` (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/selective-publish-runtime.ts:70), and the sweep's default grid is 6 deltas x 3 edges x 1 = at least 18 cells scored on the same rows that then report the winner's metrics (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/selective-publish.ts:162-180; wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:229-237). Still founder-gated: the tuner and `/api/picks/route.ts` resolve `marketP` from different places (48 of 435 rows carry the top-level `factorBreakdown.marketFairProb`), so a threshold tuned on one population would be enforced on another (data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §1a, §6 item 3).

3. **The market's de-vigged probability beats every score GSE produces, on GSE's own surface, with two recorded confounds.** `/api/ops/public-surface-truth` carries `"bestScore": "marketFairProb"`; the market beats GSE's best score on Brier (0.2337 vs 0.2474), ECE (0.0280 vs 0.0838) and Murphy resolution (0.0185 vs 0.0112), and on moneylines the confidence score's separation is -0.0018 at n 745 (data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §1c). Confound one: the scores are not on an identical row set (market coverage 0.774 vs confidence 1.000), so the size of the gap is not established; confound two: the outcomes are the C-247 record, in which 25/169 MLB, 14/48 MLS and 2/37 NCAAF stored FINAL scores disagree with ESPN's own API, 68 settled published picks sit on them and 8 moneyline results are the opposite of what happened (data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §1b, §1c). The launch command sheet records the per-score n on the truth surface as 1823 / 1132 / 1132 / 1039 and dispatches WP-7 "identical-row-set bake-off on the truth surface" on branch `claude/launch-identical-row-bakeoff` (Sports/docs/ops/LAUNCH_COMMAND_SHEET_2026-09-08.md:255; Sports/docs/ops/AGENT_LEDGER.md:353). The `bestScore` selector requires an independent or blend score kind to hold at least 40% of the moneyline/spread eligible n before it can win (`MIN_COVERAGE = 0.4`, Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/ranking-power-control.ts:150-151), which is a partial guard against thin-tail winners but not an identical-row comparison. The handoff names the identical-row-set bake-off as "the measurement nobody has run, and the next session should" (data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §1c, §7 item 1).

4. **Why the market wins is structural: the confidence score is a scorecard, not a probability, and 0 of its 100 points come from an independent estimate of who wins.** The shipping formula is an unweighted sum of hand-set components (consensus 0-30, market depth 0-20, edge component 0-25, volatility -15-0, line movement +/-15, historical form +/-10, data quality -15-0, head-to-head +/-5, venue +/-5, uncertainty -8-0, cross-market -3..+4, schedule stress +/-5, plus a bare constant 10), clamped to 0-100 (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:43-58). Positive caps sum to at least 129 into a clamp at 100, so the top of the scale is many-to-one (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:67-69). `MIN_PUBLISH_CONFIDENCE = 50` (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/constants.ts:31) combined with `rankingP = confidence/100` (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/ranking-prob.ts:57-59) means every published pick asserts p >= 0.50 by construction (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:71-76). Bookmaker count enters three additive terms worth up to 35 of 100 points (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:114-120), and roughly 44 points of the +/- range are step functions on unfitted thresholds (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:129-143). The "Pricing Edge" component reduces algebraically to `rawEdge = -q_offered * (Sigma-1)/Sigma`, the hold times the price, negated, so a vanilla -110/-110 market scores ~6.5/25 and prints public Edge Index 26 (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:80-94). The independent path (Poisson, Skellam, Elo, FPI, Kalshi, Dixon-Coles) enters only through `deriveRankingProbability`, where the priced path is `0.3 x (confidence/100) + 0.7 x trueProb` via `independentWeight ?? 0.7` (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/ranking-prob.ts:85; wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:153-163). The production confidence tail confirms the category error: picks claiming 86.69% won 52.11% (n 213), worst on SPREAD (44.44% won vs 88.04% claimed, n 99) and TOTAL (25.64% vs 83.85%, n 39), least bad on MONEYLINE (76% vs 86.37%, n 75) (Sports/docs/ops/LAUNCH_COMMAND_SHEET_2026-09-08.md:281, 292-294).

5. **The PROVEN calibration number does not measure GSE's forecast; it measures the sportsbook's price on GSE's selected slate.** The eligibility resolver `resolveMarketAnchoredCalibrationP` reads, in order, the proof receipt's `marketFairProb`, then the factor-breakdown `marketFairProb`, then the odds-table recompute, and "confidence/100 is never a fallback for the floors" (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/live-calibration-p.ts:199-217). Every one of those is the de-vigged book price (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:180-184). The moneyline scorer additionally refuses `fairProb < 0.58` (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/scoring.ts:965), so the eligibility sample is settled two-way moneyline favourites scored on the book's own probability, and the MLB reading (n 365, hit 0.649 vs meanP 0.648) is the book's calibration on favourites restated (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:184-189; Sports/AGENTS.md:70-71). The audit flags as INFERENCE, not measurement, that a market-anchored ECE passing 0.05 is consistent with the book being right on the games GSE chose, i.e. with no selection edge, and says not to put that in copy until the per-bin decomposition is run (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:191-200, 404-406). The receipt method is `mean_implied_proportional_devig` (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/publish-time-market-p.ts:89) and Shin is not on the receipt path (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/publish-time-market-p.ts:13-16).

6. **The four PROVEN floors are n >= 100, Brier <= 0.22, ECE <= 0.05, Murphy REL <= 0.05, three consecutive GREEN runs; two of them bind weakly and one binds by estimator noise.** Floors: `DEFAULT_CALIBRATION_FLOORS = { n: 100, brier: 0.22, ece: 0.05, murphyReliability: 0.05 }` (Sports/apps/web/lib/ops/calibration-eligibility.ts:69-74); streak default 3 via `CALIBRATION_ELIGIBILITY_STREAK` (Sports/apps/web/lib/ops/calibration-eligibility-durable.ts:552-556); cron `40 */6 * * *` (Sports/apps/web/vercel.json crons[18]). Brier floor: at base rate 0.69 the uncertainty term alone is `0.69 * 0.31 = 0.2139`, so a constant base-rate forecast with no skill clears 0.22 (Sports/AGENTS.md:40-42; Sports/docs/ops/CALIBRATION_GATE_SCALE_2026-09-06.md:31-42); in skill-score units the floor is `BSS >= 1 - 0.22/0.2139 = -0.0285`, permitting a forecaster 2.85% worse than climatology, while the measured 0.1898 is BSS +0.113 (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:178-182). Murphy REL floor: it averages squared per-bin gaps against the same literal 0.05, so it permits a 22.4-point RMS gap where ECE permits 5.0, a 4.47x difference in strictness (Sports/AGENTS.md:43-45; CALIBRATION_GATE_SCALE_2026-09-06.md:49-59); by Cauchy-Schwarz `REL >= ECE^2` always, so a REL floor of 0.05 permits ECE up to 0.2236, and under perfect calibration `E[REL] ~ K_eff * 0.25 / n = 0.0026` at n 475 and five bins against a measured 0.005 (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:189-194). ECE floor: GSE uses the plug-in equal-width 10-bin estimator (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/ece.ts:2-3; Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/probability-calibration.ts:380-391), and under perfect calibration its expectation is roughly `0.399 * sqrt(K_eff / n)`, i.e. 0.037-0.058 at n 475 for K_eff 4-10 against a measured 0.0466 (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:138-152). That is a derivation under a stated model, not a measurement of the live bin occupancy, which is NOT CONFIRMED (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:154, 321).

7. **The v5.2.7 stratum caveat: the pooled ECE sits below every model version it is built from, and the deployed version measures about twice the floor on its own rows.** On 2026-09-06 (16:40 UTC) the pool read n 458, ECE 0.0524, and per version v5.2.7 (n 245) 0.1089, v5.2.6 (n 110) 0.0587, v5.1.0 (n 74) 0.0729, v5.0.0 (n 29) 0.1531; `expectedCalibrationError` stores weighted absolute per-bin gaps, so signed cancellation across strata is "a plausible mechanism, not an observed one" (Sports/AGENTS.md:57-66). On 2026-09-08 (19:01 UTC) the truth surface read GREEN with consecutiveGreen 10 against streakRequired 3, n 475, ECE 0.0466, and the v5.2.7 stratum 0.0947 on 262 rows, with nothing flipped (Sports/docs/ops/AGENT_LEDGER.md:353; Sports/docs/ops/LAUNCH_COMMAND_SHEET_2026-09-08.md:281-282). The literature dossier offers the boring explanation: plug-in binned ECE is positively biased with bias growing as sqrt(bins/n), so any partition of a sample reads higher in every stratum than in the pool even if every stratum is perfectly calibrated, and 0.0947 at n 262 is near the noise band 0.049-0.078 for that n (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:17, 149, 160). Kumar, Liang and Ma state the plug-in estimators are less accurate and that their estimator needs O(sqrt(B)) rather than O(B) samples (https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.10155, fetched 2026-09-08 ~19:40Z); Roelofs et al. state that "binning-based estimators with bins of equal mass (number of instances) have lower bias than estimators with bins of equal width" (https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/roelofs22a.html, fetched 2026-09-08 ~19:40Z). The handoff keeps this founder-owned: "no floor exists per stratum; report it beside the pooled number" (Sports/docs/ops/LAUNCH_COMMAND_SHEET_2026-09-08.md:282; data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §6 item 4).

8. **The three-run streak is one sample looked at three times.** Over ~21 days GSE accumulated ~475 settled moneyline picks, ~22.6 per day, ~5.7 per six-hour run, so two consecutive runs share about 469/475 = 98.7% of their sample and repeated peeking at a growing sample against a fixed threshold inflates type-I error rather than confirming anything (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:211-215). GSE already owns the anytime-valid instrument: `forecast-skill-eprocess.ts` is a sequential likelihood-ratio martingale against the market null `y_t | F_{t-1} ~ Bernoulli(m_t)`, with `E[E_t | F_{t-1}] = 1` exactly, Ville's inequality cited, and the header rule "THE RUNNING MAXIMUM IS THE STATISTIC, NOT THE FINAL VALUE ... A crossing at pick 87 is a valid alpha-level rejection" (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/forecast-skill-eprocess.ts:1-59). The same header separates skill-vs-market, profit (`anytime-ledger.ts`) and self-honesty (`calibration-sequence.ts`) as three instruments allowed to disagree (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/forecast-skill-eprocess.ts:7-22). The dossier's replacement proposal is an e-process crossing AND a calibration confidence sequence AND settlement health, explicitly not a shorter streak or a lower floor (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:221-223). Replacing the streak changes what PROVEN means and is therefore a founder decision (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:315).

9. **De-vig: GSE runs three methods on three paths, the literature prefers Shin, and the disagreement can exceed the gate margin.** Live pick path: `removeVig` proportional normalisation (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/scoring.ts:70) stamped `marketFairMethod: "proportional"` (scoring.ts:638, 847, 1127), receipts minted as mean-implied-per-side then proportional two-way de-vig with `MIN_BOOKMAKERS = 2` (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/publish-time-market-p.ts:6-16; Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/constants.ts:105), and since C-110 a single real book resolves as `market_p_single_book` (publish-time-market-p.ts:188-191; Sports/docs/ops/AGENT_LEDGER.md:288). Market-description path: Shin per book, median across books, in `consensusNoVig` (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/market-read.ts:95-98; shin-devig.ts:43 `shinDevig`, :80 `gotoConversion`, :115 `powerDevig`). Reference oracle: seven methods (multiplicative, additive, power, shin, differential_margin_weighting, odds_ratio, logarithmic) in `devig()` (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/devig/oracle.ts:16-23, :187) plus `proportionalDevig` and `shinDevig` in edge-lab (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/edge-lab/devig.ts:62, :119). The honesty comparator exposes multiplicative vs Shin vs goto with a longshot-inflation diagnostic and says "Not wired into pick minting. MODEL_VERSION untouched." (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/honesty/devig-method-compare.ts:1-12). Strumbelj (IJF 2014) found Shin probabilities better than basic normalisation for every bookmaker/sport pair tested (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:254-256, citing https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0169207014000533; not re-fetched). On a two-way book at 4-5% hold the proportional-vs-Shin disagreement on the longshot side is "comfortably in the 0.005-0.015 range", larger than the 0.0034 margin the gate was decided on (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:260; wave2/_BUILD-PLAN.md:126); that magnitude on GSE's own odds table is NOT VERIFIED (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:324). OddsJam publishes four methods and states Shin collapses to additive on two-way markets, which wave2 verified numerically; multiplicative vs additive differ by 1.34 percentage points on the favourite at -1200/+800 and are identical at -110/-110; OpticOdds documents multiplicative-only production de-vig; Unabated's book weights are "our own Coca-Cola recipe: it's proprietary" (wave2/oddsjam-unabated.md:18-27, 88-95).

10. **"Consensus de-vig per arXiv 1710.02824" is only partly what it sounds like.** The paper (Kaunitz, Zhong, Kreiner; submitted 8 Oct 2017, revised 11 Nov 2017) says it "exploited the probability information implicit in the odds publicly available in the marketplace to find bets with mispriced odds" and reports profitability in a 10-year closing-odds simulation, a 6-month minute-level simulation and a 5-month real-money period, plus bookmaker account restrictions (https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.02824, fetched 2026-09-08 ~19:40Z; verification digest oss a3, fetched 18:56 UTC). georgedouzas/sports-betting (MIT; v0.15.1, 783 stars) implements it as `OddsComparisonBettor`, whose `_predict_proba` is `1 / mean(odds across selected odds types)` minus a scalar `alpha` in [0,1], clipped at 0, with no normalisation by the sum of implied probabilities, so it is an average-odds consensus with a constant shift, not a de-vig in the proportional or Shin sense (verification digest oss a1, a2, a4; raw _rules.py lines 17-22 and ~106-124, fetched 19:00 UTC). GSE's `consensusNoVig` (median of Shin-de-vigged per-book fairs) is a different and stricter object (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/market-read.ts:95-98). penaltyblog (MIT, v1.12.0) is the port source for `devig/oracle.ts` (oracle.ts:7-8) and separately ships Poisson, Dixon-Coles, bivariate Poisson and Kelly (verification digest oss b1-b4).

11. **Calibration techniques the repo already has, and which ones the literature says to use for what.** Murphy decomposition `BS = REL - RES + UNC` is stated correctly with its constant-p-within-bin caveat (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/murphy-res-definition.ts:13-62; wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:35). Recalibration maps: the offline bake-off runs Raw, Temperature (Newton NLL), Platt, Beta, Isotonic PAVA and CIR with full Murphy decomposition and log loss, apply OFF (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/calibration-map-bakeoff.ts:1-6); the literature's ordering is Platt (equal-variance Gaussian assumption) < temperature (one-parameter Platt) < beta calibration (contains the identity map, cannot make a calibrated forecaster worse by construction) < isotonic/PAV (nonparametric, monotone) (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:70-78). Production today: best map by Brier and by log-loss is "raw", temperature 1.039, CV recommendation identity, `mapBakeoff.applyOff` true, so no map is justified (Sports/docs/ops/LAUNCH_COMMAND_SHEET_2026-09-08.md:284, 290-291). The handoff prescribes beta/temperature tail calibration on out-of-fold predictions blended with PAVA in the dense middle, selected by Brier reliability-resolution decomposition "NOT fixed-bin ECE (ECE is optimistically biased)" (_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:47; _gse-edge-lab-final.md:15, 64). Distribution-free: IVAP produces a valid multiprobability interval (p0, p1) under exchangeability (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/calibration/ivap.ts:1-8), and the conformal inventory asserts `unlocksProven: false` and `raisesRes: false` for every method (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/conformal-calibration.ts:25-26, 39-40), which the dossier calls right about RES and over-applied about publishing (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:87, 274). Bootstrap CIs: `bootstrap-metric-ci.ts` is an iid percentile bootstrap with `DEFAULT_METRIC_CI_RESAMPLES = 200` (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/bootstrap-metric-ci.ts:30), while `stationary-bootstrap.ts` exists and is wired only to map bands (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/stationary-bootstrap.ts:8-104; wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:23); two picks on one game share the outcome completely, so iid intervals are narrower than truth (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:280). Bin-free alternatives the repo can build on existing code: CORP (PAV-based, `S = MCB - DSC + UNC`) on `calibration/pav.ts` and `isotonic-pava.ts`, and equal-mass bins already computed by `quantileBucketsFromSettled` with `QUANTILE_BIN_COUNT = 5` (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/compute.ts:263, :333; wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:37-41, 167-169).

12. **The public /calibration chart has three arithmetic defects, all in one file.** `BUCKETS` are five equal-width confidence bins 50-59, 60-69, 70-79, 80-89, 90-100 (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/compute.ts:211-217) with `MIN_BUCKET_SAMPLE = 30` (:219) and `bucketFor()` at :352; PUSH is scored as outcome 0.5 (:256-261), which lowers Brier mechanically and breaks the binary-y assumption of the Murphy identity; the Clopper-Pearson band is computed from `wins / decided` (:303) while the plotted point averages `outcome` over all rows including pushes at 0.5 (:305), so a band can miss its own point (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:198-205; wave2/_BUILD-PLAN.md:47-54). AGENTS.md records the founder acceptance that "the /calibration chart still buckets by confidence" (Sports/AGENTS.md:92-94). `computeDiscrimination` (compute.ts:430) checks monotone win rate across the five buckets with no interval and is not AUC (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:54).

13. **CLV is captured and graded but is neither the headline nor the gate, and the measured "close" is not the close.** `clv.ts` grades BEAT_CLOSE / MATCHED_CLOSE / LOST_TO_CLOSE for spread, total and moneyline; `clv-capture.ts` derives the closing snapshot as "the LAST odds snapshot before kickoff ... averaged across books" (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/clv-capture.ts:1-12); the free path queries `fetchedAt <= commenceTime` ordered desc (Sports/apps/web/lib/settlement/free-path-clv.ts:78-79) against a `refresh-odds` cron on `*/15 * * * *` (Sports/apps/web/vercel.json crons[1]), so it misses the final pre-kickoff move and biases measured CLV toward zero (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:100). The handoff's headline metric is CLV vs OBTAINABLE price, target +100 to +300 bps on the fired subset, with Pinnacle-close CLV as the internal proof bar and approximately breakeven (_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:84; _gse-edge-lab-final.md:6); CLV converges in ~50-200 bets where a settled hit-rate needs ~1000+ to separate from the 52.4% breakeven (_gse-edge-lab-final.md:67; _HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:83). The alignment audit notes CLV grades against the closing price, not the disputed final score, so it is the one proof surface the C-247 incident does not invalidate, subject to verifying `clvCapturedAt` coverage first (wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:306-314, 410). No open peer-reviewed study establishing the CLV-to-profit magnitude was found; the claims are industry pages (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:94, 322): NOT CONFIRMED.

14. **Historical accuracy benchmarks that are actually citable are few, and all are ceilings, not targets.** The edge lab's honest ceiling: a blind all-games rate is capped ~52-56% ATS-equivalent and will not beat an efficient close; ~57-60% is real only as a selective number on ~8-15% coverage, claimable only after a multi-season purged walk-forward shows positive CLV over 200+ fired bets (_gse-edge-lab-final.md:6; _HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:82-83). nfelo's +561 CLV bps is named as a bar not to expect to beat (_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:69, :107 "Do NOT" list). Lever-by-lever CLV budget: tail calibration ~0 bps standalone, +30 to +120 bps only by re-signing marginal EV; conformal gating 0 new bps, converts ~52.5-54% blind into ~57-60% stated selective; closing-line distillation +50 to +150 bps on the 15-35% soft/early subset (a Var~0.04 target is ~10x more sample-efficient than a Var~0.25 Bernoulli); hierarchical-Bayes props +100 to +250 bps on the 25-40% of prop lines with per-player n 5-30; market-residual GBM +50 to +150 bps blind ATS realised on ~10-15% coverage; EB tracking-metric shrinkage +10 to +40 bps (_gse-edge-lab-final.md:15-21). The one Kaggle datapoint in the repo: a March Mania entry won Brier 0.116 (#3 of 3485) by publishing `(1-alpha)*p_LR + alpha*q_market` with alpha 0.90, which is a forecast product and not an edge (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/edge-lab/market-consensus-q.ts:4-9). Competitor "benchmarks" are strike rates, never calibration: Dimers claims "74.9% strike rate" on CFB moneylines over four seasons, "71.6%" NFL 2024 with "+47 units", "52.7%" on NFL spreads in 2025, and a World Cup ROI of 14.2% on 114 bets, with the Pickwatch ranks NOT CONFIRMED (wave2/props-tools-rest.md:171-200). A moneyline strike rate on favourites is defeated by base rate alone (wave2/props-tools-rest.md:657-659).

15. **Feature engineering: which signals the corpus says matter, and the leakage units that are specific to sport.** The handoff's real edge sources are, in realised-edge-per-hour order: a hierarchical-Bayes props specialist (NegBinomial partial pooling), CLV-native closing-line distillation from strictly as-of open-time features, a market-residual GBM on game lines with the line as a fixed-coefficient offset (which "structurally prevents the 'my model rediscovered the line and looks 65% in-sample' illusion"), and EB-shrunk tracking metrics (CPOE, separation, xYAC, barrel%, xwOBA) plus physics/context transfer functions (air density, wind, umpire/ref EB rates) (_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:67-70; _gse-edge-lab-final.md:83-85). Feature admission is gated by market-conditional mutual information `I(features; Y | Q_close)`; if ~0 there is no beatable orthogonal information (_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:42; _gse-edge-lab-final.md:71). The most likely silent fatal bug is a closing-line-derived or post-decision feature entering `x_t` (_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:43). Leakage units differ from finance: the game (two picks share the outcome completely), the slate/day (weather, referee, news cycle), the odds snapshot (`fetchedAt > commenceTime` is look-ahead), and settlement time vs kickoff for map refits (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:108-112). `buildPurgedEmbargoedSplits` purges at NFL-week granularity (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/replay-harness.ts:72) and `honesty/placebo-leak.ts` carries a seeded shuffle, but none of it is applied to the calibration/eligibility path (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:114). For DFS, the RotoWire four-year study (2022-2025, 1,300+ team-stack seasons) measures QB-WR1 +0.31, QB-TE +0.27, QB-RB +0.07, same-team WR-WR -0.02, against folklore of +0.6 or higher that "come[s] from single-game DFS slates" (wave2/simulation-literature.md:191-203; not re-fetched).

16. **SaberSim's method is a deterministic point-projection solve run many times against sampled projection vectors; the reproducibility seam is seeded sims with a stored seed.** CEO Andy Baldacci: "for every lineup we build you, we randomly choose a bucket of sims and optimize based on the average score for each player in that bucket. Then we randomly choose another bucket for the next lineup and so on. What the slider does is it controls how large that bucket is." (https://www.sabersim.com/video/dfs-lineup-optimizers-are-obsolete-you-need-a-simulator, verified verbatim by the formulas digest 9a, curl 19:08:47Z; wave2/sabersim.md:121-129). Unpacked: `B_k = random bucket of B sims; v_k[p] = mean over B_k; L_k = argmax over legal lineups of sum(v_k[p])`, with `B = all sims` degenerating to a conventional optimizer and `B = 1` optimizing a single game script (wave2/sabersim.md:137-142). Contest sims: "Sim each lineup in your pool against a representative contest 100k times in 30 seconds or less" on the pricing page and SaberScore "repeated 100,000 times to calculate an average ROI", generic-contest at lower tiers, contest-specific only on Ultimate (wave2/sabersim.md:182-183, 217-228; formulas digest 9c: "you'll need Ultimate to access detailed, contest-specific simulations", https://support.sabersim.com/en/articles/12558411-how-saberscore-works). Their own FAQ: "Why do my lineups look different every time I build, even without changes? Each build samples a new set of simulations." (https://support.sabersim.com/en/articles/12079141-building-lineups, formulas digest 9b, curl 19:10:29Z). GSE's optimizer is the opposite by construction: an exact DP over (roster-slot fill state) x (salary bucket), "No randomness, no local search, no restarts ... the provably optimal lineup for the given objective and constraints" (Sports/apps/web/lib/fantasy/dfs-optimizer.ts:1-17), and a seeded mulberry32 PRNG already exists for edge-lab procedures that "must be reproducible from a stated seed" (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/edge-lab/rng.ts:1-11). The gap is one input and two layers: a sims-by-players score matrix, a contest-field simulator, and a portfolio selector (data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §3a; wave2/sabersim.md:667-690). The literature adds that the portfolio layer, not the projection layer, is where the measured money was: P1 and P3 bought their projections and profited from construction alone, single-entry median profit was -100%, creation order vs performance rank had Spearman 0.09, and the overlap constraint tau should be tuned 4-7 by slate size (wave2/simulation-literature.md:360-379, 679-685). Haugh and Singal's Proposition 4.1 (variance preference flips sign around the benchmark) is the DFS-shaped statement of `e = p - q` (wave2/simulation-literature.md:387-415). "Nobody publishes calibration of the simulation itself" (wave2/simulation-literature.md:667-672). Freedom to operate on contest simulation is NOT CONFIRMED: six patents are named and their claims were not read (wave2/simulation-literature.md:694-701).

17. **Competitors' architecture claims, reported as their claims.** SaberSim: "every game is simulated one play at a time, incorporating coaching tendencies, weather, injuries, and in-game strategy" and correlation is emergent, with no matrix to inspect (wave2/simulation-literature.md:179-187, citing https://support.sabersim.com/en/articles/12079199-how-contest-sims-work). Stokastic: a two-stage engine, a player-outcome simulation feeding a contest-level Monte Carlo, with metrics X-Win ("The expected win percentage of the bet, based on the projection"), X-ROI and Hold, and two unreconciled fair prices in the same product family, one market-derived and one projection-derived (wave2/stokastic.md:55-56, 152-172). Dimers: "10,000 simulations" per game and, in their own words, "advanced artificial intelligence"; BettingPros: "Sharp AI"; Rithmm: "Have AI Build One for You" and Scout, "your personal AI betting analyst" (wave2/props-tools-rest.md:638, 655; verification digest pricing, rithmm.com/pricing). Rithmm's user-built models have no hold-out, no pre-registration and no independent validation, so the leaderboard ranks the winners of a survivorship tournament (wave2/props-tools-rest.md:513-518). FantasyLabs: a 100-point user-weighted scorecard whose Trends backtester walks n from ~65,000 to 336 while Plus/Minus climbs from -0.87 to 4.58 with no holdout, purging, embargo, multiple-testing correction or CI, and an in-sample R^2 presented as confidence (wave2/fantasylabs.md:13, 77-82). PropFinder: PF Rating is an unweighted sum with hand-set caps 3.3/6.6/10, 30 of 100 NFL points binary yes/no, hit rate counted four times, no p, no q, no `e = p - q`, no calibration anywhere (_propfinder-teardown-final.md:137-216, 280-292). TeamRankings: power ratings plus game location produce win odds, rankings constrained so implied expected wins equal actual wins, models named Similar Games, Decision Tree, Power Ratings, Simulation (verification digest formulas 1, https://www.teamrankings.com/about/how-we-rank/ and /about/about-our-predictions/, curl 19:06Z). Unabated: a vig-free weighted blend of market-making books with proprietary weights (wave2/oddsjam-unabated.md:174-194). What the literature supports is narrower than any of these claims: proper scoring rules and Murphy decomposition (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:29-35), CORP and the Triptych (:37-52), Venn-Abers validity under exchangeability (:80-83), anytime-valid e-processes (:217), and for DFS the four-layer stack (marginals, dependence, field model, portfolio) with small-sample evidence: ten MLB dates for P1, 16 contests for P3, 17 weeks for P2 whose top-heavy t-statistic is near 0.1 per week (wave2/simulation-literature.md:22-32, 673-677).

18. **The bake-off of maps is not the bake-off of scores, and the sweep is in-sample.** `selectivePublishSweep` picks the max-RES cell of at least 18 correlated candidates subject to `Brier <= min(0.26, baseline + 0.03)` and reports the winner's RES, Brier and ECE on the same rows (Sports/apps/web/lib/calibration/selective-publish.ts:178-207; wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:229-237); the `calibration-pipeline` skill prescribes a time hold-out the function does not take (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:239). Hansen's SPA and White's Reality Check are the literature's answer to "I searched K configurations and picked the best; is the winner real?" (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:106, 241-244; _HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md:93). `calibration-map-bakeoff.ts` uses CV via `selectCalibrator`, but whether the folds are time-ordered and purged is NOT CONFIRMED (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:246, 323).

## What competitors do well / where they fail

Do well (cited):
- SaberSim models the opponent field as simulated lineups and derives ownership as a summary statistic, preserving joint construction: "It's the field lineups themselves, not the ownership percentages, that power SaberSim's Contest Sims." (wave2/sabersim.md:186-199). Their previous SaberScore was a three-factor formula they killed and explained why, an unusually self-aware paragraph (wave2/sabersim.md:230-235).
- Stokastic exposes leverage (your exposure minus projected field ownership) as an always-visible per-player column, structurally the same object as `e = p - q` (wave2/_BUILD-PLAN.md:118-120; wave2/_SCORE-ALIGNMENT.md:336-338).
- OddsJam publishes its de-vig menu and states that Shin collapses to additive on two-way markets, which is true (wave2/oddsjam-unabated.md:18-22, 84-92).
- TeamRankings constrains ratings so implied expected wins equal actual wins and publishes the model names (verification digest formulas 1, www.teamrankings.com/about/how-we-rank/).
- PropFinder's rating is deterministic and click-to-inspect explainable; its NBA and MLB HR grades are continuous (_propfinder-teardown-final.md:280-283, 813-815).
- The academic DFS literature publishes its objective, its solver, its tuning parameter and its real-money results including losses (wave2/simulation-literature.md:360-385, 490-497).

Fail (cited):
- None publishes a calibration curve, Brier, ECE, or a settled record of its own fair price: SaberSim (wave2/sabersim.md:16-20), OddsJam and Unabated (wave2/oddsjam-unabated.md:25-27), Dimers/Outlier/Props.Cash/Rithmm/BettingPros/Action Network (wave2/props-tools-rest.md:636-646), PropFinder (_propfinder-teardown-final.md:806-810), FantasyLabs (wave2/fantasylabs.md:13), and no open-source project either (wave2/open-source-stack.md:311).
- Non-reproducible output: "Each build samples a new set of simulations" (SaberSim FAQ, digest 9b), and play-by-play simulation makes correlation un-inspectable (wave2/simulation-literature.md:185-187, 739-744, where the category complaint is quoted as "The sim is a black box").
- Confidence decoupled from price and no track record: scores24 (_scores24-teardown-final.md:171, 184, 260).
- In-sample optimisation sold as validation: FantasyLabs Models/Trends (wave2/fantasylabs.md:77-82) and Rithmm user-built models (wave2/props-tools-rest.md:513-518).
- Strike rates on favourites and "20,000+ back-tested systems" as proof (wave2/props-tools-rest.md:657-659).
- Ownership shipped as a point estimate when the literature says it is a random vector (wave2/simulation-literature.md:250-254, 745-748); no product reports p(loss) beside EV although the better portfolio loses more often (wave2/simulation-literature.md:483-488, 749-750).
- Selling a simulated ROI that has never been shown calibrated against realised ROI, at $297/month (wave2/sabersim.md:16-20, 605-615).
