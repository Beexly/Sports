## 1. Executive Purpose

To insulate federal grant applications (NSF SBIR 26-510, ONR Division 311, DARPA, IARPA), state banking relationships, and Texas university research subawards (Rice University, UT Austin) from the post-Assad & Schuler sports betting statutory prohibitions in Texas (Texas Penal Code Chapter 47), **Galaxy Sports Network LLC** must register **Galaxy Decision Sciences** as an official assumed name (DBA) or amended division.

This guide provides the exact filing instructions, statutory citations, and fees under the Texas Business Organizations Code (BOC).

## 2. Option Selection: Form 503 (Assumed Name) vs Form 424 (Amendment)

| Feature | Option A: Form 503 (Assumed Name Certificate / DBA) | Option B: Form 424 (Certificate of Amendment) |

## 3. Step-by-Step Filing Instructions for Form 503 (Assumed Name)

### Step 1: Access Texas SOSDirect
1. Navigate to the [Texas SOSDirect Portal](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/sosda/index.shtml).
2. Log in with your SOSDirect account (or create one).

### Step 2: Complete the Form 503 Data Fields
- **Item 1 — Assumed Name:**  
  `Galaxy Decision Sciences`
- **Item 2 — Legal Name of Entity:**  
  `Galaxy Sports Network LLC`
- **Item 3 — Entity Type:**  
  `Domestic Limited Liability Company (LLC)`
- **Item 4 — Secretary of State Filing Number:**  
  *(Enter your Texas SOS Filing Number from your original Certificate of Formation)*
- **Item 5 — Jurisdiction of Formation:**  
  `Texas, USA`
- **Item 6 — Principal Office Address:**  
  *(Your current Kingwood/Houston principal office address)*
- **Item 7 — Period of Duration:**  
  `10 years` (standard statutory duration under BOC § 71.054)
- **Item 8 — County Filings:**  
  Select `Harris County` (and/or Montgomery County depending on specific Kingwood municipal boundary).

### Step 3: Execution & Payment
- Sign as `Manager` or `Authorized Representative`.
- Pay the statutory **$25.00** filing fee via credit card.
- Download the stamped Assumed Name Certificate PDF.

## 4. SAM.gov & SBA Company Registry Profile Update

Once the stamped SOS document is received:

1. **SAM.gov Update:**
   - Log into [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov).
   - Go to `Workspace` > `Entity Management` > Select your entity.
   - Under `General Information`, enter **Galaxy Decision Sciences** into the **Doing Business As (DBA) Name** field.
   - Verify that your Primary NAICS Code is set to:  
     **541715 — Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences (except Nanotechnology and Biotechnology)**.
   - Save and submit. Because the legal name and EIN remain identical, this avoids triggering a Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) CAGE code re-assignment.
2. **SBA SBIR Company Registry (SBIR.gov):**
   - Log into [SBIR.gov](https://www.sbir.gov).
   - Update your company profile to reflect DBA: `Galaxy Decision Sciences`.
   - Record your SBC Control Number for the upcoming **NSF 26-510 Project Pitch submission**.

## 5. County Assumed Name Filing (Texas BOC § 71.054)

Under Texas law, after filing with the Secretary of State, domestic LLCs should also file a copy of the assumed name certificate with the county clerk in the county of the registered office:
- **Harris County Clerk's Office (Personal Records / Assumed Names):**  
  File online or by mail ($24 fee).
