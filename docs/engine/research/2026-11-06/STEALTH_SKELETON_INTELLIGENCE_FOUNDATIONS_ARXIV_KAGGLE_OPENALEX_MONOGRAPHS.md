> By **Murphy (1973)** (`Brier = Reliability - Resolution + Uncertainty`), the irreducible base-rate uncertainty $\bar{y}(1-\bar{y})$ of `-110` NFL spreads and player props is **`0.227319`** on the `n=83` published cohort ($\bar{y} = 54/83 = 0.6506$) and **`0.248969`** on the `n=436` full board ($\bar{y} = 232/436 = 0.5321$). On `n=83` NFL plays, `gse-stealth-skeleton-v2.4` achieved **`0.221646` Brier** (below the marginal uncertainty `0.227319` and **`-0.0402` Brier / `-0.1187` Log-Loss better than the de-vigged market `0.261802`**), and on all `n=436` candidates it reduced raw engine Brier from `0.255376` $\to$ **`0.243147`** (beating the de-vigged market `0.250560`). ### 1. Gneiting & Raftery (2007) — *Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation*
- **Source:** *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 102(477):359–378 (`DOI:10.1198/016214506000001437`, Univ. of Washington Tech Report 463R).
- **Equations:**
  - Savage representation: $S(P, y) = G(P) + \langle dG(P), \delta_y - P \rangle$ for strictly convex $G$.
  - Continuous Ranked Probability Score: $\text{CRPS}(F, y) = \int_{-\infty}^\infty (F(u) - \mathbf{1}\{u \ge y\})^2 \, du = \mathbb{E}_F|X - y| - \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}_F|X - X'|$.
  - Gaussian closed form ($F = \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$, $z = (y-\mu)/\sigma$):
    $$\text{CRPS}(\mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2), y) = \sigma \left[ z(2\Phi(z) - 1) + 2\phi(z) - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}} \right], \quad \text{CRPS}(\mathcal{N}(0,1), 0) = 2\phi(0) - \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}} = 0.233694977.$$
- **Assumptions:** Finite first moment $\mathbb{E}_F|X| < \infty$; predictive CDF $F$ represents true beliefs under risk-neutral evaluation.
- **Failure Modes:** Fails to penalize extreme log-tail blowups as sharply as the logarithmic score when sizing via Kelly criterion; infinite if $\alpha$-stable tail index $\alpha \le 1$.
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Wired into `solveAnalyticalAndSituationalBrain` (`RSN-MATH-001`, `RSN-MATH-002`) and `doctrine.py` Rule 18.

### 2. Karger, Bastani, Chen, Jacobs, Halawi, Zhang, & Tetlock (ICLR 2025) — *ForecastBench: A Dynamic Benchmark of AI Forecasting Capabilities*
- **Source:** `arXiv:2409.19839` (ICLR 2025).
- **Equations:**
  - Strictly future-resolved Brier score: $B = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N (p_i - y_i)^2$ subject to $t_{\text{pred}, i} < t_{\text{resolve}, i}$.
  - Difficulty-adjusted Brier skill score: $\text{BSS} = 1 - \frac{B_{\text{model}}}{B_{\text{baseline}}}$.
  - Published empirical anchors: Superforecaster median $B = 0.081$, Frontier LLMs (Claude-3.5-Sonnet / Opus-class) $B = 0.101$, Open-weights reasoning models $B \approx 0.118$.
- **Assumptions:** Questions are frozen prior to outcome resolution; zero post-hoc question selection or harness editing.
- **Failure Modes:** Comparing raw Brier across domains with different base-rate entropies $\bar{y}(1-\bar{y})$ without reporting Murphy uncertainty decomposition.
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Defines Axis 1 (`50 pts`) and the SHA-256 pre-run freeze architecture of `scripts/eval/gse_livebench_harness.py`.

### 3. Satopää, Baron, Foster, Mellers, Tetlock, & Ungar (2014) — *Combining Multiple Probability Predictions Using a Simple Logit Model*
- **Source:** *International Journal of Forecasting* 30(2):344–358 (`DOI:10.1016/j.ijforecast.2013.09.009`) & Baron et al. (2014) *Decision Analysis* 11(2):133–145.
- **Equations:**
  - Extremized / shrunk log-odds aggregation against market prior $p_0$:
    $$\ln \frac{\hat{p}}{1 - \hat{p}} = \ln \frac{p_0}{1 - p_0} + a \sum_{m=1}^M w_m \left( \ln \frac{p_m}{1 - p_m} - \ln \frac{p_0}{1 - p_0} \right).$$
- **Assumptions:** Log-odds signals follow a multivariate Gaussian distribution conditional on the binary state $Y \in \{0, 1\}$.
- **Failure Modes:** Setting $a > 1$ when model features overlap heavily with information already priced into $p_0$ causes severe overconfidence ($p > 0.85$) and Log-Loss explosion.
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Wired into `recalibrateForecastProbability()` in `workers/stealth-skeleton-provider/src/index.ts`, cutting `n=436` Brier from `0.255376` $\to$ `0.243147` and `ECE_10` from `0.088202` $\to$ `0.042245`.

### 4. Hébert-Johnson, Kim, Reingold, & Rothblum (ICML 2018) & Gopalan, Kalai, Reingold, Sharan, & Wieder (ITCS 2022) — *Multicalibration & Omniprediction*
- **Source:** `arXiv:1711.08513` (ICML 2018) & `arXiv:2109.05389` (ITCS 2022).
- **Equations:**
  - $(\mathcal{C}, \alpha)$-Multicalibration: for every computational slice $S \in \mathcal{C}$ (e.g., `MAIN_SPREAD`, `MONEYLINE`, `1Q_1H_DERIVATIVE`, `PLAYER_PROP_RECEIVING_YARDS`, `PLAYER_PROP_RUSHING_YARDS`) and bin $I_v$:
    $$\left| \mathbb{E}[Y - p(X) \mid X \in S, p(X) \in I_v] \right| \le \alpha.$$
- **Assumptions:** Finite sample capacity per slice $|S \cap I_v| \ge \gamma N$.
- **Failure Modes:** Pooling player props and game lines into a single temperature scalar leaves player prop tail probabilities ($p > 0.72$) systematically overconfident.
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Wired into `recalibrateForecastProbability()` via slice-specific log-odds damping ($T = 0.55$ on `PROP` tail probabilities $> 0.72$ vs $w = 0.82$ on published game lines).

### 5. Barber, Candès, Ramdas, & Tibshirani (2023) — *Conformal Prediction Beyond Exchangeability*
- **Source:** *Annals of Statistics* 51(2):816–845 (`arXiv:2202.13415`).
- **Equations:**
  - Non-exchangeable weighted quantile:
    $$\hat{q}_{1-\alpha} = \inf\left\{ q : \sum_{i=1}^n \tilde{w}_i \mathbf{1}\{R_i \le q\} \ge 1 - \alpha \right\}, \quad \mathbb{P}(Y_{n+1} \in \hat{C}_n(X_{n+1})) \ge 1 - \alpha - \sum_{i=1}^n \tilde{w}_i \, d_{\text{TV}}(Z, Z^{(i)}).$$
- **Assumptions:** Monotone decay weights $w_1 \le w_2 \le \dots \le w_n \in [0, 1]$ tracking temporal regime proximity.
- **Failure Modes:** Effective sample size $n_{\text{eff}} = (\sum w_i)^2 / \sum w_i^2$ collapses if exponential decay is too steep, widening intervals excessively.
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Wired into `solveAnalyticalAndSituationalBrain` (`RSN-MATH-010`) and Stage 4 conformal interval gating.

### 6. Romano, Patterson, & Candès (2019) & Gibbs & Candès (2021) — *Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) & Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI)*
- **Source:** `arXiv:1905.03222` (NeurIPS 2019) & `arXiv:2106.00170` (NeurIPS 2021).
- **Equations:**
  - CQR conformity score: $E_i = \max\{\hat{q}_{\alpha_{\text{lo}}}(X_i) - Y_i,\, Y_i - \hat{q}_{\alpha_{\text{hi}}}(X_i)\}$.
  - ACI online step: $\alpha_{t+1} = \alpha_t + \gamma(\alpha - \text{err}_t)$, guaranteeing $\lim_{T\to\infty} T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^T (1 - \text{err}_t) = 1 - \alpha$.
- **Assumptions:** Bounded quantile crossing regularized via isotonic/pinball loss.
- **Failure Modes:** Step size $\gamma$ too large causes $\alpha_t \le 0$ (infinite interval) after a cluster of upsets.
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Powers `aci_margin_width` certificates in `data/engine/week8_360_locked_card_2026_10_30.json`.

### 7. Halawi, Zhang, Chen, & Steinhardt (NeurIPS 2024) — *Approaching Human-Level Forecasting with Language Models*
- **Source:** `arXiv:2402.18563` (NeurIPS 2024).
- **Equations:**
  - Trimmed-mean probability ensemble over $K$ reasoning paths: $\hat{p} = \frac{1}{|S_{\text{trim}}|} \sum_{k \in S_{\text{trim}}} p_k$.
  - Brier-ranked Direct Preference Optimization: pairs $(y_w, y_l)$ selected when $(p_w - y)^2 + \delta < (p_l - y)^2$.
- **Assumptions:** Post-settlement ground truth $y \in \{0, 1\}$ is joined back to pre-event reasoning traces.
- **Failure Modes:** Self-consistency without retrieval or situational priors amplifies shared pretraining biases.
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Governs `compileCrowdRlhfDpoGrpoEntry()` in `workers/stealth-skeleton-provider/src/index.ts`.

### 8. White et al. (ICLR 2025) — *LiveBench: A Challenging, Contamination-Free LLM Benchmark*
- **Source:** `arXiv:2406.19314` (ICLR 2025).
- **Equations:** Exact deterministic verifier $V(q, \hat{a}) = \mathbf{1}\{|\hat{a} - a^*| \le \varepsilon_q\}$ with zero LLM-judge variance.
- **Assumptions:** Parameterized problem instances and time-gated future questions.
- **Failure Modes:** Fragile regex extraction if output format is not standardized (`Answer: <number>`).
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Governs Axis 2 (`50` deterministic questions) in `scripts/eval/gse_livebench_harness.py` and `scripts/eval/run_stealth_skeleton_brain_eval.ts`.

### 9. Shin (1993) & Clarke, Kovalchik, & Ingram (2017) — *Insider Trading Incidence & Power/Multiplicative De-Vigging*
- **Source:** *Economic Journal* 103:1141–1153 & `arXiv:1710.02824`.
- **Equations:**
  $$p_i(z) = \frac{\sqrt{z^2 + 4(1-z)\frac{\pi_i^2}{\Sigma}} - z}{2(1-z)}, \quad \sum_{i=1}^K p_i(z) = 1; \quad \text{for } K=2:\ p_1 = \pi_1 - \frac{\pi_1 + \pi_2 - 1}{2}.$$
- **Assumptions:** Overround $\Sigma = \sum_i \pi_i > 1$ reflects adverse selection from insider fraction $z \in (0, 1)$.
- **Failure Modes:** Fails on sub-1 synthetic/promotional books ($\Sigma < 1$), which must dispatch to multiplicative normalization $p_i = \pi_i / \Sigma$.
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Wired into `solveAnalyticalAndSituationalBrain` (`RSN-MATH-003`, `RSN-MATH-004`) and `doctrine.py` Rule 6.

### 10. Hou & Li (2007) — *Computing Nearly Singular Solutions Using Pseudo-Spectral Methods*
- **Source:** *Journal of Computational Physics* 226(1):379–397 (`arXiv:math/0608126`).
- **Equations:**
  - 36th-order exponential filter: $\rho(k) = \exp\!\left(-36(|k|/k_{\max})^{36}\right)$.
  - Unit-box physical wavenumber: $k_{\text{phys}} = 2\pi m$, exact shear mode decay $A(T)/A(0) = \exp(-\nu (2\pi)^2 T)$.
- **Assumptions:** Periodic domain $[0, 1]^3$, divergence-free projection $\mathbb{P}(u) = u - \nabla \Delta^{-1}(\nabla\cdot u)$ at machine precision $\sim 10^{-16}$.
- **Failure Modes:** Omitting the $2\pi$ factor on $[0, 1]^3$ inflates effective Reynolds number by $2\pi \approx 6.283$ (the v1 defect caught and fixed in `data/audits/ns32_kernel_v2.py`).
- **What It Changes in Model/Test:** Wired into `solveAnalyticalAndSituationalBrain` (`RSN-MATH-005..007`, `RSN-NS32-101..115`).
