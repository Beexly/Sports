## 1. Origin (2026-10-03/04)

- Five earlier notes (01–05) defined a stage ladder with local strain laws. Two laws, σ = cA/k and σ = cA, disagreed. Both are cartoons.
- Three-inequality gate: (1) σ_n > νk_n² at every stage; (2) ΣΔt_n < ∞; (3) Σe_n < ∞. Diagnostics: BKM integral and persistence as N grows.

## 2. The three instruments (2026-10-05)

- **Biot–Savart ladder** (`biot_savart_ladder.py`): σ_{n+1} = Σ_{j≤n} A_j/(1−λ^{−(n+1−j)})². Inviscid: BS survives 15 stages. Viscous: BS survives to layer 6 (cA/k to 2, cA to 3). The kernel resists mild viscosity better than either local law.
- **Seed split** (`seed_split.py`): bump seed leakage is exactly 0. Gaussian leakage is erfc(1/(w√2)), never zero.
- **Polymer damper** (`polymer_damper.py`): Oldroyd-B arrests at layers 1–2. FENE-P only delays.

## 9. Rate race (note 16)

## 10. Polylog gate (note 17)

- x_j = j²x_{j−1}, log k_j = log log(2+x_{j−1}). All four conditions pass at ν ≤ 3.45e−13 over 60 stages.
- Classification: back-reaction (forces α budget) > viscosity > depletion (renormalizes) > pressure Hessian (weakest).
