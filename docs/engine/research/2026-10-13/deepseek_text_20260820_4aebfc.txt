=== HONESTY GATES ===

Every public surface must pass before merge:

1. Verification: cryptographic receipt, append-only data, third-party verifiable.
2. Transparency: what each metric measures and does not measure.
3. Evidence: in FABLE layer, PROVEN/NOT PROVEN, has kill criterion.
4. No overclaim: no "we beat," FTC-compliant.
5. Trademark: metric name protected or filing pending; outputs only, never raw archive.

Mandatory language:
- ECE page: "This ECE of 0.0044 largely measures the market's calibration through our confidence echo. It is not evidence of independent skill."
- BookGrade: "A quality score for book pricing, not a betting signal."
- PulseScore: "A liveness measure, not a fade signal."
- Kill Ledger: "These are things we tested and found not to work. We publish them so you don't pay for the same mistakes."
