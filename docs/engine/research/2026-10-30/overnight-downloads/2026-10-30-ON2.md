### #7 — @dair_ai — WM-Basin: black-box detection of distilled LLMs (Meta FAIR)
- Post: https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2115808882434158851 (pass-2 capture; payload from the opened post)
- **WHAT IT IS (VERIFIED at secondary coverage of the paper):** a spotlight on *Watermark Basins: Black-Box Detection of Distilled LLMs* (Meta FAIR / Meta Superintelligence Labs; arXiv:2610.37266 as reported; published Oct 27, 2026). The method, WM-Basin, detects whether a suspect model was trained on outputs from a watermarked teacher — using only the suspect model's generated text, no weights or logits. It passes that text through a clean probe model and reads gradient representations (how training on the text would move the probe's parameters); a student trained on watermarked traces leaves a measurable basin in that space. Reported results: **79% true-positive rate at 0.1% false-positive rate across 1,200 student models (135M–8.2B params, six architecture families, trained on OpenR1-Math reasoning traces), vs 29% for the best prior black-box token-level detector**; the signal survived quantization, pruning, and post-distillation RL, and weakened under clean-data fine-tuning and paraphrasing.
- **WHERE TO FIND IT:** arXiv:2610.37266 (as reported by the coverage); post at the URL above. The 79%/29% figures are corroborated by independent coverage of the paper this run (Cryptonomist's report of the paper matches the post's numbers exactly); the arXiv record itself was not opened this run — status VERIFIED-via-corroborating-coverage, one notch below primary-read.
- **HOW TO USE IT:** no install path assessed (paper-stage); the technique needs a probe model and gradient access to *it* (not to the suspect), so a Kaggle-GPU-lane replication on small students is conceivable but not scoped here.
- **WHICH SYSTEM IT PLUGS INTO:** Garrett's **model-routing stack and the NGS/arXiv research program** — provenance is a live question for a stack that routes across many third-party models (is this "new" model a distilled copy of a teacher?) and for eval hygiene generally (the same lab's watermark-radioactivity line detects benchmark contamination). Also **agent-fleet infrastructure** as a concept: black-box fingerprinting of models you did not train.
- **CREATIVE ANGLES:** the gradient-basin idea is a general *lineage* test — the same shape could audit whether a fine-tune in Garrett's own pipeline actually absorbed the data it was fed (train a probe on suspect outputs, read the basin) — a verification instrument for his own training runs, not just for catching copiers.
- **RISKS AND LIMITS:** detection weakens exactly where a motivated copier would push (clean-data fine-tune, paraphrase); evaluated on math-reasoning traces — transfer to other domains is the paper's open question, not a result; arXiv ID and details rest on secondary coverage until the paper is read directly.
- **FIRST TEST / VERDICT:** read the paper in the research program and file a one-page method note; no build. **LEARN-ONLY.**
