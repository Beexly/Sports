# MEMORANDUM TO COUNSEL

Baxley v. Turner Construction Company / CTS Lonestar JV
August 13, 2026

This memo reads the evidence packet against the draft charge dated July 31, 2026. Exhibit numbers refer to Part Two of the case file, bound into CASE_FILE_AND_EXHIBITS.pdf. It runs in five parts: what the evidence shows, the changes I'd make to the draft charge and why, evidence the draft doesn't use yet, what belongs in a preservation letter to Turner, and what this collection can't answer.

## 1. What the evidence supports

### The January disclosure to Corina Murguia

The Charge says I disclosed my autism to Ms. Murguia verbally in late January 2026. That conversation was spoken; there's no recording and there wouldn't be. It did leave a written trace. The Owner's Manual I sent her three weeks later opens the subject in the past tense: "We discussed that I am on the spectrum, (which only a handful of friends even know)."

That sentence was written as a reference back to a conversation that had already happened. It went to the one person who could have disputed it. It was written on a Turner form, part of an exercise Turner asked its managers to complete; the blank template came to me from Jake Overman, HR Director for the Omaha business unit, in October 2025. And it was never answered. I've found no reply from her of any kind. No correction, no follow up, nothing. If Turner claims it first learned of my diagnosis at some later date, the record shows its own manager receiving a written statement in February that the two of us had already discussed it, and letting that statement stand.

The email that carried the document points the same way. It opens, "When you chose to be vulnerable with me, it shifted something," which also refers back to a personal conversation between us. One caution about that email: its text survives in my files only as a copy pulled from my Turner mailbox on May 19, my last day, while I still had access. It's reliable enough to work from, but it isn't the email itself. The original sits on Turner's servers and is item 1 in the preservation list.

### The February 17 written disclosure

The attachment itself is in the packet. GB - Owners Manual .docx is produced at EX-026 with its full text in the binder. It reads in part:

> "We discussed that I am on the spectrum, (which only a handful of friends even know). For me, that does not look like social incompetence - it looks like internal overload when ambiguity replaces structure."

The Charge also says I informally asked for support with workplace structure as an accommodation. The same document makes that request in substance:

> "Structure regulates me. Defined standards, explicit expectations, and role clarity allow me to operate at a high level almost autonomously."
> "Give me the architecture first - the “why,” the constraints, the standards, and what success looks like. Once I have context, I move fast. Ambiguity slows me. Clarity accelerates me."
> "Be explicit. Be consistent. Be direct."

One more sentence from it deserves attention because of what happened ten days later:

> "What destabilizes me is not correction - its ambiguity compounded over time. When expectations shift without recalibration, I can begin to internalize adjustment as identity."

One date belongs beside that document. At 23:42 UTC on February 16, the same evening I finalized the Owner's Manual, I created my account with Lyra Health, Turner's own employee assistance vendor (EX-010). That record is DKIM signed and timestamped by a third party. It puts my reaching for help and my putting the disclosure in writing on the same night, eleven days before the warning.

On February 27 I was placed on a verbal final warning over a remark I was told had been taken out of context.

### The escalation record and the May 4 memorandum

Recovered since the last draft: GB.5.4.docx, produced in full at EX-041 with a thirteen page reading copy. Four byte-identical copies sit in my Drive. It is the memorandum I wrote to Kevin Ward, Senior Vice President of Human Resources, on May 4, 2026, the night before our meeting. Together with the rest of the record it fixes this sequence:

1. February 9: "Aligning on JV Execution" to Ms. Murguia, closing "I need to reset this."
2. April 9: written escalation to Mr. Overman, my prior manager.
3. April 22: written escalation, thread "CTS ER Concern - Alignment," which the memorandum recites as an escalation to Mr. Ward and Mr. Dolan. Mr. Ward replied that evening at 21:47.
4. May 4: the memorandum to Mr. Ward.
5. May 5: my meeting with Mr. Ward.
6. May 19: termination. Fifteen days after the memorandum, fourteen after the meeting.

One more piece of paper belongs with that list. Mr. Ward's own reply in the "Alignment" thread, produced at EX-034, reads: "I appreciate all of the context you provided. With Jeff out for the next 2 weeks, I'll find some time for us to talk so I can get a full understanding. Additionally, I'm planning a visit to Haskell for the 2nd or 3rd week of May." Senior HR promised engagement, in writing. What arrived in the window he named was the termination.

The memorandum is careful, and that care is itself useful. It states "I am not pursuing any further action and I am not preparing to." It names no antagonist and assigns no motive. It even contains a section, Part Two section E, listing evidence against my own position, and it closes, "I do not want a record that overstates. I want a record that is true." It also names six witnesses with what each can confirm, and lists Turner system artifacts with timestamps. That list converts directly into a document request.

One contradiction inside it matters most. On April 20, Ms. Murguia called and relayed that Joe Glowacki, a senior project leader, had said I was "not engaged." On April 27, Mr. Glowacki told me by phone that the engagement at issue was driven by the client, not by my performance, and that he had complimented my work. His own text message, produced at EX-003, says exactly that: "I complimented your engagement and appreciated you asking for assistance with the issue." A criticism attributed to a named leader was contradicted by that leader, in writing, within a week. That goes to pretext.

I also want to be straight about what the memorandum is not. It never mentions autism, the spectrum, disability, neurodivergence, accommodation, or the Owner's Manual. It reads start to finish as a structural complaint about how the project was run. If the retaliation theory needs protected activity under the ADA, that comes from the January and February disclosures, not from this document. What this document supplies is timing, a documented complaint history, and Turner's knowledge that I was formally escalating. Those are different things.

Turner will quote three passages from it: my line that "I am not perfect... There are days I have been frustrated, tired, or slower to respond than I would want to be"; my own account of ending a call with my manager ("it is Friday, I think that we should go ahead and terminate the call"); and my account of stepping out of my office on April 29 and being told a note would be made of it. Each is an admission I volunteered in advance, which is usually how these read. I'm flagging them so nothing in that document surprises anyone.

### Three people at Turner knew, not one

The charge names Ms. Murguia and Mr. Dolan. There was a third. On the March bootcamp trip I told Jill Lause, a fellow HR Manager who reported to Ms. Murguia, about my autism during a drive. That conversation is my testimony, but the trip that framed it is documented (EX-040), and she is the same Jill whom Mr. Dolan later put in charge of counseling services on March 20 (EX-032). ### The employer put my performance in writing, favorably, four days before the warning

Mr. Dolan's text of February 23 is cited below for the crisis it acknowledges, but its opening is the more useful half. He wrote: "I want you to know you have been doing great things for us under a difficult situation," and "I know you are a good person and you have good intentions. Please know you are appreciated." (EX-009) That is the VP and Senior Director of Human Resources putting an unqualified performance compliment in writing four days before he delivered the February 27 verbal final warning, and eighty five days before he signed my termination letter.

### No release was ever signed

Turner's letter of May 19 conditions the separation payments on my executing an enclosed Agreement and General Release. I never signed it and never returned it. The copy I hold, produced in full at EX-001, carries blank signature blocks on the final page: no signature from me, no date, no Turner countersignature. No release of my claims exists, and nothing I did waived them.

### No written discipline, anywhere

I received no written warning, no performance improvement plan, and no documented criticism of any kind during my Turner employment. Every criticism came verbally, by phone. The searches behind this packet went through my Google Drive, my entire Gmail archive, my iCloud library, and seven Turner Drive archives, and found nothing that contradicts that. I was the site's HR Manager; administering documented discipline was my own function, so I know what its absence means. The termination itself came in a formal letter signed by the same executive who gave the February 27 warning verbally.

## 2. The changes I'd make to the draft charge, and why

I wrote these out as actual language so none of this reads as a vague note. Take or leave any of it; the calls are yours.

**The dates box.** The draft's earliest date is January 1, 2026. No discriminatory act in the charge predates the verbal disclosure, so it should read January 26, 2026. If discovery shows the manual I sent Jake Overman in October 2025 already contained the disclosure, we amend earlier; the preservation letter asks for exactly that email (section 4, item 2).

**"No disciplinary history."** This is the line Turner will hit first, because my own text from that night says "Jeff put me on a final warning" (EX-006), and their counsel's July 24 letter claims the warning was "documented contemporaneously." Rather than let them introduce it, I'd have the charge own it:

"For the duration of his employment, Mr. Baxley received no written discipline of any kind: no written warning, no performance improvement plan, and no documented criticism. The only discipline ever referenced was a verbal 'final warning' delivered by telephone on February 27, 2026, ten days after his written disability disclosure. It was never documented to him, and no record of it has ever been shown to him."

Same facts, but now the ten day gap does the work instead of hiding.

**"An unfair comment he made in jest."** I wouldn't say "in jest." My text that evening says "I said something that I had no idea it had bad context" (EX-006). "In jest" means I knew it was a joke; the record says I didn't know how it would land. I'd write: "aside from a single remark that Mr. Baxley was told, after the fact, had been taken out of context." That matches what Turner will eventually see.

**The episode date.** The draft says on or around February 22. No document supports anything on the 22nd. What the record shows: on the 23rd I missed my counseling session that morning, Lyra billed me for it (EX-011), and Jeff texted me unprompted at 12:51 PM about my having disconnected and left my work phone at the office (EX-009). Then on the 28th, the day after the warning, I told Brandon Goings I was "having an episode and struggling really bad," asked if we had an on-site counselor (EX-004, EX-005), and called the emergency EAP line; my text to my mom sits between Lyra's two confirmation emails inside a forty minute window (EX-007, EX-012, EX-013). I'd plead it that way:

"Beginning on or about February 23, 2026, Mr. Baxley experienced a mental health crisis. He missed a scheduled counseling session that morning and disconnected from work, which management acknowledged in writing the same day. On February 28, 2026, the day after the verbal warning, he told the project's Safety Director he was having an episode, asked whether the project had an on-site mental health counselor, and called his employee assistance program's emergency line, which confirmed psychiatric care that same afternoon."

**The March conference.** It has a name and an address now: Turner's HR Manager Bootcamp, Field Club of Omaha, March 10 through 12, 2026, and Jeff's own texts from March 10 and 11, "We will be meeting in the lobby at 8:45," put the two of us there together (EX-040). The draft's line that he offered to check on me and nothing more came of it is his own text of March 21, almost word for word: "I am checking in on you... Call me if you need to talk" (EX-040). That sentence can stay exactly as written.

**The written disclosure.** The draft calls it an informal email request, which undersells the best fact we have: Turner asked for this document. The Owner's Manual was a company exercise on a company template. Jake sent me the blank in October 2025, and other managers filled out the same form; I hold a comparator completed by another employee on March 11, 2026. I'd add:

"He made the written disclosure in a document Turner itself solicited: an 'Owner's Manual' completed on the Company's own template as part of a Company exercise. In it, Mr. Baxley wrote that he is 'on the spectrum,' that 'structure regulates' him, and asked that expectations be made 'explicit,' 'consistent,' and 'direct.' Turner never responded to the document, never provided the requested support, and never engaged in the interactive process." (EX-026)

**The escalation chain is missing.** The draft pleads no written escalation after February 9. In between, I went up the entire ladder in writing, and the last rung sits fifteen days before they walked me out. One added paragraph carries it:

"Mr. Baxley escalated his concerns in writing up Turner's entire chain of command: to Ms. Murguia on February 9, 2026; to his prior manager, Jacob Overman, on April 9, 2026; to Senior Vice President of Human Resources Kevin Ward and Mr. Dolan on April 22, 2026; and on May 4, 2026, in a detailed written memorandum to Mr. Ward, which the two discussed in a meeting on May 5, 2026. Turner terminated him fifteen days after the memorandum." (EX-018, EX-041, EX-001)

**Two small ones.** A sentence is broken and needs to read: "including his concern that gaps in the work were being caused by delayed communication from his new supervisor, Corina Murguia." And the seven percent raise stays as written; I just can't paper it from my side, which is why payroll records are in the preservation letter (section 4, item 6).

**Who the respondent is.** The draft names Turner Construction Company alone, at the New York address, and leaves a second employer block half filled with a Kansas City address. This matter is captioned Turner Construction Company / CTS Lonestar JV throughout, and my own records include a February 18, 2026 exchange with Sarah Bundy and Ms. Murguia about employer of record for the JV's hiring. Whether the JV, or another Turner entity, was my employer of record is worth settling before filing, since an entity not named in the charge is generally not exhausted as to it. I have added that email chain to the preservation list.

**Which impairment we plead.** The draft pleads autism alone. That is accurate, and the Owner's Manual is the disclosure document. But the impairment this packet documents most heavily is the co-occurring condition I was in active treatment for: seventy five third party Lyra Health records, my own contemporaneous texts about panic attacks and new medication, and Turner's own on-site counseling response. No medical record of the autism diagnosis appears anywhere in the packet. Whether to plead both is your call; I raise it because the evidentiary weight sits differently than the pleading does.

**One word in my own proposed language.** Where I wrote that Turner "never responded to the document," the honest form is that I never received a response and none exists in my records. My archive could not have captured one: the exchange was work account to work account, and a scan of all my personal email found no message to or from her address at any date. If a reply exists it is on Turner's servers, which is why I have asked for it in preservation. I would rather plead it that way than have a single document contradict an absolute.

**The clock, for you to check.** The charge is dual filed with the Texas Workforce Commission, and Chapter 21 runs 180 days from each act rather than the ADA's 300. Counting from the packet's own dates: February 17 falls on August 16, February 27 falls on August 26, and the May 19 termination falls on November 15. This memo is dated August 13. The termination is comfortable either way; the two February acts are not, and one of them is three days out. You know the tolling and continuing violation law here and I do not, so I am flagging the arithmetic rather than drawing a conclusion from it.
