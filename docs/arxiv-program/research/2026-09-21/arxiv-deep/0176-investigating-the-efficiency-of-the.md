**Verdict:** ADAPT — soccer-specific Asian Handicap apparatus does not port directly, but four portable results do: (1) optimize bet-selection thresholds for total profit, not per-season ROI (Section 6, points iv & vi); (2) the average-vs-maximum-odds gap (profits 42%–296% higher on max odds) quantifies the value of GSE's multi-book line shopping; (3) the pi-ratings modifications (raw goal-difference target, k=3 new-team acceleration) are portable rating tweaks; (4) the NFL point-spread market is the structural analog of the AH market — replicate this efficiency assessment on NFL spreads/totals.

## 1. Research question
Is the Asian Handicap (AH) football betting market efficient, relative to the traditional 1X2 (home/draw/away) market? The paper presents the first published model specifically built for AH prediction and efficiency assessment — modified pi-ratings feeding a hybrid Bayesian network — and evaluates it over 13 English Premier League seasons under average vs maximum odds, all bet-decision thresholds, and profit vs ROI optimization. (Abstract, Sections 1, 5.)

## 2. Dataset / schema
- **EPL seasons 1992/93–2018/19**, data from www.football-data.co.uk and manually recorded from www.nowgoal.com.
- Ratings trained on up to 27 seasons (goal data only); BN parameterized on up to 10 seasons (ball-possession data available only from 2009/10); **betting simulation over 13 seasons (2006/07–2018/19)** since AH odds only exist from 2006/07.
- Rating optimization restricted to matches where both teams had ≥38 prior matches: **9,073 match instances**.
- Data variables used (Table 5): Date/Home team/Away team (ratings only); 1X2 outcome; home/away possession rate, shots, shots on target, goals (BN); handicap, average and maximum 1X2 odds, average and maximum AH odds (betting simulation).
- Access: public (football-data.co.uk); no download URL or code stated (BN built in AgenaRisk commercial software).

## 3. Method / model
- **Modified pi-ratings (3.1):** each team carries home and away ratings; updated after each match with learning rate λ (recency) and cross-influence rate γ (home↔away). Two modifications vs Constantinou & Fenton (2013): (1) ratings predict *actual* goal difference — the diminishing function f(e) = 3·log10(1+e) is removed because AH cares only about goal differences; (2) λ is multiplied by **k = 3** for matches involving a team with <38 prior matches (faster convergence for promoted/new teams; k integer-optimized 1–10, real-valued optimization left as future work).
- **Hybrid Bayesian network (3.2):** structure set by domain knowledge (temporal chain): Rating Difference (RD = prior home rating − prior away rating, the only observed node) → Possession → shot-generation rate per possession minute → Shots → on-target rate → Shots on Target → goal rate → Goals → Goal Difference (GD = GH − GA) → 1X2 node and AH-outcome nodes. Beta-Binomial conjugate parameterization (hyperparameters count minutes/shots/on-targets/goals); RD discretized into **23 levels** (Table 4), each with >50 data points. Built in AgenaRisk.
- **Validation (4.2):** unconventional — ratings are strictly temporal (ratings at t−1), but the BN is fit with leave-one-out CV ignoring time: predictions at time t use the full dataset minus the match under prediction. Justification: the possession→shots→goals relationships are assumed time-invariant for the average EPL team; supported by consistent accuracy across all 13 seasons including 2006/07–2008/09 (pre-BN-training-data seasons) and by the model's 2nd-place finish in the Machine Learning for Soccer competition.
- **Betting simulation (5.3):** fixed single-unit (£1) bets on any 1X2/AH outcome where the model's estimated unbiased payoff exceeds the market payoff by at least **θ** (payoff discrepancy, tested at every integer θ from 0% to 25%), under both average and maximum market odds, optimizing for profit or ROI, with θ either static across all 13 seasons (Tables 9, 12 — the "realistic" case) or re-optimized per season (Tables 10, 11, 13, 14 — the "unrealistic" case).

## 5. Features / target
**Features:** prior home/away pi-ratings (goal data, 27 seasons); in-match possession, shots, shots on target (BN parameterization). **Target:** 1X2 outcome and AH binary outcomes given the market handicap. **Horizon:** single-match; profitability aggregated over 13 seasons (4,940 matches).

## 6. Validation design
Chronological for ratings; LOOCV (non-temporal) for the BN; betting simulation strictly forward (predictions use prior ratings). Baselines: average vs maximum market odds; static θ vs per-season-optimized θ; profit vs ROI optimization; 1X2 vs AH. Metrics: RPS (1X2), Brier score (AH), realized profit and ROI at each θ. Timeseries drift analysis (Fig 4) + sample-size table (Table 8): ≥5 seasons of league data needed for well-informed BN priors.

## 7. Numerical results / baselines
- **Rating optimization (5.1):** optimal λ = 0.018, γ = 0.7 (k = 3), mean goal-difference error **1.2283** — below prior pi-ratings reports (2.625–2.66). 31 nearby (λ, γ) combos within 0.01% discrepancy (Table 6). Linear RD→expected-goal-difference relationship (Fig 3): RD level 23 (most away-favored) → −1.38; level 1 (most home-favored) → +2.18.
- **Predictive accuracy (Table 7):** RPS (1X2) average **0.195** (season range 0.184–0.213); Brier score (AH) average **0.248** (range 0.199–0.267) across 13 seasons.
- **1X2 betting, static θ (Table 9):** average odds — profit maximizes at θ=8% (814 bets, profit £44.7, ROI 5.49%); ROI maximizes at θ=18% (37 bets, ROI 23.59%). Maximum odds — profit max at θ=9% (1,049 bets, profit £177.18, ROI 16.89%); ROI max at θ=16% (132 bets, ROI 21.20%).
- **1X2 per-season optimization:** ROI-optimal-per-season θ → overall ROI 9.03% avg odds (Table 10); profit-optimal-per-season θ → overall ROI **12.96%** avg odds (Table 11) — optimizing per-season ROI *lowers* overall ROI. Max odds: 26.69% (Table 10) vs 17.37% (Table 11).
- **AH betting, static θ (Table 12):** average odds — profit max at θ=16% (201 bets, profit £9.20, ROI 4.57%); max odds — profit max at θ=9% (1,307 bets, profit £18.06, ROI 1.38%). Per-season (Tables 13/14): overall avg-odds ROI 5.70% (ROI-optimal θ) vs 6.33% (profit-optimal θ).
- **Section 6 conclusions (i–vi):** (i) maximum odds increase profits 42%–296% over average odds; (ii) AH bets target favorites (odds 1.6–1.8) while 1X2 bets target outsiders (odds >3); (iii) AH profit/ROI ≈ 2.5–5.5×/2.5–4× lower than 1X2, less volatile — but stake-scaling AH by 5.59× to match 1X2 profit eliminates the risk advantage (Fig 6); (iv) optimal θ varies dramatically between seasons — per-season threshold optimization (Tables 10, 11, 13, 14) is not realistic; only Tables 9/12 are; single-season threshold-optimized profitability reporting "should be discouraged"; (v) loss-making seasons possible under average odds even at the best θ; max odds remove the risk but some seasons barely profit; (vi) per-season ROI optimization hurts long-term ROI; per-season profit optimization guarantees maximal total profit and usually higher overall ROI.

## 9. Leakage & limitations
- BN validation is non-temporal (uses future matches to fit CPDs) — the author defends this, and 2006/07–2008/09 out-of-training-sample accuracy is consistent, but it remains an approximation of true past performance.
- AH odds for the first five seasons often reflect ±0-ball rather than the uniform AH handicap (Table 15); author claims no material effect.
- Only one handicap per match modeled; quarter-goal AH handling is approximate.
- Static-θ results (the "realistic" ones) still assume a θ chosen with hindsight over 13 seasons — real-world performance would be worse.
- Soccer-only; EPL-only; pre-COVID data (model ignores empty-stadium effects; author suggests assigning away ratings to both teams as a fix).

## 10. GSE overlap
Per existing-research-map: Constantinou's pi-ratings/Dolores already appear in Garrett's corpus (ML for Soccer competition context), but the **AH-efficiency framing and the threshold/optimization findings (iv, vi)** are new. The NFL point-spread/total markets are the direct structural analogs of AH (binary, handicap-based, ~50/50 pricing). The Beta-Binomial causal chain (possession→shots→goals) has an NFL analog worth noting (plays→first downs→explosive plays→points) but is not a GSE gap. The Kelly/staking lane (papers 0171, 0174) gains a complementary rule here: *how to choose the EV threshold*.

## 11. GSE implementation spec
1. **EV-threshold policy (from vi):** GSE's bet-selection threshold on model-vs-market edge must be optimized for **total realized profit**, not per-week/per-season ROI, on chronological backtests. Adopt the paper's exact test: sweep θ, compute both static-θ and per-window-optimal-θ profitability; if per-window-optimal θ is unstable across windows, deploy the static θ that maximizes total profit.
2. **Line-shopping quantification (from i):** replicate the average-vs-max-odds comparison on GSE's NFL backtest: run the identical bet list against (a) consensus-average odds and (b) best-available-across-books odds; the 42%–296% band is the expected uplift from multi-book shopping — set the ROI hurdle for the line-shopping feature accordingly.
3. **Rating tweak (from 3.1):** if GSE uses margin-based team ratings, test the two modifications: drop diminishing-returns transforms (optimize raw margin), and accelerate learning (×3) for teams with <1 season of data (e.g., post-draft roster turnover). 1-week test.
4. **NFL spread-efficiency replication:** run the AH protocol on NFL spreads/totals (binary, handicap-like): fixed-unit bets at model-vs-market edge ≥ θ, all θ, avg vs best odds, 2015–2024. Expect the AH findings (favorite-side bets, lower variance, threshold instability) to carry over.

## 12. Reproducible test
Dataset: NFL 2015–2024, Pinnacle/consensus closing spreads and totals + best-available odds from the Odds API. Protocol: (a) compute GSE model-vs-market edge per game; (b) sweep θ ∈ {0%, …, 15%}; (c) fixed-unit bets on spread/total sides with edge ≥ θ; (d) report profit and ROI for static θ (full sample) vs per-season-optimal θ. Baselines: the paper's qualitative predictions — profit-optimal θ < ROI-optimal θ; per-season-θ optimization lowers total ROI; static-θ results are the honest estimate.

## 13. Acceptance / rejection gate
**Adopt** the profit-maximizing static-θ threshold policy if, on the 2015–2024 NFL backtest: (i) some static θ yields total profit ≥ 20% above the current GSE threshold policy's total profit at comparable bet counts, AND (ii) per-season-optimal θ is unstable (optimal θ differs by ≥ 5 pp across ≥ 3 of the 10 seasons), confirming the paper's point iv. **Reject** the policy change if no static θ beats current policy, or if the θ-stability test fails (per-season θ is stable — then keep per-season tuning).

## 14. Improvement experiment
Go beyond the paper on two axes. First, the author's θ is a single static number chosen with 13-season hindsight; build an **adaptive θ** that updates from a rolling window (e.g., trailing 2 seasons) and test whether it recovers the per-season-optimal profit without lookahead — the paper never tests whether the "unrealistic" gains are partially attainable. Second, the paper bets fixed units; re-run the identical AH protocol with the fractional-Kelly stakes from paper 0171 and the decorrelated probability estimates from paper 0174, testing whether the 1X2-vs-AH risk ranking (point iii) survives under optimal staking — hypothesis: Kelly staking narrows the profit gap because it naturally sizes up the lower-variance AH bets.
