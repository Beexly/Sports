# [0291] Forecasting number of corner kicks taken in association football using compound Poisson distribution (arXiv:2112.13001v3)

**Citation:** Yip, S., Zou, Y., Hung, R. T. H., & Yiu, K. F. C. (2021). *Forecasting number of corner kicks taken in association football using compound Poisson distribution*. arXiv:2112.13001v3. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.13001v3
**Ledger completed:** 2026-09-21. **Read:** full text (PDF text extract, 3317 lines).
**Verdict:** ADAPT — the discrete compound Poisson machinery (geometric-Poisson with match-varying shape, market-implied TG/SUP covariates, Shin margin removal) ports directly to NFL count-prop modelling (touchdowns, sacks, explosive plays); the corner-kick application itself is soccer-only.

## 1. Research question
Corner kicks arrive in clusters (a "parent" corner spawns "offspring" corners via clearances), so total corner counts are overdispersed (variance-to-mean ratio 1.1856 vs 1.0396 for goals). The authors ask whether a Bayesian discrete compound Poisson (DCP) regression framework — modelling parent clusters as Poisson with geometrically-distributed cluster sizes, with regression structure on both the rate λ and the shape parameter, plus cross-market odds information — can forecast corner counts and profit on the HKJC corner over/under market.

## 2. Dataset / schema
20,190 matches from the Hong Kong Jockey Club (HKJC) with corner O/U markets offered, 1 Aug 2016 – 10 Jun 2021, across 90 leagues/cups; pre-match HAD, total-goals O/U, and total-corner O/U odds (HKJC sets prices ≥1 day before kickoff; collected prices are just-before-kickoff), plus post-match scorelines and corner counts. Second source: football-data.co.uk for 7 elite leagues (EPL, Scottish PL, Bundesliga, Serie A, La Liga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie) with per-team shots, shots-on-goal, fouls, cards, corners taken. Schema: match-level rows (teams, competition, date, odds, realized corners). Betting simulation: 2,057 HKJC matches, 1 Jan – 10 Jun 2021. Soccer only.

## 3. Method / model
Five Bayesian candidate models fit in Stan (NUTS, 4,000 iterations each): (A) geometric-Poisson regression; (B) geometric-Poisson with shape regression; (C) negative binomial with shape regression; (D) negative binomial; (E) naïve Poisson. Rate equation: log λ_i = β₀ + β₁ log(TG_i) + β₂ log(|SUP|_i+0.01) + β₃ TCTarget_i + β₄ log(HomeAvg3_i+0.01) + β₅ log(AwayAvg3_i+0.01) + β₆ log(HomeShotOnGoalAvg3_i+0.01) + β₇ log(AwayShotOnGoalAvg3_i+0.01), where TG/SUP are market-implied expected total goals and goal supremacy from a Maher-1982 double independent Poisson fit to de-vigged HAD + goals-O/U odds (L-BFGS-B minimising squared error between market and model implied probabilities), TCTarget is Micci-Barreca target-encoded competition-level log-mean corners (encoded from a 2016–2018 pre-training period to avoid leakage), HomeAvg3/AwayAvg3 are teams' corner counts in their last 3 matches (league-imputed when missing), plus shot-on-goal variants. Shape regression: log(κ_i) = α₀ + α₁ log(|SUP_i|+0.01) for NB; logit(θ_i) = α₀ + α₁ log(|SUP_i|+0.01) for geometric-Poisson. Four margin-removal methods compared by negative mean logarithmic scoring rule (Gneiting & Raftery 2007): multiplicative, odds ratio, power, Shin. Betting: $100 per selection whenever model expected value is positive; Sharpe ratio = annualised mean daily profit / SD (364 trade days).

## 4. Equations & assumptions
Eq. 1 (DCP): Y = Σ_{i=1}^N X_i, N ~ Poisson(λ), X_i i.i.d. discrete. Eq. 2 (pgf): G_Y(s) = exp{Σ_{i=1}^∞ α_i λ (s^i − 1)}, Σα_i = 1. NB as DCP (logarithmic cluster sizes, Quenouille 1949): p_X(x) = [−1/log(1−p)] (p^x/x), x ≥ 1; adopted parameterisation (Gelman et al. 1995): p_Y(y) = C(y+κ−1, y) (λ/(λ+κ))^y (κ/(λ+κ))^κ; E[Y] = λ, Var[Y] = λ + λ²/κ, D = 1 + λ/κ; Poisson limit as κ → ∞. Geometric-Poisson (Özel & Inal 2010): p_Y(0) = e^{−λ}; p_Y(y) = e^{−λ} Σ_{k=1}^y (λ^k/k!) C(y−1, k−1) θ^k (1−θ)^{y−k}; E[Y] = λ/θ, Var[Y] = λ(2−θ)/θ², D = (2−θ)/θ; pgf exp{−λ[1 − (1−θ)s/(1−θs)]}. Margin removal: multiplicative p_i = π_i/π; odds ratio OR = p_i(1−π_i)/(π_i(1−p_i)), p_i = π_i/(OR + π_i − OR·π_i); Shin p_i = [√(z² + 4(1−z)π_i²/π) − z]/[2(1−z)] with z iterated from 0; power p_i = π_i^{1/k}, Σ p_i = 1. Cross-market loss: (p_H−p'_H)² + (p_D−p'_D)² + (p_L−p'_L)² minimised over (λ̂₁, λ̂₂) to get TG_i = λ̂₁+λ̂₂, SUP_i = λ̂₁−λ̂₂. Target encoding: x̂^i_k = (n^i_k θ̂_k + θ̂ m)/(n^i_k + m). CMP extension (not fitted): p_Y(y) = λ^y/(y!)^ν · 1/Z(λ,ν). Assumptions: corners from open play Poisson, clusters geometrically distributed; pre-training-period target encoding is leak-free; ≥1-year-old Wikipedia-style profiles not applicable; margin-removal methods allocate bias symmetrically (fails for the O/U under-bias asymmetry — authors flag this as unresolved).

## 5. Features / target
Features: log(TG) (market-implied expected total goals), log(|SUP|+0.01) (implied goal supremacy), TCTarget (target-encoded competition log-mean corners), log(HomeAvg3+0.01), log(AwayAvg3+0.01) (last-3-match corner averages), log(HomeShotOnGoalAvg3+0.01), log(AwayShotOnGoalAvg3+0.01). Target: total number of corner kicks taken in a match. Horizon: pre-match (odds ≥1 day before kickoff).

## 6. Validation design
In-sample fit on 2016–2021 HKJC data; model comparison by leave-one-out expected log pointwise predictive density (elpd_loo; Pareto-K diagnostics all < 0.5, NUTS converged). Betting simulation is the true out-of-sample test: 2,057 matches, 1 Jan – 10 Jun 2021, $100 per positive-EV selection, cumulative profit and Sharpe ratio vs blind "bet under" baseline. Baselines: five candidate models + blind-under strategy. Margin-removal comparison by negative mean log score on held-out implied probabilities.

## 7. Numerical results / baselines
Margin-removal log scores: multiplicative best for corner O/U (14,187.07) and goals O/U (14,465.44); Shin best for HAD (19,612.15). Model comparison (elpd_loo): (A) geometric-Poisson −30,856.4 (best); (B) GP+shape −30,856.8 (se_diff 1.4); (C) NB+shape −30,858.4 (se_diff 1.7; NB+shape beats fixed-NB with se_diff 5.7, "significantly higher"); (D) NB −30,871.4; (E) Poisson −30,877.2. Key estimates (model A, 95% credible intervals): β₁ (log TG) = 0.1545 (0.1046, 0.2069) — each expected goal raises expected corners 16.70%; β₃ (TCTarget) = −0.6168 (−0.9374, −0.2813); β₄ (HomeAvg3) = 0.5957 (0.53, 0.6625); β₅ (AwayAvg3) = 0.6052 (0.5405, 0.6652); θ posterior median 0.9577 (0.9441, 0.9705) — each corner induces 0.0423 additional corners, implied variance-to-mean 1.0883. SUP, shot-on-goal terms non-significant at the mean (credible intervals cover zero) but retained for higher-moment variation. Betting simulation (Table 6): (C) NB+shape — 1,137 bets, +$6,578, +5.785%, Sharpe 3.065 (best); (B) GP+shape — 1,125 bets, +$6,256, +5.561%, Sharpe 2.922; (A) GP — +$5,308, +4.727%, Sharpe 2.427; (D) NB — +$4,805, +4.429%; (E) Poisson — +$4,283, +3.705%; blind bet under — 2,056 bets, −$3,530, −1.717%, Sharpe −1.267. Market context: HKJC average margin 7.61% (payout 92.38%), but over-selection payout 85.71% vs under 98.24% — structural under-bias. Correlation of shots/shots-on-goal with corners: 9.68%/17.37%.

## 8. Code / data availability
R package `implied` (Lindstrøm 2021) for margin removal. No code/data link stated for the model itself. Data from HKJC website and football-data.co.uk (public).

## 9. Leakage & limitations
(a) Target encoding is fit on a 2016–2018 pre-training period — fine for the simulation window, but in live use the encoding must be re-fit on rolling history to avoid leakage (they flag Zhang et al. 2013 / Prokhorenkova et al. 2017). (b) Betting simulation is one 5-month window at one bookmaker (HKJC) — no cross-book or cross-season replication. (c) Authors admit the under-bias asymmetry (over payout 85.71% vs under 98.24%) is NOT captured by any of the four margin-removal methods — a structural miss. (d) EPL and Champions League recorded negative profits — "data-rich" competitions behave differently; no mechanism identified. (e) Soccer corners only; the parent/offspring clustering story doesn't map to NFL events one-to-one. (f) The elpd_loo winner (geometric-Poisson) loses the betting simulation to NB+shape — scoring-rule best ≠ most profitable, worth noting alongside Wunderlich–Memmert cautions.

## 10. GSE overlap
Extension, not duplicate. The repo's count-model inventory lists Poisson, Dixon–Coles, Skellam, Harville, Stern — the geometric-Poisson DCP family and the shape-regression trick are new to the corpus. The cross-market information extraction (Maher double-Poisson fit to HAD+O/U odds → TG/SUP covariates via L-BFGS-B) is a concrete recipe GSE lacks: the corpus has "de-vigged consensus" and "market-implied ratings" but no published NFL recipe for extracting implied total/supremacy from moneyline+spread+total odds into model covariates. Gap #3 (market microstructure) is partially served by this paper's margin-removal bake-off (multiplicative vs Shin by log score). The 2026-09-17 gse-lab work computes EPA families but no overdispersed count models for discrete NFL events.

## 11. GSE implementation spec
(1) Port the cross-market extractor: for each NFL game, take de-vigged moneyline + spread + total odds (Odds API), fit a normal-score model (replacing Maher Poisson — use a bivariate normal on (home margin, total) with L-BFGS-B minimising squared error between market and model implied probabilities) to recover market-implied expected total and spread-implied supremacy as covariates for prop models. (2) Build a Bayesian DCP prop model in Stan: geometric-Poisson or NB for team-level count props (sacks taken, explosive plays, red-zone trips), rate equation with the TG/SUP analogs + last-3-game team averages + target-encoded opponent adjustments; shape regression on |SUP|; compare by elpd_loo and by out-of-sample prop-bet simulation. (3) Run the margin-removal bake-off (multiplicative, Shin, odds-ratio, power) on NFL HAD-analog (moneyline) and total markets by negative mean log score — pick the de-vigging method per market instead of defaulting to multiplicative. Data: nflverse, Odds API. Effort: ~3 engineer-days (Stan templates exist in the repo's gse-lab scripts; the market-fit extractor is the new piece).

## 12. Reproducible test
Dataset: NFL 2024 season, team rushing-attempt counts per game (a naturally clustered count) vs closing totals/moneylines. Metric: elpd_loo comparison of geometric-Poisson vs NB vs Poisson rate models with market-implied covariates (same structure as the paper's Table 4), plus a 2024 positive-EV prop simulation on team rushing-attempt O/U lines (any available book). Baseline: naïve Poisson with the same covariates. Window: 2024 regular season.

## 13. Acceptance / rejection gate
ADOPT the DCP prop framework if the geometric-Poisson/NB model beats naïve Poisson by elpd_loo with se_diff ≥ 3 on 2024 rushing-attempt counts AND the positive-EV simulation shows a positive Sharpe (≥ 1.0) over ≥100 bets; REJECT as a framework if the Poisson is within se_diff < 1 and the betting simulation is flat or negative — i.e., clustering adds no predictive value for NFL counts.

## 14. Improvement experiment
Go beyond the paper by fitting the CMP distribution (ν < 1) the authors mention but never fit — Geng & Xia (2022) show CMP is infinitely divisible (hence DCP-compatible) only when Poisson or geometric, but a CMP-GLM with two link functions (Guikema & Goffelt 2008) handles both over- AND under-dispersion; some NFL counts (e.g., team punts, which are bounded by drive counts) may be underdispersed, where the paper's DCP family fails. Compare CMP-GLM vs geometric-Poisson on team punts per game — a case the paper's framework cannot represent.
