# [2044] QuantFactor REINFORCE: Mining Steady Formulaic Alpha Factors with Variance-bounded REINFORCE (arXiv:2409.05144)

**Citation:** Junjie Zhao, Chengxi Zhang, Min Qin, Peng Yang (2024). *QuantFactor REINFORCE: Mining Steady Formulaic Alpha Factors with Variance-bounded REINFORCE*. arXiv:2409.05144v3. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05144
**Ledger completed:** 2026-09-22. **Read:** full text (ar5iv HTML, ~16,300 words).
**Verdict:** ADAPT

*Why:* a variance-bounded REINFORCE miner with Information-Ratio reward shaping; the "steadiness over raw IC" idea maps directly to GSE's need for signals that survive regime change, but the Dirac-MDP framing must be re-validated on weekly sports panels.

## 1. Research question
AlphaGen (Yu et al.) showed RL can mine formulaic alphas via an MDP over RPN token sequences, but it used PPO, which the authors argue is ineffective here. Can a plain REINFORCE miner — with a theoretically variance-bounded greedy baseline and Information Ratio (IR) reward shaping for "steady" factors — mine better, more volatility-robust formulaic alphas than PPO/A3C/TRPO, GP, and tree/ML baselines?

## 2. Dataset / schema
Six equity-index constituent panels: CSI300, CSI500, CSI1000, SPX, DJI, NDX; raw data from Chinese A-shares + US markets via Qlib. Six features only: open, close, high, low, volume, vwap. Target: 5-day forward asset return. Splits: train 2016-01-01–2020-01-01, validation 2020-01-01–2021-01-01, test 2021-01-01–2024-01-01 (time-ordered). Prices forward-dividend-adjusted to 2023-01-15 factors. Operator tokens: cross-sectional (Abs, Log, +/−/×/÷, Larger, Smaller) and time-series (Ref, Mean/Median/Sum/Std/Var/Max/Min/Mad/Delta/WMA/EMA/Cov/Corr with lookback l).

## 3. Method / model
**MDP formulation (from AlphaGen):** states = partial RPN token sequences; actions = next token from the operator/feature vocabulary; transition is deterministic (Dirac — the next state is uniquely the current sequence + chosen token); reward is sparse: r(s_t, a_t) = 0 for t≠T, r(a_{1:T}) = IC-bar of the completed formula's factor values vs. 5-day returns at step T. Objective J(θ) = E_{a_{1:T}~π_θ}[r(a_{1:T})]. Syntactically valid but non-evaluable expressions (e.g., Log of negative) get penalized/zeroed.
**QFR innovations:**
1. Drop the critic (no actor-critic): because transitions are deterministic and feedback is trajectory-level, PPO's critic adds bias without benefit; plain REINFORCE is argued more appropriate.
2. Variance-bounded baseline: a novel subtractive baseline generated by a greedy policy (take argmax token at each step); the paper proves an upper bound on the post-baseline variance and shows it is lower than vanilla REINFORCE (Prop. in Sec. IV-C, appendices).
3. IR reward shaping: a shaped term rewarding the Information Ratio of the mined factor, gated by an "IR test" that activates after a time delay α = 9×10⁴ steps, ramps with slope η = 2.65×10⁻⁶, capped at δ = 0.3, weighted by λ = 0.02. This pushes the policy toward factors that are consistently predictive across volatility regimes rather than short-term IC spikes.
**Training:** policy = LSTM feature extractor (2-layer, hidden 128, dropout 0.1); 5 random seeds; compared on equal footing with PPO/A3C/TRPO sharing the same extractor (PPO clip ε=0.2, TRPO δ=1.1×10⁻⁵). Hardware: single machine, i9-13900K + 2× RTX 4090.

## 4. Equations & assumptions
- J(θ) = E_{a_{1:T} ~ π_θ}[r(a_{1:T})]; r(s_t,a_t) = 0 if t≠T else r(a_{1:T}) = IC-bar (eq. 4–5).
- RankIC(z'_t, y_t) = IC(r(z'_t), r(y_t)), r(·) = ranking operator (eq. for evaluation metric).
- Shaped reward: r_shaped = r + λ · clip-IR-test term with indicator I(·), delay α, slope η, cap δ (eq. 11; λ=0.02, α=9×10⁴, η=2.65×10⁻⁶, δ=0.3).
- Theoretical: variance upper bound after greedy baseline < vanilla REINFORCE variance (Sec. IV-C propositions; proofs in appendix).
- Assumptions: deterministic (Dirac) transitions — true by construction for RPN generation; greedy-rollout baseline is near-optimal enough to reduce variance (not proven to be the optimal baseline); IR stability in training transfers to live stability; 5-day return is the right horizon; IC-bar is the right factor-quality proxy.

## 5. Features / target
Inputs: 6 raw fields (open, high, low, close, volume, vwap) + ts/cross-sectional operators with lookback windows. Target: 5-day forward asset return. Optimization metric: IC (only); evaluation: IC and RankIC; downstream: cumulative PnL of top-50 index-enhancement backtest and PnL across three CIMV volatility regimes.

## 6. Validation design
Time-ordered splits (train ≤2020 / val 2020–2021 / test 2021–2024). Baselines: RL miners (AlphaGen w/ PPO, TRPO, A3C — same extractor, 5 seeds), GP (gplearn), tree models (XGBoost, LightGBM per Qlib benchmarks), MLP. Metrics: IC, RankIC on CSI300/CSI500 mined-factor sets; RankIC learning curves on all six indices (reward not comparable due to shaping, so RankIC used instead); ablations removing baseline and removing reward shaping separately; hyperparameter study of (η, α, δ). Backtest: top-50 index-enhancement strategy on CSI300 test window + three China-IV (CIMV) volatility regimes (high vol 2021-01-01–2021-03-24 CIMV>20%; rapid decline 2022-07-01–2022-09-15; low vol 2023-01-01–2023-03-31).

## 7. Numerical results / baselines
- Table V (IC / RankIC, 5-seed std in parens). CSI300: MLP 0.0123/0.0178, XGB 0.0192/0.0241, LGBM 0.0158/0.0235, GP 0.0445/0.0673, AlphaGen 0.0500/0.0540, **QFR 0.0588(0.0022)/0.0602(0.0014)**. CSI500: MLP 0.0158/0.0211, XGB 0.0173/0.0217, LGBM 0.0112/0.0212, GP 0.0557/0.0665, AlphaGen 0.0544/0.0722, **QFR 0.0708(0.0063)/0.0674(0.0033)**.
- Vs. AlphaGen's PPO on the six indices: QFR improves RankIC by **3.83%** (headline claim), winning on all six, largest margin late in training.
- Reward-shaping hyperparameters: η=2.65×10⁻⁶ best trade-off; α below 7×10⁴ harms quality (IR test too early); robust to δ (settled 0.3).
- Backtest: QFR's mined factors give the highest terminal cumulative return among all methods on CSI300 2021–2024 (Fig. 7), and the most PnL in all three CIMV volatility regimes, with the largest edge in the high-volatility period (Fig. 8).
- Ablation: removing either the greedy baseline or the IR shaping degrades learning curves on all six indices (Fig. 9).

## 8. Code / data availability
Follows open-source implementations of AlphaGen, gplearn, Stable Baselines 3, Qlib. No new code URL stated in the paper ("None stated" for a QFR repo).

## 9. Leakage & limitations
- Reward-shaping hyperparameters (η, α, δ, λ) were tuned on the same indices used for evaluation — the "appropriate slope" claim risks tuning-to-test.
- IC-only optimization with shaping toward IR is internally inconsistent: they optimize IC but compare on RankIC because shaped rewards aren't comparable — a metric sleight-of-hand that flatters the learning curves.
- 5-day forward return target with vwap features: any feature computed with information past the trade point is lookahead; the paper doesn't do a strict point-in-time audit.
- The greedy baseline variance bound assumes the greedy rollout is a good proxy for the optimal baseline; no empirical measurement of actual gradient-variance reduction is reported.
- Backtest ignores transaction costs (index-enhancement top-50 with rebalancing is turnover-heavy).
- External validity: equity cross-sections are dense (hundreds of names × daily); a weekly NFL panel is ~32 teams × 18 weeks — the MDP's sample efficiency at that scale is unproven.

## 10. GSE overlap
New capability; no RL-based signal miner exists in the research map. Complements 2042 (grammar) and 2043 (AlphaForge): QFR is an alternative *miner* to AlphaForge's generator-predictor, with the distinctive contribution being steadiness shaping. If built, QFR-mined factors could feed the AlphaForge-style zoo + dynamic combination.

## 11. GSE implementation spec
1. MDP over sports formula tokens: state = partial RPN sequence, actions = operator/feature tokens (features = team-game stats + line-movement features), deterministic transitions, terminal reward = IC of formula vs. ATS cover residual on training seasons.
2. Policy: small LSTM/Transformer over token embeddings (sports vocab is smaller; hidden 64 suffices); REINFORCE + greedy-rollout baseline exactly as specified.
3. Reward shaping for sports: replace IR with a *regime-robustness* term — IC computed separately on early-season / mid-season / late-season splits, shaped reward = min (or harmonic mean) across splits, so the miner favors signals that work in all phases, not just one. Keep α-delay gating so shaping kicks in after the policy first finds valid formulas.
4. Serve mined formulas into the zoo; weekly re-evaluation.

## 12. Reproducible test
nflverse team-game panel 2009–2025; miner trains on 2009–2018, validates 2019–2021, tests 2022–2025. Baseline miners: random search and gplearn GP over the same token set and compute budget. Metric: test-window RankIC vs. ATS cover residual + Brier lift when added to base model.

## 13. Acceptance / rejection gate
ADAPT→build if QFR-miner beats GP by ≥ 20% relative RankIC on the 2022–2025 test block with ≤ the same GPU budget AND the top-10 mined factors each pass a White's-reality-check gate (p < 0.05) on the test block. REJECT if no better than GP, or if factors show early-season-only IC (shaping failed to generalize).

## 14. Improvement experiment
The paper shapes with IR computed on the *training* window — in sports, shape with *forward-walk* IR: evaluate candidate formulas on expanding-window out-of-sample folds during training and use the worst-fold IC as the shaped reward. This is stricter than the paper and directly targets the failure mode Garrett cares about (signals that die out of sample). Second: co-mine a small set jointly with a diversity bonus (like AlphaForge's zoo penalty) instead of mining factors one-by-one, since QFR as written mines single factors and the paper never addresses factor-set synergy.
