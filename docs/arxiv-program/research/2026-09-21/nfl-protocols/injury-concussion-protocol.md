# NFL Injury & Concussion Protocols — Machine-Readable Step Breakdown

**Purpose:** Encode the NFL's actual return-to-play rules as protocol steps for GSE, so availability modeling runs on the rules — not media noise.
**Date compiled:** 2026-09-21
**Document versions:** Concussion Return-to-Participation Protocol (NFL Head, Neck and Spine Committee; accessed via official-text mirror); Concussion Game Day Checklist (Revised October 2022 — current); Injury Report Policy (post-2016 "Probable" removal; post-2017 update rules).

> **How to read this doc:** Sections marked **[CONFIRMED]** come from official NFL / NFL-NFLPA documents. Sections marked **[REPORTED]** come from secondary reporting of official policy or verified league actions. Nothing here is invented — gaps are labeled.

## 1. CONCUSSION TRACK — step table

### 1A. Game-day sideline evaluation (Concussion Game Day Checklist, Rev. Oct 2022) [CONFIRMED]

| # | Trigger | Required action | Decision-maker | Observable output | Min time gate |
|---|---------|-----------------|----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| C1 | Head impact observed, OR player exhibits/reports concussion or stinger signs | Protocol initiated; player immediately removed to sideline (or stabilized on field) | Initiator: ATC, booth ATC, team physician, NFL official, coach, teammate, or UNC | Player leaves field; broadcast shows sideline/blue-tent evaluation | Immediate |
| C2 | Player on sideline, helmet removed | Sideline survey: No-Go check, History of Event, Concussion Signs/Symptoms, Maddock's Questions, Video Review, Focused Neurological Exam (cervical spine exam incl. ROM/pain, speech evaluation, gait observation, eye movements + pupillary exam) | Team Physician + UNC (Unaffiliated Neurotrauma Consultant) | None public (may appear as "being evaluated for concussion") | Minutes |
| C3a | Any No-Go sign observed at ANY point: **loss of consciousness (LOC), ataxia, confusion, amnesia** | **NO RETURN TO PLAY** — player ruled out of game | Team Physician / UNC | Ruled out; enters Return-to-Participation Protocol | Rest of game (minimum) |
| C3b | Normal sideline survey AND "benign" video review | Player **may return to play** | Team Physician + UNC | Returns to game | Minutes |
| C4 | Any element positive, inconclusive, or suspicious of concussion | Escort to locker room; full Locker Room Exam: Complete NFL SCAT + Complete Neurological Exam | Team Physician / UNC / ATC | "Questionable to return — concussion" (team announcement) | Remainder of evaluation |
| C5 | Progressive/worsening symptoms during exam | **NO RETURN TO PLAY** | Team Physician / UNC | Ruled out | Rest of game (minimum) |
| C6 | Abnormal locker room exam | **NO RETURN TO PLAY**; player stays in locker room; periodic evaluation + follow-up neurological exam | Medical team | Ruled out; concussion diagnosis → Return-to-Participation Protocol | Rest of game (minimum) |
| C7 | Normal locker room assessment | Player **may return to play** | Team Physician + UNC | Returns to game | — |

**No-Go list (current, Rev. Oct 2022):** LOC, **ataxia**, confusion, amnesia. [CONFIRMED]
Ataxia = "abnormality of balance/stability, motor coordination or dysfunctional speech caused by a neurological issue." Added October 2022 after the Tua Tagovailoa investigation; it replaced "gross motor instability" and eliminated the "alternative explanation" loophole — ataxia observed at any point = no return, no exceptions. [CONFIRMED via NFL-NFLPA joint statement, Oct 2022]
Impact seizure and fencing response are also treated as no-return indicators. [REPORTED — widely documented, not on the printed checklist]

**Key rules:**
- Once a concussion is **diagnosed**, the player may not return to that game ("Madden Rule" — the league's standing rule that a diagnosed concussion ends the player's day). [REPORTED as named rule; consistent with official docs]
- There is **no set timeframe** for return; every player and every concussion is unique. [CONFIRMED]
- The player must be **monitored daily** (or more frequently if the Team Physician deems it clinically indicated) through the entire protocol. [CONFIRMED]

### 1B. Return-to-Participation Protocol — the five steps [CONFIRMED]

**Gate rule (applies to every step transition):** A player may advance ONLY after demonstrating tolerance of ALL activities in the current step **without recurrence of observed or reported concussion signs/symptoms**. If a step's activities trigger recurrence → stop, return to the **prior step**. The player must stay at his pre-concussion baseline during exertion AND for a reasonable period afterward (Team Physician decides what "reasonable" is, case by case). Progression **may be accelerated** depending on severity and time to baseline. [CONFIRMED]

**Clearance authority:** The decision to return a player to participation is the professional judgment of the **Head Team Physician** (or the Team Physician designated for concussion evaluation/treatment). **ALL return-to-full-participation decisions must be confirmed by the Independent Neurological Consultant (INC).** The INC must be informed when the concussion occurs so consultation can be arranged at a medically appropriate time; the Team Physician may consult the INC as often as desired. Final clearance = team medical staff decision **confirmed by the INC**. [CONFIRMED]

| Step | Name | Required action | Decision-maker | Observable output | Min time gate |
|------|------|-----------------|----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Rest and Recovery | Physical + relative cognitive rest. Limit/avoid electronics, social media, team meetings until signs/symptoms AND neurological exam (incl. cognitive + balance tests) return to baseline. Limited stretching/balance as tolerated at medical staff discretion. External consultation if additional issues present. | Team Physician | "DNP — Concussion" on practice report; "Out" on game status | None fixed — until baseline |
| 2 | Light Aerobic Exercise | Graduated cardiovascular exercise (stationary bike, treadmill) under **direct oversight of team's medical staff**; dynamic stretching + balance training; duration/intensity increased only while player stays at baseline during + after. May attend team meetings, film study. | Team medical staff / Team Physician | Practice report may move DNP → Limited | None fixed |
| 3 | Continued Aerobic + Strength | Increased supervised CV exercise (may mimic sport-specific); **supervised strength training introduced**; non-contact sport-specific conditioning drills (change-of-direction, cone drills). | Team medical staff / Team Physician | Limited participation | None fixed |
| 4 | Football-Specific Activities | Non-contact football activities (throwing, catching, running, position-specific). **Strictly non-contact** — no contact with players OR objects (tackling dummies, sleds). | Team Physician | Limited participation (non-contact) | None fixed |
| 5 | Full Football Activity / Clearance | After tolerating non-contact football activity + meetings + conditioning + non-contact practice without recurrence AND neurocognitive testing back to baseline: (a) Team Physician clears for full **contact** activity → full practice; (b) if tolerated and physician concludes concussion resolved → clears to full participation; (c) **INC examines player**, receives ALL reports/tests incl. neurocognitive tests + interpretations; (d) if INC confirms → **cleared for team's next game or practice**. | Team Physician clears → **INC confirms** | Full practice participation; removed from injury report / no game designation | None fixed |

**Neurocognitive testing gates (within the five steps):** [CONFIRMED]
- Testing may be introduced any time after Step 1, or during Steps 2–3, but MUST be completed **before contact activities**.
- All tests interpreted by the team's neuropsychology consultant; results communicated to Team Physician.
- If not back to baseline → repeat at interval agreed by Team Physician + neuropsychology consultant (**typically 48 hours**); Team Physician must evaluate potential **non-injury causes** of any decrement.
- Player MAY do non-contact activities with abnormal testing; may NOT proceed to contact until testing is back to baseline OR Team Physician determines a **non-concussion cause**.

## 2. GENERAL INJURY TRACK — step table

No mandatory medical progression exists for non-concussion injuries. The binding constraints are **reporting** (what the club must disclose, when) and **team medical clearance** (club's judgment). [CONFIRMED structure; specifics below]

### 2A. Practice participation reports [CONFIRMED designations; cadence REPORTED]

| # | Trigger | Required action | Decision-maker | Observable output | Deadline |
|---|---------|-----------------|----------------|-------------------|----------|
| I1 | Player has an injury affecting practice (any injury causing a missed game or early exit from game/practice must be listed; clubs must specify body part + side) | Club lists player on daily practice report with participation level | Club (athletic training / medical staff) | Practice report entry | Daily during game week |
| I2 | Player takes no part of practice | Designate **Did Not Participate (DNP)** | Club | "DNP" | Same |
| I3 | Player completes only part of practice | Designate **Limited Participation (LP)** | Club | "LP" | Same |
| I4 | Player completes full practice | Designate **Full Participation (FP)** | Club | "FP" | Same |
| I5 | Veteran rest / non-injury absence | May be listed as DNP/LP with reason (e.g., NIR-Rest) — rest days are permitted but must be labeled | Club | "DNP (NIR-Rest)" etc. | Same |

**Cadence:** For a standard Sunday game: practice reports Wednesday, Thursday, Friday. Thursday/Saturday/Monday games shift the schedule (e.g., Thursday-game practice reports run on the compressed week). [REPORTED — consistent across outlets; exact league memo text not in hand]

### 2B. Game status report [CONFIRMED designations; deadline REPORTED]

| # | Trigger | Required action | Decision-maker | Observable output | Deadline |
|---|---------|-----------------|----------------|-------------------|----------|
| I6 | Final availability determination | Club assigns ONE game status to each listed player | Club / Head Coach | **Out** = will not play. **Doubtful** = unlikely to play. **Questionable** = uncertain to play. | **4:00 PM ET, two days before game day** (Friday for Sunday games; Wednesday for Thursday games) |
| I7 | Player certain to play | Remove from report (club must be able to explain to the league; discipline possible if the player is then deactivated) | Club | No designation | Same |
| I8 | Status changes after the report | Club must **update** the report | Club | Amended designation | As soon as possible |

**Designation history:** "Probable" was **eliminated in 2016** — ~95% of "probable" players played, making it meaningless. Any availability question now defaults to "Questionable"; certain-to-play players come off the report entirely. The separate "Out" category was also removed from the **practice** report — a player cannot be formally ruled out until the game-status report. [REPORTED — contemporaneous 2016 reporting, consistent across sources]

**Final availability:** The official inactive list (~90 minutes before kickoff) is the last word on who dresses. [REPORTED]

### 2C. Enforcement [CONFIRMED policy language; examples REPORTED]

Per league rules: *"A violation of the policy may result in Commissioner discipline, which may include a fine on the involved club, fines or suspensions of involved individuals, as well as the possible forfeiture of draft choices by the involved club."* [REPORTED — quoted from league rules via Sporting News]

## 3. CONCUSSION vs. GENERAL INJURY — the structural differences

| Dimension | Concussion track | General injury track |
|-----------|------------------|----------------------|
| Governing document | NFL Head, Neck and Spine Committee protocols (with NFLPA input) | NFL Injury Report Policy (league operations) |
| Mandatory progression | **Yes** — 5 graduated steps, each with tolerance gates; regression to prior step on symptom recurrence | **No** — no mandated steps; team medical judgment |
| Same-game return | **Prohibited** once diagnosed (Madden Rule); prohibited on any No-Go sign | Permitted if medically cleared |
| Independent clearance | **Required** — INC must confirm Team Physician's final clearance; INC informed at diagnosis | None — club medical staff decides |
| Cognitive testing | **Mandatory** neurocognitive baseline testing before contact; 48-hour retest cadence if abnormal | Not required |
| Minimum timeline | None fixed — but the step structure + daily monitoring + INC confirmation make sub-week returns rare | None — a player can go DNP Friday → play Sunday |
| Daily monitoring | **Required** (daily or more frequent) | Not required by policy |
| Reporting surface | Same injury report (DNP/LP/FP → Out/Doubtful/Questionable) | Same injury report |
| What moves the needle for GSE | Step-transition signals are mostly **unobservable** (only practice participation leaks); INC confirmation is the hard gate — no clearance without it | Practice participation **trend** (Wed→Thu→Fri) is the primary signal; game status is the binding label |

## 4. GSE ENCODING NOTES — what to watch for (protocol-derived, not media-derived)

1. **Concussion = absorbing state until INC confirms.** Any "concussion" designation means the 5-step progression is running. There is no legitimate same-week shortcut: beat reporters speculating about a concussed player "practicing Friday" are describing at most Step 3–4 (non-contact). Full clearance requires Team Physician + INC sign-off.
2. **The observable leak for concussions is practice participation.** DNP (concussion) → LP (concussion) maps to Steps 1–4. "Full" + no game designation after a concussion = Step 5 complete, INC confirmed. Encode the *transition*, not the beat writer's verb.
3. **Ataxia is an automatic season-of-the-day ender.** Since Oct 2022, any observed ataxia = no return to that game, no exceptions, no "back injury" alternative explanation. If video shows it and the player returns anyway, that's a protocol violation story, not a medical judgment call.
4. **For general injuries, the trend beats the label.** DNP→LP→FP = improving; FP→LP→DNP = deteriorating. Friday's designation is binding (Out = out; Doubtful ≈ out in practice). "Questionable" is the league's mandated bucket for *any* uncertainty since Probable was killed in 2016 — it is not a 50/50 coin flip, it's a range.
5. **Rest vs. injury is labeled.** NIR-Rest / veteran rest days are permitted and must be labeled as such (Roethlisberger 2019 is the cautionary fine). Don't model a rest DNP as an injury signal.
6. **Status changes must be re-reported.** A player added or downgraded after the Friday 4 PM ET report (the Bijan Robinson lesson) is itself a signal — and a finable offense if the club hides it.
