## 1. Gerard 't Hooft: *The Cellular Automaton Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics* (`978-3-319-41285-6.pdf`)

**Author:** Gerard 't Hooft (1999 Nobel Laureate in Physics, Utrecht University, Springer, 2016, 296 pp.)

### 1.1 Embedding Deterministic Permutation Automata into Unitary Hilbert Space (Ch. 2, Eq. 2.1–2.26)
Any discrete deterministic system whose ontological states $|A\rangle_{\text{ont}}$ evolve over time step $\delta t$ by an invertible permutation matrix $P_{AB}^{(\delta t)} \in \{0,1\}^{N\times N}$ can be written in quantum operator notation:
$$|A(t + \delta t)\rangle_{\text{ont}} = U_{\text{op}}(\delta t)|A(t)\rangle_{\text{ont}} = e^{-i H_{\text{op}}\delta t}|A(t)\rangle_{\text{ont}}$$
- **Three Classes of Operators (Sec. 2.1.1):**
  1. **Beables ($B_{\text{op}}$):** Diagonal in the ontological basis ($B_{\text{op}}|A\rangle_{\text{ont}} = b_A|A\rangle_{\text{ont}}$); commute with each other at equal times $[B_{\text{op}}^{(1)}(t), B_{\text{op}}^{(2)}(t)] = 0$.
  2. **Changeables ($C_{\text{op}}$):** Permute ontological states ($P_{\text{op}}$).
  3. **Superimposables ($S_{\text{op}}$):** Map ontological states into quantum template superpositions $|\psi\rangle = \sum_A \lambda_A |A\rangle_{\text{ont}}$.
- **Exact Discrete Fourier Hamiltonian of the $N$-State Cogwheel Automaton (Eq. 2.21–2.26):**
  Transforming to energy eigenstates $|k\rangle_H = \frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1} e^{2\pi i k n/N}|n\rangle_{\text{ont}}$ yields spectrum $E_k = \frac{2\pi k}{N\delta t}$ ($k=0,\dots,N-1$). In the ontological basis, the exact nonlocal Hamiltonian generating unit-step permutation is:
  $$H_{\text{op}} = \frac{\pi}{\delta t}\left(1 - \frac{1}{N}\right) - \frac{\pi}{N\delta t}\sum_{n=1}^{N-1}\left(\frac{i}{\tan(\pi n / N)} + 1\right)U_{\text{op}}(n\delta t)$$
  and in the infinite-period limit $N \to \infty$ (Eq. 2.8):
  $$H_{\text{op}}\delta t = \pi - i\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n}\Big(U_{\text{op}}(n\delta t) - U_{\text{op}}(-n\delta t)\Big)$$

### 1.2 Bell's Theorem, CHSH Inequality ($S = 2\sqrt{2} > 2$), and the 3-Point Mouse Dropping Distribution (Ch. 3, Eq. 3.12–3.23)
To reproduce the quantum singlet correlation $\langle AB\rangle = \cos(2(a-b))$ (which violates the classical CHSH bound $|S| \le 2$ with $S = 2\sqrt{2}$ at $a=22.5^\circ, a'=-22.5^\circ, b=0^\circ, b'=45^\circ$) using deterministic sign readouts $A = \operatorname{sign}(\cos(2(c-a))),\, B = \operatorname{sign}(\cos(2(c-b)))$, 't Hooft solves the integral equation:
$$\int_0^\pi dc\, W(c \mid a,b)\,\operatorname{sign}\big(\cos 2(c-a)\big)\operatorname{sign}\big(\cos 2(c-b)\big) = \cos\big(2(b-a)\big)$$
by differentiation with respect to $z = \frac{1}{2}(b-a)$, obtaining the exact **Mouse Dropping Probability Distribution** (Eq. 3.22–3.23):
$$W(c \mid a,b) = \frac{1}{2}\big|\sin(4c - 2a - 2b)\big|, \qquad W(a,b,c) = \frac{1}{2\pi^2}\big|\sin(4c - 2a - 2b)\big|$$
whose marginal over any single variable $a$, $b$, or $c$ is strictly uniform!

### 1.3 Information-Loss Automata & Info-Equivalence Classes (Ch. 7, pp. 92–93)
When a deterministic transition map $f: \mathcal{S} \to \mathcal{S}$ is non-invertible (many-to-one mergers and "Garden of Eden" transient states), defining the **Information Equivalence Relation**:
$$a \sim_{\text{info}} b \iff \exists\, t_1 > t_0 \text{ such that } U(t_1, t_0)(a) = U(t_1, t_0)(b)$$
quotients the state space into equivalence classes $\mathcal{S}/\!\sim_{\text{info}}$ on which the induced time evolution is strictly bijective and therefore **unitary in Hilbert space**.

### 1.4 Discretized PQ Lattice Hamiltonian & Discrete Liouville Theorem (Ch. 19, Eq. 19.22–19.24)
On an integer phase-space lattice $(\vec{Q}, \vec{P}) \in \mathbb{Z}^{2n}$, an integer-valued Hamiltonian $H(\vec{Q},\vec{P}) = T(\vec{P}) + V(\vec{Q}) + A(\vec{Q})B(\vec{P})$ defines energy contours $\Sigma_E = \{(\vec{Q},\vec{P}) \in \mathbb{Z}^{2n} : H(\vec{Q},\vec{P}) = E\}$. Updating coordinates via cyclic one-dimensional symplectic contour permutations $U_{\text{op}}(\delta t) = U_n^{\text{op}} U_{n-1}^{\text{op}} \cdots U_1^{\text{op}}$ exactly conserves the discrete Liouville lattice volume and converges to continuum Hamilton equations $\dot{q}_i = \partial H/\partial p_i,\, \dot{p}_i = -\partial H/\partial q_i$.

## 2. Statistical Learning Theory, Nonparametric & Causal Inference, and Algorithmic ML (`Shalizi`, `Moitra`, `Shalev-Shwartz & Ben-David`, `Unpingco`)

### 2.1 Cosma Shalizi (`ADAfaEPoV.pdf`, CMU): Nonparametric Smoothing & Pearl's Graphical Causal Calculus
1. **Nadaraya–Watson Kernel Regression & Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation Shortcut:**
   $$\hat{\mu}_h(x) = \sum_{i=1}^n w_i(x) y_i, \qquad w_i(x) = \frac{K\!\left(\frac{x - x_i}{h}\right)}{\sum_{j=1}^n K\!\left(\frac{x - x_j}{h}\right)}, \qquad \operatorname{LOOCV}(h) = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \left(\frac{y_i - \hat{\mu}_h(x_i)}{1 - S_{ii}(h)}\right)^2$$
   where $S_{ii}(h) = w_i(x_i)$ is the diagonal of the smoother matrix $\mathbf{S}(h)$ — allowing exact leave-one-out cross-validation in **one pass** without refitting $n$ times!
2. **Back-Door & Front-Door Causal Identification (`do(X = x)`):**
   - **Back-Door Adjustment Formula:** If conditioning set $Z$ blocks all back-door paths from $X$ to $Y$ ($X \leftarrow \cdots \to Y$) and contains no descendants of $X$:
     $$\mathbb{P}\big(Y = y \mid \operatorname{do}(X = x)\big) = \sum_{z} \mathbb{P}(Y = y \mid X = x, Z = z)\,\mathbb{P}(Z = z)$$
   - **Front-Door Adjustment Formula:** When an unobserved confounder $U$ affects both $X$ and $Y$ ($X \leftarrow U \to Y$), but $X$'s effect on $Y$ is mediated entirely by an observed mediator $M$ ($X \to M \to Y$) with no direct $U \to M$ path:
     $$\mathbb{P}\big(Y = y \mid \operatorname{do}(X = x)\big) = \sum_{m} \mathbb{P}(M = m \mid X = x) \sum_{x'} \mathbb{P}(Y = y \mid X = x', M = m)\,\mathbb{P}(X = x')$$

### 2.2 Ankur Moitra (`Algorithmic Aspects of Machine Learning`, MIT): Separable NMF & Jennrich's Tensor Decomposition
1. **Separable Nonnegative Matrix Factorization (`Arora–Ge–Kannan–Moitra`):**
   Given $\mathbf{M} = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}_+^{V\times D}$ where $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}_+^{V\times K}$ satisfies the **anchor-word separability condition** (each topic/factor $k \in [K]$ has an anchor row $i_k$ where $A_{i_k, k} > 0$ and $A_{i_k, k'} = 0$ for $k' \neq k$), the rows of normalized $\mathbf{M}$ lie in the convex hull of the $K$ anchor rows, which are recovered in polynomial time via successive random projections (`FastAnchorWords`).
2. **Jennrich's Algorithm for 3rd-Order Latent Variable Tensor Decomposition:**
   While matrix decomposition $\mathbf{M}_2 = \sum_{k=1}^K \lambda_k \mathbf{u}_k \mathbf{u}_k^\top$ suffers from an $O(K)$ rotational ambiguity (requiring orthogonal factors), a 3rd-order moment tensor:
   $$\mathcal{T}_3 = \sum_{k=1}^K \lambda_k\, \mathbf{u}_k \otimes \mathbf{u}_k \otimes \mathbf{u}_k \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times d\times d}$$
   has a **unique decomposition up to permutation and scaling (Kruskal's Theorem)** whenever $\{\mathbf{u}_k\}$ are merely linearly independent! Contracting $\mathcal{T}_3$ along two random vectors $\mathbf{a}, \mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^d$ forms matrices $\mathbf{T}(\mathbf{a}) = \mathbf{U}\mathbf{D}_a\mathbf{U}^\top$ and $\mathbf{T}(\mathbf{b}) = \mathbf{U}\mathbf{D}_b\mathbf{U}^\top$, and the eigenvectors of $\mathbf{T}(\mathbf{a})\mathbf{T}(\mathbf{b})^\dagger$ recover the exact latent factors $\mathbf{u}_1, \dots, \mathbf{u}_K$ with distinct eigenvalues $\langle \mathbf{a}, \mathbf{u}_k\rangle / \langle \mathbf{b}, \mathbf{u}_k\rangle$.

### 2.3 Shalev-Shwartz & Ben-David (`Understanding ML`): Empirical Rademacher Complexity Generalization Bound
For any hypothesis class $\mathcal{H}$ mapping $\mathcal{X} \to [-1, 1]$ and loss $\ell$ with Lipschitz constant $L_\ell$, with probability at least $1 - \delta$ over $S \sim \mathcal{D}^m$:
$$L_{\mathcal{D}}(h) \le \widehat{L}_S(h) + 2 L_\ell\,\widehat{\mathcal{R}}_S(\mathcal{H}) + 3\sqrt{\frac{2\ln(4/\delta)}{m}}, \qquad \widehat{\mathcal{R}}_S(\mathcal{H}) = \frac{1}{m}\mathbb{E}_{\bm{\sigma}\sim\{\pm 1\}^m}\!\left[\sup_{h\in\mathcal{H}} \sum_{i=1}^m \sigma_i h(x_i)\right]$$

### 2.4 José Unpingco (`Python for Probability, Statistics, and Machine Learning`, Springer): Conditional Expectation as Minimum-Variance $L^2$ Orthogonal Projection
For any square-integrable target random variable $Y \in L^2(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ and sub-$\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ generated by conditioning information $X$:
$$\mathbb{E}[Y \mid X] = \arg\min_{g(X)\in L^2(\mathcal{G})} \mathbb{E}\!\Big[\big(Y - g(X)\big)^2\Big], \qquad \mathbb{E}\!\Big[\big(Y - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid X]\big)\,h(X)\Big] = 0 \quad \forall\, h(X) \in L^2(\mathcal{G})$$
inducing the exact **Pythagorean / Law of Total Variance Decomposition**:
$$\operatorname{Var}(Y) = \mathbb{E}\big[\operatorname{Var}(Y \mid X)\big] + \operatorname{Var}\big(\mathbb{E}[Y \mid X]\big)$$

## 3. Spectral Graph Neural Networks (`Ma & Tang`, `Rogel-Salazar`), Differentiable Physics (`Thuerey`), Multimodal PID (`Liang et al.`), and Robust Statistics (`Zavarella`)

### 3.1 Spectral Graph Convolutions & Dirichlet Energy Over-Smoothing (`Deep Learning on Graphs` — Yao Ma & Jiliang Tang)
1. **Symmetric Normalized Graph Laplacian & Chebyshev Spectral Filtering:**
   $$\tilde{\mathbf{L}} = \mathbf{I}_N - \mathbf{D}^{-1/2}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{D}^{-1/2} = \mathbf{U}\mathbf{\Lambda}\mathbf{U}^\top, \qquad \lambda_i \in [0, 2]$$
   $$\mathbf{g}_\theta \star_{\mathcal{G}} \mathbf{x} = \sum_{k=0}^K \theta_k T_k(\hat{\mathbf{L}})\mathbf{x}, \qquad \hat{\mathbf{L}} = \frac{2}{\lambda_{\max}}\tilde{\mathbf{L}} - \mathbf{I}_N$$
   where $T_k(z) = 2z T_{k-1}(z) - T_{k-2}(z)$ are Chebyshev polynomials, avoiding $\mathcal{O}(N^3)$ eigendecomposition and localizing the filter to $K$-hop neighborhoods in $\mathcal{O}(K|\mathcal{E}|)$ operations.
2. **Dirichlet Energy & Over-Smoothing Bound:**
   The graph Dirichlet energy measuring node-feature variation across edges $\mathcal{E}$ is:
   $$E_{\text{Dir}}(\mathbf{X}) = \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{tr}\!\big(\mathbf{X}^\top \tilde{\mathbf{L}} \mathbf{X}\big) = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}} A_{ij}\left\|\frac{\mathbf{x}_i}{\sqrt{d_i}} - \frac{\mathbf{x}_j}{\sqrt{d_j}}\right\|_2^2$$
   Repeated multiplication by $\tilde{\mathbf{A}}_{\text{sym}} = \mathbf{I} - \tilde{\mathbf{L}}$ contracts $E_{\text{Dir}}(\mathbf{X}^{(\ell)}) \le (1 - \lambda_2)^\ell E_{\text{Dir}}(\mathbf{X}^{(0)}) \to 0$, collapsing node representations into the stationary degree-weighted subspace unless residual skip connections (`GCNII`) or pair-norm regularization are applied.

### 3.2 Multimodal Partial Information Decomposition (`PID` — Liang, Zadeh, Morency, CMU `arXiv:2301.04856`)
Given two signal modalities $X_1, X_2$ (e.g., Layer 1 Market Line + Layer 2 Situational Film/Injury Overlay) predicting outcome $Y$, the joint mutual information $I(X_1, X_2; Y)$ decomposes into four non-negative atoms:
$$I(X_1, X_2; Y) = \underbrace{R(X_1, X_2; Y)}_{\text{Redundancy}} + \underbrace{U(X_1; Y \setminus X_2)}_{\text{Unique to } X_1} + \underbrace{U(X_2; Y \setminus X_1)}_{\text{Unique to } X_2} + \underbrace{S(X_1, X_2; Y)}_{\text{Synergy}}$$
where marginal mutual informations satisfy $I(X_1; Y) = R + U_1$ and $I(X_2; Y) = R + U_2$, so the **Interaction Information (Co-Information)** is:
$$I(X_1; X_2; Y) = I(X_1, X_2; Y) - I(X_1; Y) - I(X_2; Y) = S(X_1, X_2; Y) - R(X_1, X_2; Y)$$
- When $S > R$, combining $X_1$ and $X_2$ yields strict **emergent synergy** (analogous to positive Shapley Interaction $I(1,2) > 0$ in `Det-MASO`); when $R > S$, naive additive fusion double-counts redundant information!

### 3.3 Differentiable Physics Unrolling (`Thuerey et al.`, TUM `arXiv:2109.05237`)
Instead of penalizing a soft PDE residual loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{PINN}} = \|\mathcal{R}(\hat{u})\|_2^2$ (which struggles with stiff multi-step trajectories), **Differentiable Physics (`DP`)** embeds the discrete physical step operator $\mathcal{P}$ inside the unrolled training loop over $K$ steps:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{DP}}(\theta) = \sum_{k=1}^K \Big\|\mathbf{u}_{t+k}^{\text{true}} - \big(\mathcal{P}\circ \mathcal{C}_\theta\big)^{(k)}(\mathbf{u}_t)\Big\|_2^2, \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\text{DP}}}{\partial \theta} = \sum_{k=1}^K \left(\frac{\partial \mathcal{P}}{\partial \mathbf{u}}\right)^\top \cdots \frac{\partial \mathcal{C}_\theta}{\partial \theta}$$
providing exact multi-step trajectory gradients through the simulator.

### 3.4 Robust Multivariate Outlier Detection & Asymmetric Association (`Zavarella` — *Extending Power BI with Python and R*)
1. **Theil's Uncertainty Coefficient ($U(X \mid Y)$):**
   $$U(X \mid Y) = \frac{H(X) - H(X \mid Y)}{H(X)} = \frac{\sum_{x,y} p(x,y)\ln\!\left(\frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)p(y)}\right)}{-\sum_x p(x)\ln p(x)} \in [0, 1]$$
2. **Yeo–Johnson + Minimum Covariance Determinant (`MinCovDet`) Robust Mahalanobis Gate:**
   $$D_{\text{MCD}}^2(\mathbf{x}) = \big(\psi_\lambda(\mathbf{x}) - \hat{\bm{\mu}}_{\text{MCD}}\big)^\top \hat{\mathbf{\Sigma}}_{\text{MCD}}^{-1} \big(\psi_\lambda(\mathbf{x}) - \hat{\bm{\mu}}_{\text{MCD}}\big) > \chi^2_{p, 1-\alpha}$$
3. **Karney Ellipsoidal Geodesic Distance + Kantorovich Transportation LP (`PuLP` / `ompr`):**
   Solves WGS-84 stadium-to-stadium travel distances to `<15 nm` precision and couples with integer transportation LP $\min \sum_{i,j} c_{ij}x_{ij}$.
