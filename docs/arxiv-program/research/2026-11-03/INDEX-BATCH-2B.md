## Lane 1 — Optical / camera player & ball tracking

**Stats LLC (2013).** "System and Method for Location Tracking." Patent No. US 8,482,612.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US8482612B2/en · File: `Stats-US8482612-LocationTracking.pdf` · 0.95 MB · 13 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: A tracking system split between an instrument embedded in the sport ball itself — an inertial first state module, wireless transmitter and battery, with its centre of mass coincident with the ball's — and an independent second state module of at least two displaced camera clusters (GPS in some claims). The ball's state is computed by combining the inertial parameters with camera data, propagating inertially between camera fixes in Kalman-filter fashion. It claims the fused ball-state pipeline, not just a camera or just an in-ball sensor.
- TRANSFER: A template for GSE's tracking ingestion: treat optical fixes as intermittent ground truth and propagate state between them with an inertial/process model instead of interpolating blindly. The "two independent state modules, fused" split is directly reusable for ball-flight and player-position reconstruction from partial vendor feeds.

**Sony Corporation / Sony Europe B.V. (2022).** "Method, Apparatus and Computer Program for Image Processing Including Generating a Different Image of at Least One Object." Patent No. US 11,244,457.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US11244457B2/en · File: `Sony-US11244457-ImageProcessing.pdf` · 2.56 MB · 28 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: Locate an object in a scene image, select a portion of the image based on the object's location, generate a smaller "different image" of that object from the selected portion, and then detect a plurality of points corresponding to parts of the object in the scene using that different image. Inventor Luke Dodd (Basingstoke) is Hawk-Eye lineage — this is a crop-then-detect keypoint pipeline. The claims cover the two-stage locate → crop → part-detection sequence.
- TRANSFER: Crop-and-detect is the practical architecture for extracting player keypoints from wide broadcast frames at fixed compute cost: a coarse detector proposes regions, a fine model reads joints only inside crops. GSE's pose ingestion can copy the staging (and the resolution trade-off it implies) rather than running a heavy pose model on full frames.

**Second Spectrum, Inc. (2020).** "Methods and Systems of Spatiotemporal Pattern Recognition for Video Content Development." Patent No. US 10,755,102.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US10755102B2/en · File: `SecondSpectrum-US10755102-SpatiotemporalPatternRecognition.pdf` · 12.21 MB · 112 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: Process a video feed through a machine-learning spatiotemporal pattern-recognition algorithm that detects an event characterised by entries in a relationship library describing spatiotemporal relationships between two visible features, at least one of them an active participant, using features such as relative direction, duration, rate and acceleration of motion of the features with respect to each other and their projected point of intersection. The participant's point of view is identified — claim 1 specifies frame-by-frame jersey-number detection stitched into a temporal jersey number track — and the event is extracted into a video content data structure carrying context information. Dependent claims add matching events across separate feeds, aligning unsynchronized broadcast, tracking and play-by-play feeds, fusing chip-based player tracking data, and building a semantic index that locates events in time and pixel space.
- TRANSFER: This is the event-detection layer that sits on top of raw tracking in GSE's ingestion: define plays as relationships between two tracked entities (e.g. defender–receiver separation and its rate of change, projected intersection of two routes) rather than as frame labels, and store detections in a queryable semantic index. The multi-feed alignment claims also name the genuinely hard part of merging vendor tracking with broadcast video and play-by-play — unsynchronized feeds need an explicit alignment stage, with validation, before any feature is computed.

## Lane 2 — Wearable athlete load / fatigue / injury-risk monitoring

**Sportzco Pty Ltd / M.B.T.L. Limited (2010).** "Monitoring Sports." Patent No. US 7,715,982.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US7715982B2/en · File: `Sportzco-US7715982-MonitoringSports.pdf` · 1.11 MB · 23 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: A wearable data logger for an athlete combining an accelerometer, a GPS receiver (position and velocity) and a heart-rate monitor, with a controller that derives and displays heart rate, speed and sport parameters, storing the data or transmitting it to a coach's computer. This is the foundational Catapult-lineage patent: the single-unit accel + GPS + HR logger. The claims are broad hardware-integration claims rather than a specific load algorithm.
- TRANSFER: Mostly historical/defensive — the transferable content is the sensor set and the derived-parameter list (speed zones plus HR from one unit) that became the standard athlete-monitoring record. Useful to GSE as the schema baseline for any wearable feed the workload module ingests, not as an algorithm source.

**Catapult Group International Ltd. (2019).** "Managing Mechanical Stress in Sports Participants." Patent No. US 10,372,975.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US10372975B2/en · File: `Catapult-US10372975-ManagingMechanicalStress.pdf` · 0.71 MB · 11 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: A system for managing ice-hockey player availability using torso-mounted data loggers (accelerometer + gyroscope) fixed to the upper torso/back: a computing device identifies data points per second and counts player movements by calculating features within sub-sections of the inertial data. The specification ties the counted load (PlayerLoad™-style accumulation, stratified into band zones) to injury probability and availability management. Claim 1 was spot-checked against the PDF text (pp. 11–12).
- TRANSFER: The closest thing in this batch to a ready-made spec for GSE's workload-hazard module: window the IMU stream, extract per-window features, accumulate a load score, band it, and map bands to an availability/injury-risk output. The honest caveat is that the band→probability mapping in the patent is asserted, not validated — GSE would need its own calibration.

**Whoop, Inc. (2017).** "Heart Rate Variability with Sleep Detection." Patent No. US 9,743,848.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US9743848B2/en · File: `Whoop-US9743848-HRVwithSleepDetection.pdf` · 4.73 MB · 63 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: A wearable continuously tracks sleep states and transitions together with heart rate, and evaluates heart-rate variability at a specific point: immediately before waking, at the end of the last phase of deep sleep. A recovery score is then calculated from that HRV measurement. The claimed mechanism is the timing of the HRV read (end of final deep-sleep phase), not HRV measurement in general.
- TRANSFER: Measurement timing as a feature-engineering rule: if the hazard module consumes HRV, sample it at a physiologically anchored moment (end of last deep-sleep phase / pre-wake) instead of averaging overnight, which is when the signal is cleanest and most comparable day to day. Cheap to implement, plausibly large signal-quality gain.

**Whoop, Inc. (2019).** "Physiological Measurement System with Motion Sensing." Patent No. US 10,264,982.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US10264982B2/en · File: `Whoop-US10264982-PhysiologicalMeasurement.pdf` · 5.60 MB · 54 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: A wearable strap measures heart rate optically (reflected light / PPG) while a motion sensor determines the user's motion status, and the system uses that motion status to control when and how the light emitters run to determine heart rate — motion-gated optical sensing. The specification builds strain/intensity scoring from a weighted heart-rate-reserve time series and recovery scoring from HRV, resting heart rate and sleep quality. The granted claims centre on the motion-aware measurement system.
- TRANSFER: Two transfers: (1) gate or down-weight optical HR samples by concurrent motion status before they ever reach a load model — a data-quality rule for any wearable ingestion; (2) the HRR-weighted time-series construction is a concrete recipe for turning raw HR into a single strain number the hazard module can consume.

## Lane 3 — Prediction markets / exchange matching & market-making

**Longitude, Inc. (2001).** "Financial Products Having a Demand-Based, Adjustable Return, and Trading Exchange Therefor." Patent No. US 6,321,212.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US6321212B1/en · File: `Longitude-US6321212-DemandBasedAdjustableReturn.pdf` · 10.01 MB · 91 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: Define a set of mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive "states" for an event; accept investments distributed across those states; let the return on each state adjust with demand (parimutuel-style); and on termination criteria, allocate the payout from the aggregate pool to the winning state. It is the foundational demand-based / event-contract pricing patent — the exchange never takes the other side, prices emerge entirely from the distribution of money across states.
- TRANSFER: The strongest market-mechanism transfer in this batch: a complete, closed-form pricing rule for mutually exclusive outcomes (win / loss / draw; over / under bands; exact-score states) that needs no bookmaker margin model and no counterparty risk. Directly portable to GSE's prediction-market-style pricing and to converting model probabilities into pool-implied prices for comparison against the engine's own numbers.

**Massachusetts Institute of Technology (2009).** "Electronic Market-Maker." Patent No. US 7,599,876.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US7599876B1/en · File: `MIT-US7599876-ElectronicMarketMaker.pdf` · 0.83 MB · 14 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: An automated market-maker decomposed into three modules that each generate trading recommendations — a Market Quality Control Module, an Inventory Control Module and a Speculation Module — plus an Arbitration Module that receives all three recommendation streams and resolves conflicts between them. The claimed invention is the modular architecture plus arbitration, not any single pricing formula.
- TRANSFER: An architecture pattern for GSE pricing: separate "keep the market fair/tight" (quality), "don't get over-exposed on one outcome" (inventory) and "trade on our model's edge" (speculation) into independent modules, and make the conflict-resolution rule explicit and auditable. That maps cleanly onto how GSE should blend model price, market price and exposure limits instead of mashing them into one formula.

**Bprotocol Foundation / Localcoin Ltd. (2021).** "Methods for Exchanging and Evaluating Virtual Currency." Patent No. US 11,107,049.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US11107049B2/en · File: `Bprotocol-US11107049-ExchangingEvaluatingVirtualCurrency.pdf` · 1.99 MB · 29 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: A smart-contract token exchange on a secure ledger in which price is determined from token status and a Constant Reserve Ratio (CRR): unit price = reserve balance / (CRR × token supply), with conversions executed against the reserve so liquidity is continuous and no counterparty is needed. This is the Bancor automated-market-maker patent. Honest note: its practical strength is doubtful — SDNY dismissed Bancor's infringement suit against Uniswap in Feb 2026, and the claims are vulnerable on patent-eligibility grounds.
- TRANSFER: The CRR formula is the constant-function AMM in its simplest closed form — a usable reference implementation for any GSE market-making simulation where a bounded-loss, always-on quote is needed (compare MIT 7,599,876's architecture and Longitude's pool). Treat as mechanism reference, not as freedom-to-operate.

## Lane 4 — Probabilistic forecasting / calibration

**The Climate Corporation (2021).** "Generating and Conveying Comprehensive Weather Insights at Fields for Optimal Agricultural Decision Making." Patent No. US 11,009,625.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US11009625B2/en · File: `Climate-US11009625-WeatherInsightsCalibration.pdf` · 6.03 MB · 40 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: Train a machine-learning model on archived forecast-model output paired with archived observed outcomes; apply that trained model to current forecast data to produce a calibrated forecast probability density function; and display the observations together with the calibrated PDF. The claim is the archive-trained calibration step that turns a raw model forecast into a full probability distribution, not a point estimate.
- TRANSFER: A direct blueprint for GSE's forecasting-engine calibration layer: keep an archive of (model forecast, realised outcome) pairs, learn the forecast→outcome error distribution, and ship calibrated PDFs (full distributions over margins/totals) instead of point predictions. The archive-pairing discipline is the part most forecasting stacks skip.

**The Weather Company, LLC (2024).** "Generation of Weather Analytical Scenarios Translating Likely Airport Capacity Impact from Probabilistic Weather Forecast." Patent No. US 12,025,768.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US12025768B2/en · File: `WeatherCompany-US12025768-AirportCapacityProbabilistic.pdf` · 1.39 MB · 19 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: Receive a probabilistic forecast expressed as a plurality of weather scenarios; predict an impact (here, airport capacity) separately for each scenario; cluster the per-scenario impact predictions; and generate at least one aggregated prediction from the clustered predictions. The mechanism is scenario → per-scenario downstream prediction → cluster → aggregate, rather than averaging the forecast first and predicting once.
- TRANSFER: Predict-per-scenario, aggregate-last is the correct order of operations for GSE game forecasting too: run the engine on each sampled game script (weather, pace, injury scenarios), cluster the resulting outcome distributions, and report the aggregate — averaging inputs first destroys exactly the tail structure that prices props and totals.

**The Weather Company, LLC (2026).** "Modifying Activities Based on Probabilistic Weather Scenarios." Patent No. US 12,631,787.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US12631787B2/en · File: `WeatherCompany-US12631787-ProbabilisticWeatherScenarios.pdf` · 1.37 MB · 19 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: A continuation of US 12,025,768: receive weather scenarios with associated probabilities; an ML model trained on historical weather and event data makes event predictions per scenario; time-series clustering combines similar scenarios into analytical scenarios whose probability is the summed share of their constituent scenarios; and an activity modification is determined from the aggregated predictions and probabilities. It adds probability-weighted scenario merging and the decision step on top of the parent patent.
- TRANSFER: The probability bookkeeping is the transferable part: when GSE merges similar simulated game scripts, scenario weights must be summed, not averaged away, so the final distribution stays calibrated. Also a clean pattern for turning a scenario distribution into a single recommended action (play / rest / price move) with the probabilities still attached.

## Lane 5 — Sensor fusion & pose estimation

**Microsoft Corporation (2013).** "Human Body Pose Estimation." Patent No. US 8,503,720.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US8503720B2/en · File: `Microsoft-US8503720-HumanBodyPoseEstimation.pdf` · 6.27 MB · 26 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: Process depth-map images to calculate, for each pixel, a probability that it belongs to a given body part (using learned depth-feature tests / decision trees on depths of offset pixels); construct body parts from the classified pixels; derive joints, nodes and centroids; and assemble a 3D body pose from them. This is the Kinect pose pipeline (Shotton et al.). The claims cover per-pixel body-part classification as the route to pose, rather than template matching a skeleton.
- TRANSFER: The per-pixel part-probability formulation is the ancestor of modern pose stacks and still instructive for GSE: keep per-keypoint probability maps (not just point estimates) through ingestion, so downstream features can weight joint positions by confidence instead of treating noisy joints as exact.

**Xsens Holding B.V. (2019).** "Integration of Inertial Tracking and Position Aiding for Motion Capture." Patent No. US 10,222,450.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US10222450B2/en · File: `Xsens-US10222450-InertialTrackingPositionAiding.pdf` · 1.30 MB · 20 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: Integrate inertial motion capture with external position aiding (e.g. optical) while explicitly addressing the misalignment between the inertial (relative) reference frame and the external positioning frame, correcting that misalignment so orientation — including yaw — can be tracked in a common frame even when magnetometers cannot be relied on. The claims centre on the frame-alignment / aiding integration, the part of fusion that naive implementations hand-wave.
- TRANSFER: When GSE fuses wearable IMU data with optical tracking (the Stats 8,482,612 pattern), the two streams live in different frames and drift differently; this patent is a worked treatment of estimating and correcting that frame misalignment. Practical ingestion rule: estimate the frame transform as a state variable, never assume the vendor streams are already registered.

**Stats LLC (2025).** "Body Pose Tracking of Players from Sports Broadcast Video Feed." Patent No. US 12,299,852.
- Google Patents: https://patents.google.com/patent/US12299852B2/en · File: `Stats-US12299852-BodyPoseTrackingBroadcast.pdf` · 1.42 MB · 23 pp
- WHAT IT CLAIMS: Generate refined, denoised body-pose data from a broadcast video feed: use tracking-data player locations to assign poses to players by correspondence (e.g. the pose whose midpoint of key footpoints is closest to the tracked location); refine poses by estimating missing limbs and correcting unusual limb-length ratios; filter out spectator and other noisy poses; and make the refined poses usable for 3D projection onto the play surface. The claimed mechanism is the refinement/assignment layer on top of raw pose estimates, not the pose estimator itself.
- TRANSFER: Directly applicable to any GSE pose ingestion from broadcast video: raw pose output is not usable until it is (a) assigned to tracked identities by footpoint correspondence, (b) repaired for missing/implausible limbs, and (c) stripped of crowd false positives. This is the checklist for the cleaning stage between a pose model and any downstream feature.

## 1. Statistical mechanics — the two textbooks

**Sethna, J. P. (2021).** *Statistical Mechanics: Entropy, Order Parameters, and Complexity.* 2nd edition, Oxford University Press (author-posted full manuscript, Cornell).
- Source: https://sethna.lassp.cornell.edu/StatMech/EntropyOrderParametersComplexity20.pdf · File: `Sethna-2021-StatisticalMechanicsEntropyOrderParametersComplexity.pdf` · 19.12 MB · 494 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: The complete second-edition book, not a sample: random walks and diffusion (Ch. 2, including stock-volatility and derivative-pricing exercises), temperature and ensembles, entropy treated three ways (irreversibility, disorder, ignorance/information), free energies, quantum statistical mechanics, interacting particles and phase transitions, order parameters and broken symmetry, and a full renormalization-group chapter, with Sethna's large computational exercise sets throughout. Graduate level, unusually interdisciplinary.
- TRANSFER: The entropy-as-ignorance chapters are the physics root of the forecasting engine's distribution thinking — a probability distribution over game outcomes *is* the state description, and its entropy is the honest uncertainty number. The order-parameter/RG material feeds regime modeling: a team crossing a "phase boundary" (QB change, scheme change) is not a smooth drift, and Sethna's machinery says what changes qualitatively at the boundary. The random-walk chapter's volatility exercises transfer directly to modeling line movement and margin diffusion.

**Kardar, M. (2013).** *Statistical Mechanics I: Statistical Mechanics of Particles* — MIT OpenCourseWare 8.333 lecture notes (Fall 2013). The lecture-notes substitute for his Cambridge textbook *Statistical Physics of Particles* (2007), which is paywalled — see gap list.
- Source: MIT OCW, https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/pages/lecture-notes/ · File: `Kardar-2013-LectureNotesStatisticalMechanicsParticles.pdf` · 1.32 MB · 72 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: **Nine of the 26 OCW lecture-note PDFs, merged into one file, each verified individually before merging: Lectures 1–6 and 12–14 — i.e., Chapter I (Thermodynamics) complete, Chapter II (Probability) complete, and Chapter IV (Classical Statistical Mechanics: microcanonical/canonical/grand-canonical ensembles, partition functions) complete.** Lectures 7–11 (Kinetic Theory), 15–19 (Interacting Particles), 20–21 (Quantum Statistical Mechanics), and 22–26 (Ideal Quantum Gases) are NOT in hand — see gap list. Graduate level, terse and mathematical, the notes the *Statistical Physics of Particles* textbook was built from.
- TRANSFER: Lectures 5–6 are a self-contained probability course inside the physics course — cumulants and cumulant-generating functions, joint Gaussians and Wick's theorem, the CLT with its exact conditions, and the saddle-point method. That is diagnostic machinery for the calibration program: real margin distributions are non-Gaussian, and Kardar's cumulant apparatus measures *how* (skew, kurtosis) instead of assuming it away; the saddle-point method is the Laplace approximation used when engine integrals have no closed form. The ensemble chapters give the partition-function/softmax structure behind Boltzmann-weighted model combination in the engine.

## 2. Information theory and inference

**MacKay, D. J. C. (2003).** *Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms.* Cambridge University Press (author-posted full book, on-screen-viewing terms as printed on every page).
- Source: https://www.inference.org.uk/itprnn/book.pdf (also served identically at https://www.inference.org.uk/mackay/itprnn/book.pdf — byte-identical, md5-verified) · File: `MacKay-2003-InformationTheoryInferenceLearningAlgorithms.pdf` · 11.13 MB · 640 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: The complete book: Shannon theory and source/channel coding, then the inference half — probability and entropy as inference, Bayesian model comparison and Occam factors, Monte Carlo methods, message passing, variational approximations, neural networks, and decision theory, with 400+ exercises. Senior-undergraduate-to-graduate level; the single most readable rigorous treatment of "probability as extended logic" in the library.
- TRANSFER: This is the calibration program's theory text. MacKay's identity between code length and log-probability makes log-loss a *coding* quantity — the engine's forecasts can be graded as compression of game outcomes, and a model that doesn't compress doesn't know anything. His Bayesian model-comparison chapters (evidence, Occam factors) are the principled version of the engine's model-selection problem, and the Monte Carlo / message-passing toolbox is the inference machinery behind posterior player and team ratings.

## 3. Stochastic thermodynamics and fluctuation theorems

**Seifert, U. (2012).** "Stochastic Thermodynamics, Fluctuation Theorems, and Molecular Machines." *Reports on Progress in Physics*, 75, 126001. (arXiv version, complete.)
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.4176 · File: `Seifert-2012-StochasticThermodynamics.pdf` · 0.93 MB · 105 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: The field's defining review, in full: trajectory-level first and second laws (work, heat, and entropy defined along a single fluctuating trajectory, not just on average), one master fluctuation theorem from which the integral, detailed, Jarzynski, and Crooks theorems are all derived, nonequilibrium steady states with a generalized fluctuation–dissipation theorem, and efficiency/efficiency-at-maximum-power for molecular machines via cycle decomposition of entropy production. Research level, self-contained given statistical mechanics.
- TRANSFER: The core move — assigning entropy production to *individual trajectories* of a driven, non-stationary system — is exactly the shape of a game: a single driven trajectory through state space, never in equilibrium. Seifert's framework turns "how irreversible was that game script" into a computable quantity (log-ratio of forward to time-reversed path probabilities), which is a candidate non-stationarity/drift diagnostic for the forecasting engine: a team whose trajectory statistics are strongly irreversible is being driven (schedule, workload, tanking/rest incentives), not fluctuating. The cycle-decomposition of entropy production also maps onto workload-hazard work: a player's week is a driven cycle, and dissipation concentrates where the cycle is forced hardest.

**Jarzynski, C. (2011).** "Equalities and Inequalities: Irreversibility and the Second Law of Thermodynamics at the Nanoscale." *Annual Review of Condensed Matter Physics*, 2, 329–351. (Author-posted copy, University of Maryland.)
- Source: https://terpconnect.umd.edu/~cjarzyns/CHEM-CHPH-PHYS_703_Spr_20/resources/Jarzynski_AnnuRevCondMattPhys_2_329_2011.pdf · File: `Jarzynski-2011-EqualitiesInequalitiesSecondLaw.pdf` · 0.60 MB · 23 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: Jarzynski's own synthesis of the nonequilibrium work relations: how the second law's inequality becomes exact equalities (⟨e^(−βW)⟩ = e^(−βΔF)) once fluctuations are accounted for, the Crooks relation, experimental verifications on single molecules, and the statistical reason macroscopic irreversibility looks absolute. Shorter and more conceptual than Seifert — the right first read of the pair. (The 1997 PRL primary paper itself is paywalled at APS; this review is the author-sanctioned substitute — see gap list.)
- TRANSFER: The Jarzynski equality is an importance-reweighting identity: an *equilibrium* quantity (ΔF) recovered exactly from samples taken under a *driven, nonequilibrium* protocol, by exponential reweighting. That is the formal template for a recurring GSE problem — estimating a rested/baseline performance level from observations made under fatigued, driven conditions (back-to-backs, short weeks): reweight the driven samples correctly and the baseline is recoverable in principle, and Jensen's inequality applied to the same identity tells you which direction the naive average is biased. It also disciplines the calibration program's expectations: exponential averages are dominated by rare trajectories, so the estimator's variance problem is named, not discovered later.

**Crooks, G. E. (1999).** "Entropy Production Fluctuation Theorem and the Nonequilibrium Work Relation for Free Energy Differences." *Physical Review E*, 60, 2721–2726. (Author-posted copy, threeplusone.com — Crooks's own publication archive.)
- Source: https://threeplusone.com/pubs/Crooks1999a.pdf · File: `Crooks-1999-EntropyProductionFluctuationTheorem.pdf` · 0.08 MB · 6 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: The primary paper for the Crooks fluctuation theorem: for microscopically reversible stochastic dynamics, the probability of a forward trajectory over its time-reversed counterpart is exactly exp(entropy production), P_F(ω)/P_R(−ω) = e^ω; the paper derives the generalized theorem and gets the Jarzynski work relation from it as a corollary. ## 4. Stochastic processes — the working text

## 5. Nonequilibrium / large deviations — the optional survey, landed

- WHAT IT IS: The primary paper that introduced network motifs — small interaction patterns (feed-forward loop, bi-fan, bi-parallel) that recur in real transcription, neuronal, and food-web networks at rates far above randomized networks with the same degree sequence, implying they are selected building blocks, not accidents.
- TRANSFER: The motif method is a ready-made detector for the GSE engine: enumerate small recurring subgraphs in passing/play networks and lineup-interaction networks, score them against degree-preserving randomized slates, and only the over-represented patterns earn a place as named, reusable features — the same logic also catalogs recurring interaction motifs inside the agent fleet's message traffic.

**Alon, U. (2007).** "Network Motifs: Theory and Experimental Approaches." *Nature Reviews Genetics*, 8, 450–461.
- Source: university course host, Sapienza University of Rome (biofold.org) — https://biofold.org/pages/courses/pibn/docs/alon2007.pdf · File: `Alon-2007-NetworkMotifsTheoryExperimental.pdf` · 0.44 MB · 12 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: Alon's definitive review of what each motif *does* dynamically, with experimental evidence: negative autoregulation speeds and stabilizes responses, the feed-forward loop works as a sign-sensitive delay and persistence detector that filters out transient input pulses, and single-input modules generate temporal programs.
- TRANSFER: This is a signal-filtering design library for the GSE engine: the feed-forward loop's persistence-detection function is the biological template for requiring a signal (a player's usage shift, a scheme change) to persist before the engine acts on it, instead of reacting to one-game pulses — and the same circuit logic maps onto fleet design, where an agent's output should need corroboration through a second path before it triggers action.

**Kitano, H. (2002).** "Systems Biology: A Brief Overview." *Science*, 295, 1662–1664.
- Source: university course host, Duke University — https://courses.cs.duke.edu/spring13/cps262/pdf/kitano.2002.pdf · File: `Kitano-2002-SystemsBiologyBriefOverview.pdf` · 0.20 MB · 4 pp. (citation page + article pp. 1662–1664.)
- WHAT IT IS: The founding manifesto of systems biology: understanding a system means modeling structure *and* dynamics together, robustness emerges as a central design property of complex systems, and progress requires standard, exchangeable model formats (the SBML/SBW effort) plus a tight experiment–model cycle.
- TRANSFER: Kitano's robustness-as-design-property framing is the systems-biology warrant for how Garrett's engine and fleet should be judged — not by best-case output but by graceful behavior under perturbation (missing data, a dead agent, a shifted distribution) — and his standards argument transfers directly to the fleet's shared formats: agents that exchange models and verdicts in one standard schema, the way SBML let biology tools interoperate.

## 3. Decision science

**Kahneman, D., & Tversky, A. (1979).** "Prospect Theory: An Analysis of Decision under Risk." *Econometrica*, 47(2), 263–292.
- Source: MIT course host (web.mit.edu) — https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf · File: `KahnemanTversky-1979-ProspectTheory.pdf` · 0.54 MB · 29 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: The primary text of prospect theory: experimental demonstrations that people violate expected-utility axioms in predictable ways (certainty effect, reflection effect, isolation effect), and the replacement model — reference-dependent value that is steeper for losses than gains, plus nonlinear decision weights on probabilities.
- TRANSFER: This is the descriptive model of the humans on both sides of Garrett's calibration & human-judgment program: it predicts how bettors (and Garrett's own reviewers) will overweight small probabilities in parlay-style products, frame the same pick as a loss or a gain depending on the reference point, and chase after losses — so published reasoning and package framing can be designed against those distortions instead of discovering them in the results.

**U.S. Government (2009).** *A Tradecraft Primer: Structured Analytic Techniques for Improving Intelligence Analysis.* CIA Center for the Study of Intelligence.
- Source: official CIA publication, free at cia.gov — https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Tradecraft-Primer-apr09.pdf · File: `USGovernment-2009-TradecraftPrimerStructuredAnalyticTechniques.pdf` · 0.77 MB · 45 pp.
- WHAT IT IS: The intelligence community's official field manual of structured analytic techniques — Analysis of Competing Hypotheses, Key Assumptions Check, Devil's Advocacy, Red Teaming, Pre-Mortem, Indicators/Signposts, and more — each with when to use it, how to run it, and the specific cognitive trap it counters. It is the applied companion to the heuristics-and-biases literature (its framework descends from Heuer's *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*).
- TRANSFER: This is a ready-made operating protocol for the fleet and the calibration program: ACH becomes the required format when agents disagree on a pick or a model verdict (list every hypothesis, score evidence by diagnosticity, try to *disprove* rather than confirm), and the Key Assumptions Check becomes a standing pre-publication step for engine reasoning — name the load-bearing assumptions, then check what breaks if each one is wrong.
