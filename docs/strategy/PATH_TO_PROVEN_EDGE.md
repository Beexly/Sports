# Path to Proven Edge

> Replaces the "path to 70%" framing. The target was wrong, not the ambition.
> This is the research program that points seven months of work at a summit that
> actually exists — and a more defensible one than any win-rate headline.

## The core correction

A sustained **70% win rate against the spread does not exist** — not for us, not for
anyone, no matter how much we research. The closing line is the most accurate public
prediction on Earth for a game, because it is the *aggregated* output of every sharp
bettor, syndicate, model, and the books' own quants, all pushing money until the price
balances at ~50/50 by design. Out-researching a standard NFL spread means out-researching
the entire planet's betting intelligence *already priced into the number.* The best
professional operations alive live at **53–55% ATS**. 57% sustained is legendary. 70% is
a mirage, and chasing it burns the very months we could spend winning.

**Win rate is a tout's metric** — it sounds impressive and means almost nothing. We do not
chase it.

## The real north star: Closing Line Value (CLV)

The metric the smartest operations chase — and the one the **books themselves** use to
decide who to limit — is CLV: did we get a better number than the line closed at? Beat the
close consistently and we are, provably, ahead of the market; the wins follow whether or
not any single bet hits. CLV is:

- **Measurable now** — we already have `/clv`, the bet tracker, and closing-line capture bones.
- **Leading, not lagging** — it tells us we have edge *before* a 500-bet sample confirms it.
- **Unfakeable** — a tout can cherry-pick a win streak; nobody can fake a sustained CLV beat.

ROI / expected value is the bottom line; CLV is the leading indicator that predicts it.

## Where edge actually lives

Not in the mainstream NFL spread — that market is dead-efficient. Real, capturable edge
concentrates where the world's money *isn't* watching closely:

1. **Smaller / less-covered markets** — lower-tier college, niche leagues, secondary sports.
   Softer lines, fewer sharps, slower corrections.
2. **Player props before they sharpen** — props post soft and move fast; speed wins.
3. **Live / in-game spots** — models that price faster than the book reprices.
4. **Slow-moving / stale lines** — capturing a number before it steams.
5. **Specific, narrow model edges** — a factor the market underweights, proven out-of-sample.

The engine's job is to **hunt these gaps**, measured by CLV and EV — not to "pick more winners"
on markets where winning more is structurally impossible.

## The honest "70%" that IS real — keep it

We can build an engine that says *"on this specific pick, we are truly, calibratedly 70%
confident"* and be right 70% of the time on those picks. That is honest and achievable. The
catch: high-confidence spots are usually priced fairly, so 70% confidence ≠ free money. The
edge is the rare spot where our honest 70% meets a market price implying 65%. **That gap is
the whole game.** Calibrated high-confidence picks are a *feature of edge*, never a win-rate
headline.

## The research program (replaces "path to 70")

**Phase 1 — Make CLV the engine's primary metric.**
Wire CLV capture + reporting as the north-star scoreboard (build on `/clv`, the tracker,
closing-line snapshots). Every pick gets a CLV record at close. This is the instrument; we
can't improve what we don't measure.

**Phase 2 — Map the inefficiency.**
Systematically score which markets / bet-types / time-windows show real CLV opportunity vs.
which are dead-efficient. Output: a ranked "where we have edge" map. This is months of
*productive* research with a concrete deliverable.

**Phase 3 — Tune the engine to hunt the gaps.**
Point selection and modeling at the high-CLV markets the map surfaces. Measure by CLV/EV
out-of-sample, never by in-sample win rate. Calibration (already validated, ECE 0.198→0.044)
keeps the confidence honest throughout.

**Phase 4 — Prove it in the open.**
Publish the CLV beat rate and the calibrated record as it accrues. The pricing ladder already
encodes this: **PROVEN** (≥100 settled + published calibration) → **ESTABLISHED** (≥500 settled
+ verified CLV ≥52.4%) → **AUTHORITY** (multi-season ROI). The milestones are the proof, and
the proof is the moat.

First-of-its-kind, defensible, *true*, and unfakeable. That is the most intelligent engine of
