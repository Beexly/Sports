# Organic Launch Playbook — zero ad spend

> The owner is cash-constrained, so the launch is **organic / near-zero ad spend**. The
> product's honesty IS the distribution: a tool on real, cleared data + public ADP receipts
> is the shareable unit. No external auto-sends — `draft-only.mjs` blocks them; everything
> below is human-posted (or owner-approved drafts from the existing `studio.ts` generator).

## Channels (ranked, fantasy-specific)
1. **X / "#FFTwitter"** — the real-time hub of fantasy; news breaks here first. The #1 channel
   for a new fantasy brand. Post daily ADP-value/sleeper takes; engage writers + mid-size
   creators.
2. **Reddit — r/fantasyfootball + r/BestBall** — dense, intent-rich, but self-promo is gated
   (the 90/10 rule; per-subreddit rules in the sidebar). **Lead with value, never bare links:**
   answer questions, share free tool screenshots, contribute original analysis to weekly threads.
3. **YouTube Shorts + TikTok** — repurpose mock-draft clips + tool demos (screen capture).
4. **Newsletter (owned audience)** — start capturing emails immediately (the Matthew-Berry /
   Fantasy Life playbook: a newsletter that compounded into a media company). NOTE: sending is
   blocked by the `draft-only` guardrail today — **capture now, draft issues now, wire a sender
   later** (deliberately, behind owner approval).
5. **Discord** — community retention hub; bundle access as a perk later.

## Content formats that win during draft season
Rankings · **sleepers/breakouts at ADP** (the recurring "bargains by ADP" format) · tiers ·
**mock drafts** · **tool demos** · and **"receipts"** — public, timestamped ADP calls that
later hit (this doubles as the platform's calibration/track-record ethos).

## Anchor beats beyond day 30
**Aug 6 (HOF Game)** and **Aug 13 (preseason Wk1)** = draft-urgency spikes — schedule the
Founding push + a creator cross-promo burst into those dates. **Sept 9 kickoff** = convert the
remaining free audience + flip the in-season suite live.

## The honest wedge (positioning)
Most fantasy content sells certainty; we sell **decision quality on real data + receipts**.
Every claim is checkable, every projection will publish its own calibration, and we say plainly
what's preview. That restraint is the brand.
