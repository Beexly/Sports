# Scores24 Leverage → GSE Revenue

> Rights posture: this plans GSE's **original** revenue path, informed by competitive analysis of a
> publicly observable business model. No Scores24 content/data is used or derived. See
> `docs/competitive/SCORES24_BUSINESS_MACHINE_TEARDOWN.md`.

## The reframe

Scores24 proves the demand: millions of people want a per-match page with predictions, trends, odds,
and tools. It monetizes that demand through a sportsbook-bonus affiliate funnel. GSE serves the same
demand but converts on **trust**, not on **fake certainty** — and trust compounds where a funnel
churns. The leverage is to take the machine's *traffic shape* and route it to an honest product the
machine cannot build.

## Where the demand becomes revenue (named, proof-gated)

The pricing ladder is the single source of truth in `apps/web/lib/pricing/pricing-phases.ts`:
Free → Pro → Elite, with founding rates live and every step-up gated by a verified milestone
(FOUNDING → PROVEN → ESTABLISHED → AUTHORITY). Founding members are grandfathered for life.

| Lever (from the teardown) | Honest GSE conversion | Gate before it earns |
|---|---|---|
| SEO route factory → organic traffic | genome-generated, indexable match pages (real data) | rights + live-data path green; fixtures stay `noindex` |
| Prediction "tips" demand | Pro tier: all picks + confidence + factor trail | public-performance gate stays closed until settled, calibrated history |
| Trends hooks | Pro: trend passports with fragility surfaced | no `lock`/`guarantee` copy; trend caps at WATCH |
| Accumulator interest | Slip MRI as a *risk* tool | never a parlay push; strongest verdict "proceed with caution" |
| Bonus-affiliate funnel | compliance-gated bonus integrity layer | owner-configured links only; no "current"/"legal"/"best" without verification; GSE never operates betting |
| Retention notifications | Elite: real-time honest alerts | every alert reason + proof, no bet-now pressure |

## Why trust out-earns the funnel over time

A bonus funnel's value per user decays — once a user has claimed the sign-up offers, the affiliate
revenue is largely spent. A proof institution's value per user *compounds*: the calibration track
record, the autopsy history, and the published CLV become the reason to stay and to pay. The moat is
the receipt trail, which a fake-certainty competitor cannot copy without dismantling its funnel.

## The founding-revenue path (this build's role)

This build ships the *proof* that the honest product exists and is rigorous — the Event Genome slice,
the Prediction Court, the Trend Passports, the Bonus Integrity layer, the Slip MRI, and the Odds-credit
accountant that keeps the data cost honest. None of it opens a revenue gate by itself; each is a brick
in the credibility wall that the named pricing ladder is gated on.

## Hard constraints on monetization

- No public-performance claim until settled + calibrated live history exists.
- No affiliate link unless the owner configures it; no "current"/"legal"/"best" bonus claim without
  verification and stated criteria.
- No `priced=true` flip, no `canPublishProjections`, no gate weakening in this build.
- Revenue follows proof, never the reverse.
