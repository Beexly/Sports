# Galaxy Community Moderation Policy — v1

> Status: **ADOPTED as written policy** (the Stage-2 written-policy gate).
> Live rooms still require the other two gates before launch: moderation
> **tooling** built and tested, and a **privacy review** of profiles/presence.
> Doctrine source: `docs/design/NFL_HOUSE_DOCTRINE.md` (Stage 2).
> The culture line governs every call: **"We do not force action. We protect
> decision quality."**

## Scope

Applies to all user-generated content surfaces: the Stage-2 live rooms
(The Sunday Couch, Brotherhood Table, No-Shame Room), user-submitted parlay
analysis, comments, display names, avatars, and any future UGC surface.
It binds users, moderators, and the platform itself (automated systems
included).

## The three protections (from the founder directive)

1. **No harassment.** Zero tolerance for abuse, slurs, dogpiling, doxxing,
   or targeting a member for their record, bankroll, or skill level.
2. **Protect beginners.** "Learning football" is a first-class identity.
   Mocking a beginner question is a moderation event, full stop.
3. **No shame for sitting out.** Passing on a slate is a respected decision.
   Pressuring anyone to bet — directly or by ridicule — is a violation.

## Hard rules (removal + escalation)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| No harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing | Protection #1 |
| No tout behavior: selling picks, DMs hawking "locks", outside paid groups | The desk voice is the house voice; touts poison trust |
| No "guaranteed winner" / "can't-lose" language | Same standard the platform's own copy is held to (analyst voice law) |
| No pressuring others to bet, mocking sitting out, or loss-shaming | Protections #2 and #3 |
| No chasing-losses encouragement ("double up", "make it back tonight") | Responsible-play law: behavior signals trigger nudges, never upsells |
| No underage participation; no circumventing self-exclusion | Legal + responsible-play floor |
| No sharing pirated streams, account credentials, or scraped paid content | Mirrors the platform's own rights posture |
| No personal data of others (real names, addresses, screenshots with PII) | Privacy review prerequisite extends to user behavior |
| No spam, referral-code flooding, or affiliate drops | Rooms are belonging surfaces, not funnels |

## Moderation actions (graduated, logged)

1. **Nudge** — automated or moderator note, no penalty (first soft violation).
2. **Remove** — content taken down with a reason shown to the author.
3. **Mute** — temporary room-level silence (24h / 7d ladder).
4. **Suspend** — account-level, time-boxed, with appeal.
5. **Ban** — permanent; reserved for hate, threats, doxxing, repeat touting,
   or self-exclusion circumvention.

Every action is logged with actor, reason, and content reference.
**Appeals**: any suspend/ban can be appealed once; a different reviewer than
the original actor decides. Decision within 7 days.

## Responsible-play integration (standing law restated)

- Distress signals in chat (chasing language, panic, "rent money") trigger
  **support nudges and cool-down resources — never offers, never upsells**.
- The platform never uses room activity to market higher tiers.
- Self-excluded users are excluded from rooms too — betting-adjacent spaces
  count.

## What the platform itself is held to

- House content in rooms speaks in the desk voice: no hype, no "locks",
  uncertainty stated plainly.
- Picks discussed in rooms carry the same calibration/freshness laws as
  pick surfaces. No member-only "secret" picks that dodge the public
  track record.
- Moderator identity is always disclosed; no pseudonymous staff posting.

## Launch checklist (Stage-2 gates)

- [x] Written moderation policy (this document)
- [ ] Moderation tooling: report button, action ladder, audit log, appeal
      flow — built and tested
- [ ] Privacy review: profiles, presence indicators, message retention
      schedule, data-deletion path
- [ ] Responsible-play signal wiring (nudges live before rooms do)
- [ ] Moderator coverage plan for live windows (Sunday slate minimum)

Rooms do not open until every box is checked. Owner sign-off required.
