# Galaxy Sports Edge — Legal / IP Binder

This directory is the early-stage intellectual-property binder for Galaxy
Sports Edge. The goal is not paperwork. The goal is **asset defensibility**.

When an investor, acquirer, or attorney does diligence on Galaxy, they will
ask the same questions every time:

- Who owns the code?
- Who owns the content?
- Who owns the brand?
- Who has access to the repo?
- Are the algorithms actually secret?
- Were contractors under IP assignment?
- Were any secrets leaked to public tools, screenshots, repos, or chats?
- Can a competitor reverse-engineer the methodology from the frontend?
- Are the trademarks filed?
- Are there invention records?
- Are the data rights clean?
- Are the security controls real or "trust me"?

These documents exist so that every one of those questions has a paper trail.

## Operating principle

Galaxy is a **trade-secret-first** company.

The public can know that Parlay MRI, the No-Bet Engine, Market Mirage, Roster
Shock, the Coaching Edge Model, Post-Bet Autopsy, and the Personal Briefing
exist. The public must not know **how** they work — what weights, thresholds,
prompts, formulas, decision trees, calibration rules, or data transformations
sit behind each one.

The public site sells **what Galaxy sees**.
The private backend protects **how Galaxy sees**.
