CRITICAL STATISTICAL CORRECTION:
Use the SIDE-ADAPTIVE ASYMMETRIC FRACTIONAL E-PROCESS for the composite null \(p_t \le m_t\). The symmetric likelihood ratio is invalid for that null.

For each game, select ONE side predictably before the game:

- If \(q_t > m_t\), bet the OVER.
- If \(q_t \le m_t\), bet the UNDER.

Let \(Y_t\) be the indicator for the CHOSEN side.

For an OVER bet:
\(m_t^{O} =\) Shin-de-vigged no-vig over probability at entry.
\(q_t^{O} =\) model over probability.
\(Y_t = 1\) if the game total goes over the line, else 0.

For an UNDER bet:
\(m_t^{U} = 1 - m_t^{O}\).
\(q_t^{U} = 1 - q_t^{O}\).
\(Y_t = 1\) if the game total goes under the line, else 0.

The side-adaptive asymmetric fractional increment is:

If OVER:
\[
E_t = 1 + 0.3\left( Y_t \frac{q_t^{O}}{m_t^{O}} + (1-Y_t)(1-q_t^{O}) - 1 \right)
\]

If UNDER:
\[
E_t = 1 + 0.3\left( Y_t \frac{q_t^{U}}{m_t^{U}} + (1-Y_t)(1-q_t^{U}) - 1 \right)
\]

This is a valid nonnegative supermartingale under the composite null for each side.

=== JAMES-STEIN SHRINKAGE MODEL SPEC ===

The hierarchical outcome model must use James-Stein shrinkage. Hermes implements this spec exactly.

1. **Outcome target:** total runs in each MLB game.

2. **Model form:** hierarchical Poisson regression on total runs, or separately on home/away runs.

3. **Feature inputs, all pre-game only:**
   - Team rolling offensive and defensive metrics, last 30 days.
   - Starting pitcher FIP/xFIP and recent form.
   - Bullpen recent usage: pitches thrown in prior 3 days.
   - Park factor.
   - Weather forecast: temperature, wind, humidity.
   - Umpire plate zone history.
   - Rest days, travel distance, home/away.

4. **Shrinkage construction:**
   - Convert team and pitcher metrics to approximately normal scale. For binary or proportion outcomes, use the arc-sine transformation:
     \[
     y = \arcsin(\sqrt{p}), \qquad \text{Var}(y) \approx \frac{1}{4n}
     \]
     where \(p\) is the observed proportion and \(n\) the sample size.
   - Apply the positive-part James-Stein estimator for unequal sample sizes:
     \[
     \hat{\theta}_i = \bar{\theta} + \max\left(0, 1 - \frac{(p-2)\sigma^2}{\sum_{j=1}^p (\theta_j - \bar{\theta})^2}\right)(\theta_i - \bar{\theta})
     \]
     where \(\bar{\theta}\) is the weighted grand mean, \(p\) is the number of parameters, and \(\sigma^2\) is the pooled variance estimated from the data.
   - Use the multiple sample size variant: shrink more aggressively for parameters with small sample sizes.
   - Back-transform arc-sine values to probabilities:
     \[
     p = \sin(\hat{\theta})^2
     \]

5. **Online update:** Refit the model after every game using only past data. The shrinkage parameters must be recomputed from the expanding history only.

6. **Over probability:** From the posterior predictive distribution of total runs, compute:
   \[
   q_t^{O} = P(\text{total runs} > L \mid \text{shrunk model})
   \]
   where \(L\) is the over/under line at entry.
