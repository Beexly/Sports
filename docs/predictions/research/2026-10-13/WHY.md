# Why it looks like this

**Five-minute read for engineering and the founder.**

## The problem with what we had

NEBULA v7 (teal-black ground, violet cards, ember action) was a real improvement over cyan-magenta cosmic-neon. It still read as *brand costume*: cosmic names, multi-accent atmosphere, cinematic entrance, dense marketing sections. The product promise is an instrument that does not lie. The design still dressed like a show.

## The idea

**OBSERVATORY.** One idea: *measured signal, sealed record.*

- **Signal** (cadmium ember `#E85D3C`) — action, edge rank, primary CTA. One job.
- **Seal** (brass `#C9A46C`) — receipts, verification, the public record. The other job.

Everything else is ground, bone, hairlines, and semantic color for win/loss/void only.

## What we deliberately removed

- Multi-neon / cyan / plasma magenta as brand
- Cinematic cold-open as the default front door
- Feature-card forests and slogan stacks
- Charts without n
- Locked picks that blur a fake number

## What we kept from product truth

- "We're not AI. We're math you can read."
- "We detect. You decide."
- Holds and no-edges as first-class public decisions
- Calibration gate honesty (publish gap to the floor)
- Responsible-play footer and 1-800-GAMBLER
- Bone and ember continuity so the brand does not feel alien overnight

## Type as architecture

Space Grotesk for structure. Source Serif 4 for editorial trust. IBM Plex Mono for odds, hashes, labels. Serif body is intentional: methodology and record need to feel like something you can read for ten minutes, not a dashboard.

## Signature

The **Spine** — a measurement rail with ticks for edge / sealed / floor. It encodes a real constraint (calibration floor), not decoration. Blueyard gets a fluid orb; we get a scale.

## Age 14–80

Readable body, 44px targets, five destinations, bottom tab bar, no terminal-only density. Terminal direction was studied and rejected as the primary language (kept as board *density* on paper tables, not as the brand voice).
