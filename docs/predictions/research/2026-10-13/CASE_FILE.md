### The absence of written discipline

Every criticism I received at Turner came verbally, by phone. I received no written warning, no performance improvement plan, and no documented performance criticism of any kind. Nothing located in this collection contradicts that: across my Google Drive, a Gmail archive of 189,064 messages, my iCloud library, and seven Turner Drive archives, no written warning, corrective action notice, or performance record originating from Turner was found.

Three facts sit alongside that absence. I was the site's HR Manager, so administering documented discipline fell inside my own function. Turner maintains written policy on conduct, harassment, and retaliation, produced at EX-022. And the termination itself came in a formal letter over the signature of the executive said to have delivered the warning verbally. [EX-001] Whether any contemporaneous record of the warning exists is a question for Turner's files. Note 3, item 9.
