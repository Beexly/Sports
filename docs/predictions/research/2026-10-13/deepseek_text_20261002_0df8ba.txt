===============================================================================
PART ONE — THE VISION
===============================================================================

GSE is a REASONING ENGINE. An expert system. Not a model. Not a
classifier. A reasoner.

It ingests everything knowable about a game — lineup discrepancies,
pace, in-field and out-of-field conditions, cognitive state, nutrition,
travel, rest, weather, officials, coaching tendencies, narrative,
historical settlement, market pricing, calibration outputs, Brier,
ECE, Kelly — and REASONS THROUGH IT to produce a pick.

Signals are FACTS. The reasoning layer is an INFERENCE ENGINE. The
reasoning trace is the PRODUCT. Brier, ECE, Kelly, calibration are
FACTS in working memory — inputs, not objectives. There is no loss
function. There is an expert system reasoning over everything it
knows.

The edge is coverage. Ingest more. Weight better. Reason more
completely. The engine does not beat the book by minimizing a scalar.
It beats the book by being smarter about everything than the book is.

A reasoning engine that cannot show its reasoning is not reasoning.
We are not fitting.

PART FOUR — IRON LAWS
===============================================================================

Each law ends with HOW.

LAW 1 — PRODUCERS FIRST.
No signal is real until a producer generates its facts.
HOW: For every signal, ask "what produces this fact?" Check three
places: the local repo, the HF Spaces, the HF buckets. If none
produce it, build the producer. Name it. Commit its output. Then
wire the signal.

LAW 2 — THE ENGINE IS AN EXPERT SYSTEM.
Brier, ECE, Kelly, calibration are facts, not objectives.
HOW: Put them in the reasoning trace as facts. Never report as
headline.

LAW 3 — FIXTURES ARE NOT DATA.
A green test on a fixture is not a verified result.
HOW: Every data file gets a manifest — source, producer, hash, row
count, date range, commit. No manifest → it is a fixture. Label it.
Unit tests may use fixtures. Integration tests may not.

LAW 4 — YOU CANNOT CALIBRATE AN UNFINISHED ENGINE.
HOW: Leave the seam unfitted. Move on.

LAW 5 — ORDER OF OPERATIONS IS FIXED.
Producers → wired → weighted → reasoning traces → THEN calibrate.
HOW: Return to the current stage. It is not complete until every
artifact at that stage exists.

LAW 6 — NOTHING SHIPS HALF-WIRED.
HOW: If a signal has two call sites and one is wired, wire the other
or remove the unwired one.

LAW 7 — EVERY SIGNAL GETS ALL SEVEN.
Producer. Wired. Weighted. Tested. Demonstrated. Backed by real data.
Honest about its gate.

LAW 8 — GUARDRAILS STAY STRICT.
HOW: Reword the change. Do not loosen the guardrail.

LAW 9 — NO FITTED MAPS. NO FABRICATED ROWS. NO SILENT FALLBACKS.
HOW: Raise `DataGapError` naming the artifact, producer, refit
command. Never 0.5. Never league mean. Never a synthetic row.

LAW 10 — THE DB CREDS ARE NOT A BLOCKER. THE DISK IS. AND SO IS THE
HF RUNTIME.
HOW: Free the disk. Check the HF runtime. Stop chasing creds.

LAW 11 — WORK GETS COMMITTED. FROM A CLEAN STATE.
HOW: Commit. Push. PR. Re-run suites from clean after edits settle.

LAW 12 — ALWAYS DIG ONE LAYER DEEPER.
HOW: Every gap hides another. CSVs, parquet, producer, disk, HF
runtime, bucket license. Find the next layer.

LAW 13 — STATE THE FOUR-TIER ARCHITECTURE.
HOW: Write `docs/architecture/FOUR_TIERS.md`. One paragraph per
tier. Where facts cross boundaries. Which platform owns which
artifact.

LAW 14 — RESPECT THE TIMESFM LICENSE.
HOW: TimesFM 3.0 is Non-Commercial. Any signal derived from TimesFM
output inherits NC. Document the constraint in the signal's manifest.
Do not silently route TimesFM output into a commercial path.
