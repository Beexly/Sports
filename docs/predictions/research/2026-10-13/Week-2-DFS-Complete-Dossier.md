## §0 — How to read this dossier

**Provenance tags (used everywhere):**
- `[2P]` — second-party salary (The Huddle "DFS Fantasy Domination: Week 2," published 2026-09-19; or DK Network Week 2 rankings/salaries, dated 2026-09-15). The DK endpoint was Akamai-blocked on 2026-09-19, so **no salary in this dossier is lobby-verified**. DK Network primetime (SNF/MNF/TNF-slate) salaries are second-party twice over — treat as placeholders.
- `pOWN` — projected ownership. Public OWS/DKN ownership figures cover the **13-game Sunday main slate**, not Garrett's 15-game contest. They are directionally useful, not directly applicable.
- `[FLAG]` — uncertain, disputed, stale, single-sourced, or internally contradictory. Every flag is explained in place.
- All Week 1 statistics are **one-game samples (n=1)**. Scheme splits on sub-10-attempt samples are directional only. This is repeated per section because it governs every number in the file.

**Percentile-flag conventions (they differ by source file — preserved as published):**
- The 101-player WR/TE pool file: `[ELITE]` = ≥90th percentile of the qualifying pool; `[WEAK]` = ≤25th percentile. Percentiles are *within the 101-player pool*, not league-wide.
- The advanced-metrics file: `[ELITE]` = above 85th percentile (published rank or implied top-15%, e.g. 1st–4th of 32); `[WEAK]` = below 15th. `†` = derived flag (usage profile implies the band; directional, not verified).

**Formulas used in this dossier:**
- *Role-vs-salary value score* (WR/TE pool): mean qualifying-pool percentile − salary percentile within the salaried pool. OUT/Doubtful players excluded.
- *Optimizer floor/ceiling*: floor = 0.55 × projection; ceiling = 1.6 × projection (mechanical, not model-derived).
- *xFP* = expected fantasy points (Sharp Football Analysis xFP tool).
- *TPRR* = targets per route run. *Route share* = % of dropbacks with a route run. *aDOT* = average depth of target. *YPRR* = yards per route run. *Air-yard share* = share of team air yards.
- *PROE+* = Pass Rate Over Expectation × Pace (StatRankings proprietary). *ARBY* = Adjusted Run Blocking Yards/Carry. *CoverageIQ* = man/zone + 8-shell coverage metric (StatRankings proprietary). *PROE* (SumerSports) = pressure rate over expected.
- *DK fantasy points* (standard): the scoring assumed throughout is the standard DK slate scoring used by every cited source. (The methodology file also contains non-NFL — e.g. NBA — definitions; those are non-actionable and were not reproduced.)

**Team-assignment conflicts:** the corpus contains 2026 roster assignments that contradict stale aggregators (Likely = NY Giant; Pittman = Steeler; Okonkwo = Washington; Gibson = Cleveland; Walker = Chief; Waddle = Bronco; Deebo = 49er; Kirk = 49er on IR; Evans = 49er per the pool file, **uncorroborated**). Each is flagged in place. Where two corpus files disagree on a player's team, both are preserved and the conflict is named — nothing is silently resolved.

**The non-invention rule:** no salary, ownership, projection, injury, role, team, or status in this dossier is invented. Where the corpus doesn't surface a number it says so ("not publicly surfaced from allowed sources" / "not surfaced in extraction"). Gaps are listed in §10–§11.
