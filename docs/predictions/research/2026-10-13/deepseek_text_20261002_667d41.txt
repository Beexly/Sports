===============================================================================

discrepancies, pace, weather, officials, coaching tendencies, cognitive
state, market pricing, calibration outputs, Brier, ECE, Kelly — and
reasons through all of it to produce a pick.

Kelly, calibration are facts in working memory — inputs, not objectives.
There is no loss function. Coverage is the edge.

A reasoning engine that cannot show its reasoning is not reasoning.
