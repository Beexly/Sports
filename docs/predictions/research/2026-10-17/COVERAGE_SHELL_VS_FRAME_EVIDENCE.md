# Coverage shell vs visual evidence — 2026 pre-Week-6

**Scope:** NFL 2026 regular season, Weeks 1–5 only (Week 6+ fenced). Real data only.
**Date:** 2026-10-17
**Terms searched:** coverage, Cover 0/1/2/3/4/6/9, Cover 2, Cover 3, Cover 4, press, zone, man coverage,
man/zone, charting label, shell label, defender alignment, coverage_shell, coverage_type, defense_coverage_type,
defense_man_zone_type, primary_coverage_shell, shell_label, charting_label.

## Verdict (short)

- **0 plays** in 2026 Weeks 1–5 have **both** a charting coverage label and a vision coverage label. The
  agreement test the task asks for cannot be run on this corpus — the charting side does not exist for 2026.
- **3 unique vision coverage labels** exist in the repository, all emitted by a verification harness with
  **hardcoded** shell strings, `weight = 0.0`, `validation_spec_met = false`.
- **1 of the 3** is attributable to a real play with frames. The frames **contradict** the assigned shell
  (`Cover 0 Blitz`): two safeties are 10–12 yd deep at the snap and 20–25 yd deep post-snap; zero frames show
  a zero-deep-safety shell.
- **2 of the 3** reference game ids that exist in **no** 2026 dataset (and fall on Tuesdays with no NFL games),
  so no frames and no charting row can be attached to them.
- A separate artifact claiming a calibrated coverage-shell distribution (`TARGET_2_MET`) is generated by
  `np.random.multinomial` from fixed priors and then chi-squared against those same priors — circular, not evidence.

## Findings

**F1 — Charting coverage is a 2026 input gap, not a labelling gap.**
Every 2026 participation/pbp/charting table was checked at the schema level. The FTN-sourced 2026 file has
pressure charting (box count, pass rushers, blitzers) for 13,438 plays but no coverage classification. This is
consistent with the loader note that 2026 participation is `RECONSTRUCTED_2026_FTN_PBP_SNAPS` (13,055 rows) plus
`MEASURED_NGS_HIGHLIGHT_PARTICIPATION_2026` (787 rows), neither of which carries coverage.

**F2 — The only vision coverage labels are hardcoded constants in a verification script.**
`scripts/verify_and_calibrate_cv_pipeline.py` lines 347/372/397 define
`"coverage_shell": "Cover 0 Blitz" / "Cover 2 Zone" / "Two-High Disguise -> Cover 3 Buzz"` inside a literal
`plays_metrics` list, alongside fabricated pocket depth (4.15 / 7.65 / 6.10) and strain values. The same values
are written to `intelligence/vision/shadow/vision_shadow.jsonl` (6 records) and to the two proof JSONs.
No coverage classifier exists in the pipeline (`cv_shadow_pipeline.py`, `cv_detector_adapter.py`,
`tracklet_adapter.py` contain no shell logic). All records are `weight: 0.0`, `validation_spec_met: false`.

**F3 — Two of three vision play ids are unattributable.**
`2026092200_1820` and `2026092900_2740` do not exist in: the 79-game 2026 pbp, the 128-game 2026 NGS set, or any
other 2026 table. 2026 Weeks 1–5 ran 2026-09-09 → 2026-10-12; 2026-09-22 and 2026-09-29 are Tuesdays with no games.
No tracking rows exist for plays 1820 or 2740. Nothing can confirm or deny those two labels.

**F4 — The one attributable play is contradicted by its frames.**
`2026101200 / 4579` maps to `2026_05_BUF_LA` play 4579 (real pbp: `(8:48) (Shotgun) 17-J.Allen pass short middle
to 10-K.Shakir to LA 22 for 57 yards`). Tracking: `intelligence/vision/data/proof_play_2026101200_4579.parquet`,
303 frames @ 10 Hz, 6,791 rows, 11 defenders — an exact row-for-row match to
`ngs_highlight_tracking_2026.parquet` (6791/6791, max coordinate delta 0.0).

Defensive alignment (LOS x = 88.58, depth = yards downfield):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (pre-snap) | 14.3, 13.0 | 8.8 | 7.8, 10.2, 11.8 | 4 |
| 160 (pre-snap) | 12.4, 10.0 | 1.5 | 2.2, 3.3, 3.3 | 2 |
| 161 (ball appears) | 12.4, 9.9 | 1.4 | 2.2, 3.2, 3.3 | 1 |
| 181 | 12.5, 9.5 | 0.9 | 1.5, 1.6, 3.3 | 1 |
| 201 | 17.1, 6.8 | −2.9 | 3.2, 3.6, 5.0 | 1 |
| 241 | 25.0, 20.6 | 0.8 | 10.7, 17.4, 29.8 | 5 |

- Pre-snap alignment is unambiguously **two-high** (Kinchens 12.4/14.3 yd, Lake 9.9/13.0 yd), with Kam Curl (SS)
  walked into the box and three corners pressed at 2–3 yd.
- Across frames 161–260, **0 of 100 frames** show zero defenders ≥10 yd deep; 52 show ≥2.
- Six defenders cross the LOS by frame 201 (3 DT, 2 OLB, 1 SS) — the pressure element is real.
- Verdict: **"Blitz" supported; "Cover 0" not supported.** The frame evidence is consistent with a two-high
  man/2-man pressure look, not a zero-deep-safety shell. Tracking alone cannot resolve man-vs-zone assignments,
  but it can and does falsify "no deep safeties."

**F5 — The coverage-shell "calibration" is RNG, not measurement.**
`scripts/verify_and_calibrate_cv_pipeline.py:739-742`:
`shell_priors = [0.24, 0.34, 0.15, 0.13, 0.08, 0.06]`;
`np.random.multinomial(len(train_plays), shell_priors)` and the same for holdout; then
`stats.chisquare(holdout_counts, priors * n)`. The resulting `CV_CALIBRATION_WEEKS_1_TO_5.json`
(`TARGET_2_MET`, chi2 p = 0.541, train/holdout distributions) tests random draws against the priors that generated
them. The "five extreme plays" in the same file cite game ids `2026100500/01/02`, none of which exist.
This artifact must not be read as evidence that coverage shells were measured in 2026.

**F6 — Agreement testing is blocked, not merely absent.**
With 0 charting labels and 3 hardcoded vision labels, the joint set is empty:
`{plays with charting label} ∩ {plays with vision label} = ∅`, and even the vision-only set has frames for 1 play.

## Gaps logged

1. **2026 coverage charting absent** — 0 / 13,842 plays (W1–5) carry `defense_coverage_type` or
   `defense_man_zone_type`. Comparison against charting requires either an FTN/nflverse coverage pull for 2026 or
   an accepted proxy (pressure charting is present; shell is not).
2. **Vision shell classifier absent** — no code path computes a shell; the three labels are literals in a test script.
3. **Two vision play ids unresolvable** — `2026092200_1820`, `2026092900_2740`.
4. **No raw broadcast frames in repo** — tracking provenance is `broadcast_projected_from_ngs_10hz`; no play-level
   MP4/JPG exists for 2026. All frame evidence here is NGS-derived coordinates, not pixels.
5. **Calibration claim unsupported** — `CV_CALIBRATION_WEEKS_1_TO_5.json` target 2 is RNG; do not cite it as
   measured shell distribution.
6. **2026 roster provenance caveat** — `roster_2026.parquet` assigns players to teams inconsistent with their
   real-career status (e.g. Aaron Donald, retired after 2023, listed on LA; Trent McDuffie and Jaylen Watson on LA).
   Treat the 2026 corpus as simulated/regenerated when describing frame evidence.
7. **Out of scope but available** — 2025 charting coverage (48.8% of plays) would support a real charting-vs-vision
   agreement test if a 2025 vision shell label set were produced; none exists today either.
