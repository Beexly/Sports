# Nova Dossier — Information Architecture (2026-11-07)

## Layer Separation
- **Layer 1 (Market Foundation)**: Respects live consensus spread, total, and implied team totals as the starting baseline.
- **Layer 2 (Situational & Film Overlay)**: Overlays coaching tendencies, trench disruption probabilities, receiver YPRR vs Man/Zone splits, and second-half fatigue decay.
- **Voice & Presentation**: Translates situational factors into natural football-film analysis without exposing internal pipeline jargon.
