## ALTITUDE — the created metric, methodology VERIFIED from stats.mockingboard.app/glossary/altitude
- What: weekly score for QB/RB/WR/TE = a historical percentile 0–100 against every qualifying game at the position since 2016. Bands: Grounded <10, Turbulence <40, Level <60, Cruising <90, Soaring ≥90. 50 = median.
- QB formula: EPA per dropback with the least reliable outcomes priced out — interception plays re-priced at that season's mean incompletion value (a single game of INTs is ~1% signal); sack fumbles priced as clean sacks.
- WR/TE/RB formula: EARNED yards — targets priced at the league rate for their depth; carries at the league rate for their box count. "Opportunity earned is the player; weekly conversion is mostly context and luck, so conversion is priced, never observed."
- Ingredient selection: split-half method — split a player's snaps at random, compute candidate stats on each half, keep only stats that agree with themselves inside one game.
- Qualifying gates: QB 15+ dropbacks; WR/TE 10+ routes; RB 8+ touches; regular season only.
- Published data views: qb_altitude_week / rb_altitude_week / wr_altitude_week / te_altitude_week with raw inputs beside the score (QB view carries raw AND de-noised EPA/dropback; receivers carry earned vs delivered yards). Explore at /explore.
- Measured reliability: single-game QB 0.147 (vs 0.099 passer rating, 0.109 raw EPA/dropback); TE 0.449; WR 0.356 (vs 0.267 raw yards); RB 0.307 (vs 0.201 delivered scrimmage yards). QB score reaches half signal in ~6 games. Their own words: "Even the best weekly number is mostly weather."
- Design rule: "delete provable luck, never add identity." An early composite leaning on rushing identity was discarded after it ranked an MVP season 22nd at the position (their "absurdity test").
- Stated limits: adjacent bands are often noise ("the band is the claim"); receiver Altitude grades opportunity earned, not catch outcomes; can't see pass-protection assignments, untargeted coverage, or pre-snap gravity.
- Related metrics on the card: EPA per dropback, CPOE, EPA (nflverse play-by-play).
