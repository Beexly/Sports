# Ingest-and-Learn Doctrine (2026-09-28, founder — HARD)

## The rule
**DO NOT CLAIM THINGS ARE DEAD UNTIL TESTED AND CITED AND CONFIRMED.**

Nothing — no dataset, model, Space, repo, paper, feed, or tool — is discarded, skipped,
marked dead, or written off without the founder's consent. An item that has not been run
and inspected is **UNTESTED — QUEUED FOR EVALUATION**. That is the only verdict an
untested item may carry.

The following are **not** evidence of deadness and may never be used as the basis for a
discard verdict:
- Low likes, stars, forks, or download counts
- An old last-commit date
- "Looks like a personal project"
- A vibe, a guess, or a summary written without opening the thing

## We ingest, we learn
**WE INGEST, WE LEARN.** We do not use commercial data commercially, and we do not sell
their data. We learn from it with intelligence and reasoning — its methods, patterns,
structures, and ideas — then give our own analysis.

- Ingesting material to study it is the default posture, not a violation.
- A restrictive license (CC-BY-NC, research-only terms, gated cards) means
  **research/learn-only, never in the product** — it does NOT mean "throw it away."
  Methods remain ingestible even when data and weights are not.
- What stays out of the product: commercial redistribution of someone else's data,
  weights, or content; anything that would put proprietary material behind our paywall.
- What the product may always contain: our own analysis, our own models, our own words —
  informed by everything we studied.

## How to evaluate (the procedure, not a shortcut)
1. **Ingest** — pull it, open it, read the actual contents. Record provenance: URL, commit,
   license, date pulled.
2. **Test** — run it if it runs. Record what it did, with outputs or logs cited.
3. **Learn** — write down the method/pattern/idea worth keeping, in our own words.
4. **Verdict** — only now: USE / USE-WITH-ATTRIBUTION / RESEARCH-ONLY / QUEUED (blocked on
   something named) / REJECT (with the test evidence attached — and REJECT never counts
   toward a quota; it gets replaced).

## Why this exists
On 2026-09-28 a research sweep marked Hugging Face sports predictor Spaces "all dead" and
NFL datasets "unlicensed scraps, SKIP" without running or opening any of them. The founder
overruled it: we had thrown away far too many things without his consent. This doctrine is
the correction. It sits alongside the NGS internal-only doctrine and the public/private
surface doctrine — same shape: reason from the material, keep the product clean.

## Scope
Applies to every agent working this repo — strategists, researchers, and coding agents —
and to every kind of external material: datasets, models, Spaces, repos, papers, feeds,
APIs, and tools.
