## Topic
- Market/scenario topic
- Sport and market type
- Evidence source (internal + external)

## Claim level
- Level 0: Observation only
- Level 1: Interpretation
- Level 2: Recommendation framing

No level may include outcome guarantees or predictive superiority claims.

## Evidence required
- Source URL(s) or internal extraction record(s)
- Data recency and validity window
- Confidence notes and uncertainty bands
- Counterexamples and failure mode notes

## Sources
- Primary source files in `docs/` and approved feeds
- Any manual notes tagged with consent and review timestamp

## Uncertainty
- Model noise windows
- Data freshness caveats
- Sample-size limitations
- Market condition sensitivity

## No-bet / decision-hygiene framing
- Always include: "This is educational/decision process support, not a betting guarantee."
- Document at least one decision rule that can be falsified.
