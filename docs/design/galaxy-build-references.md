## 2026 trend checklist (from Figma's web design trends article)

3D/immersive · experimental navigation · vibrant palettes · bold typography ·
dark mode · motion/scrollytelling · gamification · neumorphism ·
retrofuturism · maximalism · collage · neo-brutalism/anti-design ·
sustainable/accessible design. AI layer: agentic chatbots, voice interfaces,
progressive lead nurturing.

Galaxy discipline still applies (see `GALAXY_2026_PUBLIC_WORLD.md`): motion is
restrained "lock-on" precision (200–300ms), honor `prefers-reduced-motion`,
every visual property carries meaning — engineered, not flashy.
