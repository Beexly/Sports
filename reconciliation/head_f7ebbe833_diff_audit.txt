    Gate G3: an unidentified effect may not be reported as a causal one
    
    The engine's causal path ends in `runInterventionTest`, which compares
    the mean residual of STRONGLY ACTIVATED games against the mean residual
    of WEAKLY ACTIVATED ones. That is an ASSOCIATION between activation
    strength and residual. It is not an intervention: nothing was
    intervened on, and a game with a strong activation may differ from a
    game with a weak one in every way that has nothing to do with the
    premise.
    
    The spec requires that a shock evaluation pass
    `runShpitserPearlIDC(ySet, xSet, zSet, gameADMG)` with ok === true
    before a causal trace can be minted, and that the spec's own Case 1 --
    a confounded bow-arc -- be REFUSED with
    "REFUSED_G3_NON_IDENTIFIABLE_HEDGE" and the conclusion
    "ASSOCIATION_ONLY".
    
    WHAT LANDED
    
      causal-claim-gate.ts: evaluateCausalClaim() runs the ID/IDC algorithm
      on the graph the estimate was computed in and decides whether the
      effect is publishable as causal. The graph is REQUIRED: a caller that
      omits it gets a refusal, not an assumption. Identifiability is a
      property of a graph, so checking it against a graph nobody estimated
      in proves nothing, and defaulting to an acyclic graph would make
      every claim identifiable and the gate worthless.
    
      runInterventionTest() now consults the gate before it can return a
      causal verdict. An unidentified effect returns INCONCLUSIVE with the
      refusal code and the hedge's enabling edges -- the measurements that
      would make the question answerable.
    
      runCausalPromotionSweep() threads the conclusion through, and reports
      causally_identified and association_only counts.
