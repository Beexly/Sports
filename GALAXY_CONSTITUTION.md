# Galaxy Sports Edge — Constitution

The Constitution is the highest authority in this repository, beneath only
the human owner's explicit instruction. Every autonomous cycle — Claude,
Codex, agent, or human collaborator — must read and obey it.

A change that violates the Constitution does not ship, even if the build
is green, the tests pass, and the feature is desirable.

## Prime Directive

Galaxy Sports Edge is a sports intelligence operating system: a
deterministic, evidence-backed, trust-safe decision environment that
helps users read the board, understand signal, respect uncertainty,
avoid false confidence, track process quality, and become sharper over
time.

The target is **best overall website/product experience of 2026** —
not "good enough to launch."

## Public / Internal / Protected — permanent identity

- **Public Galaxy** is deterministic scoring, transparent factor analysis,
  evidence-backed sports research. _Math you can read._
- **Internal Galaxy** is the operator system: cockpit surfaces, Studio,
  calibration, autonomous QA, AI-assisted workflows.
- **Protected Galaxy** is the methodology: weights, thresholds, prompts,
  prompt chains, calibration rules, aggregation formulas, source
  normalization, and internal evaluation rubrics.

The public product must not collapse into generic "AI picks." The
internal system may use AI extensively. The protected layer never leaves
the server.

## Constitutional Laws

1. **No fake data.** No fabricated picks, stats, performance, odds, win
   rates, or confidence.
2. **No fabricated evidence.** Every published analytical claim must be
   traceable to a source, snapshot, model output, or clearly labeled
   educational/demo context.
3. **No frontend-only paywalls.** Entitlement enforcement is server-side.
4. **No secrets in code.** All keys stay in environment variables.
5. **No stale-data deception.** Every data card carries freshness and
   source context. A data card without source and freshness is incomplete.
6. **No certainty language.** No "lock," "guarantee," "free money,"
   "easy money," "sure thing," "can't lose," or outcome certainty
   anywhere in shipping copy.
7. **No tout culture.** Galaxy sells disciplined interpretation, not ego.
8. **No public AI-overclaim.** Public copy is "deterministic scoring with
   evidence." Not "AI picks."
9. **No pick without a failure case.** Every pick includes why it can
   fail.
10. **No No-Bet erasure.** No-bet is a first-class decision with its own
    surface, its own card, its own autopsy.
11. **No leaderboard that rewards recklessness.** Leaderboards rank
    process quality, sample-size-adjusted discipline, CLV, no-bet
    restraint, and risk-adjusted performance — not vanity win rate.
12. **No confidential methodology client-side.** Exact weights,
    thresholds, prompts, formulas, calibration rules, and aggregation
    logic stay server-only.
13. **No admin/operator exposure.** Cockpit, Studio, calibration, source
    intelligence, internal content workflows, and operator tools stay
    auth-protected and noindex.
14. **No autonomous publishing.** No auto-publish, no auto-send, no
    external posting, no automated betting.
15. **No design spectacle that weakens decision clarity.** The entrance
    can be cinematic. The workflow must be surgical.
16. **No orphan pages.** Every page must connect into the system:
    Enter → Scan → Understand → Decide → Track → Review → Improve.
17. **No unreviewed data source.** Every sports, odds, injury, logo,
    news, or third-party source must be tracked in
    `docs/legal-ip/DATA_RIGHTS_REGISTER.md`.
18. **No undocumented major system.** If it matters, it needs a doc, an
    owner, and an audit path.
19. **No weakening of guardrails to make tests pass.** If a guardrail
    blocks a feature, redesign the feature.
20. **No stopping at "good."** Every cycle ends by identifying the next
    highest-leverage improvement and recording it in
    `docs/ops/NEXT_BEST_ACTION_LEDGER.md`.
