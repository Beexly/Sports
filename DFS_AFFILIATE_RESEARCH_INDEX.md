## Key Facts at a Glance

### Geographic Coverage
- **38 US states** + **Ontario, Canada** (sportsbook only)
- **12 prohibited states**: Alaska, Alabama, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Mississippi, Nevada, New Mexico, South Dakota, Utah, Washington
- **6 DFS-prohibited states**: Washington, Idaho, Montana, Nevada, Hawaii, Oregon
- **International**: ❌ Zero affiliate programs (UK, EU, Australia, Asia all unavailable)

### State Licensing
- **Free**: New Jersey (vendor registration)
- **$200–$500**: Colorado, Indiana, Michigan, Tennessee, Virginia
- **$1,000+**: Arizona ($1,500), West Virginia, Pennsylvania ($2,500+)
- **No requirement**: Iowa
- **Typical RevShare cost**: $2,000–$7,500+ (varies by state)

### Compliance Floor (AGA Code, March 2023)
- ❌ No "risk-free," "guarantee," "lock" language
- ✅ 21+ targeting only
- ✅ NCPG link (1-800-GAMBLER) in all promos
- ✅ "Past performance ≠ future results" disclaimer
- ✅ Geo-blocking to legal states only
- ✅ No college partnerships promoting wagering

### Offshore Sportsbook Ban
- **FanDuel**: 150-day notice + regulatory coordination + licensing suitability impact
- **DraftKings**: Immediate suspension + regulatory impact
- **GSE Action**: Explicit contract clause + termination trigger

### Commission Structures
- **DraftKings**: 25%–40% RevShare; $100–$600 CPA (state-dependent)
- **FanDuel**: 35% RevShare (flat); $25–$35+ CPA (tiered by volume)
- **Both**: 30-day attribution window; $50 min payout threshold

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do all affiliates need state licenses?
**A**: No. Only 10–15 states require affiliate licensing. Most states do not. Iowa requires none. See Part 2 matrix in DFS_AFFILIATE_RESTRICTIONS for state-by-state breakdown.

### Q: What happens if an affiliate promotes in a prohibited state?
**A**: Immediate suspension + 30-day cure period (affiliate must fix geo-blocking). If not cured, termination + operator notification. See AFFILIATE_CHECKLIST Violation Response Flowchart.

### Q: Is the offshore sportsbook ban enforced?
**A**: Yes, strictly. FanDuel: 150-day notice + regulatory coordination. DraftKings: immediate suspension. Both operators monitor actively. Breach triggers termination + licensing suitability impact. See DRAFTKINGS_FANDUEL Part 6.

### Q: Can affiliates operate DFS in states where sportsbooks are prohibited?
**A**: Yes. DFS is legal in California, Texas, Florida (and others where sportsbooks are prohibited). Affiliates can operate DFS in 44 states; only 6 states ban DFS entirely. See DFS_AFFILIATE_RESTRICTIONS Appendix A.

### Q: How often must GSE audit affiliates?
**A**: Minimum monthly traffic audits (50 affiliates/month) + quarterly content audits + annual legal review. DraftKings/FanDuel conduct their own audits separately. See AFFILIATE_CHECKLIST Ongoing Monitoring section.

### Q: What's the most critical compliance requirement?
**A**: Offshore sportsbook co-promotion ban. Violation triggers immediate termination + regulatory licensing impact. Second: geo-blocking (traffic from prohibited states). Third: AGA Code compliance (banned phrases). See RESEARCH_SUMMARY Risk Summary.
