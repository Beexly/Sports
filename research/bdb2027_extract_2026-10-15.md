# BDB 2027 repos — transferability extract, 2026-10-15

Question asked: which features in these three repos can be stamped pre-kickoff and joined
to the frozen n=2462 games, and can any of them be scored under the current locks without
new tracking data? Answer first: **none of the three produces a margin-level predictive
feature for the frozen filter.** All three are player-level Combine→rookie studies whose
units (receivers, linemen) do not join to game margins, and whose outcomes are separation
yards and Brier loss on pressure, not CRPS on a spread residual. Detail per repo, then
the one genuinely transferable finding.

Verification: repos cloned --depth 1 to scratch at 2026-10-15; feature definitions read
from src/ and docs/ at HEAD. No scores copied into any gate attempt. walk_forward.json
untouched. Emission ZERO_OBJECT.

## 1. conormalone/BDB27 ("Combine ceiling vs max-effort routes")

Status: **Phase 0 only.** README: "**Not yet implemented** — this cycle is Phase 0 (setup)
only." notes/pending_full_run.md: full game tracking absent; only a non-representative
2023 sample for plumbing. So there are no measurement claims to transfer yet — the claim
surface is a spec.

Feature definitions (from src/combine_features.py, config-driven, no magic numbers):
- `env_vmax, env_tau, env_a0` — exponential acceleration envelope fit v(t)=vmax*(1−exp(−t/tau))
  on 10 Hz combine drill attempts, recomputed from x,y only (provided s/a/dis distrusted).
- `entry_speed, exit_speed, cod_deficit` — change-of-direction metrics on cutting drills.
- `icc_1_1` — test–retest reliability across repeated attempts; `vif_values` — collinearity;
  `shrink_hierarchical` — empirical-Bayes per-player shrinkage.
- Game side (`game_features.py`): `load_ceilings` joins the per-player ceiling table
  (env_vmax/env_a0 keyed by nfl_id) to in-game max-effort route kinematics.

Pre-kickoff stampability: Combine attempts occurred years before any NFL game; a prospect's
envelope is fixed at the draft. **Stampable in principle (draft-time constant).** Join path
exists: nfl_id → the repo's existing crosswalk chain → gsis games.

Scoreability under the locks: **No.** (a) The engine is unrun — no ceiling table outputs
exist in the repo (outputs/ is Phase-0 scaffolding; data/ gitignored). (b) The features are
per-player athletic ceilings; the frozen filter is 2,462 team margins. A QB-agnostic
11v11 aggregate of route-ceiling closeness would be a new measurement, and the repo itself
warns single drill reps repeat poorly. (c) BDB-2027 data is CC BY-NC 4.0 — the license
blocker already logged in the GSE vision stack. Transfers: the envelope-fit and
recompute-from-x,y discipline; nothing scoreable today.

## 2. will-rice/bdb-2027 ("Frame First" — Kaggle notebook is the source of truth)

Measured claims (docs/SUBMISSION_WRITEUP.md, all printed by notebooks/frame_first.ipynb):
- Outcome: **role-adjusted separation** (yards at the throw, residualized against the
  same route, alignment, coverage type, season), receiver mean over reps. Cohort: 62 WRs
  with ≥100 routes (2023–25 classes).
- Model features that are pre-kickoff/draft-time: **weight, height** (combine anthropometrics).
  Result: out-of-class (leave-one-draft-class-out) R² = 0.338 [0.07, 0.48], permutation
  p=0.001; r(weight)=−0.60, r(height)=−0.53; +10 lb at fixed height costs 0.079 yd.
- Negative results, exact: official 40 time −0.016 out-of-class, draft slot +0.006, frame
  +0.320, +tracked top speed +0.334, +position-drill tracking **drops to 0.169**; tracking
  without frame −0.079. Split-half reliability of drill reps: braking 0.19, re-accel 0.51,
  top speed 0.44 (1,529 reps, 15 drills) — the stated reason tracking adds nothing.
- Mechanism: play speed (peak speed snap→throw, same route/TTT) −0.063 yd/s per 10 lb;
  tracked combine speed predicts play speed out-of-class +0.150 where the 40 does not (−0.037).
- Trenches: DL get-off time r(weight)=+0.71, r(hoop peak speed)=−0.53, hoop adds nothing
  with weight in model (p=0.24). OL pressure-probability repeatable (0.84) but unrelated to
  any combine metric (−0.01..+0.11).

Pre-kickoff stampability: weight/height/draft slot are draft-time constants. **Stampable.**

Scoreability under the locks: **No, at the margin level.** The entire model is on WR
cohorts n=62/56 with separation as the outcome. There is no game-margin target anywhere in
the repo, and receiver-roster joins to 2,462 team margins would require a season-level
aggregation the author explicitly warns against (role-adjusted residual is "a stable trait"
per receiver, r-half=0.69-0.84 — not a per-game feature). What transfers is the
**reliability discipline**: before any combine/athletic feature enters a GSE bind, its
split-half reliability must clear a preregistered floor; their position-drill tracking at
0.19–0.51 is exactly the kind of covariate the wave binds should have rejected on test-retest
grounds. That is a falsifier rule we can adopt without new data.

## 3. Hermes-25/nfl-bdb-2027-mirror-recovery ("Relative Mirror Recovery and Rookie Protection")

Measured claims (README + paper, frozen 2025 eval: 19 rookies, 7,801 charted plays, 668
pressures, equal-player Brier loss, lower better):
- Development-mean constant **0.083513**; conventional combine **0.083684**; combine + mirror
  profile **0.084587**; mirror improvement **−0.000902**, player-bootstrap 95% [−0.001734,
  −0.000179]; refit interval [−0.002077, +0.000160]. Their headline: the mirror descriptor
  **worsened** forecast; conventional measurements barely beat a constant; adding movement
  detail made it worse.
- Feature: lateral-reversal "effective seconds of speed-relative movement loss" (integrated
  velocity shortfall over 0.5 s after a sign-crossing reversal, normalized by incoming speed;
  5-frame quadratic smoothing, coordinate-derived velocity). Eligibility: ≥50 charted
  observations, possession-team O-line alignments, explicit non-null pressure labels.

Pre-kickoff stampability: mirror profile is a draft-time constant. **Stampable.**

Scoreability under the locks: **No.** Outcome is per-pass play pressure probability for 19
rookie linemen — not team margin, not CRPS. Brier on play-level pressure cannot enter the
n=2462 margin gate without a full pass→game aggregation this repo does not provide. What
transfers: a **negative prior** — fine-grained combine movement descriptors added −0.0009 to
equal-player Brier over conventional measures; a strong reason not to expect athletic-CV
features to move margin CRPS, consistent with our entropy-floor result (7.099 unreachable
from spread/total alone).
